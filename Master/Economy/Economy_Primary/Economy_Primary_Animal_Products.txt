Animal Produce

  Live / Household

    Milk
    Fresh milk of cattle, goats, or sheep.
    Spoils fast; rarely a long-haul good.
    Base: 4 bc per kg
    Bulk: 1,000 kg/m³
    Packing: pail / cask
    Pail (10 kg) → 3 sc 4 bc
    Cask (50 kg) → 16 sc 8 bc
    Cart load (800 kg) → 266 sc 8 bc

    Butter
    Base: 12 bc per kg
    Bulk: 900 kg/m³
    Packing: cask
    Small cask (20 kg) → 20 sc
    Cart load (800 kg) → 800 sc
    Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 1,500 sc

    Cheese

  Meat
All listed prices are raw carcase.
Preserved (smoked / salted / dried) is the same meat after labour and materials.

Preserve modifier
+ salt, fuel, and time.
When Preserved is checked, the meat or fish is treated as salted, smoked, or dried.
Apply +80% to +120% to the raw price (use +100% as default).
