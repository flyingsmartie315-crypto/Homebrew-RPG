# Primary goods — Earths and Salts
# Law: Economy_Primary_Goods.txt (currency, bulk, packing)
# Base unit: 1 kg unless the line says otherwise.
# 1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc. Cart 800 kg / 2.5 m³. Heavy cart 1,500 kg / 4 m³. Barge 12,000 kg / 30 m³.

Earths and Salts

  Clay
  Base: 2 bc per kg
  Bulk: 1,600 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 133 sc 4 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 250 sc

  Salt
  Base: 18 bc per kg
  Bulk: 1,200 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (20 kg) → 30 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 1,200 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 2,250 sc

  Peat
  Base: 2 bc per kg
  Bulk: 350 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 133 sc 4 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,400 kg, volume) → 233 sc 4 bc
