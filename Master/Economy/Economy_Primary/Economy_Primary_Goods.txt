# Economy_Primary_Goods.txt
# Router and format law for all primary goods.
# Do not add a good here. Add it to its category file.
# Status: DRAFT FORMAT GUIDE.

HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
Prices shown are base market values and are subject to ±50% (or greater) fluctuation by region, season, supply, and demand.
Base unit is 1 kg wherever practical. Larger lots are provided for convenience.

Bulk is packed density for the good in its listed packing.
A vehicle is full at whichever comes first: weight or volume.
Standard cart: 800 kg or 2.5 m³
Heavy cart: 1,500 kg or 4 m³
River timber barge: 12,000 kg or 30 m³
A "cart load" is one standard cart.
A "heavy-cart load" is one heavy cart.
A "barge-load" is one river timber barge.

Packing
The Bulk figure already includes the listed packing.
Use the modifiers below only when the good is packed differently from its listed method.

Sacks
+2% weight. +5% volume.
Default for grain, salt, charcoal, dried meat, dried fish.

Baskets
+15–25% volume.
Default for eggs, berries, greens, moss, and anything that must breathe or not be crushed.

Casks / Crates
+8–12% weight.
Volume is the cask's outside measure.
Default for butter, tallow, pitch, milk, and breakable dry goods.

Loose pile
No extra penalty.
Default for ore, coal, stone, bricks, clay, peat, and logs.

--------------------------------------------------
BASELINE AND INTACT-UNIT GOODS
--------------------------------------------------
Primary goods are the baseline.
Most tickets are bulk commodities: value is per kilogram,
and any kilogram of the same grade is interchangeable.

Forestry and Stone are the exception.
A workshop’s use of timber or stone often requires a single
intact unit — a log of sufficient length and diameter,
a beam, a stave blank, a freestone block of stated size.
A pile of equivalent weight that has been broken, checked,
or cut short is not the same good.

For Forestry and Stone entries:
- Keep the Base per kg for bulk trade and cart calculations.
- Add an Intact Unit line when the workshop value depends
  on the piece remaining whole.
- State the minimum dimensions or the named blank
  (e.g. “Stave blank: 1.2 m × 15 cm”, “Structural log: 6 m+”).
- Note that broken or undersized pieces fall back to
  the bulk kg price or to a lower grade (firewood, rubble).
- Convenience lots may list both a cart-weight price
  and a price per intact piece.

All other primary categories remain pure bulk kg tickets.
Do not apply the intact-unit rule to grain, ore, salt,
or forage unless a later lock says otherwise.

--------------------------------------------------
FORMAT — PRIMARY GOOD ENTRY
--------------------------------------------------
Every primary good in a category file follows this block.
Empty lines are allowed. Do not invent fields.

Name
  One line. The common market name.
  Optional second line for clarification or lock
  (e.g. “The stone brass is made from. Not a dye.”).

Base
  Price per kg (or per stated unit).
  Use bc, sc, or gc as appropriate.
  Example: Base: 8 bc per kg
  Example: Base: 1 sc 2 bc per kg

Bulk
  Packed density in kg/m³.
  Example: Bulk: 2,200 kg/m³

Packing
  The default method already included in Bulk.
  One of: sack, basket, cask / crate, loose pile,
  or a named exception.

Convenience lots (optional)
  Pre-calculated prices for common trade sizes.
  Always derive from Base. Do not invent a second price.
  Examples:
    Sack (20 kg) → 30 sc
    Cart load (800 kg) → 533 sc 4 bc
    Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 1,000 sc
    Small lot (10 kg) → 11 sc 8 bc

Notes (optional)
  Scarcity, use, hazard, or cross-reference.
  Keep to one or two lines.
  Do not put processing steps here. Those belong in
  Economy_Processed_*.txt or Economy_Finished_*.txt.

Intact Unit (Forestry and Stone only)
  Optional but required when workshop utility depends
  on the piece remaining whole.
  State the minimum size or the named blank.
  Example: Intact Unit: Structural log, 6 m+ length, 30 cm+ diameter.
  Example: Intact Unit: Freestone block, 60 × 30 × 30 cm.
  Broken or short pieces use the Base kg price only.

--------------------------------------------------
RULES
--------------------------------------------------
- Do not add a good to this file. Add it to its category file.
- Base unit is 1 kg unless the entry explicitly says otherwise.
- Cart-load and heavy-cart figures must match the vehicle limits above.
- If volume fills the cart before weight, note it
  (e.g. “Heavy-cart load (1,400 kg, volume)”).
- Prices are base market. Regional and seasonal modifiers
  are applied at the table, not written into the entry.
- Primary goods are unprocessed or minimally processed.
  Smelted metal, milled flour, tanned leather, grained powder,
  and finished arms are not primary goods.
- Forestry and Stone may carry an Intact Unit line.
  All other categories are bulk kg only.
