  Oak
  Mixed and red oak. The common structural ticket.
  Not white oak. Wet work and tight cooperage use White Oak.
  Base: 0.7 bc per kg
  Bulk: 650 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 46 sc 8 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 87 sc 6 bc
  Barge-load (12,000 kg) → 700 sc
  Monumental pillar: 2,500 kg at 1.5× → 218 sc 9 bc

  White Oak
  Closed grain. Takes water and a tight cask.
  Frames, treenails' rival is locust; this is the plank and the stave.
  Not a bow-wood.
  Base: 1 bc per kg
  Bulk: 750 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 66 sc 8 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 125 sc
  Barge-load (12,000 kg) → 1,000 sc

  Hickory
  Shock wood. Handles, spokes, axe hafts, mauls.
  Not a hull timber. Small clear lengths.
  Base: 1.4 bc per kg
  Bulk: 830 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile / wrapped blanks
  Cart load (800 kg) → 93 sc 4 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 175 sc
  Selected handle lot (20 kg) → 2 sc 4 bc

  Black Locust
  Ground-contact wood. Posts, pins, treenails, sills in the wet.
  Hard on a saw. Splits clean. Not a bow. Not a mast.
  Base: 1.2 bc per kg
  Bulk: 770 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 80 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 150 sc
  Barge-load (12,000 kg) → 1,200 sc

  Cornelian Cherry
  Small tree. Not saw-timber. Spear, javelin, peg, mallet.
  Fruit is a tart forage, not this ticket.
  Base: 2 bc per kg
  Bulk: 950 kg/m³
  Packing: wrapped blanks
  Workshop lot (20 kg) → 3 sc 4 bc
  No barge-lot. The stick is the good.

  Yew
  Base: 2 sc 6 bc per kg
  Bulk: 700 kg/m³
  Packing: wrapped / loose
  5 kg bow-stave → 12 sc 6 bc
  20 kg workshop lot → 50 sc

  Svart-Viðr / Svartwood
  Dauðavík fault-ribbons only. Not a blanket forest.
  Grade follows proximity to the live geothermal line and how much
  vent-metal the wood has taken up. Same tree. Different cuts.
  Bark and sawdust sicken unless wet-wrapped. Burns foul.
  Do not book any grade as charcoal-wood.

  Rim / pale (outer ribbon, cool side)
    Slightly denser than ash. Hull strakes, deck, light frames.
    A whole hull may be this grade. Heart members still named.
    Base: 3 bc per kg
    Bulk: 600 kg/m³

  Mid (ribbon body)
    Waterline planks, knees, ordinary frames.
    Base: 5 bc per kg
    Bulk: 720 kg/m³

  Heart / vent (closest to the boil, highest mineral)
    Keel, stem, stern, tiller, durable hafts.
    Not a whole hull. Too heavy and too glassy.
    Base: 8 bc per kg
    Bulk: 850 kg/m³

  Packing: loose pile / wrapped members
  Cart load of rim (800 kg) → 200 sc
  Cart load of heart (800 kg) → 533 sc 4 bc
  Heart member blank (200 kg at 2× the heart rate) → 266 sc 8 bc
  No barge-lot of heart off the island unless a Jarn hull is already in the water.
  Rim strakes may travel as cargo. Heart does not travel as a pile.

  Svart-bark (unworked)
  Same tree. Vent-metals in the skin.
  Not a timber. Not a dye until someone who knows the cost works it.
  Base: 2 bc per kg
  Bulk: 300 kg/m³
  Packing: wet-wrap / basket
  Basket (10 kg) → 1 sc 8 bc
  Working it dry is a Hazard, not a workshop day.

  Charcoal
  Base: 6 bc per kg
  Bulk: 280 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (15 kg) → 7 sc 6 bc
  Cart load (700 kg, volume) → 350 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,120 kg, volume) → 560 sc
  Barge-load (8,400 kg, volume) → 4,200 sc

  Resin / Pitch

  Bulk: 1,100 kg/m³
  Packing: cask
  Cask (20 kg) → 20 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 800 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 1,500 sc
