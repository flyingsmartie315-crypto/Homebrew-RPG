Forestry

Primary timber remains a bulk kg ticket for mixed or broken wood.
Workshop value for structural, stave, mast, and blank work
requires an intact unit. That unit is generated from the living tree.

--------------------------------------------------
SPECIES LINK
--------------------------------------------------
Each timber species ticket points to one or more Flora_ entries.
Flora files hold Domain, Biome, HSI, Rarity, and Use.
This file holds the economic output model.

When a stand is felled, the GM (or a generator) uses the
species model below. The model takes base diameter (DBH)
and returns approximate intact outputs on a bellcurve.

Do not invent new species here. Add the plant to the
correct Flora_[Biome].txt first, then add or update
the output model in this file.

--------------------------------------------------
INTACT OUTPUT MODEL
--------------------------------------------------
Base diameter (DBH) is measured at breast height.
Outputs are approximate intact pieces a workshop can use.
Broken, checked, or short wood falls back to the bulk kg price
of that species (or to firewood).

Distribution
  For a given DBH band the intact yields follow a simple bellcurve.
  Most trees produce the modal (typical) output.
  Fewer produce the low or high extremes.
  The three columns are Low / Modal / High.

Yield types (examples)
  - Structural log (length × small-end diameter)
  - Stave blank
  - Mast / spar
  - Beam blank
  - Firewood / bulk kg only (when the tree is too small or flawed)

A single tree may yield more than one intact piece
if the species and DBH allow (e.g. one structural log + staves).

--------------------------------------------------
SPECIES MODELS (DRAFT)
--------------------------------------------------
DBH bands in cm. Yields are approximate intact counts or sizes.
Low / Modal / High.

Oak (Flora_Temperate, Flora_Oceanic, etc.)
  Use: structural, stave, beam
  20–30 cm:  Low: bulk only / Modal: 1 short beam / High: 1 beam blank
  30–45 cm:  Low: 1 beam / Modal: 1 structural log (4–6 m) / High: 1 log + staves
  45–60 cm:  Low: 1 log / Modal: 1 long structural (6–8 m) + staves / High: 2 logs or mast-grade
  60 cm+:    Low: 1 long log / Modal: mast or multiple beams / High: exceptional clear
  Base kg price remains the species ticket below.
  Intact premium is negotiated or taken from the convenience line.

Pine / Fir
  Use: mast, spar, light structural, pitch
  20–30 cm:  Low: bulk / Modal: 1 spar / High: 1 light mast
  30–40 cm:  Low: 1 spar / Modal: 1 mast (moderate) / High: clean spar + poles
  40 cm+:    Low: 1 mast / Modal: long mast / High: exceptional spar
  Master-mast premium already noted on the Pine ticket.

Ash
  Use: haft, handle, light structural
  15–25 cm:  Low: bulk / Modal: haft blanks / High: multiple hafts
  25–35 cm:  Low: hafts / Modal: 1 light beam + hafts / High: clean beam
  35 cm+:    Low: 1 beam / Modal: structural + hafts / High: exceptional

Beech
  Use: general timber, mast in good years
  Similar band structure to Oak but lower intact length yield.
  Prefer bulk or short beam unless the tree is clear.

Birch
  Use: light timber, bark, fuel
  Rarely yields long structural intact units.
  Most output is bulk kg or short blanks.
  Bark may be a separate small-ticket by-product.

White Oak
  Use: tight cooperage, wet-work plank, stave
  Follows Oak bands but with higher stave yield in the modal and high columns.
  Short or checked wood loses the cooperage premium.

--------------------------------------------------
EXISTING BULK TICKETS
--------------------------------------------------
The kg prices below remain the baseline for broken,
mixed, or non-intact wood. Intact units use these
prices as the floor and add the piece premium
when the model produces a usable blank.

  Timber (mixed)
  Base: 0.2 bc per kg
  Bulk: 450 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 13 sc 4 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 25 sc
  Barge-load (12,000 kg) → 200 sc

  Pine / Fir
  Base: 0.25 bc per kg
  Bulk: 450 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 16 sc 8 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 31 sc 3 bc
  Barge-load (12,000 kg) → 250 sc
  Master-mast: 2,000 kg at 1.5× → 62 sc 6 bc
  Intact Unit: see species model above

  Birch
  Base: 0.3 bc per kg
  Bulk: 500 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 20 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 37 sc 6 bc
  Barge-load (12,000 kg) → 300 sc
  Intact Unit: rare; mostly bulk or short blanks

  Alder
  Base: 0.3 bc per kg
  Bulk: 450 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 20 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 37 sc 6 bc
  Barge-load (12,000 kg) → 300 sc

  (Further species — Oak, White Oak, Ash, Beech, Elm, Hickory, etc. —
   keep their existing Base/Bulk/Packing lines and gain an
   Intact Unit pointer to the model above on next edit.)

--------------------------------------------------
RULES
--------------------------------------------------
- Flora_ file is the species authority for presence and biome.
- This file is the economic output authority.
- DBH bellcurve is an approximation for play, not a forestry table.
- One tree → zero or more intact units + remainder as bulk kg.
- Siege and architecture jobs that call for “structural timber”
  or “stave blanks” should draw from the intact model,
  not from generic mixed kg.
- Charcoal and firewood may be produced from the bulk remainder
  or from species that rarely yield intact units.

--------------------------------------------------
NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
Model is draft. Bands and yields can be tightened per species
once more Flora entries are locked.
Add new species models only after the plant exists in a Flora_ file.
Intact Unit line satisfies the distinction locked in
Economy_Primary_Goods.txt.


--------------------------------------------------
REFINED INPUT → OUTPUT (UPDATE)
--------------------------------------------------
Living tree (Flora species + DBH)
  → Intact yields (structural log, beam blank, stave blank,
     mast/spar, haft blank, knee) on the bellcurve
  → Remainder as bulk kg

Bulk kg
  → Ordinary mixed timber ticket only
  → Cannot feed structural, stave, mast, or haft forms

Intact structural log / beam blank
  → Input to Processed_Woodwork hewn beam or long plank

Intact stave blank
  → Input to Processed_Woodwork cooper’s staves

Intact mast/spar
  → Input to Processed_Woodwork spar / pole blank
  → Master-mast premium stays on the Pine/Fir ticket

Intact haft blank
  → Input to Processed_Woodwork shaft / oar / handle blanks
  → Also the preferred input for Finished_Arms hafts

Knee
  → Rare. Direct to ship or specialist work.
