Stone and Volcanic Products

Primary stone remains a bulk kg ticket for rubble, broken stone,
and mixed quarry waste.
Workshop and architectural value often requires a single intact unit —
a freestone block, ashlar blank, slate sheet, or marble slab
of stated size and soundness.
Broken, checked, or undersized pieces fall back to the bulk kg price
or to rubble.

--------------------------------------------------
INTACT UNIT MODEL
--------------------------------------------------
Unlike timber, stone has no living diameter.
Intact yield is determined by quarry face, bed, and the size
of sound stone that can be freed without fracture.

For play, each stone type uses size bands.
Most blocks fall in the modal band.
Fewer are small/flawed (low) or exceptionally large and clear (high).

Yield types
  - Rubble / bulk kg only
  - Small block or sett
  - Freestone / ashlar blank (stated dimensions)
  - Large structural block
  - Specialty (slate sheet, marble slab, millstone blank)

A quarry day may produce a mix: some intact blanks + remainder as bulk.

--------------------------------------------------
STONE TYPE MODELS (DRAFT)
--------------------------------------------------
Sizes are approximate finished or rough-blank dimensions.
Low / Modal / High.

Common Stone
  Use: rubble, wall-core, rough foundation
  Most output: bulk kg or small irregular blocks
  Intact: occasional rough foundation block (40–60 cm)
  Rarely produces clean ashlar. Default to bulk price.

Granite
  Use: heavy foundation, millstones, durable paving
  Small: sett or rough block
  Modal: foundation block or millstone blank
  High: large clear structural block
  Hard to work. Intact premium is real but labour is high later.

Slate
  Use: roofing sheets, damp-proof courses
  Low: broken or thick waste (bulk)
  Modal: workable sheets
  High: large, thin, even sheets
  Intact Unit: roofing sheet of useful size.
  Broken slate is bulk or fill.

Marble
  Use: fine facing, carving, monuments
  Low: flawed or small block (near bulk price)
  Modal: carving or facing blank
  High: large clear slab
  Intact Unit: sound block free of major veins or cracks.
  Highest premium of the common stones.

Basalt
  Use: foundation, paving, heavy wall
  Mostly bulk or rough blocks.
  Intact large blocks possible but heavy to move.
  Pumice is almost always bulk (light, abrasive, or fill).

Obsidian
  Use: edges, tools, specialist blades
  Intact Unit: nodule or blank large enough to knap.
  Small chips are bulk or waste.
  Wrapped / crate packing already noted.

--------------------------------------------------
EXISTING BULK TICKETS
--------------------------------------------------
The kg prices below remain the baseline for rubble,
broken stone, and mixed quarry output.
Intact units use these prices as the floor and add
a piece premium when the model produces a usable blank.

  Common Stone
  Base: 3 bc per kg
  Bulk: 2,200 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 200 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 375 sc
  Intact Unit: rare; mostly rubble or rough foundation block

  Granite
  Base: 6 bc per kg
  Bulk: 2,700 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 400 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 750 sc
  Intact Unit: foundation block or millstone blank (see model)

  Slate
  Base: 8 bc per kg
  Bulk: 2,600 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 533 sc 4 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 1,000 sc
  Intact Unit: roofing sheet of useful size

  Marble
  Base: 1 sc 5 bc per kg
  Bulk: 2,700 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 1,133 sc 4 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 2,125 sc
  Intact Unit: sound carving or facing blank; large clear slab at premium

  Basalt / Pumice
  Base: 5 bc per kg
  Basalt bulk: 2,800 kg/m³
  Pumice bulk: 400 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 333 sc 4 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 625 sc
  Intact Unit: basalt rough block possible; pumice almost always bulk

  Obsidian
  Base: 2 sc per kg
  Bulk: 2,400 kg/m³
  Packing: wrapped / crate
  Bundle (5 kg) → 10 sc
  Intact Unit: nodule or knapping blank

--------------------------------------------------
RULES
--------------------------------------------------
- Bulk kg is the floor for all stone.
- Intact Unit is required language when a workshop or building
  needs a sound block, sheet, or blank of stated size.
- Broken, checked, or undersized stone uses the Base kg price only
  (or rubble rate).
- Quarry output is a mix. The model approximates the proportion
  of intact blanks versus bulk.
- Architecture, fortification, and millstone jobs that specify
  “ashlar”, “freestone”, “slate sheet”, or “millstone blank”
  draw from the intact model, not from generic kg.
- Transport of large intact blocks is often limited by piece size
  and handling, not only by cart weight.
