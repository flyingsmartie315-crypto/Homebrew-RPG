Wilderness Foraging

  Moss / Lichen
  Base: 4 bc per kg
  Bulk: 80 kg/m³
  Packing: basket
  Basket (20 kg) → 6 sc 8 bc
  Cart load (200 kg, volume) → 66 sc 8 bc
  Reindeer lichen uses this ticket. Winter graze. Slow. Not in the hot ribbons.

  Fault-moss
  Dauðavík greenhouse aisle under Svart-Viðr.
  Wrap, packing, trench-seal. Not generic moss.
  Base: 8 bc per kg
  Bulk: 80 kg/m³
  Packing: basket / wet-wrap
  Basket (10 kg) → 6 sc 8 bc
  Cart load (200 kg, volume) → 133 sc 4 bc

  Tundra sedge
  White floor between the ribbons. Poor fodder and thatch.
  Base: 1 bc per kg
  Bulk: 80 kg/m³
  Packing: bundle
  Bundle (15 kg) → 1 sc 3 bc
  Cart load (200 kg, volume) → 16 sc 8 bc

  Bog Berries
  Base: 8 bc per kg
  Bulk: 400 kg/m³
  Packing: basket
  Basket (5 kg) → 3 sc 4 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 533 sc 4 bc
  Cloudberry uses this ticket. Wet tundra pockets. Not in the hot ribbons.

  Lingonberry
  Low evergreen. Stores through Longnight. Polar / boreal / alpine forage.
  Same plant as Flora_Boreal. Not a garden strawberry.
  Base: 6 bc per kg
  Bulk: 400 kg/m³
  Packing: basket
  Basket (5 kg) → 2 sc 6 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 400 sc
