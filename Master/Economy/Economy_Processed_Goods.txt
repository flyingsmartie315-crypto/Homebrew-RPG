# Economy_Processed_Goods.txt
# Processed Goods – Base Unit: 1 kg (or single natural item)

HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
Prices shown are base market values and are subject to ±50% (or greater) fluctuation by region, quality, supply, and demand.
Base unit is 1 kg wherever practical. Larger lots are provided for convenience.

Processed goods normally sell for 2.5–6× the value of their primary inputs after labour and wastage, except for high-skill or luxury items.
