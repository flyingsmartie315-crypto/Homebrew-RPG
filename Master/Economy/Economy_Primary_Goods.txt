# Primary Goods – Base Unit: 1 kg (or single natural item)

HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
Prices shown are base market values and are subject to ±50% (or greater) fluctuation by region, season, supply, and demand.
Base unit is 1 kg wherever practical. Larger lots are provided for convenience.

Bulk is packed density for the good in its listed packing.
A vehicle is full at whichever comes first: weight or volume.
Standard cart: 800 kg or 2.5 m³
Heavy cart: 1,500 kg or 4 m³
River timber barge: 12,000 kg or 30 m³
A “cart load” is one standard cart.
A “heavy-cart load” is one heavy cart.
A “barge-load” is one river timber barge.

Packing
The Bulk figure already includes the listed packing.
Use the modifiers below only when the good is packed differently from its listed method.

Sacks
+2% weight (cloth and ties).
+5% volume (voids and slack).
Default for grain, salt, charcoal, dried meat, dried fish.

Baskets
+15–25% volume.
Default for eggs, berries, greens, moss, and anything that must breathe or not be crushed.

Casks / Crates
+8–12% weight (wood).
Volume is the cask’s outside measure.
Default for butter, tallow, pitch, and breakable dry goods.

Loose pile
No extra penalty.
Default for ore, coal, stone, bricks, clay, peat, and logs.
Stow it: cover or fasten ore and coal; stack bricks; wedge and bind logs; sheet clay and peat against rain.

These goods form the economic foundation of Virath.

Agriculture
Crops planted, tended, and taken from the field or grove.

  Grain
  The staple seed-crop of settled land: wheat, barley, rye, or local equivalents.
  Comes from ploughed fields in temperate and warmer regions.
  Acquired by farm labour through the agricultural year.
  Used as bread-corn, ale-corn, fodder, and the baseline of most diets.
  Base: 3 bc per kg
  Bulk: 750 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (40 kg) → 10 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 200 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 375 sc

  Root Vegetables

  Base: 2 bc per kg
  Bulk: 550 kg/m³

  Sack (30 kg) → 5 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 133 sc 4 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 250 sc

Forestry
Wood, and what is taken from the standing tree besides timber.

Note on timber value
The per-kilogram price is for sound, usable forest-cut wood.
Scraps lose any species premium and are valued as Mixed Timber or firewood.

Note on masts, spars, and monumental trunks
A great trunk is still only its species, by weight.
Intact-piece premium: +25% to +100% (1.25× to 2×).
Haulage is costed separately.
