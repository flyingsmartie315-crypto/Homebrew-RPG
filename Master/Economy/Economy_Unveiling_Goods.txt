# Unveiling Commodity & Bounty Economy

HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
Prices shown are base border-market values for fresh, typical-quality material from one adult specimen.
High-end prices reflect special buyers (alchemists, master armourers, physicians, ritualists, etc.).
Prices can fluctuate significantly (±50% or more) based on rarity, condition, location, and risk.

Since the Unveiling, hunters, vermin-slayers, and coastal raiders harvest monstrous beasts. Guilds in border towns buy raw materials cheaply, process them, and sell refined products at a premium.

Metallurgy of the Unveiling (Veil-Drop / “Valyrian” Steel)

When the False Moon swells low and heavy, comets sometimes blaze across the sky. From these falls come cold, unearthly lumps of metal known as veil-drop.

- Raw veil-drop (standard 200 g lump): 20 gc
- Refined Valyrian-style steel (1 kg ingot): requires approximately 1.6 kg raw veil-drop
- Finished high-quality 2 kg ingot: 800–1200 gc in major markets

Only specialised crucible forges can work this metal. Fortune-seekers risk their lives on False Moon nights to claim it.

Trade & Black Market
Primary hubs: border towns, coastal wrecks, ruined crypts, steppe caravans.
Many of these materials are considered heretical or restricted. False Moon nights increase both potency and danger.
