# Economy_Unveiling_Goods.txt
# Unveiling Commodity & Bounty Economy

HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
Prices shown are base border-market values for fresh, typical-quality material from one adult specimen.
High-end prices reflect special buyers (alchemists, master armourers, physicians, ritualists, etc.).
Prices can fluctuate significantly (±50% or more) based on rarity, condition, location, and risk.

Since the Unveiling, hunters, vermin-slayers, and coastal raiders harvest monstrous beasts. Guilds in border towns buy raw materials cheaply, process them, and sell refined products at a premium.

Spider Silk
Source: Silk glands of any spider (Orb Wright, Pale Lurker, Skitterling, Weber Fisher, etc.)
Unit: intact gland extraction or clean skein from one specimen
Property: Universal material. Strength and use are the same; only quantity differs by species and size.
Uses: Rope, padding, thread, lightweight work
Notes: Only useful as a material when taken from the silk glands.
       Web scrap, old sheet, or contaminated silk is waste or bulk.
       Intact Unit: the full gland yield or one clean skein from that specimen.
       Quantity scales with the beast (see Unveiling_Beasts slaughter lines).

VENOM (SPECIMEN-DERIVED)
--------------------------------------------------
No universal venom sac.
Each venom is extracted from the named beast and has its own application.
Cross-ref: Pathogens_Poisons_Curses.txt for effect, transmission, and recovery.
Intact unit: the full extracted yield from one adult specimen, sealed.
Partial or spoiled yield is bulk or waste.
Quantity scaled from the earth equivalent by body mass.

Orb Wright Venom
Earth equivalent: Orb weaver
Source: Orb Wright (adult female preferred)
Yield: 8–15 ml intact from one adult (scaled from orb-weaver venom glands)
Intact Unit: one sealed extraction
Application: Fast drop. Deer or man in seconds (see bestiary).
             Strong paralytic / neurotoxic use.
Notes: High value. Refer to Pathogens_Poisons_Curses for the named agent.

Skitterling Venom
Earth equivalent: Cellar spider
Source: Skitterling
Yield: 0.5–1.5 ml intact from one adult
Intact Unit: one sealed extraction (often pooled from several for a useful lot)
Application: Mild paralytic. Limb or child, not a stag.
             Chitin is not useful; venom is the only commercial cut.
Notes: Low yield per beast. Nest clearances produce volume.

Pale Lurker Venom
Earth equivalent: Goliath birdeater
Source: Pale Lurker
Yield: 20–40 ml intact from one large adult (scaled by mass)
Intact Unit: one sealed extraction
Application: Not yet detailed. Bestiary notes heat-steal / slow collapse rather than instant drop.
             Effect block still open in Pathogens_Poisons_Curses.txt.
Notes: Large yield. Use remains provisional until the agent is written.

Vorax Soldier Venom / Acid
Earth equivalent: Giant forest ant (soldier)
Source: Vorax Soldier
Yield: 5–12 ml acid/venom glands intact from one soldier
Intact Unit: one sealed extraction (or the paired glands)
Application: Acid burn, etching, corrosive. See bestiary acid notes.
             Distinct from spider venoms.
Notes: Soldiers only. Workers have negligible commercial yield.

General
- Extraction must be intact and sealed to hold the premium.
- Spoiled, dried, or mixed venoms drop to low or zero value.
- Applications are not interchangeable. An Orb Wright dose is not a Pale Lurker dose.
- Pathogens_Poisons_Curses.txt is the authority for what the venom does.

Metallurgy of the Unveiling (Veil-Drop / “Valyrian” Steel)

When the False Moon swells low and heavy, comets sometimes blaze across the sky. From these falls come cold, unearthly lumps of metal known as veil-drop.

- Raw veil-drop (standard 200 g lump): 20 gc
- Refined Valyrian-style steel (1 kg ingot): requires approximately 1.6 kg raw veil-drop
- Finished high-quality 2 kg ingot: 800–1200 gc in major markets

Only specialised crucible forges can work this metal. Fortune-seekers risk their lives on False Moon nights to claim it.

Trade & Black Market
Primary hubs: border towns, coastal wrecks, ruined crypts, steppe caravans.
Many of these materials are considered heretical or restricted. False Moon nights increase both potency and danger.
