# Economy_Product_Yield.txt
# How to write non-food take for a Local Region.
# Router and labour pool: Economy_Yield.txt
# Agri (food): Economy_Agri_Yield.txt
# Raw prices: Economy_Primary_Goods.txt
# Processed prices: Economy_Processed_Goods.txt
# Finished recipes: Economy/Finished_Goods/
# Site scores: Geography_Rules.txt

This is the product method. It is not a region ledger.
Filled ledgers live beside the parent Local Region file:

Local_Region_[Name]_Product_Yield.txt

If that file does not exist, use this method on the parent Local Region and its settlements.
Do not invent a basin ledger.
Do not write empty product sheets for regions that have not been ledged.


--------------------------------------------------
WHAT PRODUCT IS
--------------------------------------------------

Product is a good: raw, processed, or finished.
It is not a calorie.

Extractive
Timber, charcoal-wood, peat, stone, ore, bog-iron, clay, salt, trade-hide, pitch-wood.

Processed
Boards, charcoal, bloom or bar, dressed stone, tanned hide as a workshop take, pitch.

Finished
Only where a named workshop exists and a recipe in Finished_Goods can be filled.
A region product sheet lists capacity and place. It does not reprint every hammer.

Food that leaves the basin as a good (smoked fish, surplus grain, wool sacks) may be listed here.
Agri must already show that surplus. Do not grow it twice.


--------------------------------------------------
DO NOT USE CROP HSI ON A VEIN
--------------------------------------------------

Gates by class

Timber / charcoal-wood / pitch-wood
- Domain: Land
- Needs standing Flora 3–5 on the worked feature
- Needs a float or a road (Hydrology 3+ or a named trail)
- Law and Forest Law cut the take the same way agri forage does
- Wood-hands come from W, not from F

Peat
- Needs a Wetland feature and an edict or Economy line
- No edict → household hearths only, not a product line

Ore / bog-iron / stone
- Geology is a fact on a named feature or settlement
- No HSI range. Either the vein is written or it is not
- Pit-hands come from W
- Output is limited by hands and by the place, not by Flora

Salt / clay
- Named pan, pit, or bed. Same rule as ore: written or not

Trade-hide / fur
- Starts from agri trap and slaughter offtake
- Only the share that leaves the house is product

Processing
- Needs a named place (saw-yard, kiln, bloomery, tannery, dock-yard)
- Needs fuel. Charcoal cannot exceed the wood given to the pits
- Conversion wastes matter. Use Economy_Processed_Goods.txt (about a third to half of a log is lost to slab and sawdust; metals follow that file)

Finished goods
- Recipe + workshop + labour from W
- If Architecture, Armour, Clothing, Household, or Transport files are empty, do not invent a finished line beyond Tools and Arms already written


--------------------------------------------------
LABOUR POOL
--------------------------------------------------

Same W as Economy_Yield.txt.

W = Population midpoint × 0.40

Book product hands out of W after agri field-hands and named working fishers.
If the parent Economy block says timber-hub, mine, or kiln first, book those product hands before extra field-hands.

Do not create a second working population for the woods.

A collier is not also a full plough-hand.
A pit village (Askgruv-style) spends most of its W on product. Its agri sheet should show thin fields and imported grain, not a second labour census.


--------------------------------------------------
OUTPUT SHAPE (for a future instance file)
--------------------------------------------------

1. Region and year-basis (Harvest, unless the Log says otherwise)
2. Labour pool W, then the split already taken by agri
3. Extractive list
   - Good
   - Feature or settlement
   - Hands
   - Raw take
4. Processing nodes
   - Place
   - Input (must exist on the extractive list)
   - Output
   - Fuel
   - Waste
5. What leaves the region (gate, road, shore)
6. What is burned in-country (Kolby forges, hearths, yards)
7. Unveiling goods only if a site actually yields them

Report weights in kg, then cart / barge lots only when a road is in question.


--------------------------------------------------
ROUGH HAND YIELDS (stub)
--------------------------------------------------

Use these until a region sheet replaces them.
They are order-of-magnitude, not a census.

Good                         kg / hand / year     Notes
Timber, raw log              8,000–12,000         Needs float or sled
Sawn board from those logs   ~ half the log mass  Saw-yard place required
Charcoal                     1,500–2,500          From wood allocated to pits
Peat                         6,000–10,000         Wetland + edict
Bog-iron ore                 1,000–2,000          Named fen-work, guarded
Hard-rock ore                800–1,500            Named pit
Bloom / bar iron             from ore as processed file
Trade-hide                   agri offtake × share that leaves

Do not run these numbers into a Kannavald total until the instance sheet exists.

--------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT DO
--------------------------------------------------

- It does not feed anyone. That is agri.
- It does not price the take.
- It does not assign who owns the pit or the barge.
- It does not treat region Human Density as a copper grade.
- It does not let processing exceed the raw take or the fuel.
- It does not count a veil-drop fall as annual yield.
