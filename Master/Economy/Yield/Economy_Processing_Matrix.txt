# Economy_Processing_Matrix.txt
# How a processed good is made.
# Prices stay on Economy_Processed_Goods.txt.
# Regional hands and places: Economy_Product_Yield.txt.
# Wage bands: Economy_Labour.txt.
# Piece objects (swords, axes): Finished Goods recipes, not this file.

This file does not sell anything.
It sets the floor under the listed price and the plant that must exist before the first honest batch.

Unit is 1 kg or 1 L of output unless a batch is named.
Labour days are at the listed Tech. Apply the tech table before you cost a year.


--------------------------------------------------
TECH LEVELS
--------------------------------------------------

Virath’s ceiling is early-modern. It is not even.

T0  House
Hand tools, open fire, family labour. Quern, kettle, crock, smoke-rack.

T1  Craft
Dedicated shed. Journeyman kit. Village oven, pot-kiln, hand-press, single bloomery.

T2  Mill
Water, wind, or a large standing team. Millstones, saw-pit, fulling-stocks, proper tan-pits.

T3  Works
Concentrated early-modern plant. Ropewalk, dye-house, battery of hearths, ship-yard try-works.

T4  Rare
Restricted specialist. Crucible steel, fine silk city-work, lens and clockwork shops.
Not a regional default. Exists only where a file names it.

Default
Hamlet / village: T0–T1
Town: T1, T2 if a mill-race or saw-pit is written
City / rich coast: T2–T3
Frontier and deep wood: T0–T1 unless a named works says otherwise
T4: written or not at all

A place cannot skip a level for that process.
A place cannot work above the world ceiling.
Gunpowder shops and plate-armour works are T3 and uncommon. They do not unlock T4.

What a level changes (from the row’s listed Tech)

                 Labour days    Waste     Year throughput    Plant cost
T0 → T1          ×0.75          ×0.90     ×1.3               ×2
T1 → T2          ×0.65          ×0.80     ×2.0               ×3
T2 → T3          ×0.70          ×0.85     ×1.8               ×3
T3 → T4          ×0.80          ×0.75     ×1.2               ×5  and specialist labour

If the row says Min T2, T0 and T1 cannot make a market lot.
House use may still exist one level below Min, at worse waste, and not as product.

Below Min: no product line.
Above listed Tech: apply the multipliers; do not invent a new good.


--------------------------------------------------
HOW TO READ A ROW
--------------------------------------------------

[Good]
From: inputs as named on Primary or Processed lists
Input: qty to make 1 kg or 1 L
Fuel / store: charcoal, wood, salt, honey, tannin
Labour: band × days per batch (batch size named)
Facility: the shed-word Product Yield should use
Tech: listed / min / max
Time: hands-on + wait
Waste: mass lost
Capital:
  Kit: journeyman tools (sc)
  Plant: building and fixed works
  Plant cost: order of magnitude at listed Tech
  Life: years
Output: 1 kg or 1 L
Floor: holds / house-labour / bottleneck

Floor means:
input + fuel + (days × wage) + wear ≲ listed price at listed Tech.
Wear = plant cost ÷ (life-years × honest annual kg).
House-labour = the listed price only works if family time is not fully cashed.
Bottleneck = price sits well above floor; skill, rarity, or a T4 gate.


Wage midpoints for costing
Rural common 3 bc
Urban common 5 bc
Basic skilled 30 bc
Higher skilled 65 bc
Hazardous skilled 50 bc

--------------------------------------------------
USING THIS WITH A REGION
--------------------------------------------------

1. Read the settlement or region Economy block for the plant words (mill, saw-pit, bloomery, tannery).
2. Assign Tech from that plant and the table at the top.
3. If Tech < Min, that good is house-only or absent.
4. Apply labour and waste multipliers from the starting listed Tech.
5. Book hands from W (Economy_Yield.txt). Do not invent a second workforce.
6. Fuel (charcoal, wood) must exist on the product sheet or as an import.
7. Finished Arms and Tools then take Wrought Iron, Steel, and shaft-blanks from this ladder. They do not remake the bloomery.


--------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT DO
--------------------------------------------------

- It does not change the listed sale price by itself.
- It does not place a water-mill on a dry ridge.
- It does not let T4 appear because a player wants cheap fine steel.
- It does not cost a sword. That is Economy_Finished_Arms.txt.
- It does not replace Local_Region product ledgers.
