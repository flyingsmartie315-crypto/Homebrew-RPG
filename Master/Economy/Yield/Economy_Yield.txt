# Economy_Yield.txt
# Router for yearly take.
# Agri method: Economy_Agri_Yield.txt
# Product method: Economy_Product_Yield.txt
# Prices: Economy_Primary_Goods.txt and Economy_Processed_Goods.txt
# Site scores: Geography_Rules.txt and Bestiary_Rules.txt

This file does not calculate bushels or boards.
It says which file to open and who owns the baseline.


--------------------------------------------------
LOAD ORDER
--------------------------------------------------

1. Roleplay_AI_Loader.txt if this is a live scene.
2. The Local Region file for the place.
3. Local_Region_[Name]_Agri_Yield.txt if it exists.
4. Local_Region_[Name]_Product_Yield.txt if it exists.
5. Settlement files only as modifiers (a mine, a kiln, a dock, a gate).
6. Campaign_Log.txt if the year has already changed.

If an instance yield file does not exist, say so.
Then run the matching method file against the parent Local Region.
Do not invent a finished basin ledger.


--------------------------------------------------
WHERE INSTANCE FILES LIVE
--------------------------------------------------

Same folder as the parent Local Region.

Master/Geography/Geographic_Regions/[Geographic Region]/

Do not put yield sheets under Settlements/.

Missing file means “no baseline yet,” not “take is zero.”


--------------------------------------------------
WHAT EACH SHEET OWNS
--------------------------------------------------

Agri
What goes into a mouth or a byre this year.
Crops, burgage, forage, trap-meat, fish-as-food, yard, herd.
Method: Economy_Agri_Yield.txt

Product
What is counted as a good: raw, processed, or finished.
Timber, charcoal, peat, ore, bog-iron, stone, trade-hide, pitch, boards, bars.
Method: Economy_Product_Yield.txt

Same physical thing may appear twice.
- Fish eaten → agri. Fish smoked and sent through a gate → product, surplus only.
- Hide on a hamlet wall → agri by-part. Hide in a warehouse → product.
- Grain eaten → agri. Grain in a barge → product, and agri must already show that surplus.

Never invent a product food line that agri did not grow.


--------------------------------------------------
BASELINE IS THE LOCAL REGION
--------------------------------------------------

The basin is the ledger.
Settlements are taps and workshops.

A settlement Economy block may say:
- Takes one-third of regional charcoal.
- Grows no grain.
- Smelts the Askflow ore.

It does not reprint the country’s totals.


--------------------------------------------------
ONE LABOUR POOL
--------------------------------------------------

Regional working hands W = Population midpoint × 0.40

W is the full-time working share of the basin.
Children, the old, house-work, burgage, forage, and ordinary yard-herd are the rest.
They are not booked as full hands.

Split W. Do not add a second Kannavald.

Typical claims on W
- Agri field-hands F
- Named working fishers (if the Economy block names docks or a fleet)
- Product wood-hands, pit-hands, colliers, peat-cutters
- Product process-hands (saw, kiln, bloomery, dock-yard)
- Garrison, toll, and service when those are the point of a place

A body cannot be a full plough-hand and a full collier.
If agri and product would together exceed W, cut product first unless the parent Economy block says the place is a pit, kiln, or timber-hub first.

Field-hands F inside agri are a share of W, not Population × field fraction × 0.40 stacked on top of W.

WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT DO
--------------------------------------------------

- It does not price goods.
