# Economy_Livestock.txt
# Livestock, Beasts of Burden, War Mounts, Game & Sea-Take

HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
Prices shown are base market values for healthy adult animals and are subject to significant regional and seasonal fluctuation (±50% or more).
Livestock requires ongoing space, feed, water, and care. Neglect rapidly reduces value or kills the animal.

Live / Household output is a healthy adult’s yearly take in milk, eggs, wool, and the like.
It assumes ordinary keep, not a prize dairy or a starved yard.
Wool is taken in life. Milk and eggs are taken in life. None of these are slaughter yields.

Slaughter turns a live beast into the goods in Economy_Primary_Goods.txt.
Meat weights are carcase meat, priced raw or preserved from that file.

How to price a year
Add the live output at Primary Goods rates.
How to price a kill
Add the slaughter parts at Primary Goods rates.
The live beast is worth more than the carcase while it still works, breeds, or gives this yearly take.

UPKEEP & LOGISTICS NOTES

- Grazing land reduces feed costs for sheep, goats, cattle and horses.
- Urban animals cost more to keep; all feed is bought.
- War mounts often need dedicated grooms.
- Transporting livestock long distances incurs losses.
- Breeding stock, young, and pregnant females vary from the adult baselines.
- Gryphons, royal gryphons, and pegasus are in Economy_Unveiling_Beasts.txt.
