# Economy_Livestock.txt
# Livestock, Beasts of Burden, War Mounts & Exotic Creatures

HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
Prices shown are base market values for healthy adult animals and are subject to significant regional and seasonal fluctuation (±50% or more).
Livestock requires ongoing space, feed, water, and care. Neglect rapidly reduces value or kills the animal.

Animals are listed with:
- Purchase price (healthy adult)
- Typical upkeep (daily or monthly feed cost equivalent)
- Space / housing needs
- Primary uses

---

COMMON LIVESTOCK

Chicken
Price: 8–15 bc
Upkeep: Negligible (scraps + foraging)
Space: Minimal
Uses: Eggs, meat, feathers

Duck / Goose
Price: 15–30 bc
Upkeep: Low
Space: Access to water preferred
Uses: Eggs, meat, feathers, down

Sheep
Price: 1 sc – 1 sc 10 bc
Upkeep: 1–2 bc per day (grazing + winter feed)
Space: Pasture
Uses: Wool, meat, milk, hides

Goat
Price: 1 sc – 1 sc 15 bc
Upkeep: 1–2 bc per day
Space: Pasture or rough ground
Uses: Milk, meat, hides, brush clearing

Pig
Price: 1 sc 10 bc – 2 sc
Upkeep: 2–3 bc per day (omnivorous, can use scraps)
Space: Sty or woodland pannage
Uses: Meat, fat, hides

Cattle (Cow / Ox)
Price: 4–8 sc (oxen toward the higher end)
Upkeep: 3–5 bc per day
Space: Significant pasture
Uses: Milk, meat, hides, labour (ploughing, hauling)

Donkey
Price: 3–6 sc
Upkeep: 2–3 bc per day
Space: Modest
Uses: Pack transport, light draught

Mule
Price: 5–9 sc
Upkeep: 2–4 bc per day
Space: Modest
Uses: Superior pack and draught animal

Horse (Common / Work)
Price: 6–12 sc
Upkeep: 4–6 bc per day
Space: Stable + pasture
Uses: Riding, light cavalry, draught

WAR & QUALITY MOUNTS

Riding Horse (trained)
Price: 12–25 sc
Upkeep: 5–7 bc per day
Notes: Basic trained riding animal

Destrier / War Horse
Price: 1–3 gc
Upkeep: 8–12 bc per day
Notes: Heavy war mount. Requires experienced handler. High status.

Courser / Light War Horse
Price: 18–40 sc
Upkeep: 6–9 bc per day
Notes: Faster than a destrier, still combat-capable.

Camel (where climatically appropriate)
Price: 15–35 sc
Upkeep: 3–5 bc per day (efficient in arid regions)
Uses: Long-distance pack and riding in deserts

---


UPKEEP & LOGISTICS NOTES

- Grazing land dramatically reduces feed costs for sheep, goats, cattle and horses.
- Urban animals are significantly more expensive to keep due to the need to purchase all feed.
- War mounts and exotic beasts often require dedicated staff (grooms, handlers).
- Transporting livestock long distances incurs high losses and costs (see future Transport file).
- Slaughter value is usually 40–70% of live purchase price depending on condition and local demand.

This file covers the main categories of living animals used by societies on Virath. Specialised breeding stock, young animals, and pregnant females will vary in price from the adult baselines above.
