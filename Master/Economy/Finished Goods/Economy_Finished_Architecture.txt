# Economy_Finished_Architecture.txt
# Engine for built works. Not a stall catalogue.
# Instances live as Blueprints under the Geographic Region.
# Plant inside a [Process] shell: Economy_Processing_Matrix.txt
# Materials: Economy_Processed_Goods.txt
# Wages: Economy_Labour.txt

HEADER NOTE
A building is a recipe: mass, labour split, calendar, ticket, then MCI.
It is not a dagger. Do not sell a keep by the piece on a market stall.

1 MU = 1,000 kg of structural mass before biome translation.
Every recipe also prints kg so it talks to Processed Goods.

Cost by trade-rate, not by hat.
If one crew does two trades under one roof, book each block at that
trade's wage. Parallel days shorten the calendar. They do not cheapen
the sum.

Yard calendar. Not shipyard calendar.

Craft cap on every recipe in this file:
  Poor x0.8 / Good x1.25 / Best x2
Best is commission (named master, named scantlings).
Do not use Weaponry x10 on a house.


--------------------------------------------------
FORMULA
--------------------------------------------------

  (Scale MU + tag add-on MU) x Tier factor
        --biome default-->  local purchased kg
        --labour table-->   split days
        --culture tag-->    skin and modifiers
        --MCI-->            yearly upkeep

Haulage for imported mass is extra. It is not inside the factor.


--------------------------------------------------
FAMILY
--------------------------------------------------

Buildings
  Envelope plus rooms.
  Tags legal: Domestic, Storage, Process, Animal Shelter, Military.
  Military here means rooms on a building (slit, bar, guard-room),
  not a curtain wall.

Works
  Linear or spanning pieces.
  Tags legal: Infrastructure, Civic-as-work, Sacred-as-work,
  and Military as a work (wall, tower, gate) when it is not a
  room on a house.
  Priced per rod or per span. Do not stack Works MU onto a cottage
  as if a mill-race were a loft.

Monument
  A Building or a Work that is Palace-scale or larger, or a Work
  whose total MU is 200+.
  Same formula. Same biome defaults. It must be phased.


--------------------------------------------------
SCALE (Buildings)
--------------------------------------------------

Scale is the envelope. Tags add function. Tags do not reprint the house.

| Scale            | Base MU | What it is                         |
|------------------|---------|------------------------------------|
| Booth / shed     | 0.5     | lean-to, barrow-shed, watch-hut    |
| Cottage          | 3       | one-hearth dwelling                |
| House / workshop | 10      | urban plot, smithy shell, inn      |
| Hall / barn      | 25      | longhouse, tithe barn, small hall  |
| Works / keep     | 60      | mill-house, gatehouse, small keep  |
| Palace / temple  | 200     | seat, great temple, small cathedral|
| Cathedral /      | 500     | city cathedral, royal palace,      |
|  citadel         |         | fortress-palace                    |
| Monument         | write it| anything above 500 MU. Name the kg.|

Pick one Scale per blueprint.
Palace / temple and above are multi-year by default.
See MULTI-YEAR. Do not pretend a cathedral is a 60 MU keep.


--------------------------------------------------
FUNCTIONAL TAGS
--------------------------------------------------

Add-on MU is added once per tag unless the blueprint says a second
loft, a second stall-range, or a second plant.

Buildings

  [Domestic]           +2 MU
    Quarters, hearth, light kitchen draught.
    A Cottage or Hall that is already a dwelling still takes this
    tag if people sleep in it. The add-on is the fit-out, not a
    second house.

  [Storage]            +3 MU
    Loft, cellar, toll-bin, granary floor.

  [Process]            +2 MU shell only
    The walls around a plant.
    The plant itself is the matching row in Economy_Processing_Matrix.txt
    (bloomery, kiln, saw-pit, millstones, tan-pits, ropewalk).
    Architecture does not invent a second kiln recipe.
    If the matrix plant does not exist at this site's Tech, the
    tag is illegal.

  [Animal Shelter]     +4 MU
    Stalls, pens, byre. Not a barn Scale by itself unless the
    envelope is Hall / barn.

  [Military]           +3 MU
    Arrow-slit, iron bar, guard-room on this envelope.
    Not a tower. A tower is Works.

  [Sacred]             +8 MU
    Sanctum, choir, shrine-fit. Not the whole cathedral.
    The envelope is Scale. This tag is the cult fit-out.
    Culture tag still supplies the skin.

  [Civic]              +6 MU
    Audience, chancery, court, archive. A palace without this
    tag is only a large [Domestic].

Works extra

  [Infrastructure] also, at monument length:
    aqueduct arcade            12 MU / rod  (channel + piers)
    canal cut (navigable)      15 MU / rod  (cut, bank, lock extra)
    lock chamber               25 MU each
    city sewer main            8 MU / rod   (vaulted)
    sewer branch               3 MU / rod
    water-castle / header      40 MU each

  [Sacred] as a work
    precinct wall, cloister walk: use Infrastructure rod rates.
    The church itself is a Building at Palace or Cathedral scale.

Works

  [Infrastructure]
    Priced per rod (3 m) or per span, not as a house tag.
    Default MU per rod:
      road paving / causeway     2 MU / rod
      retaining wall / quay      4 MU / rod
      mill-race / sluice         3 MU / rod
      bridge span (timber)       8 MU / span
      bridge span (stone)       20 MU / span
    Write the length on the blueprint.

  [Military] as a work
    curtain / bailey wall        6 MU / rod
    gatehouse                    use Scale Works / keep
    watch-tower                  15 MU each


--------------------------------------------------
MATERIAL TIERS
--------------------------------------------------

Tier is wealth and instruction. It is not a biome.

| Tier            | Factor | Who must be on the split                         |
|-----------------|--------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Utilitarian     | 0.7    | Rural common. Neighbour days. No guild.          |
| Freeholder      | 1.0    | Basic skilled carpenter or village builder.      |
| Standardised    | 1.3    | Higher skilled mason / carpenter + smith ties.   |
| Fortified       | 2.0    | Master architect / engineer on direction days.   |

Factor multiplies (Scale + tags) before biome translation.

Utilitarian is not 0 gc.
Turf, willow, fieldstone, and thatch may be unpurchased (ticket 0
on those lines). Labour still exists. Book family and neighbour
days at rural common (3 bc) even when the settlement does not pay
them in coin. MCI still eats thatch and days.
If you print 0 gc and 0 days, neglect cannot happen.

Fortified may import mass the biome does not grow.
That is the point of the tier. Charge haulage. Do not forbid
ashlar in pine country.


--------------------------------------------------
BIOME DEFAULTS
--------------------------------------------------

Read the site line from Geography (same seven scores yield uses):
  Temperature | Rainfall | Human Density | Flora | Fauna |
  Topography  | Hydrology

Translation sets the primary structural mass. It does not ban the
rest. A minority of other material is always legal.

Default primary (about 75-85% of structural kg)

  Boreal / Alpine
    Flora 4-5 and Topography 3-5
    Primary: pine / spruce / fir rounds and hewn timber.
    Footings may be fieldstone.
    Horizontal log or stave jointing is the default skin.

  Lowland / Riverine
    Flora 0-2 and Topography 0-2 and Hydrology 3-5
    Primary: brick, adobe, pise, reed thatch.
    Timber is roof-shoring and openings, not the wall.

  Arid mountain
    Rainfall 0-2 and Flora 0-2 and Topography 3-5
    Primary: dry-stone, cleaved basalt, mortarless sandstone.
    Dome or stone-slab roof is the default skin.
    Timber is an import.

Everywhere else
    Mixed. Use the higher of Flora (timber share) and Topography
    (stone share). Hydrology 4-5 pulls brick / earth if Flora is
    low. Write the split on the blueprint.

Minority (15-25%)
    Always allowed from local odds: stone in timber country,
    a roof-tree in brick country, a timber lintel in stone country.

Import
    Legal at Freeholder+ if the road exists.
    Required for Fortified when the default cannot cut ashlar,
    fired brick, or long beams.
    Haulage is a separate line. It is not hidden in the tier factor.


--------------------------------------------------
CULTURE TAGS (stubs)
--------------------------------------------------

The culture tag is the skin. It does not change Scale.
It may change jointing, roof, openings, and a small modifier.
Name one on every blueprint. Invent no fifth until these four
have instances.

  Kannavald stave
    Stave or horizontal log. Steep birch-bark or shingle roof.
    Small openings. Smoke-bay or gable-vent.
    Modifier: +10% timber kg. -10% heating labour in winter
    if Flora 4+ (wood is cheap). Wet-rot MCI +1% if Hydrology 4+.

  Westmark close-stud
    Close-stud timber on a brick or stone plinth. Tile or
    shingle. Jetty legal in town.
    Modifier: iron ties required at Standardised+. +10% iron kg.

  Riverine pise
    Rammed earth or brick. Deep eaves. Reed or tile.
    Modifier: illegal as Fortified unless imported facing-stone.
    Hydro 4+: +2% MCI (splash and salt).

  Ashlar seat
    Dressed stone. Lead or stone-flag roof. Narrow lights.
    Modifier: Fortified default. Direction days must include
    Master architect. Haulage almost always on the ticket.


--------------------------------------------------
LABOUR SPLIT
--------------------------------------------------

Book from purchased-and-foraged kg, not from a vibe.

Direction
  Utilitarian: none, or a neighbour who has raised a roof before.
  Freeholder: Basic skilled x 5-10% of assembly days.
  Standardised: Higher skilled x 10-15% of assembly days.
  Fortified: Master architect / engineer x 10% of assembly days
             plus Higher skilled under them.

Assembly
  Utilitarian: rural common.
  Freeholder: Basic skilled carpenter / builder + common haul.
  Standardised: Basic skilled + Higher skilled mason / carpenter
                + smith for ties.
  Fortified: Higher skilled mason majority + smith + common haul.

Excavation and haulage
  Rural common on a village site. Urban common in a town yard.
  Hazardous skilled only for height, pit, or coffer.

Wages (same bands as Transport)
  Rural common              3 bc/day
  Urban common              5 bc/day
  Basic skilled            30 bc/day
  Hazardous skilled        50 bc/day
  Higher skilled           65 bc/day
  Master architect /
  Master engineer         130-200 bc/day   (Labour.txt)

A [Process] shell uses this split for the building.
The matrix row owns the days to make the first honest batch
once the shell stands.


--------------------------------------------------
TICKET
--------------------------------------------------

Sum of:
  purchased kg x catalogue rate
  + imported kg x catalogue rate
  + haulage (if any)
  + labour days x wage
  + unpurchased mass listed at 0 gc but with its days booked

Utilitarian purchased ticket may be 0 gc. Days are not 0.

Yard cap applies to the labour-and-skill part, not to the iron
in the gate.


--------------------------------------------------
EXTRACTION
--------------------------------------------------

A mine is not a crop. The vein is a Product Yield fact
(written feature, or not). Architecture does not create ore.
It creates the hole that can take it, and the plant that keeps
the hole open.

Family
  Headworks          Building. Scale Booth to House.
                     Tags: [Process] + optional [Storage] [Domestic].
                     [Process] plant is the winding / wash / first
                     roast. Matrix owns the bloomery. Architecture
                     owns the house over the hole.
  Shafts and levels  Works. Linear. Not a loft on the cottage.
  Quarry face        Works. Open cut. Cheaper than a shaft.
                     No air plant. Water still matters.

Works rates (timbered unless the blueprint says stone lining)

  Adit / drift / level     4 MU / rod
  Vertical shaft           8 MU / rod of depth
  Open quarry bench        2 MU / rod of face
  Sump                     3 MU each

Timber is structural, not firewood. Boreal default helps.
Arid stone country may line in dry-stone and cut the timber
share. It does not cut MCI to cottage levels. Ground still moves.

Optional plant
  None of these are required to declare a pit.
  Depth, water, and length make them required to work.

  Crane / whim / horse-gin     +2 MU on the headworks
    T1+. Iron and rope. Raises ore and men.
    Without it, a deep shaft is common-labour backs and a winch.

  Water pump / rag-and-chain   +3 MU  (T2)
    T3 works: chain-pump or water-wheel pump +6 MU.
    Required if Hydrology 3+ at the workings, or the shaft
    is below the local water-table written on the site.
    Without it the sump fills. Output goes to zero.

  Air pump / bellows-vent / furnace-draught
                               +1 MU  (T1) or +3 MU furnace (T2)
    Required if a level runs more than 2 rods from free air.
    Without it the face is a short shift and a death.

  Each optional plant is a [Process] shell. Days to run it
  come from W every year, not from the build split.

Audit
  A working mine takes a yearly survey (Higher skilled or
  Hazardous pit-captain, days in the MCI labour).
  The audit is how the GM learns flood, crush, and fire-damp
  before Decay hits the gate.
  No audit booked = treat as missed MCI labour that year.

Extraction MCI (overrides the tier table for shafts and levels)
  Headworks use the ordinary tier table.
  Underground Works use this, because props die faster than roofs:

    Structural timber / year     12% of timber kg in the Works
    Tools / rope / tar / year    5% of that purchased line
    Pump and gin labour          the days to run the plant
    Audit                        included in those days

  Fortified stone lining may drop timber MCI to 6%. It does not
  drop to 2%. A keep-roof and a wet adit are not the same job.

Decay underground
  Same 3 / 5 / 10 gates, with these names:
    3   props gone to rot. Tier drop. Output halved.
    5   Unsafe. Flood, crush, or gas. [Process] off.
        Recovery is 2x MCI plus a season the pit stands idle.
    10  Lost workings. Hazard / Dungeon. Vein still exists
        on the map. The hole does not.

W
  Pit-hands come from Product Yield's W rule.
  Building the shaft is Architecture days from the same W.
  You cannot raise a new level and keep full offtake in the
  same season unless W is written large enough for both.


--------------------------------------------------
MULTI-YEAR
--------------------------------------------------

The engine already has days. Multi-year is how those days meet
the settlement's labour pool.

When this fires
  Scale Palace / temple or larger.
  Any Work whose total MU is 200 or more.
  Any blueprint that writes Time in years.

W
  Same pool yield uses: W = population x 0.40.
  Architecture draws from W. So do fields and the yard.
  A cathedral that takes every mason-day starves the mill
  and the harvest. Write the draw.

Phases
  Split the structural MU into named phases. Each phase is one
  season or one year of booked days that W can actually spare.

  Typical cuts:
    1  Foundation / cut / coffer
    2  Envelope / arcade / cut of canal
    3  Roof / vault / water-tight
    4  Fit-out (Sacred, Civic, plant, lead, glass)

  A phase has its own inputs, split, and calendar.
  Sum of phases = the blueprint total. Do not invent extra mass.

Utility while unfinished
  No phase complete:         footprint only. No tag utility.
  Foundation only:           no [Domestic], [Sacred], [Process].
  Envelope up, no roof:      [Storage] at half if dry enough.
  Roof on, fit-out undone:   [Domestic] / [Sacred] / [Civic]
                             at half. [Process] still off.
  Aqueduct / canal / sewer:  water or boats only when that
                             reach is open end to end.
  A cathedral without a roof is not a temple.

Lead times that are not labour
  Foundation settle, stone seasoning, lime slake: wait.
  Same law as loft and tan-pit. Do not book masons to watch it.

Tech
  Palace / temple: min T2.
  Cathedral / citadel / city sewer / navigable canal: min T3.
  Monument aqueduct across a valley: T3, Master engineer on
  direction, Fortified or Standardised facing.
  T0-T1 settlements do not start these. They can haul for someone
  else's project.

Abandoned works
  If a year passes with no phase days and no MCI on what stands,
  the unfinished mass takes Decay as if it were a building of
  its current tier.
  At 5 points the works is unsafe to continue without a recovery
  season. At 10 the scaffolding is a Hazard site.

MCI on a monument
  Charge MCI only on completed phases.
  A half-built nave does not pay 2% of the finished cathedral.
  It pays 2% (or the unfinished-works rule above) of what is up.

Coin
  The ticket may be paid across years. That is a treasury problem,
  not a second formula.


--------------------------------------------------
MCI
--------------------------------------------------

Annual upkeep if the settlement wants the building to stay at tier.

| Tier            | Material / year      | Labour / year        |
|-----------------|----------------------|----------------------|
| Utilitarian     | 10% of structural kg | 8% of original days  |
| Freeholder      | 5%                   | 5%                   |
| Standardised    | 3%                   | 3%                   |
| Fortified       | 2%                   | 2%                   |

Culture modifiers add on top (Kannavald wet-rot, riverine splash).

Material upkeep is in the same goods the blueprint used
(thatch, shingle, lime, tar, iron ties), not a mystery "repair kit."


--------------------------------------------------
DECAY
--------------------------------------------------

If the year's MCI material or days are not allocated:
  +1 Decay Point that year.

  3 points   Tier decline. Drop one Material Tier.
             Standardised loses tile and ties and is booked as
             Freeholder until recovered. Fortified becomes
             Standardised masonry with a bad roof. Freeholder
             becomes Utilitarian. Utilitarian has no lower tier;
             go to the Unsafe gate at 5.

  5 points   Unsafe gate. The building loses its primary
             [Domestic] or [Process] utility. People move out.
             The plant cannot run. Storage and Animal may limp
             at half.

  10 points  Collapse. Struck from the census. The footprint
             becomes a Hazard / Dungeon site and takes local
             pests or worse.

Recovery
  One Decay Point costs 2 x that building's annual MCI
  (material and days) spent in a single season.
  You cannot skip from 9 to 0 in a week of hand-waving.

--------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT DO
--------------------------------------------------

- It does not list every cottage in Virath.
- It does not let [Process] exist without a matrix plant.
- It does not treat a mill-race as a bedroom.
- It does not ban stone in timber country.
- It does not print Utilitarian as 0 days.
- It does not apply Weaponry Best x10 to a keep.
- It does not run HSI as a crop. Scores here pick materials, not yield.
- It does not finish a cathedral in a season.
- It does not let a hamlet start an aqueduct.
- It does not charge full MCI on a roofless nave.
- It does not create a vein. Product Yield writes the ore.
- It does not let a deep wet shaft work without a pump.
