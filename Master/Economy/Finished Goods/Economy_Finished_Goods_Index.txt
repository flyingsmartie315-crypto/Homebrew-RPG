# Economy_Finished_Goods_Index.txt
# Pointer index for piece-priced wares

HEADER NOTE
Finished goods are sold as objects, not by the kilo.
The metal, wood, or hide in a piece is never cheaper than the processed material it was made from.
Each item is recorded as inputs + labour + modifiers + price.
Recipes live in the files below, not here.

--------------------------------------------------
RULE
--------------------------------------------------
Look up the category, then open that file.
Do not add full recipes to this index.

--------------------------------------------------
FILES
--------------------------------------------------

Economy_Finished_Tools.txt
Farm, yard, wood, and household tools.
Hammers, axes, saws, tongs, knives, bits, and trade kits.
Not knightly arms.

Economy_Finished_Arms.txt
Weapons.
Swords, spears, bows, pollaxes, and other fighting pieces.
Steel heads and selected shafts belong here.

Economy_Finished_Armour.txt
Worn defence.
Mail, plate, helmets, jacks, shields.

Economy_Finished_Clothing.txt
Worn cloth and hide.
Shirts, cloaks, boots, gloves, and made garments.

Economy_Finished_Household.txt
Domestic objects.
Pots, chests, furniture, candles, and common house-wares.

Economy_Finished_Transport.txt
Things that move people or goods.
Carts, saddles, tack, boats, and related gear.
