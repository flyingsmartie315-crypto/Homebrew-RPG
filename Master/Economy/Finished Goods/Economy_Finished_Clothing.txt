

--------------------------------------------------
LEATHER GRADES (UPDATE)
--------------------------------------------------
Leather garments and components use
Economy_Processed_Animal_Goods.txt grades.

- Soft clothing, linings, light gloves  → Sheep/Goat, Calfskin, or Oil-tanned
- Heavy coats, work boots, belts        → Cattle or Horsehide
- Fine court or presentation pieces     → Calfskin

Generic leather remains 40 bc/kg only when the garment
does not name a grade.
