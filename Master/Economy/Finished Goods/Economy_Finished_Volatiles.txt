HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
These are finished or semi-finished volatile compounds, not primary goods.
They are almost never sold in ordinary shops.
Prices below are material + processing value at a specialist works
or fortress magazine. Transport, guards, and local scarcity sit on top.

Stability Class
  C — Stable under ordinary dry storage.
      Becomes dangerous if wet, cracked, or exposed to wind/spark.
  Higher classes (B, A) are not yet locked for these substances.

Transit Rule
  Heavy Separation — components or the mix itself must travel
                     apart or in small sealed lots. Vibration and
                     moisture destroy homogeneity or safety.
  Stable — may travel as a finished grained product without
           special separation beyond ordinary dry packing.

Power Factor (PF)
  Relative propellant strength. 1.0 is a working baseline
  for ordinary early cannon. Used by Siege_Weapons.txt
  when calculating range or structural effect.

--------------------------------------------------
BLACK POWDER — PROPELLANTS
--------------------------------------------------

Substance ID: BPD_01
Name: Serpentine / “Meal” Powder
Base Formulation: Un-grained raw dust mix
  (saltpetre, charcoal, sulfur in rough proportion).
Processed Cost: Low (raw crushing and mixing only).
  No wet-milling, no cake, no graining.
Transit Rule: Heavy Separation.
  Components often travel apart and are mixed near the point of use.
  Finished meal powder settles and separates under vibration.
Siege Yield: Fuses, fire-arrows, rocket fuel, primitive mortars,
             early hand-gonnes and light falconets when nothing better exists.
Power Factor (PF): 0.5
Storage:
  Dry, sealed casks or jars. Keep away from spark and damp.
  Settles; must be re-mixed before use.
Weight / Unit: typically 10–20 kg cask.
Base price: 8–15 sc per kg
Notes: Cheap and common where saltpetre and charcoal exist.
       Unreliable in heavy guns. The default for rockets,
       fire-pots, and RACK OF FIRE payloads.

Substance ID: BPD_02
Name: Corned / “Grained” Military Powder
Base Formulation: Wet-milled, cake-pressed, and grained.
Processed Cost: High.
  Requires specialized mechanical powder-mills, pressing,
  and controlled drying.
Transit Rule: Stable.
  Retains homogeneity under ordinary vibration and handling.
Siege Yield: Mandatory for Culverins, Sakers, Demi-Cannons,
             and heavy Bombards when wall-shattering kinetic
             effect is required.
Power Factor (PF): 1.5
Storage:
  Dry magazine, sealed barrels. Standard fortress practice.
  Longer shelf life than meal powder.
Weight / Unit: 20–40 kg barrel typical.
Base price: 40–70 sc per kg
Notes: The military standard once mills exist.
       Scarce outside major arsenals and siege trains.
       Do not substitute meal powder in heavy guns if the
       engine profile assumes PF 1.5.

--------------------------------------------------
CHEMICAL / OBSCURANT
--------------------------------------------------

Substance ID: CH_01
Name: Quicklime Dust Cloud — “Blinding Ash”
Base Formulation: Calcinated limestone crushed to fine particulate.
Processed Cost: Low.
  Cross-ref: Economy_Primary_Earths_and_Salts.txt (limestone / lime).
  Calcining and fine grinding only.
Weight per Unit: 5 kg canvas satchel.
Stability Class: C
  Stable when dry. High wind causes self-contamination
  and loss of the cloud.
Transit Rule: Ordinary dry packing. Keep sealed.
Siege Yield: Thrown or lofted to create a blinding particulate cloud.
             Used against breaches, approaches, and enclosed spaces.
Base price: 3–6 sc per kg (satchel ~15–30 sc)
Notes: Not a propellant. Obscurant and irritant.
       Handlers wear cloth over mouth and eyes.
       Useless in rain or heavy damp.

--------------------------------------------------
TOXIC SMOKE
--------------------------------------------------

Substance ID: TX_01
Name: Arsenic Smoke / Orpiment Scent
Base Formulation: Realgar / Orpiment minerals baked with sulfur
                  and scrap tallow.
Processed Cost: Critical.
  Extremely hazardous to manufacture.
  Cross-ref: Economy_Primary_Minerals.txt for the arsenic minerals.
Weight per Unit: 15 kg slow-burning earth pot.
Stability Class: C
  Stable until ignited.
  Poisonous to handlers if the shell cracks.
Transit Rule: Heavy Separation or sealed specialist packing.
  Cracked pots are a handling hazard.
Siege Yield: Slow-burning toxic smoke pot.
             Lofted or placed to deny ground, breaches, or enclosed works.
Base price: 2–4 gc per pot (material + hazard premium)
Notes: Not a propellant. Area denial and terror.
       Manufacture kills or cripples careless workers.
       Use is rare and usually a last resort or specialist arsenal item.
       Living targets in the smoke must test vs Poison or Disease
       as the GM rules; the pot itself does structural damage only
       if it strikes as a blunt mass.

--------------------------------------------------
GENERAL RULES
--------------------------------------------------
- Ammunition and compounds are never included in the engine price
  in Economy_Finished_Siege_Arms.txt or Siege_Weapons.txt.
- PF multiplies effective propellant performance for range and
  structural calculations when an engine lists a powder requirement.
- Stability Class C items may be stored in ordinary dry magazines
  with the precautions noted. They are not “safe.”
- Local scarcity of saltpetre, sulfur, or arsenic minerals
  multiplies the listed price. Charcoal is rarely the constraint.
- These compounds sit outside ordinary Finished Arms and
  Finished Goods shops. They are arsenal, siege-train, or
  specialist alchemical goods.
