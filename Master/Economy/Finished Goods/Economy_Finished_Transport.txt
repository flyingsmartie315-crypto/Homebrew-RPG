# Economy_Finished_Transport.txt
# Things that move people or goods.
# Two categories: Land and Water.
# Law: Economy_Processing_Matrix.txt
# Tickets: Economy_Processed_Goods.txt
# Wages: Economy_Labour.txt

HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
A listing is one finished piece (one hull, one cart, one saddle).

Cost by trade-rate, not by hat.
If one craftsman does two trades under one roof, book each block
at that trade's wage. Do not invent extra men. Do not pay the
higher wage for the lower trade. Parallel days shorten the calendar.
They do not cheapen the sum.

Listed kg is purchased kg.
Shipyard and wheelwright offcuts go back as firewood. They are not
a second timber debit and they are not extra waste on the ticket.

Wages used here
  Rural common              3 bc/day
  Urban common / yard-hand  5 bc/day
  Basic skilled            30 bc/day
  Hazardous skilled        50 bc/day    tar-pit, caulk, pitch-boil
  Higher skilled           65 bc/day    smith, shipwright, wheelwright
  Master shipwright /
  Master wheelwright      130 bc/day

A vehicle is full at whichever comes first: weight or volume.
That is the same law as Economy_Processed_Goods.txt.

Craft
  Small stock (barrow, hand-cart, Nid, Zille):
    Poor / Good / Best as Weaponry  x0.5 / x3 / x10
  Architecture-scale (farm-cart is the edge; wain, tumbrel,
  Kalak, and every sea hull):
    Yard cap  Poor x0.8 / Good x1.25 / Best x2
    Best is commission, not Weaponry x10.

LAND
--------------------------------------------------

Do not hide iron tyres inside "wood."
Do not hide harness inside "the cart."
Elm hub blanks (5 bc/kg) are not ash spokes.
Oak hewn beams (4 bc/kg) are not oak planks.

Unbooked (tack and winter)
  Pack saddle
  Riding saddle and bridle
  Sledge / winter slide
  Travois

--------------------------------------------------
WATER
--------------------------------------------------

Stock tickets are munitions hulls: a last, a scantling, sold off the yard.
Anything not munitions is commissioned to the owner.
Commission premium on the munitions ticket:
  Made to measure           x1.5
  Named master on the lines x2.0
A Great Nau is never munitions. Its ticket is already a commissioned hull.

Oars and sweeps use Ash shaft / oar / handle blanks at 7 bc/kg.
They are not pine planks.
T2+ frames use Oak knee / compass-timber blanks at 6 bc/kg.
They are not oak planks.
Seams use Tar at 8 bc/kg.
Masts and yards use Pine spar / pole blanks at 1.2 bc/kg unless named otherwise.

Pine plank 1 bc/kg. Oak plank 2.6 bc/kg. Wrought iron 50 bc/kg.
Rope 18 bc/kg. Canvas 16 bc/kg.

WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT DO
--------------------------------------------------

- It does not stack a second waste factor on listed kg.
- It does not let T0 lay a barge or a cog.
- It does not price a saddle, sledge, or travois yet.
- It does not treat compass-timber as plank.
- It does not treat oars as pine.
- It does not apply Weaponry x10 to a season-built hull.
