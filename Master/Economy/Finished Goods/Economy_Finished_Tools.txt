# Economy_Finished_Tools.txt
# Ordinary trade tools. Not knightly arms.

HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
Finished tools are sold by the piece.
The iron in the tool is never cheaper than wrought iron.
Hafts use a shaft / handle blank, not a board.

--------------------------------------------------
GENERAL
--------------------------------------------------

Tools
The ordinary kit of a trade: hammers, axes, saws, tongs, knives, and bits.
Input: Wrought iron, plus an ash or similar handle-blank where the tool has a haft.
Labour: Forged, fitted, and quenched as the job needs.
Use: Farm, yard, wood, and household work. Not knightly arms.

--------------------------------------------------
GROUPINGS
--------------------------------------------------

Small tool
Type: Small tool
Typical pieces: knife, bit, awl, small hammer
Inputs:
  - 0.2–0.4 kg wrought iron
  - haft only if needed (scrap or short ash-handle)
Labour: Smith, part of a day
Modifiers:
  - Poor: scrap-forged iron
  - Fine: steel edge; +labour
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 5–8 sc

Common tool
Type: Common tool
Typical pieces: hand-hammer, hatchet, tongs, drawknife
Inputs:
  - 0.5–1 kg wrought iron
  - 1–2 kg ash handle-blank if hafted
Labour: Smith, most of a day, plus hafting
Modifiers:
  - Poor: mixed-timber haft; scrap-forged head
  - Fine: steel edge or better finish; +1 day
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 8–14 sc

Heavy tool
Type: Heavy tool
Typical pieces: felling axe, mattock, large hammer, pit-saw
Inputs:
  - 1.5–3 kg wrought iron
  - 2–3 kg ash handle-blank if hafted
Labour: Smith, a day or more. A pit-saw longer.
Modifiers:
  - Poor: scrap-forged; mixed-timber haft
  - Fine: steel bit or better temper; selected ash
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 16–30 sc
