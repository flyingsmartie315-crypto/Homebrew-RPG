# Economy_Finished_Arms.txt
# Fighting pieces. Not tools.

HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
Arms are sold by the piece.
The metal in the weapon is never cheaper than the grade named in the inputs.
Hafts and shafts use selected blanks, not boards.
Waste is in the bought mass, not a second ticket.

Cost by trade-rate, not by hat.
If one craftsman does two trades under one roof, book each block
at that trade's wage. Do not invent extra men. Do not pay the
higher wage for the lower trade. Parallel days shorten the calendar.
They do not cheapen the sum.

Wait is not labour.
Seasoning, loft-years, and drying sit as storage and a small premium on the stave.
They are not bowyer-days and they are not smith-days.
Same rule as tan-pits in Economy_Processing_Matrix.txt.

Stay inside the categories below. Named examples are types within a category, not separate files.
Core/Weaponry.txt Common cost is the mid of the band.

Pre-iron alloys are not an Iron Grade. One metal.
Shop factor on the listed low-carbon ticket
(Economy_Processed_Goods.txt):
  Bronze  ×1.00   ingot 6 sc/kg, matches Steel
  Brass   ×0.90   ingot 5 sc/kg
  Copper  ×0.75   ingot 50 bc/kg
Enc and damage stay Weaponry.txt §2.
Not in these metals: great blades, H&H blades,
long polearms, lance, any crossbow.
Cast waste 1.15 on the head. Haft stays ash or named wood.

Svartwood is structure, not spring. See PREMIUM: SVARTWOOD below.
Do not tiller a bow-limb from it. Do not replace a steel prod with it.

BOWS
--------------------------------------------------

Two builds. Do not price them as one bow with a surcharge.

Self / longbow
  One stave. Ash, elm, or yew. Seasoning is loft, not labour.
  A stave sits for years. That is storage and a little tied coin.
  Do not multiply bowyer-days by the years in the rack.
  Yew costs more because the stick is scarce.

Composite
  Wood core, horn belly, sinew back, hide glue.
  Not a yew stick with horn nailed on.
  Cure is months. That is the loft rule again. Not bowyer-days.
  Tickets: livestock horn 8 bc/kg is cattle scrap.
  Bow-horn is selected (ibex, buffalo, wild horn) at 1–2 sc/kg.
  Sinew is Gut / Sinew, 12 bc/kg, then prepared.
  Glue is hide or fish size, a few sc, not a timber.

--------------------------------------------------
PREMIUM: SVARTWOOD
--------------------------------------------------

Dauðavík only. Structure, not spring.
Grade is geothermal proximity and mineral take.
  Rim / pale     600 kg/m³   hull strakes, deck. Not a haft.
  Mid            720 kg/m³   waterline, frames.
  Heart / vent   850 kg/m³   keel, stem, stern, tiller, durable haft.
Processed tickets: Economy_Processed_Goods.txt.
Wet-wrap. Hazardous dust. Heart saws slower.

Locked geometry for hand-weapon hafts (heart only)
  5-10% slimmer than the ash pattern. Not more.
  Diameter x0.90 to x0.95. Mass still about x1.3 to x1.45 vs ash.
  Enc tax stays. Do not pencil the shaft to kill the density.
  A rim haft is not the premium. It is only slightly heavy ash
  and does not buy the weather or the fire.

What heart is for
  Short stiff staffs that live in spray, ice, or fire-pots:
  pollaxe, boarding-axe, mace, short war-spear.
  Weather and rot are the reason you paid.
  Langets remain iron.

What it is not for
  Bow-limbs. Crossbow prods. Glaive or bill that should live a little.
  A marching field-haft you expect to ruin and replace.
  A whole hull of heart. Hull skin is rim.

Haft modifier (on the ash-shaft weapon)
  Replace the ash blank with heart Svartwood at the processed rate.
  Add Hazardous shaping days.
  Coin: about +1 to +3 gc on a Common pollaxe / mace, more if the
  blank is a selected heart member.
  Still a named piece, not a rack weapon.

Tiller (locked hybrid)
  Heart Svartwood makes the finest crossbow tiller in the book.
  Pair it only with a spring-steel prod.
  The frame takes repeated heavy bolts without the tiller
  opening. The spring is still steel.
  High-Poundage / siege: 20-40 gc band above.
  Ship and shore weapon. Not a goat's-foot for the road.
