# Economy_Finished_Arms.txt
# Fighting pieces. Not tools.

HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
Arms are sold by the piece.
The metal in the weapon is never cheaper than the grade named in the inputs.
Hafts and shafts use selected blanks, not boards.

Stay inside the categories below. Named examples are types within a category, not separate files.

--------------------------------------------------
BLADES
--------------------------------------------------

Small Bladed
Type: Small Bladed
Examples: knife, dagger, rondel dagger
Inputs:
  - 0.3–0.6 kg steel
  - small fittings as needed
Labour: Smith, part of a day to a day
Modifiers:
  - Poor: wrought iron
  - Fine: fine steel
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 12–25 sc

Single Bladed
Type: Single Bladed
Examples: arming sword, sabre, scimitar
Inputs:
  - 1.0–1.4 kg steel
  - grip, guard, and scabbard fittings
Labour: Smith, several days
Modifiers:
  - Poor: wrought iron / low steel
  - Fine: fine steel
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 2–5 gc

Long Bladed
Type: Long Bladed
Examples: longsword, bastard sword
Inputs:
  - 1.4–1.8 kg steel
  - longer grip and fittings
Labour: Smith, a week or more
Modifiers:
  - Poor: common steel
  - Fine: fine steel or better
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 5–10 gc

Great Bladed
Type: Great Bladed
Examples: zweihander, claymore
Inputs:
  - 2.0–3.0 kg steel
  - long grip, substantial fittings
Labour: Smith, specialised, two weeks or more
Modifiers:
  - Fine: fine or crucible steel
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 10–20 gc

--------------------------------------------------
AXES
--------------------------------------------------

Single Axe
Type: Single Axe
Examples: fighting axe, horseman’s axe
Inputs:
  - 1.0–1.5 kg steel
  - 2–3 kg ash shaft-blank
Labour: Smith, a day or two, plus hafting
Modifiers:
  - Poor: wrought iron head
  - Fine: steel bit, selected ash
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 18–35 sc

Long Axe
Type: Long Axe
Examples: long fighting axe, two-handed field axe
Inputs:
  - 1.5–2.2 kg steel
  - 3–4 kg ash shaft-blank
Labour: Smith, several days, plus hafting
Modifiers:
  - Poor: wrought iron head
  - Fine: steel head, selected ash
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 2–4 gc

Great Axe
Type: Great Axe
Examples: great axe, pollaxe
Inputs:
  - 2.0–3.5 kg steel
  - 3 kg selected ash shaft-blank
Labour: Smith, specialised, plus careful hafting
Modifiers:
  - Fine: fine steel head; weapon-grade ash
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 4–8 gc

--------------------------------------------------
BOWS
--------------------------------------------------

Low-Poundage Bow
Type: Low-Poundage Bow
Examples: light bow, self bow
Inputs:
  - 1 selected bow-stave (ash, elm, or lesser yew)
Labour: Bowyer, several days
Modifiers:
  - Fine: better stave and tiller
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 15–30 sc

Medium-Poundage Bow
Type: Medium-Poundage Bow
Examples: cavalry bow, skirmish bow
Inputs:
  - 1 selected bow-stave
  - sinew / glue / horn if composite
Labour: Bowyer, a week or more
Modifiers:
  - Composite: extra materials and time
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 2–5 gc

High-Poundage Bow
Type: High-Poundage Bow
Examples: longbow, warbow
Inputs:
  - 1 selected yew stave (5 kg class) or lesser substitute
Labour: Bowyer, skilled, long seasoning before the tiller
Modifiers:
  - Yew: as Forestry yew stave
  - Substitute stave: cheaper, worse
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 5–12 gc

--------------------------------------------------
CROSSBOWS
--------------------------------------------------

Low-Poundage Crossbow
Type: Low-Poundage Crossbow
Examples: hand-drawn; stirrup and belt-hook
Inputs:
  - steel or horn prod
  - tiller of hardwood
  - lock and simple fittings
Labour: Bowyer / smith together, several days
Modifiers:
  - Poor: iron prod, mixed-timber tiller
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 2–4 gc

Medium-Poundage Crossbow
Type: Medium-Poundage Crossbow
Examples: goat’s-foot lever
Inputs:
  - heavier steel prod
  - hardwood tiller
  - lock, stirrup, and lever
Labour: Specialist, a week or more
Modifiers:
  - Fine: better lock and prod
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 5–10 gc

High-Poundage Crossbow
Type: High-Poundage Crossbow
Examples: windlass or cranequin
Inputs:
  - heavy steel prod
  - hardwood tiller
  - lock and winding gear
Labour: Specialist, two weeks or more
Modifiers:
  - Windlass or cranequin counted in the fittings
Output: 1 piece
Base price: 12–25 gc
