# Base unit: 1 kg, or 1 litre where noted. 1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc.

Drink and Pressed Goods
Liquids taken from farm produce by brewing, fermenting, or pressing.

  Beer
  Small beer and common ale.
  Made from malted grain, water, and labour. Hops when the bine is grown.
  Used as daily drink and a safer store than water in many towns.
  Base: 12 bc per litre
  Bulk: 1,000 kg/m³
  Packing: cask
  Small barrel (50 L) → 50 sc
  Large barrel (100 L) → 100 sc
  Cart load (800 L) → 800 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 L) → 1,500 sc

  Wine
  Fermented grape-drink.
  Made from grapes in warm hill-country.
  Used as drink, store, and trade.
  Base: 24 bc per litre
  Bulk: 1,000 kg/m³
  Packing: cask
  Small barrel (50 L) → 100 sc
  Large barrel (100 L) → 200 sc
  Cart load (800 L) → 1,600 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 L) → 3,000 sc

  Cider
  Fermented apple-drink.
  Made from apples. The orchard drink of temperate country.
  Base: 10 bc per litre
  Bulk: 1,000 kg/m³
  Packing: cask
  Small barrel (50 L) → 41 sc 8 bc
  Large barrel (100 L) → 83 sc 4 bc
  Cart load (800 L) → 666 sc 8 bc

  Perry
  Fermented pear-drink.
  Made from pears. Thinner on the road than cider.
  Base: 12 bc per litre
  Bulk: 1,000 kg/m³
  Packing: cask
  Small barrel (50 L) → 50 sc
  Cart load (800 L) → 800 sc

  Vinegar
  Sour wine or sour cider. Store and kitchen.
  Made from wine or cider left to go sharp.
  Base: 8 bc per litre
  Bulk: 1,000 kg/m³
  Packing: cask / jar
  Jar (10 L) → 6 sc 8 bc
  Small barrel (50 L) → 33 sc 4 bc
  Cart load (800 L) → 533 sc 4 bc

  Oil (olive)
  Pressed fruit-oil.
  Made from olives.
  Used for cooking, lamps, and some craft.
  Base: 20 bc per litre
  Bulk: 900 kg/m³
  Packing: cask / jar
  Jar (10 L) → 16 sc 8 bc
  Small barrel (50 L) → 83 sc 4 bc
  Cart load (800 L) → 1,333 sc 4 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 L) → 2,500 sc

  Linseed oil
  Pressed flax-seed oil.
  Made from flax seed after the fibre is taken, or from a seed crop.
  Used for paint, wood, and some lamps. Poor eating oil.
  Base: 16 bc per litre
  Bulk: 900 kg/m³
  Packing: cask / jar
  Jar (10 L) → 13 sc 4 bc
  Small barrel (50 L) → 66 sc 8 bc
  Cart load (800 L) → 1,066 sc 8 bc

  Poppy oil
  Pressed seed-oil.
  Made from poppy. Watched more than linseed.
  Used for the lamp and the kitchen in warm country.
  Base: 24 bc per litre
  Bulk: 900 kg/m³
  Packing: jar / cask
  Jar (10 L) → 20 sc
  Small barrel (50 L) → 100 sc

  Whale Oil
  Rendered oil of great sea-beasts.
  Taken from coastal hunts.
  Used for lamps and some waterproofing.
  Base: 40 bc per litre
  Bulk: 900 kg/m³
  Packing: cask
  Small barrel (50 L) → 166 sc 8 bc
  Cart load (800 L) → 2,666 sc 8 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 L) → 5,000 sc
