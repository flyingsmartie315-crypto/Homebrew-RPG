Stone — Processed
Intact quarry units dressed, squared, split, or sized for ordinary building use.
About one-fifth to one-third of a raw blank is lost to dressing, chips, and irregular faces.
The input is an Intact Unit from Economy_Primary_Stone.txt.
Broken, flawed, or undersized stone cannot enter these forms; it stays bulk kg or rubble.

Bespoke and commission work (statues, carved monuments, finished millstones, specialist facing)
belongs in Economy_Finished_Stone.txt. Those pieces may take an Intact Unit directly
or a processed blank, at the craftsman’s choice.
This file is the ordinary dressed product that a build site expects.

Packed dressed blocks: Bulk ~2,200–2,600 kg/m³, loose or lightly crated.
Cut slate: Bulk ~2,400 kg/m³, bundled or crated.

--------------------------------------------------
YIELD TYPE → PROCESSED FORM
--------------------------------------------------
Common Stone intact
  → Rough squared foundation block or walling stone
  Limited dressing. Still irregular.

Granite intact
  → Heavy foundation block, sett, or millstone blank (dressed faces)
  Hard labour. High loss.

Slate intact
  → Cut roofing sheet or damp-course piece
  Split and trimmed to regular size.

Marble intact
  → Facing blank or carving blank (squared, major flaws removed)
  Further carving is Finished work.

Basalt intact
  → Rough foundation or paving block

Obsidian intact
  → Knapping blank (sized nodule)
  Edge work is Finished or specialist.

Bulk kg / rubble
  → No processed structural form. Fill or hardcore only.

--------------------------------------------------
FORMS
--------------------------------------------------

Rough Squared Block
Input: Common Stone or Basalt intact unit.
Labour: Hammer and punch, faces roughly trued.
Use: Foundation, wall-core, rough retaining.
Property: Sits true enough to lay. Not ashlar.
Loss: Low to moderate.

Ashlar / Freestone Block
Input: Sound freestone or marble intact unit of sufficient size.
Labour: Sawn or chiselled to consistent dimensions, beds and faces dressed.
Use: Face work, quoins, precise walling, architectural stone.
Property: Uniform size. Build site can lay to a regular bond.
Loss: One-fifth to one-third.
Note: This is the standard “dressed block” a mason’s yard sells for building.

Foundation Block (heavy)
Input: Granite or large Common/Basalt intact.
Labour: Squared, major projections removed. Faces may stay rough.
Use: Footings, heavy plinths, bridge and mill bases.
Property: Mass and soundness over fine finish.

Sett / Paving Block
Input: Granite or hard Common Stone.
Labour: Split and dressed to regular small blocks.
Use: Streets, yards, hard standing.
Property: Uniform enough for camber and drainage.

Cut Slate Sheet
Input: Slate intact sheet or block that splits cleanly.
Labour: Split, trimmed, holed if required.
Use: Roofing, damp-proof courses, wall-hang.
Property: Regular size and thickness. Broken pieces drop to bulk.

Millstone Blank (dressed)
Input: Granite or suitable hard stone intact of diameter.
Labour: Circumference roughed, faces flattened, eye marked or cut.
Use: Input to finished millstone (Finished tier completes the dressing and tooling).
Property: One piece. Not yet a working stone.

Carving / Facing Blank
Input: Marble or fine freestone intact.
Labour: Squared, major veins or cracks removed, overall dimensions set.
Use: Sold to statue yards, monument masons, or fine-face work.
Property: Ready for the Finished craftsman. Not itself a finished piece.

Knapping Blank
Input: Obsidian intact nodule.
Labour: Trimmed to workable size, obvious flaws removed.
Use: Edge tools, specialist blades (Finished or craft tier).
Property: Small. Wrapped.

--------------------------------------------------
PRICES (DRAFT)
--------------------------------------------------
Base remains the Primary Stone species kg price.
Uplift covers:
- dressing loss (one-fifth to one-third)
- mason or dresser labour
- intact premium already paid on the input

Rough Squared Block
  Modest uplift over primary kg. Common on local sites.

Ashlar / Freestone Block
  Clear premium. The ordinary architectural ticket.
  Marble ashlar higher still.

Cut Slate Sheet
  Premium by sheet size and evenness.
  Priced per sheet or per kg equivalent.

Millstone Blank / Carving Blank
  Higher premium. These are selected pieces, not bulk dressed stone.
  Often sold singly.

Convenience
  Cart and heavy-cart loads follow the same vehicle limits as Primary.
  Large ashlar or foundation blocks may be limited by piece size and handling
  before weight is reached.

--------------------------------------------------
RULES
--------------------------------------------------
- Input authority is Economy_Primary_Stone.txt Intact Unit.
- If the quarry model did not produce a suitable intact piece,
  the processed form cannot be made.
- Bulk kg and rubble do not become ashlar.
- Economy_Finished_Stone.txt is for bespoke commission pieces
  (statues, finished monuments, completed millstones, specialist carving).
  Those may consume an Intact Unit directly or a processed blank from this file.
- Ordinary build sites that call for “ashlar”, “dressed freestone”,
  “cut slate”, or “squared foundation stone” draw from this file.
- Seasoning is not required for stone. Curing of mortar is separate.
