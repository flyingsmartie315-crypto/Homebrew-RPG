# Economy_Processed_Milled.txt
# Processed goods — Milled and Baked
# Law: Economy_Processed_Goods.txt (currency, bulk, packing)
# Making: Economy_Processing_Matrix.txt
# Base unit: 1 kg, or 1 litre where noted. 1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc.

Milled and Baked

  Common flour
  Mixed or unnamed grain-meal from the mill.
  Use this when the crop is not named.
  Base: 6 bc per kg
  Bulk: 600 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (40 kg) → 20 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 400 sc

  Wheat flour
  Leavened white or mixed wheat-meal.
  Made from wheat. Fails where wheat fails.
  Base: 7 bc per kg
  Bulk: 600 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (40 kg) → 23 sc 4 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 466 sc 8 bc

  Rye flour
  Dark meal of poor and cold floors.
  Made from rye.
  Base: 6 bc per kg
  Bulk: 600 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (40 kg) → 20 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 400 sc

  Barley meal
  Coarse meal. Pottage and poor bread.
  Made from barley.
  Base: 5 bc per kg
  Bulk: 600 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (40 kg) → 16 sc 8 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 333 sc 4 bc

  Oatmeal
  Crushed oats. Porridge and horse-feed that keeps.
  Made from oats.
  Base: 5 bc per kg
  Bulk: 550 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (40 kg) → 16 sc 8 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 333 sc 4 bc

  Buckwheat meal
  Grey meal of short-season ground.
  Made from buckwheat.
  Base: 6 bc per kg
  Bulk: 550 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (40 kg) → 20 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 400 sc

  Chestnut flour
  Sweet meal of the bread-tree.
  Made from dried sweet chestnut.
  Base: 8 bc per kg
  Bulk: 550 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (30 kg) → 20 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 533 sc 4 bc

  Pulse meal
  Pease or bean-meal for pottage.
  Made from field pea, fava, or lentil.
  Base: 5 bc per kg
  Bulk: 650 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (40 kg) → 16 sc 8 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 333 sc 4 bc

  Malt
  Kiln-dried sprouted barley (or mixed grain).
  Made for the brewer. Not a bread-flour.
  Base: 8 bc per kg
  Bulk: 500 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (40 kg) → 26 sc 8 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 533 sc 4 bc

  Bread
  Common loaf. Wheat where the floor allows; rye or mixed on a cold vale.
  Made by the baker. Eaten the same day or the next.
  Sold by the loaf more often than by the cart.
  Base: 10 bc per kg
  Bulk: 350 kg/m³
  Packing: basket / crate
  Loaf (1 kg) → 10 bc
  Basket (10 kg) → 8 sc 4 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 666 sc 8 bc

  Rye bread
  Dense loaf of rye-meal. Keeps longer than wheat-bread.
  Base: 8 bc per kg
  Bulk: 400 kg/m³
  Packing: basket / crate
  Loaf (1 kg) → 8 bc
  Basket (10 kg) → 6 sc 8 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 533 sc 4 bc
