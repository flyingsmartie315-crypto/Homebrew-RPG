# Economy_Processed_Metal.txt
# Processed goods — Metal
# Law: Economy_Processed_Goods.txt (currency, bulk, packing)
# Making: Economy_Processing_Matrix.txt
# Base unit: 1 kg, or 1 litre where noted. 1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc.

Metal
Ore reduced and refined. Each step costs more than the last.
Ingots stack dense. Bulk: 4,000 kg/m³. Packing: loose pile.
Carts always fill by weight.

  Crude Iron
  Bloom or rough pig from the furnace.
  Made from iron ore or bog iron.
  Used only to be worked again.
  Base: 24 bc per kg
  Single ingot (1 kg) → 2 sc
  Ingot lot (50 kg) → 100 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 1,600 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 3,000 sc

  Wrought Iron
  Hammered, usable iron.
  Made from crude iron by smith’s labour.
  Used for tools, fittings, and common arms.
  Base: 50 bc per kg
  Single ingot (1 kg) → 4 sc 2 bc
  Ingot lot (50 kg) → 208 sc 4 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 3,333 sc 4 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 6,250 sc

  Steel
  Hardened, refined iron.
  Made from wrought iron with more fire and skill.
  Used for good edges and better arms.
  Base: 6 sc per kg
  Single ingot (1 kg) → 6 sc
  Ingot lot (50 kg) → 300 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 4,800 sc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 9,000 sc

  Fine Steel
  Clean, even steel for the best common work.
  Made from steel by specialist smiths.
  Used for fine blades and precision tools.
  Base: 12 sc per kg
  Single ingot (1 kg) → 12 sc
  Ingot lot (30 kg) → 360 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 9,600 sc

  Crucible Steel
  Rare, very even steel.
  Made from fine steel by restricted, costly refinement.
  Used for exceptional blades and prized work.
  Base: 12 gc per kg
  Single ingot (1 kg) → 12 gc
  Small lot (5 kg) → 60 gc

  Note on metal scrap
  Any piece that can be melted or faggoted keeps value.
  Price scrap as a fraction of the parent metal’s per-kg rate.

  Clean, sorted scrap of one metal: 50–70% of that metal’s ingot price
  Rusty but still one metal: 35–50%
  Mixed or dirty scrap: 20–35% of the cheapest metal in the heap
  Precious or steel scrap keeps its own ladder.

  Examples, per kg
  Clean wrought-iron scrap → 25–35 bc
  Rusty nails and offcuts → 18–25 bc
  Mixed iron heap → 10–18 bc
  Clean steel scrap → 3–4 sc

  Copper
  Smelted copper.
  Made from copper ore.
  Used for pots, fittings, roofing, and bronze.
  Base: 50 bc per kg
  Bulk: 4,000 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Single ingot (1 kg) → 4 sc 2 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 3,333 sc 4 bc

  Tin
  Smelted tin.
  Made from tin ore.
  Used with copper to make bronze.
  Base: 4 sc per kg
  Bulk: 4,000 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Single ingot (1 kg) → 4 sc
  Ingot lot (50 kg) → 200 sc

  Bronze
  Copper and tin together. About nine parts copper, one part tin.
  Made by the founder. Arms and the short armour list in Armour.txt.
  Ingot matches Steel (6 sc/kg). That is why a bronze piece keeps the listed shop price.
  Base: 6 sc per kg
  Bulk: 4,000 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Single ingot (1 kg) → 6 sc
  Ingot lot (50 kg) → 300 sc

  Zinc
  From calamine. Feedstock for brass. Not a weapon metal alone.
  Base: 3 sc per kg
  Bulk: 4,000 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Single ingot (1 kg) → 3 sc
  Ingot lot (20 kg) → 60 sc

  Brass
  Copper and zinc. Softer than bronze. Fittings, cast heads, the short armour list.
  Ingot under Steel. Shop ticket is listed ×0.90.
  Base: 5 sc per kg
  Bulk: 4,000 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Single ingot (1 kg) → 5 sc
  Ingot lot (50 kg) → 250 sc

  Pre-iron shop rule
  Listed arm and armour prices are Low-carbon (Steel, 6 sc/kg).
  Swap the metal. Do not also apply Iron Grade.
    Bronze   ×1.00    ingot matches Steel
    Brass    ×0.90    ingot 5 sc/kg
    Copper   ×0.75    ingot 50 bc/kg; arms only, not armour
  Enc and damage or AC are Core (Weaponry.txt §2, Armour.txt §9).
  Not legal: great blades, long polearms, lance, any crossbow,
  mail, solid cuirass, closed helm. Copper is not armour.

  Lead
  Smelted lead.
  Made from lead ore.
  Used for roofs, pipes, shot, and seals.
  Base: 22 bc per kg
  Bulk: 5,000 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Single ingot (1 kg) → 1 sc 10 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 1,466 sc 8 bc

  Silver (refined)
  Fine silver.
  Made from silver ore.
  Used for coin, plate, and fine work.
  Base: 200 sc per kg
  Bulk: 5,000 kg/m³
  Packing: chest
  Ingot (1 kg) → 10 gc
  Small lot (5 kg) → 50 gc

  Gold (refined)
  Fine gold.
  Made from gold ore.
  Used for coin and high ornament.
  Base: 200 gc per kg
  Bulk: 5,000 kg/m³
  Packing: chest
  Ingot (1 kg) → 200 gc
