Ceramic
Earth fired into ware.

  Pottery
  Common fired ware.
  Made from clay and kiln-labour.
  Used for pots, jars, and household vessels.
  Base: 10 bc per kg
  Bulk: 400 kg/m³
  Packing: crate / basket
  Crate (40 kg) → 33 sc 4 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 666 sc 8 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 1,250 sc

  Brick
  Fired clay for building.
  Made from clay and kiln-labour.
  Used for walls, hearths, and linings.
  Base: 5 bc per kg
  Bulk: 1,600 kg/m³
  Packing: loose pile
  Cart load (800 kg) → 333 sc 4 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 625 sc
