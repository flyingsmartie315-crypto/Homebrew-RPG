Preserve and Kitchen
Fruit, leaf, and root made to keep.

  Sauerkraut
  Salt-fermented cabbage.
  Made from cabbage. Cool-store through Longnight.
  Base: 4 bc per kg
  Bulk: 800 kg/m³
  Packing: cask
  Small cask (20 kg) → 6 sc 8 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 266 sc 8 bc

  Dried fruit
  Prune, dried apple, raisin, lingon, and their like.
  Made from plum, apple, grape, date, or Longnight berries already dried.
  Lingon keeps. Cloudberry is the wet-pocket pot-fruit.
  Base: 12 bc per kg
  Bulk: 600 kg/m³
  Packing: sack / crate
  Sack (20 kg) → 20 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 800 sc

  Fruit preserve
  Honeyed or boiled garden-fruit. Jelly and pot-fruit.
  Made from berries, cherry, currant, or quince with honey or sugar-store.
  Base: 20 bc per kg
  Bulk: 1,200 kg/m³
  Packing: jar / pot
  Jar (5 kg) → 8 sc 4 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 1,333 sc 4 bc

  Quince paste
  Stiff cooked quince. Keeps.
  Made from quince.
  Base: 16 bc per kg
  Bulk: 1,200 kg/m³
  Packing: box / crate
  Box (5 kg) → 6 sc 8 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 1,066 sc 8 bc

  Ground spice
  Opened and mixed spice for the kitchen.
  Made from the Spice chest. Cheaper cumin at the farm is not this line.
  Base: 4 sc per kg
  Bulk: 400 kg/m³
  Packing: chest / pot
  Pot (1 kg) → 4 sc
  Small chest (5 kg) → 20 sc
