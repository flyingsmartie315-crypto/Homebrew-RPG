HEADER NOTE
All prices use the universal currency (1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc).
Prices shown are base market values and are subject to ±50% (or greater) fluctuation by region, quality, supply, and demand.
Base unit is 1 kg wherever practical. Liquids use 1 litre (≈ 1 kg).
Larger lots are computed from the per-kg / per-litre base (1 sc = 12 bc).

Processed goods normally sell for 2.5–6× the value of their primary inputs after labour and wastage, except high-skill or luxury items.
A 1 kg scrap of a processed good is only worth its price if a workshop can use it.
How a good is made — inputs, labour, plant, and tech level — is Economy_Processing_Matrix.txt.

Bulk is packed density for the good in its listed packing.
A vehicle is full at whichever comes first: weight or volume.
Standard cart: 800 kg or 2.5 m³
Heavy cart: 1,500 kg or 4 m³
River timber barge: 12,000 kg or 30 m³
A "cart load" is one standard cart.
A "heavy-cart load" is one heavy cart.
A "barge-load" is one river timber barge.

Packing
The Bulk figure already includes the listed packing.
Use the modifiers below only when the good is packed differently from its listed method.

Sacks
+2% weight. +5% volume.
Default for sack-goods.

Baskets
+15–25% volume.
Default for pottery and anything that must breathe or not be crushed.

Casks / Crates
+8–12% weight.
Volume is the cask's outside measure.
Default for beer, wine, oil, and other liquids.

Loose pile
No extra penalty.
Default for ingots, planks, beams, and stacked stone-ware.
