# Economy_Processed_Cloth.txt
# Processed goods — Cloth, Fibre, and Dye
# Law: Economy_Processed_Goods.txt (currency, bulk, packing)
# Making: Economy_Processing_Matrix.txt
# Base unit: 1 kg, or 1 litre where noted. 1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc.

Cloth and Fibre
Spun, woven, or felted goods.

  Wool Cloth
  Ordinary woven woollen.
  Made from wool by spinning and weaving.
  Used for hose, kirtles, cloaks, and common clothing.
  Base: 1 sc per kg (≈ 3–4 m²)
  Bulk: 220 kg/m³
  Packing: bolt / bale
  Bolt (10 kg) → 10 sc
  Cart load (550 kg, volume) → 550 sc

  Silk Cloth
  Woven silk.
  Made from raw silk by specialised weavers.
  Used for rich clothing and display.
  Base: 8 sc per kg
  Bulk: 180 kg/m³
  Packing: bolt
  Bolt (5 kg) → 40 sc
  Cart load (450 kg, volume) → 3,600 sc

  Linen
  Woven flax-cloth.
  Made from retted flax by spinning and weaving.
  Used for shirts, sails, and household cloth.
  Base: 1 sc 5 bc per kg (≈ 4–5 m²)
  Bulk: 250 kg/m³
  Packing: bolt / bale
  Bolt (10 kg) → 14 sc 2 bc
  Cart load (625 kg, volume) → 885 sc 5 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,000 kg, volume) → 1,416 sc 8 bc

  Felt
  Beaten wool-cloth.
  Made from wool without a true weave.
  Used for hats, padding, and cheap warm layers.
  Base: 25 bc per kg
  Bulk: 180 kg/m³
  Packing: roll / bale
  Roll (15 kg) → 31 sc 3 bc
  Cart load (450 kg, volume) → 937 sc 6 bc
  Heavy-cart load (720 kg, volume) → 1,500 sc

  Velvet
  Fine dyed pile-cloth.
  Made from good wool or silk, dye, and skilled weaving.
  Used for rich clothing and display.
  Base: 6 sc per kg
  Bulk: 200 kg/m³
  Packing: bolt
  Bolt (5 kg) → 30 sc
  Cart load (500 kg, volume) → 3,000 sc

  Rope
  Laid cordage from flax (or hemp where grown).
  Made by the ropemaker.
  Used for carts, ships, wells, and binding.
  Base: 18 bc per kg
  Bulk: 250 kg/m³
  Packing: coil
  Coil (10 kg) → 15 sc
  Cart load (625 kg, volume) → 937 sc 6 bc

  Hemp cloth
  Coarse woven hemp.
  Made from hemp. Cheaper and harsher than linen.
  Used for sacks, work-clothes, and cheap sail-patches.
  Base: 10 bc per kg (≈ 3 m²)
  Bulk: 250 kg/m³
  Packing: bolt / bale
  Bolt (10 kg) → 8 sc 4 bc
  Cart load (625 kg, volume) → 520 sc 10 bc

  Canvas
  Heavy hemp or flax sail-cloth.
  Made from hemp or coarse flax.
  Used for sails, tents, and covers.
  Base: 16 bc per kg
  Bulk: 280 kg/m³
  Packing: bolt
  Bolt (15 kg) → 20 sc
  Cart load (700 kg, volume) → 933 sc 4 bc

  Tar
  Pine or fir boiled to ship-pitch.
  Made from fatwood, kiln-smoke, or a tar-pit.
  Used for seams, standing rigging, and weatherproofing.
  Not a plank. Not turpentine.
  Base: 8 bc per kg
  Bulk: 900 kg/m³
  Packing: barrel
  Barrel (50 kg) → 33 sc 4 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 533 sc 4 bc
  Heavy-cart load (1,500 kg) → 1,000 sc

Prepared dye
Crop dye made ready for the vat.
Primary “Dyes” is the raw plant or lump. This is the workshop take.

  Woad balls
  Fermented, dried woad.
  Made from woad leaf. The blue of cool country.
  Base: 2 sc 6 bc per kg
  Bulk: 400 kg/m³
  Packing: sack / crate
  Sack (10 kg) → 25 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 2,000 sc

  Madder extract
  Prepared red from the root.
  Made from madder after the two- or three-year wait.
  Base: 3 sc per kg
  Bulk: 400 kg/m³
  Packing: pot / crate
  Pot (5 kg) → 15 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 2,400 sc

  Weld cake
  Dried yellow dye-stuff.
  Made from weld stalk.
  Base: 1 sc 8 bc per kg
  Bulk: 350 kg/m³
  Packing: sack
  Sack (10 kg) → 16 sc 8 bc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 1,333 sc 4 bc

  Indigo cake
  Prepared hot-country blue.
  Made from indigo. Out-prices woad on the long road.
  Base: 8 sc per kg
  Bulk: 500 kg/m³
  Packing: chest
  Cake lot (5 kg) → 40 sc
  Cart load (800 kg) → 6,400 sc
