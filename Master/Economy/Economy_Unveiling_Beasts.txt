# Economy_Unveiling_Beasts.txt
# Unveiling beasts — live value and slaughter bounties
# Format aligned with Economy_Livestock.txt
# Status: DRAFT
# Exclude: humanoids (Ghoul, Mintrox, etc.)
# Currency: 1 gc = 20 sc = 240 bc

HEADER NOTE
Live price is for a captured, broken, or bonded specimen where such a market exists.
Many Unveiling beasts have little or no ordinary slaughter value against their live worth.
Gryphon, Royal Gryphon, and Pegasus are the clear cases: the living beast is the prize;
the carcase is hide, bone, and trophy at best.

Slaughter bounty is the value of parts taken from a slain adult.
It is not a reason to kill a beast that can be kept.
Parts prices assume Primary or Processed tickets where they exist;
special parts (ivory, plates, venom) are noted as bounties until those tickets are written.

Regional ±50% or more. A burned, ruined, or young carcase yields less.

--------------------------------------------------
SLAUGHTER VS LIVE
--------------------------------------------------
Wonders and war-mounts (Gryphon, Royal Gryphon, Pegasus):
  Live value ≫ slaughter bounty.
  Slaughter is trophy and material only.

Heavy Unveiling (Trolls, some insectoids):
  Live value is high and specialised.
  Slaughter bounty is real (hide, bone, plates) but still usually below a successful live capture.

Ordinary-threat beasts:
  Slaughter bounty may approach or exceed casual live price
  when no one wants the living animal.

--------------------------------------------------
ENTRIES
--------------------------------------------------

Gryphon
Live: high (trained or bonded; market thin)
Upkeep: high (meat, space, handler)
Slaughter bounty (adult):
  Hide          15–30 kg workable → heavy leather grade
  Bone / beak   trophy + workable bone
  Feathers      modest
  Total parts   well below live value
Notes: Living gryphon is the prize. Carcase is not worth the hunt by itself.

Royal Gryphon
Live: effectively priceless / legend
Slaughter bounty: as Gryphon but larger; still negligible against live worth
Notes: Do not price the living beast as meat or hide.

Pegasus
Live: effectively priceless
Slaughter bounty: minimal ordinary material; sacred or forbidden in most places
Notes: Outside normal market. Slaughter is not an economic act.

River Troll
Live: 8–25 gc (young, broken; adults rarely)
Upkeep: high, wet, specialised
Slaughter bounty (adult):
  Hide          see Economy_Unveiling_Goods.txt
                35–55 kg raw intact (22–35 kg workable)
                Durable, waterproof
  Bone          80–120 kg total, by intact type
                (skull, long bones, rib/spine)
                Very durable
  Ivory         male tusks only, 1.5–3 kg pair
  Total parts   real but usually under live capture price
Notes: Hide knits unless burned. Burned hide loses intact value.
       Surface-area model from scaled gorilla. See Unveiling_Goods.

Rock Troll
Live: higher than river (rarer, harder)
Upkeep: high, dry or cave
Slaughter bounty (adult):
  Hide          see Economy_Unveiling_Goods.txt
                70–100 kg raw intact (45–70 kg workable)
                Very durable, thicker, heavier
                Back-and-shoulder plate up to ~40–60 kg dressed on large adult
  Bone          180–280 kg total, by intact type
                Same density as river, larger pieces
  Ivory         male tusks only, 3–6 kg pair
  Total parts   significant trophy and material bounty
Notes: Surface-area / volume scale from gorilla via river troll.
       Plate and ivory are the main named cuts.

Crag Hart
Live: trophy / rare mount potential
Slaughter bounty:
  Hide, antler or horn, meat (if taken)
  Modest against a live trophy animal
Notes: Treat more like high game than Unveiling labour beast.

Centaur
Excluded from ordinary slaughter pricing if treated as people.
If bestiary runs them as beasts only: hide and bone as large game.
Default: do not list a slaughter bounty.

Cloven / Horned Ones / Minotaur / Pigmen / Somnagor
Beastmen line. Exclude from this slaughter pass if they are run as people.
If strictly beasts: hide and bone as oversized game; no standard bounty yet.

Skinwight
Slaughter: hide is the point (skin-thief).
Bounty: the taken hide itself, variable quality.
Not a normal leather ticket.

Deepwight / Fenwight
Slaughter bounty: low ordinary material.
Parts may have ritual or local value. Not standard hide/meat.

Earth Eater
Slaughter bounty:
  Chitin or hide plates, mandibles
  Modest material; more hazard than profit

Sky Hornet
Slaughter: venom, wings, light chitin
Bounty small per individual; swarm changes the arithmetic

Vorax (see Soldier / Worker lines below for output)
Queen is not a slaughter target in normal play

Orb Wright
Slaughter output:
  Silk          gland yield — largest of the common spiders (orb-weaver scale)
                Intact skein or full gland extraction
  Venom         8–15 ml intact (see Unveiling_Goods)
  Chitin        light, limited use
Notes: Silk is the main material cut. Venom is the high-value fluid.

Pale Lurker
Slaughter output:
  Silk          large gland yield (goliath-birdeater scale, heavy specimen)
                Intact skein or full extraction
  Venom         20–40 ml intact (effect still open)
  Hide / fur    thick; secondary
Notes: High silk quantity. Venom provisional.

Skitterling
Slaughter output:
  Silk          small gland yield (cellar-spider scale)
                Often pooled
  Venom         0.5–1.5 ml intact
  Chitin        not useful
Notes: Venom is the only real commercial cut per beast.
       Silk useful only in volume from a nest.

Weber Fisher
Slaughter output:
  Silk          moderate gland yield (fishing-spider scale)
  Venom         present, quantity modest
Notes: Silk primary when glands are taken clean.

Vorax Soldier
Slaughter output:
  Acid / venom glands   5–12 ml intact
  Mandibles             workable pair
  Chitin                plates, moderate
Notes: Acid is the distinctive cut. See Unveiling_Goods.

Vorax Worker
Slaughter output:
  Chitin        light
  Mandibles     small
  Glands        negligible
Notes: Low commercial yield. Volume from a nest only.

Scylla
Slaughter: hide, teeth, trophy
Large and dangerous; bounty exists but hunt cost is high
Notes: Not a livestock analogue.

--------------------------------------------------
RULES
--------------------------------------------------
- Live price follows the same logic as Livestock: the beast is worth more alive if it works, bonds, or is a wonder.
- Slaughter bounty is parts only. It does not include the cost of the hunt.
- Gryphon, Royal Gryphon, and Pegasus: slaughter value is trophy-grade at best and must not be used to justify killing them for profit.
- Troll hide and rock-troll plate/ivory are the first Unveiling yields that need proper Primary → Processed → Finished tickets.
- Humanoids are excluded.
- Young, burned, or ruined carcases take the low end or nothing.
