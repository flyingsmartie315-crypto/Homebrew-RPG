# Armour_Rules.txt
# Virath armour — law. How layers, slots, and soak work.
# Status: DRAFT for Core.
# Companion files:
#   Armour.txt                          Models, Forms, stats
#   Core_Combat.txt                     hitting, damage
#   Core_Injury.txt                     Wallace + Limb location
#   Weaponry.txt                        Armour Piercing, type
#   Core_Characteristics_Skills.txt     Enc Limit, NAC

This file is the playable law. Piece numbers live in Armour.txt.

PURPOSE
Worn AC is per location and per type. A helm does not cover
an arm. Mail does not treat a hammer like a sword.

Law only. Layers, Limb locations, Slots, soak.
A cuirass, pauldrons, and jack chains sit on one man
because Slots match Limb locations. Stats: Armour.txt.

ASSESS

  Locations
    After a hit stands, two d100s (Injury §3).
    First: Wallace location (the limb).
    Second: Limb location (the Slot).
    Coverage d100 is a third die. Slots are
    the same list as Limb location.

  Soak (section 5)
    Hit stands. First d100 Wallace. Second
    d100 Limb location. Then layers on that
    Slot, top down.
    Modular present on this Slot? If no,
    skip to Maille.
    If yes: Coverage die vs that piece.
    Pass: that piece’s AC + Maille on the
    Slot + Textile on the Slot. Stop.
    Fail: Maille. Pass: Maille + Textile.
    Fail: Textile. Pass: Textile only.
    Fail: NAC only.
    Then NAC. That sum is Protection.
    Scale is Modular, not Maille.

  Injury
    Wounds above 0 are a dink. Stop
    (Core_Injury §1). The blow that
    empties the track is a Finishing Blow.
    Then the Slot table
    (Injury_Tables_Bipedal.txt).

  Layering
    Layers: None, Clothing, Textile,
    Maille, Modular.
    Each Slot: one piece per layer.
    Clothing is 0 AC.

  Not imported
    Calradia culture tag lists. Cosmetic tags may be
    written on a piece; they do not change AC.
    Durability Score. Condition tags (Worn, Ruined)
    may cut AC later. Enc is points.

--------------------------------------------------
0. KEYWORDS
--------------------------------------------------

Where (do not mix them)

  Wallace location
    The limb the first d100 named after the
    hit stood. Locked bands (Injury §3):
    Head, Torso, Right Arm, Left Arm,
    Right Leg, Left Leg.

  Limb location
    Where on that limb the second d100 named.
    Locked bands (Injury §3). This is the
    combat “where” for soak and for Injury
    tables. Print it on the sheet next to
    Wallace location.

      Arm:     Hand, Forearm, Elbow
               (Outer Elbow / Inner Elbow),
               Upper Arm, Shoulder
      Leg:     Foot, Shin / Calf, Knee
               (Front Knee / Back Knee),
               Thigh, Hip / Buttock
      Torso:   Chest / Upper Back,
               Armpit,
               Abdomen / Lower Back,
               Groin / Pelvis
               (Glutes / Sacrum from behind),
               Collarbone / Base of Neck
      Head:    Eyes, Skull / Occipital,
               Lower face / Back of neck,
               Throat

    Facing picks the front or back name on
    a pair (Injury §3).

  Slot
    The same list as Limb location.

    Write L or R when the piece is one side.
      L Hand    R Hand
      L Forearm R Forearm
      L Elbow   R Elbow
      L Outer Elbow   R Outer Elbow
      L Inner Elbow   R Inner Elbow
      L Upper Arm   R Upper Arm
      L Shoulder    R Shoulder
      L Thigh   R Thigh
      L Knee    R Knee
      L Shin / Calf   R Shin / Calf
      L Foot    R Foot
      L Hip / Buttock   R Hip / Buttock
      L Armpit          R Armpit

    No L or R means both sides. Textile and
    mail use that. A pair of chausses is
    Thigh, Knee, Shin / Calf — not L Thigh.
    A vambrace is L Forearm or R Forearm.
    A piece only answers the Limb location
    the second die named, on that side.
    L Forearm does not answer R Forearm
    or L Upper Arm.

    Every Slot may hold one Textile, one
    Maille, and one Modular piece.

  Coverage
    Chance that piece caught the Limb
    location the second d100 named.
    A Slot written with no number is 100%
    of that Slot. Write a number only when
    it is not 100% (Upper Arm 50%, Knee 50%).
    Pierce 0 on a Slot means Coverage 0
    against Pierce. Cut and Blunt still use
    the printed %. Jack chains are Pierce 0.

  Short: Wallace is the limb. Limb location
  is the spot. Slot is that spot on the
  wardrobe. Coverage is the chance on that
  spot. The Coverage d100 is a third die.

AC (Cut / Pierce / Blunt)
  Three soak numbers on a piece. Use the column that
  matches the declared blow.

Layer
  None, Clothing, Textile, Maille, Modular.
  Each Slot may hold one Textile, one Maille,
  and one Modular piece. Clothing lists dress
  Slots for weather and sense only. Those
  marks are not soak and do not fill a layer.

How a worn piece is named

  Model
    What it is made as. The listed item.
    A Model has Coverage, Slots, and (on Textile,
    mail, and Modular torso) flat AC, Enc, Cost.
    Modular arms and legs: the Model is shape
    plus listed cost and Enc. Material and
    Iron Grade apply % and AC (Armour.txt §9).
    Textile: Aketon, Gambeson, Arming Doublet,
    Pourpoint, and the lists in section 7.
    Maille: Mail Models, Material Riveted
    or Butted. Scale is Modular Segmented.
    Lists in Armour.txt §8 and §9.
    Modular torso: Scale, Coat of Plates,
    Laminar, Cuir Bouilli, Brigandine, Plate.
    A Modular Model lists the Slots it claims
    at minimum (Plate is Chest or Abdomen,
    same stats).

  Form
    Added cut on that Model. Does not change
    the Model’s AC. Changes Slots claimed and
    modifies Coverage, Enc, and Cost.
    Textile / mail Form: sleeve length and hem
    length.
    Modular torso Form: Backplate, Pauldrons,
    and the other front plate (Plate only).
    Example: Plate is the Model, Chest or
    Abdomen at the same stats. Adding the
    other front plate, Backplate, and
    Pauldrons is Form on that Model.

  Upgrade
    A field or making extra on a finished
    piece (Armour.txt Upgrades). Applied after Model
    and Form. May change AC, Enc, Cost, or
    add a special. Dense quilting, tempered
    rings, and so on.

  Order: pick Model, apply Form, then Upgrades.
  Craftsmanship (Good / Best / Poor) sits on
  the finished piece after those three.

--------------------------------------------------
0b. CATALOGUE LINE (Armour.txt)
--------------------------------------------------

Every worn piece in Armour.txt uses this line.
Clothing uses Item name. Every other layer
uses Model.

  Name or Model
  AC:                 C / P / B   (Clothing is 0/0/0)
  Slots:              each Limb location this piece
                      answers. No number = 100%
                      of that Slot. Write % only
                      when it is not 100%.
                      L / R if one side. Bare
                      name = both (Textile, mail,
                      hose).
  Base cost:          sc or sc.bc
  Base Enc:
  Note:               special, required-under,
                      layer law, or none

Do not hide Coverage in a paragraph. Write it
on the Slot.

  Aketon
  AC: 2/1/2
  Slots: Chest / Upper Back 100%,
         Abdomen / Lower Back 100%,
         Collarbone / Base of Neck 100%,
         Armpit 100%, Shoulder 100%,
         Upper Arm 50%
  Base cost: 10 sc
  Base Enc: 30
  Note: under plate or mail. Enc includes
        default Short Sleeves and Short Hem.

Textile and Maille keep Form
(sleeves, hem, collar) as a table under that
Model group. Form is not a sixth field on
the line. Apply Form after the line; it
adds or changes Slots and Enc, never AC.

Modular arms and legs may list Coverage and
Slots on the Model line and put AC on the
Material line. The six fields still exist.
They are just split across Model + Material.

Catalogue order stays:
  Clothing, Textile, Maille,
  Modular (segmented then solid), Boots,
  Upgrades.

--------------------------------------------------
1. LOCATIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Combat location d100 (Wallace bands)
  01–10   Head          10%
  11–50   Torso         40%
  51–60   Right Arm     10%
  61–70   Left Arm      10%
  71–85   Right Leg     15%
  86–100  Left Leg      15%

  85 was written on both legs in the draft.
  Left Leg starts at 86 so the bands do not
  overlap and still sum to 100.

A piece lists which of those it covers and at what %.
Feet are Legs. Hands are Arms. Face and visor are Head.
Tassets and Faulds hang from Groin / Pelvis
onto Thigh. They do not take the Thigh Slot.

--------------------------------------------------
1a. SUB-LOCATIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Sub-locations are wardrobe slots. Every slot may
hold three layers: Textile, Maille,
Modular. They do not replace the location d100. They do not
replace Coverage. Coverage stays on the piece.

Facing (common sense, no extra roll)
  The second die already named Chest or Upper
  Back, Abdomen or Lower Back. Face-to-face
  uses the front name. From behind, the back
  name. Do not sum plates across Slots.

TORSO Slots (Limb locations)

  Slot                         Typical piece
  Chest / Upper Back           Breastplate; Backplate
                               (facing picks the name)
  Armpit                       Voiders; Besagew
                               L or R
  Abdomen / Lower Back         Plackart; lower backplate
  Groin / Pelvis
  (Glutes / Sacrum behind)     Faulds; tassets hang here
  Collarbone / Base of Neck    Gorget

  A cuirass is Chest + Abdomen, plus Upper / Lower
  Back if a backplate Form is on. Facing names
  which side of the pair is live.

  Faulds sit on Groin / Pelvis (or Glutes / Sacrum
  from behind). Tassets hang from that Slot onto
  Thigh. They do not take the Thigh Slot. Cuisses
  may sit on Thigh under them.

  Shoulder is an Arm Limb location, not Torso.
  Pauldrons live there.

  Armpit is a Torso Limb location. It is not
  Shoulder. Voiders and Besagews live here.
  Ordinary blows can land on it (5% of Torso).

ARM Slots (Limb locations, L and R separate)

  Slot            Typical piece
  Hand            Gauntlet
  Forearm         Vambrace; Bracers; Jack chains
  Elbow           Couter tags Elbow = both
                  Outer and Inner at 100%.
                  Voider tags Inner Elbow only.
  Upper Arm       Rerebrace; Jack chains
  Shoulder        Pauldron

  Full Gauntlet claims Hand + Forearm.
  Jack chains claim Upper Arm + Forearm on that
  side. They leave Elbow and Hand free. They do
  not claim a Torso slot.

  L and R are separate wardrobes.
  One Modular piece per slot per side. A solid
  vambrace and segmented bracers cannot share a
  forearm. A cuirass plus pauldrons plus jack
  chains is legal: different slots.

LEG Slots (Limb locations, L and R separate)

  Slot            Typical piece
  Foot            Sabaton; boots
  Shin / Calf     Full greaves; rigid boot greaves
  Knee            Poleyn tags Front Knee.
                  Back Knee is facing, from
                  behind. Mail Knee 50% is
                  the back of the joint.
  Thigh           Cuisses
  Hip / Buttock   (pair with Groin / Pelvis on
                  Torso when facing says so)

  Faulds and tassets are Torso Groin / Pelvis,
  not this list. Tassets still hang coverage
  onto Thigh. Cuisses and tassets are not
  exclusive.

  Hem-H Slots use the same Limb location
  names with H (Thigh-H, Knee-H, Shin-H,
  Hip-H). Trouser Slots are the plain names.
  Hem AC and trouser AC do not stack.

  L and R are separate.
  One Modular piece per slot per side.

HEAD Slots (Limb locations)

  Slot                         Typical piece
  Eyes                         Visor; full visor
  Skull / Occipital            Bowl; skullcap; sallet
                               (facing picks the name)
  Lower face / Back of neck    Nasal; bevor;
                               sallet tail; coif ventail
  Throat                       Gorget (if worn as Head);
                               mail standard

  Empty Eyes is the visor zone: 0 AC until a
  visor sits there.

  Open Helmet
    Face is open on the Model. Eyes and
    Lower face are empty unless an add-on
    sits there. Skullcap, nasal, kettle,
    open sallet, open bascinet.

  Closed Helmet
    The Model itself claims Lower face
    (and usually Eyes once a visor is
    down). Armet, barbute, greathelm,
    enclosed helm, great bascinet,
    frog-mouth. An Open Helmet with a
    visor down is Closed while it is down.

  A helm may claim more than one slot.
    Skullcap              Skull          Open
    Nasal / kettle        Skull          Open
    Sallet                Skull + Occipital
                                         Open
    Bascinet              Skull          Open
    Armet / barbute       Skull + Face   Closed
    Great bascinet,
    frog-mouth,
    visored great helm    Skull + Face + Occipital
                          Eyes if a visor is fitted
                                         Closed
    Greathelm / enclosed  Skull + Face + Occipital
                                         Closed

  Helm Form (Armour.txt)
    Visor shapes          Eyes. Down = Closed
                          Houndskull, pig face,
                          bellows, klappvisor,
                          grotesque, sparrow’s
                          beak, grill, gryphon,
                          wolf/dog grill
    Falling Buffe         Face + Throat.
                          Open drops Face 75%
    Bevor                 Face + Throat (own piece)
    Duckbill              Closed. Fixed mask
    Burgonet              Open. Buffe-friendly

  One Modular piece per slot. A nasal and a full
  visor cannot both take Face. A skullcap cannot
  sit under a bascinet. Visor tags stay cosmetic
  once Eyes is filled.

--------------------------------------------------
2. LAYERS
--------------------------------------------------

  None               no worn AC
  Clothing           0 AC. Weather and Fellowship only.
  Textile            padded, leather, quilt. Soft. Strong
                     vs Blunt relative to its cost.
  Maille            Mail, Riveted or Butted.
  Modular            slot pieces. Segmented coats,
                     jack chains, solid plate. Uses Slots (section 1a).

Typical band (not a substitute for the piece table)

  Layer                C / P / B     Notes
  Clothing             0 / 0 / 0
  Textile              2–3 / 1–2 / 2–3
  Butted mail          2 / 2 / 2
  Riveted mail         4 / 4 / 4
  Scale                4 / 3 / 5
  Modular (segmented)  4–6 / 4–6 / 4–5
  Modular (solid)      5–8 / 5–9 / 4–7


Iron Grade
  Eligible: Coat of Plates, Brigandine,
  Scale, Splinted (and Jack chains),
  Laminar, Solid Plate (torso, limbs,
  iron helmets).
  Not: Cuir bouilli, Maille, Textile,
  Clothing, boot leather.
  Listed stats are Low-carbon.
  Wrought ×0.80 cost ×1.15 Enc −1 AC.
  Low-carbon ×1 ×1 +0.
  Hardened ×1.25 cost ×0.85 Enc +1 AC.
  After construction Material %
  (Splinted / Laminar only). Cuir
  bouilli never takes Grade.
  Catalogue §9.


--------------------------------------------------
3. LAYERING
--------------------------------------------------

Outer to inner. Sum AC from every piece that applies.

  Each slot: one Textile, one Maille, one
  Modular. A torso garment may claim many slots at
  once. Default body if unsaid:
    Chest / Upper Back 100%
    Abdomen / Lower Back 100%
    Collarbone / Base of Neck 100%
    Short Sleeves: Armpit 100%, Shoulder 100%,
                   Upper Arm 50%
    Form None leaves Armpit 50% and no other
    arm Slots. Long Sleeves keep Armpit 100%
    and take the full arm.
    Short Hem: Groin / Pelvis 95%, Hip / Buttock 95%,
               Thigh 50%
  Write those Slots on the catalogue line when they
  apply. Do not hide Short Sleeves as a footnote.
  Two pieces of the same layer cannot share a slot.
  Textile under mail under Modular is legal,
  except on Hand.
  Mail voiders only fill a gap the outer piece left.
  Armpit voiders sit on Armpit.
  Elbow: the second d100 that landed 36–45 is
  Outer Elbow if even, Inner Elbow if odd.
  A piece tagged Elbow covers both at 100%.
  An Elbow voider tags Inner Elbow only.
  A couter tagged Elbow answers both. It
  does not need a second piece for the crease.

When the outermost piece on that location applies
(coverage success), a Textile layer under it applies
in full with no roll. Other inner layers still roll.

Under-layers
  Modular on Arms requires an Arming Doublet. Body
    plate alone does not.
    Jack chains do not require a Doublet. A Doublet
    under them is legal.
  Modular on Body only (no Modular on the Arms) is
    fine over an Aketon or Pourpoint. No chafing.
    An Arming Doublet also serves. A Gambeson does
    not — it is standalone.
  Modular on Legs requires Arming Hose.
  Chausses that tag Shin / Calf may not
    share that Slot with a boot that also
    tags Shin / Calf. Cuffed or riding boots
    and greaved boots count. Short Chausses
    exist to leave the shin to the boot.
    Forced together: −10 Agility tests and
    −1 AC on Shin / Calf (bulk).
    Arming hose is exempt. It is thin and
    cut for boots.
  Boot Armour Material is legal only on
    Cuffed boots and Riding boots. Foot-only
    Models (Turnshoes, Ankle, Poulaine, Clog)
    cannot take it. Coverage cannot appear
    on a Slot the Model does not have.
  Padded on Cuffed or Riding boots is Modular.
    The Textile word on that line is Upgrade
    eligibility only. Soak treats it as Modular
    on Shin / Calf.

  Mail coif or a Closed Helmet requires an
    Arming cap.
    A Padded coif may stand in for the cap
    under an Open Helmet or a mail coif.
    A Padded coif under a Closed Helmet is
    bulk, same as a Gambeson under Modular
    torso: −10 Agility tests and −1 AC on
    Head.
    A Padded turban is legal under an Open
    Helmet. Under a Closed Helmet it is the
    same bulk penalty as the Padded coif.
  Maille on Body or Arms requires a Textile
    torso whose sleeve Slots cover at least
    the mail’s sleeve Slots (no sleeves under
    no sleeves is legal; Short under Long is
    not). Scale is Modular. It uses the same
    sleeve rule against its Textile under-layer.
  Hands
    Textile under mail is wanted on Hand.
    A gauntlet is one Modular piece. Textile
    and Maille tracks on Hand stay empty.
    Nothing goes under it. No chafing for a
    naked palm inside plate. Fingerless under
    mail is legal. Fingerless under a
    gauntlet is not.

  Missing the required piece, or sleeves too short:
    −10 Agility tests and −1 AC on that location
    (chafing). Mail on Hand without Textile
    takes this. A gauntlet does not.

Enc
  A point cost on a worn piece. Not kilograms
  on the catalogue line. Add every worn piece
  (armour, clothing, and whatever else is on
  the body). Do not round Enc. Cost may
  be listed as sc.bc. Add the numbers as
  written after Form, Material, Grade,
  and Upgrades.

  Strength 30 is the ordinary adult.
  Strength 40 is the trained adult.

  Bridge until Economy maps real mass:
  10 Enc ≈ 1 kg.

  Bands (Characteristics §3d)

    Unencumbered    Enc < Strength × 5
                    Agility tests +5
    Light Kit       Strength × 5 ≤ Enc
                    < Strength × 10
                    no modifier
    Heavy Kit       Enc = Strength × 10
                    Agility tests −5
                    Fatigue as §3a
    Over the cap    Enc > Strength × 10
                    cannot fight in it
                    Drop or crawl

  Enc Limit (combat load) stays
  Strength × 10. That is Heavy and the cap.
  Strength 30 → 300. Strength 40 → 400.

  The ±5 is Agility tests (Dodge, Silent
  Move, climb). It does not rewrite M.


--------------------------------------------------
4. CRAFTSMANSHIP
--------------------------------------------------

Listed AC is Common.

  Good   +1 to each AC column. Cost ×3.
  Best   +2 to each AC column. Cost ×10.
  Poor   −1 to each column. Cost ×½.

--------------------------------------------------
5. RESOLUTION ON A HIT
--------------------------------------------------

The hit already stood (WS/BS, then Dodge or Parry).
Now find where it landed and what caught it.

  1. Location. Two d100s on a biped (Injury §3).
     First: Wallace limb.
     Second: Slot on that limb.
     3+ DoS or Called Shot may name the limb,
     the Slot, or both. These dice are not the
     Coverage die.

  2. Type. Slash / Thrust / Bash from the weapon.
     Incoming = 1d10 + SB + type + DoS (Combat §9).

  3. One coverage die. Roll 1d100 once. Keep it.
     Attacker DoS: −10 Coverage per 2 DoS,
     only on a piece whose Coverage on this
     Slot is less than 100%. A listed 100%
     is not reduced.

     Standing target. A shot or swing at a man
     on his feet does not hunt the femoral or
     the visor slit unless the attacker
     Called Shot that gap or has 3+ DoS and names
     it. Armpit is a real Torso band now. It
     can be hit without a Called Shot. Padded
     chausses and arming hose are 100% on the
     Slots they list against an ordinary blow.
     Mail chausses use the Coverage on their
     Form. The holes that still exist
     on a closed harness are the face (Eyes
     empty, visor up) and the groin (no fauld
     or codpiece, or a declared gap). A rondel
     in the bind may use the old lower Coverage.
     An arrow at twenty yards may not.

  4. Modular. Is a Modular piece tagged to
     this Slot? If no, go to step 5.
     If yes, compare the Coverage die to that
     piece’s Coverage on this Slot.
     Pass: Soak AC = that piece’s column,
     plus Maille on this Slot,
     plus Textile on this Slot.
     Log the piece (“the blow rings on the
     vambrace”). Stop. Go to step 7.
     Fail: go to step 5.
     A Chest plate does not answer Abdomen.

  5. Maille. If the same d100 is greater
     than the Modular catch, compare it to the
     Maille Coverage on this Slot.
     If the d100 ≤ that Coverage:
       Soak AC = Maille AC on this Slot,
       plus Textile AC on this Slot.
       Stop. Go to step 7.

  6. Textile. If the same d100 is greater than the
     mail Coverage, compare it to the Textile
     Coverage on this Slot.
     If the d100 ≤ that Coverage:
       Soak AC = Textile AC on this Slot.
     If not: no worn AC. NAC only.

  7. Hem and trousers on Textile or mail do not
     stack. Use the higher column of the two.
  8. Armour Piercing: −1 worn AC after the total
     (minimum 0).
  9. Protection = NAC + Soak AC.
     Incoming − Protection = Wounds.
     If Current Wounds remain, stop. Dink only
     (Core_Injury §1).
     If this hit takes Current Wounds to 0,
     it is a Finishing Blow. Hand the Slot,
     the column, and Overflow to
     Injury_Tables_Bipedal.txt.

The same d100 is never rerolled. It is read down
the layers until one catches or none do.

Do not add plates
  One Slot, one Modular piece. Its AC is
  the plate number. Mail and quilt add.
  A second plate on a different Slot is
  a different second die, not more soak.

  Face-to-face, a Thrust, second die Abdomen.
  Plackart is tagged Abdomen, Pierce 7,
  Coverage 100% of that Slot.
  Breastplate is Chest. It does not roll.
  Coverage die 31: the plackart catches.
  Soak is 7 from the plackart, plus mail
  and quilt on Abdomen, plus NAC.
  Say the spear fetched up on the belly
  plate.
