# Core_Traits.txt
# Traits. Not Talents.
# A Talent is learned or born on a person.
# A Trait is how a non-human Entity is built.
# The sheet names the Trait. This file is the law.
# Status: DRAFT.

--------------------------------------------------
0. HOW A TRAIT IS READ
--------------------------------------------------

The Entity sheet lists the name and the (x).
Do not reprint the law on the sheet.
A Trait is always on. It is not a skill rank.
It is not lost by fatigue unless the line says so.

--------------------------------------------------
1. DWELLER (BIOME)
--------------------------------------------------

The sheet names the tags. Wetland, pond,
cave, canopy, high steppe. One Trait.
Immune to environmental hazards of those
tagged biomes. The tag is the bestiary
HSI index for that Entity.
No Outdoor Survival test while it is in
a tagged habitat. Hunger, wounds, and a
blade still apply. The ground does not.

--------------------------------------------------
2. AMPHIBIOUS
--------------------------------------------------

Lives in water and in air.
Underwater, the suffocation clock does not
start (Hazards.txt). It surfaces when it
chooses, not when the breath window ends.
On land it breathes as normal. A sealed
airless room is not water. The clock runs.

--------------------------------------------------
3. REGENERATION (X)
--------------------------------------------------

At the start of its Turn, a Toughness test.
Success: remove (x) Wounds. Failure: none.
It cannot rise above its Wound maximum.
Slain: the Trait is gone. A corpse does not
knit. Do not roll it after the finishing blow
has killed it.

--------------------------------------------------
4. NATURAL WEAPONS (NAME)
--------------------------------------------------

The names are the weapons. Fists, tusks, claws.
It counts as armed with nothing in its hands.
Damage is 1d10 + Strength Bonus. Type is on
the sheet. If the sheet does not name a type,
fists are Blunt, tusks and claws are Cut.
Attack with Weapon Skill.
No Parry with a natural weapon.
No Disarm, unless the limb is hacked off.
A held weapon uses the weapon, not this Trait.
Both may be used if the sheet gives Attacks
enough to swing each.
