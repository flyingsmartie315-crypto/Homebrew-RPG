# Core_Traits.txt
# Traits. Not Talents.
# A Talent is learned or born on a person.
# A Trait is how a non-human Entity is built.
# The sheet names the Trait. This file is the law.
# Status: DRAFT.

--------------------------------------------------
0. HOW A TRAIT IS READ
--------------------------------------------------

The Entity sheet lists the name and the (x).
Do not reprint the law on the sheet.
A Trait is always on. It is not a skill rank.
It is not lost by fatigue unless the line says so.

--------------------------------------------------
1. DWELLER (BIOME)
--------------------------------------------------

The sheet names the tags. Wetland, pond,
cave, canopy, high steppe. One Trait.
Immune to environmental hazards of those
tagged biomes. The tag is the bestiary
HSI index for that Entity.
No Outdoor Survival test while it is in
a tagged habitat. Hunger, wounds, and a
blade still apply. The ground does not.

--------------------------------------------------
2. AMPHIBIOUS
--------------------------------------------------

Lives in water and in air.
Underwater, the suffocation clock does not
start (Hazards.txt). It surfaces when it
chooses, not when the breath window ends.
On land it breathes as normal. A sealed
airless room is not water. The clock runs.

--------------------------------------------------
3. REGENERATION (X)
--------------------------------------------------

At the start of its Turn, a Toughness test.
Success: remove (x) Wounds. Failure: none.
It cannot rise above its Wound maximum.
Slain: the Trait is gone. A corpse does not
knit. Do not roll it after the finishing blow
has killed it.

--------------------------------------------------
4. NATURAL WEAPONS (NAME)
--------------------------------------------------

The names are the weapons. Fists, tusks, claws.
It counts as armed with nothing in its hands.
Damage is 1d10 + Strength Bonus. Type is on
the sheet. If the sheet does not name a type,
fists are Blunt, tusks and claws are Cut.
Attack with Weapon Skill.
No Parry with a natural weapon.
No Disarm, unless the limb is hacked off.
A held weapon uses the weapon, not this Trait.
Both may be used if the sheet gives Attacks
enough to swing each.
The sheet’s Attack Profile names the
damage, the type, and the Weapon Skill.
This Trait is why it counts as armed.

--------------------------------------------------
5. DOG-GAITED
--------------------------------------------------

Runs on all four limbs.
Tight, low tunnels and opened scrapes
are not difficult ground for it.
No terrain penalty for that squeeze.
A man still pays the ground. It does not.
Forced upright is not this gait.
It does not grant a Parry.

--------------------------------------------------
6. BURROWER (X)
--------------------------------------------------

Moves through soil, loose rock, and sand.
(x) is the Movement while burrowing.
It replaces ground M for that move.
The sheet names anything harder. Stone
that is not named is not burrowed.
It leaves a tunnel. An Entity one Size
smaller can use that tunnel with no
penalty. Each Round after it passes,
1–50 on 1d100: the tunnel collapses.

--------------------------------------------------
7. CRAWLER
--------------------------------------------------

Worms, serpents, and the like. They do
not walk. Ground M is halved, round up.
Difficult ground does not penalise that
crawl. A Flyer that is also a Crawler
uses flight when it is airborne.

--------------------------------------------------
8. DARK-SIGHT
--------------------------------------------------

Sees in total darkness. No penalty for
dim light or no light on a sight test,
or on a fight that needs sight.
It does not see through walls. Smoke
and a blindfold still block it.

--------------------------------------------------
9. FLYER (X)
--------------------------------------------------

Wings, a gas sac, or a veil-lift. The
sheet names which. (x) is Flying
Movement. It replaces M while airborne.
If the sheet has no number, x is
(Agility ÷ 10) × 2, one decimal.
Law of the air: Characteristics §3d.

--------------------------------------------------
10. QUADRUPED
--------------------------------------------------

Hind legs and forepaws. Ground M is
double the sheet M. This is distance,
not a squeeze. Dog-gaited is the
tunnel. Both may be on one sheet.
A mount is still this Trait. The rider
does not gain it.

--------------------------------------------------
11. SONAR SENSE (X)
--------------------------------------------------

It does not see that volume. It emits.
Solid objects within (x) metres are
located. Awareness tests that would
be sight may be hearing instead,
inside that range.
Another Entity in range may hear the
keening: Awareness −10.

--------------------------------------------------
13. WALL-RUNNER
--------------------------------------------------

Arachnids, hunting insects, and the like.
More than one purchase on the surface.
Ground M is not halved by this Trait.
A vertical face — stone, an old bole, a
rift — and an inverted face — a cave
ceiling, a dense branch canopy — are
flat ground for Movement and Actions.
Difficult ground does not penalise M
and does not force a footing test.
Loose scree, deep mud, heavy foliage.
High-tensile silk, a catching web, and
bark do not take an Athletics test to
climb or to cross.
Spider sheets carry this. Pale Lurker,
Orb-Wright, Skitterling, Weber-Fisher.

--------------------------------------------------
14. BESTIAL
--------------------------------------------------

A tag. Not a modifier. The Entity does
not use the complex list. Core_Combat.txt
still has the Action. This Trait says
the body does not take it.

  Aim
  Called Shot
  Disengage
  Ready
  Reload
  Cast / Channel
  Feint
  Guarded Attack
  Manoeuvre
  Parrying Stance

A bite, a claw, a charge, a standard
strike, and a dodge stay available if
the sheet has them. A shield or a
loaded sling is not this Trait.
Mintrox do not carry it.

--------------------------------------------------
12. FEAR (X)
--------------------------------------------------

The body is wrong to look at. (x) is 1–4.
Law and the Shock table: Core_Fear.txt.
A Willpower test when the Entity is
first seen this scene. The rating is
the penalty. Fail: Shock, +10 on the
Shock roll per Degree of Failure.

--------------------------------------------------
15. FLUID BULK (X)
--------------------------------------------------

The mass is there. The outline is not
a barn. (x) offsets the Size to-hit
bonus against this body, the Size
penalty when this body strikes a
smaller target, and the Size stealth
penalty on it.
Core_Characteristics_Skills.txt §3.
It does not change Size.
It does not stack past the Size number.
A Size 7 body is +30 to be hit, −30
to strike a man, and −30 to hide.
Fluid Bulk (20) leaves +10, −10,
and −10. Floor 0.
