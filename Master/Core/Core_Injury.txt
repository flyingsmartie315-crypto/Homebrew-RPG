# Core_Injury.txt
# Virath injury — the finishing blow, conditions, recovery
# Status: DRAFT for Core.
# Companion files:
#   Core_Combat.txt                     how damage is dealt
#   Core_Characteristics_Skills.txt     Wounds, NAC, AC, Heal, Surgery
#   Core_Resolution.txt                 tests, Degrees, Fate spends sit on FP
#   Injury_Tables_Bipedal.txt           Slot tables (Cut / Pierce / Blunt)

PURPOSE
Combat ticks Wounds. This file says what happens when they run
out. Boxes above 0 stay glancing. The blow that reaches 0 is
the one that maims, drops, or kills.

Do not drip crits, bleed tables, or ruined knees across the
Wounds track. That was locked in Characteristics §3c.

--------------------------------------------------
0. KEYWORDS
--------------------------------------------------

Finishing Blow
  The hit that takes Current Wounds to 0. The only blow
  that inflicts a real Injury.

Overflow
  Incoming damage left after Current Wounds are emptied.
  Overflow sets Severity.

Severity
  How bad the Finishing Blow is. Equal to Overflow.
  Minimum 1 (the blow that spends the last Wound exactly).

Wallace location
  The limb the first d100 named. Locked bands
  in this section: Head, Torso, Right Arm,
  Left Arm, Right Leg, Left Leg.

Limb location
  Where on that limb the second d100 named.
  Locked bands in this section. This is the
  “where” Injury tables use. It is the same
  list as armour Slots (Armour_Rules.txt §0, §1a).
  Facing picks the front or back name on a
  pair.

Location
  Both dice together. The Finishing Blow uses
  that same pair. Do not roll again.

Down
  On the ground. Out of the fight unless a rule stands
  them up. Not necessarily dead.

Helpless
  Cannot act, Dodge, or Parry.
  Melee attacks against a Helpless target automatically
  succeed. No WS test. Damage is SB + weapon. The
  attacker may roll WS only to add DoS; a fail on that
  optional roll does not miss.
  Ranged attacks still roll BS.

Stunned
  Cannot take Full or Half Actions. Free Actions only
  if the GM allows a gasp or a drop. Ends when the
  listed Rounds or a condition says so.

Minor Bleed
  Blood loss a dressing can hold. Each hour untreated:
  Toughness or lose 1 Wound (minimum 0). Heal stops it.

Major Bleed
  A vessel is open. Each Round untreated: Toughness
  or lose 1 Wound. Heal (or pressure and a kit) is
  needed to stop it. Fail the first Heal and it stays
  Major. A messy stump is Difficult to control.

Catastrophic Bleed
  A trunk vessel. Collapse and death in minutes
  unless the bleed is controlled now. After three
  Turns, Toughness to stay conscious. Each Turn
  after that the test is +10 harder on the d100
  (Turn 4 vs TB, Turn 5 vs TB −10, Turn 6 vs
  TB −20, and so on). Fail: unconscious, then
  dying. Heal or Surgery at once, or they go.

Stable
  Not dying this hour. Still Down or injured.

Dead
  The Entity is dead. Fate may have already interrupted
  this. Nothing else in this file undoes it.

--------------------------------------------------
1. WHEN INJURY HAPPENS
--------------------------------------------------

Damage through Protection reduces Current Wounds
(Combat §9). While Current Wounds remain after the hit,
describe a dink, a bruise, scored kit, winded breath.
Stop there.

The hit that reduces Current Wounds to 0 is a Finishing
Blow. Resolve it in this order:

  1. Note Overflow
       Overflow = damage through Protection − Wounds
       the Entity still had before this hit.
       If the last Wound is spent exactly, Overflow = 1.
  2. Use the Location already set by the attack
       (Combat §5). Do not roll Location again.
  3. If this Limb location has a Slot table
       (section 4), roll it. Result =
       1d10 + Overflow − TB. Minimum 1.
       10 or more uses the 10+ row.
       1 is best, 10+ is worst.
       Use the column that matches the blow
       (Cut / Pierce / Blunt).
     If no Slot table exists yet, use the
     old Severity bands (section 2) on the
     Wallace location.
  4. Apply conditions and Bleed. The Entity
     is at 0 Wounds unless the row says a
     Wound cap.

Further hits on a target already at 0 are not more
Finishing Blows on a Wounds track. They are blows on a
Helpless body (section 5).

Fate (Combat §13) may be spent before step 4 to stay at
1 Wound, Down, out of the fight, no table.

--------------------------------------------------
2. SEVERITY
--------------------------------------------------

Overflow is Severity.

  Severity    What it is
     1–2      Dropped. Hurt. Will walk if treated.
     3–4      Debilitating. A part fails. Days or weeks.
     5–6      Maiming. A part is ruined unless Surgery
              holds it. Dying is close.
     7–8      Mortal. Bleeding or shock. Toughness or
              Heal this hour, or death.
     9+       Dead. Describe it. Fate was the only door.

Overflow does not invent extra corpses behind the first
one. One Finishing Blow, one result. A later blow on a
Helpless body may kill (section 5).

--------------------------------------------------
3. LOCATION
--------------------------------------------------

Location is rolled on every successful hit (Combat §5),
not only on the Finishing Blow. The Finishing Blow uses
that same pair of dice. Do not roll again.

Biped (human, most Entities). Two d100s. Print both
bands on the character / stat sheet.

First d100 — Wallace (which limb)
  01–10    Head         10%
  11–50    Torso        40%
  51–60    Right Arm    10%
  61–70    Left Arm     10%
  71–85    Right Leg    15%
  86–100   Left Leg     15%

Second d100 — where on that limb (the Slot)

  Arm (locked)
    01–10    Hand         10%
    11–35    Forearm      25%
    36–45    Elbow        10%
    46–80    Upper Arm    35%
    81–00    Shoulder     20%

  Leg (locked)
    01–08    Foot              8%
    09–47    Shin / Calf       39%
    48–57    Knee              10%
    58–95    Thigh             38%
    96–00    Hip / Buttock     5%

  Head (locked)
    01–10    Eyes                         10%
    11–60    Skull / Occipital            50%
    61–85    Lower face / Back of neck    25%
    86–00    Throat                       15%

    Facing picks the pair where there is one.
    Face-to-face: Eyes, Skull, Lower face, Throat.
    From behind: Eyes read as Occipital; Skull
    is Occipital; Lower face is Back of neck;
    Throat is Back of neck.
    Flank: GM names which of the pair.

  Torso (locked)
    01–45    Chest / Upper Back           45%
    46–85    Abdomen / Lower Back         40%
    86–95    Groin / Pelvis
             (Glutes / Sacrum from behind) 10%
    96–00    Collarbone / Base of Neck     5%

    Facing picks the front or back name.
    Face-to-face: Chest, Abdomen, Groin / Pelvis,
    Collarbone.
    From behind: Upper Back, Lower Back,
    Glutes / Sacrum, Base of Neck.
    Flank: GM names which of the pair.

Three or more DoS, or Called Shot (Combat §3): name
the limb, the Slot, or both. Do not roll what was
named.

A hand hit uses the Hand table, not the Arm table.
That is the point. A finishing blow on the fingers
does not become a dead man because the old table
only knew “Arm.”

Giant or wrong-shaped bodies: pick the analogue
(wing for arm, coil for leg) or the nearest column.
The second die may be dropped for a beast if the
sheet says so.

Armour (Armour_Rules.txt §5) tests Coverage only against
pieces tagged to the Slot the second die named.
A vambrace does not answer an Upper Arm hit.

--------------------------------------------------
4. SLOT TABLES
--------------------------------------------------

The rows live in Injury_Tables_Bipedal.txt.
This file only says how to roll them.

  Result = 1d10 + Overflow − TB.
  Floor 1. 10 or more reads the 10+ row.
  Column = Cut, Pierce, or Blunt.

“Max N wounds inflicted” means the table does
not pile the rest of Overflow on as more
Wounds. The row is the result.

All bipedal Slots are live in
Injury_Tables_Bipedal.txt. Section 4z
is only for beasts and odd anatomy.

--------------------------------------------------
4z. FALLBACK (no Slot table yet)
--------------------------------------------------

Read Severity across the Wallace location.
Beasts, wings, coils, and anything that
is not on the bipedal map still use this.

HEAD
  1–2  Down. Stunned 1 Round. Ringing skull, split
       scalp. Stands with a Stand Action after that.
  3–4  Down. Stunned 1d5 Rounds. Cannot see straight
       (−10 all tests until treated).
  5–6  Down and Helpless until Heal. One eye or the
       jaw is wrong. −20 Perception or Fellowship
       until Surgery. Toughness test now or also
       unconscious 1d10 minutes.
  7–8  Dying. Helpless. Toughness each Round or
       lose the hour — death at the third fail.
       Heal (Known+) stops the count.
  9+   Dead.

ARM
  1–2  Drops what that hand held (not a strapped
       shield). −10 tests with that arm for the
       scene.
  3–4  Arm useless until Heal. Drops the thing.
       Cannot wield two-handed on that side.
  5–6  Arm ruined. Surgery or it is lost below the
       joint. Bleeds: Toughness each Round until
       Heal or the third fail is death.
  7–8  As 5–6, and Dying as Head 7–8.
  9+   Dead. The blow opened more than the limb.

BODY
  1–2  Down. Winded. Stand Action to rise. −10
       physical tests for the scene.
  3–4  Down. Cannot Stand until Heal. Breathing
       is work. −20 physical tests.
  5–6  Helpless. Something inside is wrong.
       Toughness now or unconscious. Heal to
       move at all. Travel is a stretcher.
  7–8  Dying. Helpless. Toughness each Round;
       third fail is death. Heal (Known+) stops
       the count.
  9+   Dead.

LEG
  1–2  Stumbles. Next Turn is Half Actions only.
       M halved for the scene.
  3–4  Down. Cannot Stand until Heal. M = 1
       crawl. No Dodge.
  5–6  Leg ruined. Surgery or it is lost below
       the joint. Down. No Dodge. Bleeds as Arm
       5–6.
  7–8  As 5–6, and Dying as Head 7–8.
  9+   Dead.

Lost parts (after the fight, if they lived)
  Lost hand     −20 on work that wants two hands.
                No two-handed weapons. Shield may
                strap to the stump.
  Lost arm      As lost hand. No shield strap.
  Lost foot     M halved. −20 on movement Skills
                and Dodge.
  Lost leg      As lost foot. No Dodge Skill.
  Lost eye      −20 BS and −20 on sight tests.
  Two eyes      Blind. Those tests are not rolled.

Two permanent losses: write both. If the body cannot
carry that and live (two legs gone in the wild with
no aid), the GM says whether they make the next camp.

--------------------------------------------------
5. HELPLESS AND ALREADY AT ZERO
--------------------------------------------------

An Entity at 0 Wounds is Down and, unless Severity
1–2 and they have stood, Helpless.

A new hit on a Helpless body does not roll Location
again unless the attacker takes time to aim (−20, or
a Full Action and no other Attack). Otherwise:

  Incoming through Protection 1–4    one step worse
                                     on the existing
                                     Injury, or a
                                     new Dying count
                                     if there was none
  Incoming 5+                        Dead, unless Fate

Do not farm a dozen crits on a corpse. One coup and
the scene moves.

--------------------------------------------------
6. CONDITIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Stunned
  Listed Rounds. No Full or Half Actions. Then gone.

Down
  On the ground. Stand / Mount (Half) to rise, if
  the Injury allows standing.

Helpless
  No Turn, no Dodge, no Parry.
  Melee attacks automatically succeed (section 0).

Dying
  Toughness test each Round. Fail count 3 = Dead.
  Heal at Known or better, taking a Full Action,
  stops the count and leaves them Stable and
  Helpless. Surgery is not required for that stop.

Unconscious
  Helpless. Minutes as listed. Wakes Stunned 1 Round.

Bleeding
  The same count as Dying when the table says it
  bleeds. Heal stops it.

These are not Wounds and not Fatigue. They do not
tick the Wounds track.

--------------------------------------------------
7. RECOVERY
--------------------------------------------------

Current Wounds return only after the Entity is not
Dying.

  Not from 0, night’s rest, no treatment
    1 Wound at dawn if they can sleep.
  Not from 0, Heal (Known+) used that day
    1d5 Wounds, once per day.
  From 0, after a Finishing Blow
    No Wound returns until Heal or Surgery has
    been applied at least once. Then 1 Wound per
    day of rest, or 1d5 with daily Heal.
  Surgery (Known+)
    Required to save a part listed as ruined.
    One test. Success: the part is kept, useless
    for 1d10 days, then −10 until a week of rest.
    Failure: the part is lost. Fumble: Dying
    starts again.

Travel while at 0 or with a ruined part is a GM
call. Usually a cart, a stretcher, or they do not
come.
