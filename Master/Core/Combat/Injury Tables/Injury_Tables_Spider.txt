# Injury_Tables_Spider.txt
# Virath Slot injury tables — spider Entities
# Status: DRAFT for Core.
# Wallace and Slots live in this file.
# Law: Core_Injury.txt (Bleed, Overflow, how to roll)
# Use for: giant spiders, hunting spiders,
# trapdoor things, and any eight-legger built
# on a cephalothorax and an abdomen.
# Not a crab. Not a scorpion (tail is its own
# table later).

Roll 1d10 + Overflow − TB.
Floor 1. 10 or more uses the 10+ row.
1 is best. 10+ is worst.
Use the column that matches the blow.

Bleed on this table is hemolymph. Same
keywords. It looks wrong. It still drops
them.

One lost leg is not Down. Two legs gone
on the same side, or three gone anywhere,
is Down. A ruined Pedicel or opened
Abdomen venter at 8+ is the kill.

Facing: face-to-face is Eyes, Fangs,
Pedipalps, Sternum, Abdomen venter.
From above is Carapace and Abdomen
dorsum. From behind is Spinnerets.

Order: Cephalothorax, Abdomen, Leg.

WALLACE (first d100)
  01–16    Cephalothorax     16%
  17–54    Abdomen           38%
  55–65    Right Fore pair   11%
  66–76    Left Fore pair    11%
  77–88    Right Hind pair   12%
  89–00    Left Hind pair    12%

  Fore pair = walking legs I and II
  on that side.
  Hind pair = walking legs III and IV
  on that side.
  Pedipalps live on Cephalothorax, not
  on a Fore pair.

SLOTS (second d100)

  Cephalothorax
    01–15    Eyes                         15%
    16–35    Fangs                        20%
    36–50    Pedipalps                    15%
    51–80    Carapace                     30%
    81–00    Sternum                      20%

  Abdomen
    01–35    Dorsum                       35%
    36–55    Venter                       20%
    56–75    Pedicel                      20%
    76–00    Spinnerets                   25%

  Leg pair (any)
    Even result = forward leg of the pair
    Odd  result = rear leg of the pair
    Then read the tens as the Slot:
    01–20    Coxa                         20%
    21–45    Femur                        25%
    46–70    Tibia                        25%
    71–00    Tarsus                       30%

  Read even/odd on the same second die
  that names the Slot. 44 even + Tibia =
  forward leg of that pair, Tibia.

==================================================
EYES
==================================================

A cluster, not a pair. 7 ruins the set
on that face, not one globe.

CUT
  1   Rim nick. Dress it.
  2   One lens split. −10 Perception until
      bound.
  3   Cluster grazed. Minor Bleed. Most
      eyes spared.
  4   Several lenses opened. −20 Perception
      until Heal. Minor Bleed.
  5   Front row ruined. No precise strike.
      Minor Bleed.
  6   Cluster flayed. Blind on that face.
      Major Bleed.
  7   Eyes gone on that face. Permanent.
      Major Bleed. Hunts by vibration.
  8   Carapace opened at the eyes.
      Permanent. Major Bleed. Down unless
      Toughness.
  9   Through the cluster into the head.
      Catastrophic Bleed. Helpless.
 10+  Face taken off the carapace.
      Catastrophic. Toughness or dying.

PIERCE
  1   Grit in a lens. −10 Perception
      1 Round.
  2   One eye transfixed.
  3   Between lenses. Minor Bleed.
  4   Into the cluster, brain spared.
      Stunned. Minor Bleed.
  5   Several eyes burst. −20 Perception.
      Minor Bleed.
  6   Cluster burst. Blind that face.
      Permanent. Major Bleed.
  7   Through the eyes into the head.
      Major Bleed. Toughness or Down.
  8   Deep into the cephalothorax.
      Catastrophic. Unconscious.
  9   Through the head. Dead unless Fate.
 10+  Spike through the cluster. Dead.

BLUNT
  1   Glare bang. Flinch.
  2   Cluster bruise. −10 Perception.
  3   Lenses shocked. Agility or it
      misses the next lunge.
  4   Rim cracked. No Bleed.
  5   Cluster crushed, some eyes hold.
      Stunned. −20 Perception.
  6   Front row smashed. Permanent −10
      Perception unless the carapace
      is left alone.
  7   Cluster pulp. Blind that face.
      Permanent.
  8   Eyes and carapace stove. Down.
  9   Head crushed from the front.
      Unconscious.
 10+  Face destroyed. Dead unless Fate.
