# Injury_Tables_Gryphon.txt
# Earthbound gryphon. Not the royal. Not the cat.
# Law: Core_Injury.txt
# Entity: Entity_Gryphon.txt
# Bestiary: Bestiary_Gryphon.txt
# Predator rows are not this file.

Roll 1d10 + Overflow − TB.
Floor 1. 10 or more uses the 10+ row.
1 is best. 10+ is worst.
Use the column that matches the blow.

Head and Body do not cap Wounds unless
the row says so. A talon or a hind paw
may cap.

Down: cannot stand on three legs.
Helpless: the beak is ruined, or both
fore-palms are gone, or the spring
(both hind thighs) is gone.

Order: Head, Body, Fore, Hind.

WALLACE (first d100)
  01–14    Head              14%
  15–48    Body              34%
  49–66    Right Fore        18%
  67–84    Left Fore         18%
  85–92    Right Hind         8%
  93–00    Left Hind          8%

The fore is the mass. The hind is the spring.
