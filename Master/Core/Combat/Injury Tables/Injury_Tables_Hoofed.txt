# Injury_Tables_Hoofed.txt
# Virath Slot injury tables — hoofed quadrupeds
# that are not horse-type.
# Status: DRAFT for Core.
# Wallace and Slots live in this file.
# Law: Core_Injury.txt (Bleed, Overflow, how to roll)
# Use for: elk, deer, bison, cattle, pig, sheep,
# goat, and similar. Cloven hoof or pig hoof.
# Horse, destrier, unicorn: Injury_Tables_Equine.txt.
# Anything on paws: Injury_Tables_Predator.txt.

Roll 1d10 + Overflow − TB.
Floor 1. 10 or more uses the 10+ row.
1 is best. 10+ is worst.
Use the column that matches the blow.

Head and Body rows do not cap Wounds unless
a row says otherwise. Hoof amps may cap.

A hoofed beast that cannot stand on three
legs is Down. Collapse is Helpless.
8+ on a weight-bearing long bone is field
loss unless Fate or Surgery.

Horns and antlers are not their own Slot.
Called Shot names Skull. A broken horn is
Skull Blunt 4–6 flavour.

Order: Head, Body, Fore, Hind.
Hoof is shared.

WALLACE (first d100)
  01–12    Head              12%
  13–56    Body              44%
  57–68    Right Fore        12%
  69–80    Left Fore         12%
  81–90    Right Hind        10%
  91–00    Left Hind         10%

  Face-to-face weights Head and Fores.
  From behind, swap Fore/Hind shares.
  Horned head-on: GM may treat 01–18 as
  Head (the rack is a bigger target).

EYES
==================================================

CUT
  1   Lid nick.
  2   Lid split. −10 Perception until bound.
  3   Canthus opened. Minor Bleed. Globe spared.
  4   Globe grazed. −20 Perception that side
      until Heal. Minor Bleed.
  5   Cornea laid open. Blind that side until
      Surgery. Minor Bleed.
  6   Lid flayed. Eye shut. Major Bleed.
  7   Globe sliced. That eye gone unless
      Surgery this hour. Permanent. Major Bleed.
      Panic: Toughness or bolt.
  8   Orbit opened. Blind that side. Permanent.
      Major Bleed. Down unless held.
  9   Both orbits. Blind. Catastrophic Bleed.
 10+  Face through the sockets. Catastrophic.
      Toughness or dying.

  1   Grit in the eye. −10 Perception 1 Round.
  2   Lid transfixed.

  4   Orbit, globe intact. Stunned. Minor Bleed.
  5   Into the white. Heal this hour or gone.
  6   Globe burst. Blind that side. Permanent.
      Major Bleed. Bolts unless held.
  7   Through the orbit. Blind that side.
      Major Bleed.
  8   Deep orbit. Toughness or unconscious.
      Major Bleed.
  9   Into the cranium. Catastrophic. Down.
 10+  Both sockets. Dead unless Fate.
