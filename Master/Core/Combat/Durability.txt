# Virath — hitpoints on worn pieces.
# Status: DRAFT. First pass. Armour only.
# Law companions:
#   Armour.txt            AC columns, grades, upgrades
#   Armour_Rules.txt      which layer caught the blow
#   Weaponry.txt          incoming before soak
#   Core_Terrain.txt      Rainfall, Hydrology

Weapons are not in this pass.
Nets and bolas are. Weaponry.txt.

--------------------------------------------------
1b. NETS AND BOLAS
--------------------------------------------------

A hold has hitpoints. It is not armour
on the body. It does not join the harness
pool.

  Cast net            15
  Spider-silk net     25
  Bolas               10

Cut only. Pierce and Blunt do not
reduce these. AC is on the weapon line.
Incoming Cut above that AC costs
hitpoints, one for one. Floor 0.
At 0 the hold is cut. Entangle or Bolas
ends.
Craft applies. Apprentice ×0.25.
No Iron Grade. The cord is the piece.

--------------------------------------------------
1. WEAR (LOCKED)
--------------------------------------------------

Use the piece that caught the blow
(Armour_Rules coverage). A layer that
failed its coverage roll was not struck.

Compare incoming to that piece’s AC in
the blow’s column (Cut, Pierce, or Blunt),
after Iron Grade and upgrades.

  Incoming ≤ that AC:  no hitpoint loss.
  Incoming > that AC:  loss = Incoming − AC.

Loss is on that piece only. Textile under
a mail catch does not tick. NAC is not
in this comparison.

Hitpoints floor at 0. A blow cannot drive
them negative. Surplus past 0 is Wounds
on the body if soak says so. It is not
extra hitpoint damage.

At 0 the piece is scrap for soak (AC 0
on the columns it had). Kraken sinew
leaves the straps. Straps are not a
soak layer.

Clothing (0 AC) has no hitpoint track.
A blow does not wear a shirt.

--------------------------------------------------
2. BASE HITPOINTS
--------------------------------------------------

One track per piece, not per Slot.
Forms do not change the base.

  Textile                 30
  Cuir bouilli            35
  Maille, Butted          30
  Maille, Riveted         40
  Modular Segmented       40
  Modular Solid Plate     50

Cuir is the keyword, torso or limb or
boot armour-material. It does not take
the Segmented 40.

Boot Base Material is Textile 30.
Boot Armour Material uses the Modular
base of that material (cuir 35, splint
40, greaves as solid 50, padded as
textile 30).

Stack
  Listed base, then Grade, then
  Upgrades, then Craft. Floor once
  at the end. One grade. Making is
  still one upgrade. Field groups
  still one each. Multiply each
  upgrade that has a hitpoint line.
  Moisture bonuses are not this stack.

--------------------------------------------------
3. IRON GRADE ON THE TRACK
--------------------------------------------------

Same eligibility as Armour.txt §9 and
Weaponry.txt §2. This file is the HP
multiplier only. Cost, Enc, damage, and
which weapon may take the metal are
Weaponry.txt. Floor the result.

  Wrought iron       ×0.75
  Low-carbon steel   ×1.00
  Hardened steel     ×1.25
  Crucible steel     ×1.50
  Zenith steel       ×1.75
  Bronze             ×0.65
  Brass              ×0.55
  Copper             ×0.50

Order on the soft metals: wrought, then
bronze, then brass, then copper.

One metal. An alloy does not also take
an iron grade. Sea-serpent scale replaces
Iron Grade. Do not also apply a grade.

Armour alloys are bronze and brass only.
Copper is not used on armour. Eligible
pieces are Coat of Plates, Laminar,
Bracers, Greaves, Skullcap, and Nasal
helm (Armour.txt).

Riveted mail: any grade.
Butted mail: Wrought or Low-carbon only.
Grade shifts AC off 2/2/2 and multiplies
hitpoints. Butted wrought is AC 1/1/1
and 22 hitpoints (30 × 0.75). Butted
low-carbon is AC 2/2/2 and 30.

Cuir, textile, clothing: no grade.

  Riveted low-carbon hauberk     40
  Riveted hardened hauberk       50
  Riveted zenith hauberk         70
  Butted wrought hauberk         22
  Hardened solid breastplate     62
  Wrought coat of plates         30

--------------------------------------------------

Field groups: one finish, one climate,
one binding. Beeswax may sit with both

Old +15 on that textile is ×1.50.
Old +30 on cuir 35 is ×1.85.
Old +20 on cuir 35 is ×1.55.
Old +10 on a 40-point piece is ×1.25.
Old +5 on that piece is ×1.10.
Langets +15 on a spear 50 is ×1.30.

  Dense quilting        ×1.35   Textile
  Spider-silk lining    ×1.50   Clothing, Textile

                                Clothing; the
                                multiplier is
                                only if the
                                piece has a
                                Textile track)
  Rock troll hide       ×1.85   Cuir keyword
  River troll hide      ×1.55   Cuir keyword
  Kraken sinew          ×1.25   straps already
                                on the piece
  Troll-hide ties       ×1.10   straps already
                                on the piece
  Langets               ×1.30   polearms except
                                the lance
  Svartwood shaft       ×1.25   wood shaft or haft.
                                Not a bow. Not a
                                crossbow prod.
                                Tiller may.
                                Weaponry.txt.
  Ferrule               ×1.10   staff, spear, staff
                                sling, polearm butt,
                                lance included.

Double mail changes AC and Enc. It does
not multiply hitpoints in this pass.

Sea-serpent scale replaces Iron Grade.
Do not also apply a grade shift.

At 0 hitpoints the scales are untarnished
and may be reused on a new piece.

AC from an upgrade still raises the
threshold in section 1. Dense quilting
is both +1/+1/+1 and ×1.35 hitpoints.
That is intentional.

--------------------------------------------------
5. MOISTURE
--------------------------------------------------

Once per day spent in that weather.
Not per hour. Not in a fight.

Penalty = (Rainfall + Hydrology) × 5.
Scores are Core_Terrain.txt. Cap 50.

Threshold = current hitpoints
            + weather bonus below
            − penalty.

Roll 1d100. If the roll is greater
than the threshold, the piece loses
1d3 hitpoints. Floor 0.

A day at Rainfall 0 and Hydrology 0
is penalty 0. Still roll if the piece
is worn outdoors. A day indoors, dry,
no roll.

Weather bonus (one finish only)

  Beeswax / resin     +10    if waxed this week
  Blue / black        +10    if oiled this week
  Plate / tin         +15    while the tin holds
  Paint / lacquer     +10    while the film holds

Unmaintained beeswax or blue/black:
bonus 0 that day.

Tin scrape: after any campaign week
where the piece took a hitpoint loss
from a blow, tin bonus falls by 5.
At 0 the tin is gone until a smith
rets it. Cost is the upgrade again.

Paint chip: the first blow that
costs hitpoints cracks the film.
Bonus becomes −10 until scraped
and repainted. Hidden rust. The
−10 is the trapped wet.

River troll hide: skip the moisture
roll. The piece does not oxidise
and does not take water-rot.
Blows still wear it.

Bronze, brass, and copper: +30 on the
moisture threshold. A failed roll
still costs 1d3 hitpoints. That loss
is the backing — wood, leather, cord,
the haft — not the alloy. The metal
does not tarnish away. At 0 the
backing is scrap. The metal may be
recovered.

Sheath: swords and daggers only, and
only if the ticket was bought
(Weaponry.txt). While the blade is
sheathed, +20 on the moisture
threshold. Drawn, the bonus is off.
The sheath matches that blade’s
craftsmanship (section 10).
It is not part of the weapon.

Bow case: bows and crossbows, if bought.
Unstrung and in the case, +20. Strung
or in the hand, off. A crossbow case
does not cover a spanned prod.
Waxed string: bows only, +10 while
waxed this week. Does not stack with
the case.
Head wedge: no hitpoint multiplier.
Below half current maximum, it stops
the next moisture loss from being the
haft-split that ruins the weapon.
Then it is spent.

Kraken sinew does not stop the roll.
At 0 hitpoints the straps remain.
They hold a ruined piece together.
They do not soak.

Sea-serpent scale: +30 on the moisture
threshold. The scales do not tarnish.
At 0 hitpoints they may be taken off
and set on another eligible piece.
The ruined foundation is scrap.

--------------------------------------------------
6. HARNESS POOL
--------------------------------------------------

Piece hitpoints still move (section 1).
The wearer does not keep a second
condition band per helm or greave in
the fight. Read the kit as one pool.

  Max     = sum of Max HP on equipped
            pieces (Textile, Maille,
            Modular Segmented, Solid)
  Current = sum of their Current HP
  %       = (Current ÷ Max) × 100

Clothing is not in the pool.
A piece at 0 still counts in Max.
Its Current is 0.

--------------------------------------------------
7. WEAR TIERS
--------------------------------------------------

Heavy metal
  WS, BS, and Agility penalties apply
  only if the wearer has Modular
  Segmented or Solid Plate on, and
  that piece is not an exemption.
  Textile and Maille alone never
  impose those penalties, even at 0%.

  Exempt, even if ruined:
    Open Helmet
    Bracers
    Couter
    Poleyn
  Those pieces sit in the pool.
  They do not by themselves turn
  the heavy-metal clause on.
  A breastplate beside them does.

Social
  Fellowship from the tier applies
  to every layer, cloth and mail
  included. One tier rider. It does
  not stack with itself across pieces.
  Upgrade Fellowship (paint, and the
  like) is separate and still once
  per upgrade.

  100–95%   Pristine
            No WS / BS / Agility penalty.
            +10 Fellowship.
  94–75%    Maintained
            No combat penalty.
            +5 Fellowship.
  74–50%    Common worn
            Heavy metal: −5 WS, −5 BS,
            −5 Agility.
            Fellowship +0.
  49–25%    Campaign worn
            Heavy metal: −10 WS, −10 BS,
            −10 Agility.
            −10 Fellowship.
  24–0%     Ruined
            Heavy metal: −15 WS, −15 BS,
            −15 Agility.
            −15 Fellowship.
            Strip the kit before a
            smith salvages it.

Agility already moves M and the
physical skills. Do not apply a
second movement penalty.

Context
  The tier is what they see before
  a word. Apply the room, not both
  rooms at once.

  Court, monastery, polite hall
    Pristine or Maintained: wealth,
    discipline, a right to be there.
    Guards and nobles grant the
    professional respect.
    Common worn, Campaign worn, or
    Ruined: vagabond, sellsword, or
    wrong. Hands on hilts. Coin up
    front. A noble looks past.
  Tidings
    Waive the Campaign and Ruined
    Fellowship penalty if this court
    knows the ruin is the deed.
    A knight off a night ride with
    the news, harness shattered,
    is proof, not an insult.
    The waiver is this audience.
    It does not repaint the kit.
  Camp, frontier, underworld
    Common worn or Campaign worn:
    one of them. A veteran. No
    extra Fellowship from the room.
    Pristine or Maintained: outsider.
    Spy, soft envoy, or a purse.
    The +10 and +5 still sit on the
    sheet. The room may spend them
    as suspicion, not respect.

blade in this pass.

  loss = AC − type value.

--------------------------------------------------
10. CRAFTSMANSHIP
--------------------------------------------------

This is the only copy. Tiers are the
proficiency list in Economy_Labour.txt.
Listed pieces are Journeyman. It sits
after Model, Form, Upgrades, and metal
grade. It does not replace a grade.
It does not change AC or damage.

  Apprentice              cost ×0.25   HP ×0.25
  Journeyman              cost ×1      HP ×1
  Experienced             cost ×1.25   HP ×1.25
  Local Renown            cost ×2      HP ×2
  Region / Kingdom        cost ×5      HP ×5
  World-renowned          cost ×10     HP ×10

Floor the hitpoints. The ×5 and ×10
are commissions, not the stall price.
Same note as Economy_Labour.

A sheath uses the blade’s tier.
It is not a second craft on its own.

--------------------------------------------------
11. WHAT THIS PASS DOES NOT DO
--------------------------------------------------

No hitpoint change from Form, hem,
or sleeve.
No second moisture roll in the same day.
No durability on Natural Armour.
No weapon Fellowship tier.
