# Weaponry.txt
# Virath weapons — ordinary and specialist, Cut / Pierce / Blunt
# Status: DRAFT for Core. Numbers from the revised weapon set.
# Companion files:
#   Core_Combat.txt                     hitting, Reach, Reload as Actions
#   Shield_Rules.txt                    boards, Defensive(x)
#   Core_Characteristics_Skills.txt     Enc Limit, Size, WS, BS
#   Core_Injury.txt                     finishing blow; type effects later
#   Economy files                       coin and availability

PURPOSE
This file is the weapon list. Combat says how a Turn is spent.
This file says what is in the hand, how far it reaches, what
it does when it lands, and who may use it without a Talent.

ASSESS (locked here, not left as contradictions)

  Soak
    Incoming is reduced by NAC + AC of the location hit.
    Not TB + “Armour Points.” AP (x) ignores
    (x) AC after the soak total, minimum 0.
    on that location. No armour there: the quality does
    nothing.

  Damage
    Incoming = 1d10 + SB + type value + DoS
    Type value is Cut, Pierce, or Blunt from the profile.
    A 0 DoS hit still rolls the d10 and adds SB + type.
    Impact: roll 2d10, keep the higher, then add SB + type
    + DoS.
    Unarmed stays Combat §9 (no weapon type profile).

  Reload
    Source notes used “AP.” This system does not.
      1 AP  →  Half Action
      2 AP  →  Full Action
      4 AP  →  Extended: two Full Actions across two
               Rounds (or one Turn spent and the shot
               ready at the start of the next)

  Specialist
    Without the matching Specialist Weapon Group Talent,
    WS or BS with that weapon is halved (round down).
    Ordinary weapons need no Talent.
    The −20 untrained chip is not used. Halve is the law.

  Currency and Enc
    Listed costs are Common shop prices, taken from
    the Economy_Finished_Arms.txt band for that type.
    Iron Grade uses §2 (same table as Armour.txt).
    Do not also apply Poor / Good / Best.
    Enc is points. 10 points ≈ 1 kg.
    Bow tickets cost the tiller and the dry stave.
    Loft-years are not bowyer-days.
    Siege engines stay narrative. They are not
    Finished Arms rows.

--------------------------------------------------
0. KEYWORDS
--------------------------------------------------

Ordinary Weapon
  No Talent required.

Specialist Weapon
  Needs the named Group Talent. Without it, half WS/BS.

Damage Profile (Cut / Pierce / Blunt)
  Three numbers. Declare Slash, Thrust, or Bash when
  you hit. Use that column. 0 means that style is not
  available.

Versatile (*)
  Any of the three types, each attack.

Reach
  Melee striking distance in yards.

Range
  Missiles: short / long in yards. Long is −10 BS
  unless a quality says otherwise.

Iron Grade
  Same table as Armour.txt section 9. See section 2.

2. IRON GRADE
--------------------------------------------------

Same table as Armour.txt. One grade. Do not also
apply Best / Good / Common / Poor.

Economy_Processed_Goods metals (usable;
Crude Iron is feedstock only):
  Wrought iron     = Wrought Iron
  Low-carbon steel = Steel
  Hardened steel   = Fine Steel
  Crucible steel   = Crucible Steel
  Zenith steel     = Zenith Steel (veil-drop)

Listed cost and Enc on an eligible weapon are
Low-carbon. Apply Grade % to listed cost and Enc.
The AC column of the armour table is damage here.

  Grade              Cost     Enc      Damage
  Wrought iron       ×0.80    ×1.15    −1
  Low-carbon steel   ×1.00    ×1.00     0
  Hardened steel     ×1.25    ×0.85    +1
  Crucible steel     ×2.50    ×0.75    +2
  Zenith steel       ×8.00    ×0.65    +3

Zenith is worked veil-drop, not a shop grade.
Nicknames Hollow Steel, Void Steel.
Isernvolk: Lich-Stál. Syllvani: Anfarael.
See Economy_Unveiling_Goods.txt.

Damage from Grade applies after the d10 + SB
+ type, before soak. It does not grant Armour
Piercing by itself.

Eligible
  Any weapon whose working edge, head, or prod
  is iron or steel: blades, axes, maces,
  polearm heads, crossbow prods, metal arrow-
  and bolt-heads when the ticket is the head.

Not eligible
  Staff, wooden haft alone, bow limb, sling,
  shield (Shield_Rules.txt).
  A Svartwood tiller does not take Iron Grade.
  The steel prod does.
  Zenith takes the table. It does not stack on
  crucible. One grade.

Stack order: listed weapon → Iron Grade % and
damage (if eligible). Cost may be sc.bc.
Do not round Enc.

--------------------------------------------------
3. REACH
--------------------------------------------------

A melee attack needs the target inside this weapon’s
Reach. No Reach, no strike without a Move or Charge.

When two melee fighters first close inside both
Reaches, the longer Reach gains +10 WS on that first
exchange only.

Corridors and doors 1 yard or narrower: Reach greater
than 2 suffers −10 WS.

Mounted: Lance / cavalry polearm Reach doubles on a
Charge from horseback.

Versatile melee: the wielder may shorten grip by 1
yard of Reach for +10 WS in a cramped space. Declare
before the attack.

--------------------------------------------------
4. HOW A HIT DEALS DAMAGE
--------------------------------------------------

  1. Hit stands (Combat §5–6). Location is set.
  2. Declare Slash / Thrust / Bash if the profile
     allows a choice. Versatile: any. Else only a
     column that is not 0.
  3. Incoming = 1d10 + SB + type + DoS
     Impact: 2d10 keep higher, then SB + type + DoS.
  4. Protection = NAC + AC of that location
     Armour Piercing: AC on that location is
     reduced by AP (x), minimum 0.
     (minimum 0) for this hit.
  5. Leftover ticks Wounds. Finishing Blow is Injury.

Ammunition modifiers apply to type or qualities
before step 3.

--------------------------------------------------
5. QUALITIES
--------------------------------------------------

AP (x)             Ignore (x) AC on the location hit.
                   Minimum 0. No AC there: no effect.
                   A bare AP on an old line is AP (1).
Balanced           No penalty for a second weapon in
                   the off hand (two-weapon rules still
                   Open in Combat).
Concealable        +20 Concealment to hide it.
                   Enc over 50: quality does not apply.
Defensive          +10 Parry while wielding it.
                   Shields use Defensive(x):
                   Shield_Rules.txt. (x) is the
                   Parry bonus. Free Parry. May
                   Parry shooting. Not this +10.
Experimental       Attack roll 96–00: weapon ruins.
                   Wielder takes 1d10 + SB incoming,
                   NAC only (no worn AC).
Fast               Defender −10 on Parry or Dodge
                   against this weapon.
Fast (x)           As Fast. The number replaces 10.
                   Fast (30) is −30.
Inaccurate         Roll the BS test twice. Take the
                   higher result. Roll-under, so the
                   higher roll is the worse one.
                   No Aim. Fired from the hip.
Inaccurate (Aim)   The same two rolls. Aim is allowed.
Formation Specialist
                   +10 WS and +1 Pierce per adjacent
                   ally in the same ranked body
                   (max +30 WS / +3 Pierce).
                   Alone: −20 WS. No Charge, no Run.
Impact             2d10 keep higher for the damage
                   die.
Inconspicuous      Passes as a tool or stick.
                   +10 Charm or Disguise to treat it
                   as harmless. Ambush: foes −10
                   Initiative the first Round.
Mounted Specialist +1 Pierce and ignore Tiring while
                   mounted. On foot: halve Reach,
                   gain Slow.
Precise            +1 DoS on a hit, after the roll.
                   Helps Called Shot / 3 DoS location.
Pre-Loaded         Stays loaded until fired.
                   +10 Initiative the first Round if
                   that shot is the opening.
                   Jostled travel: 91–00 discharges
                   at a random nearby Entity.
Pummelling         +10 Strength when the blow is used
                   to stun (that Action is still Open).
Shrapnel           Cone to max range. Each Entity in
                   it tests Agility or takes the hit.
Shrapnel (20)      One d100 opens the firing.
                   Inaccurate applies to that roll.
                   Experimental and Unreliable read
                   it only. A miss puts no pellets out.
                   A hit lands 10 + (DoS × 2), maximum 20.
                   Point-Blank: closest takes all.
                   Close: closest takes half, round up.
                   The rest scatter inside 10 yards.
                   Long: scatter across the cone. No one
                   takes a fourth until each has one.
Slow               Defender +10 on Parry or Dodge
                   against this weapon.
Snare              On a standing hit, target is held.
                   Opposed Agility to act or move.
                   Opposed Strength each Round to
                   break. Held: no Charge/Run, −20
                   other tests.
Special            See the weapon or ammunition line.
Throwing           May be thrown at the listed range.
                   Returns to hand on 3+ DoS and a
                   successful Agility test.
Tiring             First attack or first Round only
                   at full value.
                   Bows: −10 BS each extra Round the
                   string is held drawn.
Unreliable         96–00: 1d10. 1–5 fail to fire.
                   6–10: incoming to the wielder as
                   Experimental, weapon ruined.
Versatile          Choose type each attack. Some
                   entries restrict the two-hand or
                   heavy grip to Hulking.
