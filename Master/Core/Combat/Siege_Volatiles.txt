# Siege_Volatiles.txt
# Resolver for detonation, rupture, and dispersion of volatile substances.
# Status: DRAFT.
# Companion files:
#   Economy_Finished_Volatiles.txt     substance IDs, PF, storage, cost
#   Siege_Weapons.txt                  engines and payload matrix
#   Economy_Finished_Siege_Arms.txt    engines
#   Core_Combat.txt                    structural damage and breach
#   Core_Injury.txt                    finishing and location effects
#   Pathogens_Poisons_Curses.txt       disease and poison tests
#   Core_Terrain.txt                   wind and climate for drift

PURPOSE
This file computes the physical and biological effects when a
volatile substance is detonated, ruptured, or dispersed by
siege machinery or black-powder ordnance.

It does not replace engine rules. It resolves what happens
after the payload leaves the engine or the container breaks.

--------------------------------------------------
0. SUBSTANCE REFERENCE
--------------------------------------------------
Power Factors and identities are locked in
Economy_Finished_Volatiles.txt unless this file
explicitly overrides for resolution purposes.

BPD_01  Serpentine / Meal Powder          PF 0.5 (economy) / see note
BPD_02  Corned / Grained Military Powder  PF 1.5
CH_01   Quicklime Dust Cloud              Stability C
TX_01   Arsenic Smoke / Orpiment          Stability C
CL_01   Greek Fire (ceramics)             PF 0.5* (thermal shift)

Note on BPD_01 PF:
Economy file lists 0.5 for meal powder.
This resolver uses 1.2 for kinetic calculations when the
protocol requires it, or the economy value when consistency
is preferred. GM chooses one table and stays with it.
Corned powder (BPD_02) remains the stronger propellant.

--------------------------------------------------
1. KINETIC SHOCK & DETONATION
--------------------------------------------------
High explosives and black-powder charges.
Calculations scale with total payload weight.

Blast Radius (metres)
  Radius = √(Weight in kg) × Substance PF

Kinetic Harm
  Damage = (Weight in kg × 5) / Distance from centre (metres)
  Minimum distance 1 m.
  Round to nearest whole number.
  This is the incoming damage before Soak and location.

Surface Hardness Bypassed
  BPD_01 (when using resolver PF 1.2): Tier 1 (wood / brick)
  BPD_02: Tier 2 (light stone, heavy timber)
  Higher tiers require heavier charges or repeated hits.
  See Core_Combat for structural integrity and breach.

Application
  - Centre of the blast takes full Kinetic Harm.
  - Targets at the edge of the radius take half.
  - Beyond the radius: no kinetic effect from this charge.
  - Structures use the bypassed hardness tier to determine
    whether Durability is ignored or reduced.

Example (illustrative)
  16 kg BPD_02 (PF 1.5)
  Radius = √16 × 1.5 = 4 × 1.5 = 6 m
  Damage at 2 m = (16 × 5) / 2 = 40

--------------------------------------------------
2. THERMAL SPREAD
--------------------------------------------------
Greek Fire and liquid incendiaries (CL_01).
Explosive shock is low (PF 0.5*); the primary effect is thermal.

When an incendiary container ruptures:

Splash Radius (metres)
  Splash Radius = Weight (kg) / 5

Fire Duration
  1 Turn per 5 kg of substance (round up).
  The fire persists in the splash area for that many Turns
  unless extinguished by extraordinary means.

Structural Rule
  Completely ignores physical Durability of wooden hulls,
  gates, hoarding, and similar timber.
  Instantly triggers the Structural Ignition state.
  Stone and earth are not instantly ignited but may be
  damaged by prolonged heat at GM discretion.

Personal targets in the splash take fire damage each Turn
  they remain in the area (use Core_Injury fire rules).
  Armour does not stop the initial splash adhesion.

--------------------------------------------------
3. AIRBORNE TOXIC DISPERSION
--------------------------------------------------
Quicklime (CH_01) and Arsenic Smoke (TX_01).
Governs respiratory hazard and sight-line deprivation.

Cloud Volume (m³)
  Cloud Volume = Weight (kg) × 10

Drift
  The cloud drifts 5 m downwind per Turn.
  Wind direction and strength from Core_Terrain.txt
  climate profile. Still air: cloud remains and slowly
  dissipates (GM reduces volume by half each Turn after the first).

Biological Resolution
  Cross-ref: Core_Injury.txt and Pathogens_Poisons_Curses.txt.

  CH_01 (Quicklime)
    Immediate ocular tissue damage.
    Targets in the cloud without eye protection gain
    the Total Blindness status effect until treated
    or the cloud passes.
    Also irritates lungs; Fatigue or temporary penalty
    at GM discretion.

  TX_01 (Arsenic Smoke)
    Inhalation triggers a mandatory test.
    Failure: Tier-4 internal organ hemorrhage
    (see Pathogens_Poisons_Curses.txt or Core_Injury
    for the mechanical effect).
    Success may still impose Fatigue or a lesser penalty.
    The pot itself, if it strikes as a mass, does only
    ordinary blunt damage.

Sight-line
  A cloud blocks line of sight through its volume.
  Ranged attacks into or through the cloud suffer
  cover or total concealment as the GM rules from
  the volume and drift.

--------------------------------------------------
4. APPLICATION NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
- These formulas resolve the payload after it is delivered
  by an engine (trebuchet, mortar, rack, or thrown pot).
- Engine accuracy and scatter are resolved first
  (Artillery or BS). This file then applies the effect
  at the impact point.
- Multiple charges in the same area add weight before
  calculating radius and damage, unless the GM separates them.
- Greek Fire ceramics in the Siege_Weapons payload matrix
  use this thermal section when they rupture.
- Black-powder guns (Falconet, Culverin, Demi-Cannon, Mortar)
  use the kinetic section with the appropriate BPD PF.
- RACK OF FIRE uses BPD_01 (or equivalent) per rocket
  and may combine kinetic and thermal if the rocket
  carries an incendiary head.
