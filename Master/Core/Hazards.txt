# Hazards.txt
# Virath — environmental and situational threats that are
# not a weapon and not an Agent.
# Status: DRAFT for Core.
# Companion files:
#   Core_Fatigue.txt                    the track this feeds
#   Core_Combat.txt                     Round = 10 seconds,
#                                       Helpless
#   Core_Injury.txt                     Finishing Blow, Body
#   Core_Characteristics_Skills.txt     current Toughness
#   Pathogens_Poisons_Curses.txt        disease that steals air
#   Core_Terrain.txt                    place, not this clock

PURPOSE
A Hazard tests the body on a clock. Suffocation is
the first. Later entries (fall, fire, exposure) use
the same file, not a third track.

Fatigue is still Core_Fatigue.txt. This file only
says when the Hazard adds a point, and when the
air running out is a Finishing Blow.

--------------------------------------------------
1. SUFFOCATION AND ASPHYXIATION
--------------------------------------------------

Airway gone — drowned, choke-damp, a hand on
the throat, a crush row that closed it, or a
named Agent that collapsed the lung.

This is a Toughness clock. Failures write
Fatigue. Fatigue drops every Characteristic,
including the Toughness used on the next test.

--------------------------------------------------
1a. BREATH WINDOW
--------------------------------------------------

An average human holds 30 to 90 seconds.
Base window is 30 seconds, plus 1 second per
point of current Toughness (after Fatigue
already on the sheet).

  Breath Window Seconds = 30 + Toughness

Structured Time: one Round is about 10 seconds.

  Safe Breath Rounds = floor((30 + Toughness) ÷ 10)

Remainder discarded.

  T 15    45 s    4 Rounds
  T 30    60 s    6 Rounds
  T 38    68 s    6 Rounds
  T 50    80 s    8 Rounds
  T 105  135 s   13 Rounds

If they are pulled into air and put under
again before a Short Rest, use current
Toughness. Fatigue already taken has
already shrunk the window.

--------------------------------------------------
1b. ASPHYXIATION CLOCK
--------------------------------------------------

Safe Rounds spent. Each Round without air:
Toughness test, or collapse.

  1st   −10     Challenging
  2nd   −20     Difficult
  3rd   −30     Very Hard
  4th   −40     Extreme
  5th+  −50, −60, …   Extreme, still climbing

Fail: +1 Fatigue immediately.
Win: ten more seconds. Test again next Round.

The next test uses Toughness after that
Fatigue (−10 to the Characteristic, so the
TN falls). That is the cascade.

Do not also run a Tired Hour test for the
same Round. This clock is the hour.

--------------------------------------------------
1c. COLLAPSE AND THE FINISHING BLOW
--------------------------------------------------

Fatigue = sheet TB, or any Characteristic
at 0 or below: Unconscious and Helpless
(Combat §5, Injury Helpless).

If air is not restored at once:

  The next Round ends.
  The environment deals a Finishing Blow
  to Body (Injury). No extra location roll
  unless the fiction names the throat.
  An ally may spend a Full Action before
  that Round ends to pull them into air
  or clear the airway.
  No Full Action: they are Dead.

Restored air: they are still Unconscious
and Helpless until they can be roused
(Heal, or the GM’s call after air and
time). Fatigue stays until rest.

--------------------------------------------------
1d. NARRATIVE TIME
--------------------------------------------------

No Rounds. Use Breath Window Seconds.
When that count crosses zero, run the
same ladder every 10 seconds until they
are dug out, reach air, or die as 1c.

--------------------------------------------------
2. FALL AND IMPACT
--------------------------------------------------

Worn kit does not soak a landing. Plate,
maille, and quilt are 0 AC against a fall.
NAC still applies. NAC = sheet TB
(Characteristics). Fatigue does not shrink
that NAC for this formula.

Damage type is Blunt unless the surface is
a Man-Made Hazard (spikes, glass, punji):
then Pierce.

Wounds lost
  (Base Damage × Surface Mult) − NAC
  Floor at 0. One point over NAC is one
  Wound. No layer soak. No coverage roll.

If the result is 0 they are winded. They
are not Prone unless the GM names a slip.

Landing Slot: Feet and Legs if they meant
to land. Uncontrolled or a shove: roll
Wallace as Combat. Spikes use that Slot.

--------------------------------------------------
2a. BASE DAMAGE (YARDS)
--------------------------------------------------

Look up height fallen. Between two listed
rows, use the higher.

  Yards    Base
     3       3
     6       5
     9       7
    12       9
    15      11
    18      13
    21      15
    24      17
    27      20
    30      24
    35      29
    40      34
    45      39
    50+     45    Terminal. Cap.

Under 3 yards and not a Safe Drop: Base 0
unless the GM names a nasty lip.

--------------------------------------------------
2b. SURFACE
--------------------------------------------------

Multiply Base, then subtract NAC.

  Man-Made Hazard    ×2.0   spikes, glass,
                            punji
  Hard Rugged        ×1.5   rubble, roots,
                            broken stone
  Hard Flat          ×1.2   cobble, brick,
                            keep floor
  Natural Ground     ×1.0   turf, packed
                            earth
  Soft               ×0.5   hay, grain,
                            tilled loam
  Water              see 2c

--------------------------------------------------
2c. WATER
--------------------------------------------------

Not a multiplier at every height.

  Under 12 yards
    Base becomes 0. Dodge or Athletics:
    win — clean entry, 0 Wounds.
    Fail — 2 Damage, then − NAC as usual.
  12–24 yards     Soft (×0.5)
  25–49 yards     Natural Ground (×1.0)
  50+ yards       Hard Flat (×1.2)
                  45 × 1.2 = 54 incoming
                  before NAC.

--------------------------------------------------
2d. CONTROLLED DROP
--------------------------------------------------

Declared at the ledge, not after a shove.
Half Action. Not Incapacitated, Stunned,
or Bound. Hands and feet free.

  Safe Drop Yards = floor((TB + AB) ÷ 2)

Drop that far or less: 0 Damage. No
surface. They stay on their feet.

Farther: only the yards over the ceiling
go to 2a.

--------------------------------------------------
2e. HANGING LEDGE
--------------------------------------------------

Lower, hang, let go. Full Action.
Same condition as 2d.

Subtract Size height, then Safe Drop.

  Size 3          −1 yard
  Size 4          −2 yards
  Size 5–6        −3 yards
  Size 7+         −4 yards

  Adjusted = Height − Size yards
             − Safe Drop Yards

Adjusted ≤ 0: 0 Damage, on their feet.
Adjusted > 0: fall from that many yards
through 2a–2c.

Example
  Size 4, Safe Drop 3, window 6 yards
  onto cobble.
  Hang: 6 − 2 = 4.
  Let go: 4 − 3 = 1 yard adjusted.
  Under the 3-yard row. Base 0.

--------------------------------------------------
3. CATALOGUE
--------------------------------------------------

Suffocation and Fall are written.
Fire and exposure wait here.
Do not generate them to fill the file.
}
