# Status: DRAFT for Core.
# Companion files:

#   Core_Combat.txt                     travel day, Intense hours
#   Core_Resolution.txt                 Toughness tests

#   Economy_Labour.txt                  COMMON LABOUR as a band

PURPOSE
Fatigue is how long a body can stay up and working.
It is not a Wound. It hits Characteristics. Everything
derived from a Characteristic follows.

Human sleep and rest clocks are written here.
Animals and other kinds are a GM call.

--------------------------------------------------
0. KEYWORDS
--------------------------------------------------

Fatigue
  A counted condition on the Entity.

Sheet TB
  Toughness Bonus from the Characteristic
  before Fatigue is applied. The collapse
  line.

Rested Hours
  Hours after sleep the Entity may act
  without a Tired test.

Tired Hours
  Hours after Rested Hours run out.

Short Rest / Sheltered Short Rest
  One hour of inactivity. See section 3.

Half Enc
  Strength × 5 points. Half the combat-load
  cap (Strength × 10). Characteristics §3d.

--------------------------------------------------
1. THE TRACK
--------------------------------------------------

Count Fatigue on the Entity. Start at 0
after a full recovery.

Cap = sheet TB.
At Fatigue equal to sheet TB the Entity is
incapacitated and must rest.

Each Fatigue: −10 to every Characteristic.
Strength 41 with 2 Fatigue is 21.
SB, M, Skill Base, Enc bands, NAC follow
the new number. Do not also penalise Skills.

If any Characteristic is 0 or below after
the shift, the Entity is incapacitated
even if Fatigue is still under TB.
Fellowship 25 and 3 Fatigue is Fel −5.

Incapacitated: cannot act. Forced to rest.

Do not halve Movement as a separate rule.
Agility already fell. M follows Agility.

--------------------------------------------------
2. SLEEP
--------------------------------------------------

Human basis. Hours slept, Fatigue removed:

  8 hours     4
  6 hours     3
  4 hours     2
  2 hours     1
  under 2     none
  over 8      still 4. No fifth point.

Rested Hours = 24 − hours slept.
No sleep: Rested Hours = 0. Tired starts
with the next hour.
8 hours sleep: 16 Rested Hours.

After Rested Hours, Tired Hours begin.
Each Tired Hour: Toughness test. Fail: +1
Fatigue.
First Tired Hour: no extra difficulty.
Each hour after: −10, then −20, then −30,
and so on, on that test.

A Tired Hour that is also a work hour
(section 4) uses one Toughness test at
the worse modifier. Do not roll twice
for the same hour.

--------------------------------------------------
3. SHORT REST
--------------------------------------------------

Taken in the wake window between sleeps.
Inactivity. Not a march, not a watch that
is work, not a fight.

Short Rest
  One hour. Relative shelter, warm and dry
  (a hedge-lee, a barn corner, a cloak in
  a dry ditch).
  Once per wake window.
  Removes 1 Fatigue.

Sheltered Short Rest
  One hour. Established, provisioned
  shelter — a made camp, a tavern, a hold.
  Warm and dry.
  Once per wake window.
  Removes 2 Fatigue.

Both may be taken in the same wake window,
not the same hour.
They do not replace sleep. They do not
reset Rested Hours.

--------------------------------------------------
4. WORK HOURS
--------------------------------------------------

A prolonged hour of the listed kind.
Foot travel, labour, or fighting. One
Toughness test per such hour. Fail: +1
Fatigue.

Light Active
  Foot travel / march at Enc less than
  Half Enc.
  COMMON LABOUR (Economy_Labour).
  Test at +20 Toughness.

Active
  Foot travel / march at Enc Half Enc
  or more.
  Heavier COMMON LABOUR.
  Test at +10 Toughness.

Strenuous
  Foot travel / march at Enc Half Enc
  or more, on Hard or worse ground
  (Core_Terrain.txt Ground Band).
  Hard manual labour.
  Test at +0 Toughness.

Intense
  Active fighting, a prolonged battle,
  running skirmish, or hard labour under
  battlefield conditions.
  Test at −10 Toughness.
  A fight that spans less than an hour
  is still one Intense hour if they
  fought.

Sitting a wagon, standing a quiet watch,
or talking in a hall is not a work hour
unless the GM names it labour.

--------------------------------------------------
5. OTHER SOURCES
--------------------------------------------------

A named Agent may add Fatigue. That is
Pathogens_Poisons_Curses.txt. Same track.

Combat travel: eight hours is a working
day (Combat). Hours past that are Tired
or work hours as above, not a third clock.

--------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT DO
--------------------------------------------------

It does not treat animals.
Difficult ground is Core_Terrain.txt.
It does not restore Wounds. Sleep that
clears Fatigue is not Wound recovery
(Characteristics / Injury).
