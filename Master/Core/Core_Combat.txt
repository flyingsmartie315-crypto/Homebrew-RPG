# Core_Combat.txt
# Virath structured-time combat — time, actions, hitting, movement
# Status: DRAFT for Core. First full pass.
# Companion files:
#   Core_Resolution.txt                 tests, Degrees, Narrative / Structured Time
#   Core_Characteristics_Skills.txt     WS, BS, M, Attacks, NAC, AC, Wounds, Size, Dodge
#   Core_Injury.txt                     finishing blow, locations (not yet written)

PURPOSE
This file is Structured Time when seconds decide who lives.
Resolution rolls the dice. Characteristics supply the numbers.
This file says who acts when, what a Turn holds, how a hit
becomes damage, and how bodies move in a fight and on the road.

Use Narrative Time until someone draws, flees under fire, or
the seconds matter (Core_Resolution §1a).

Locked already, not imported from another game:
  Initiative is rerolled every Round.
  Dodge and Parry are Opposed Tests against the hit’s Degrees.
  Wounds above 0 are glancing. The blow that reaches 0 is the
  blow that counts.
  Protection = NAC + AC. Size to-hit is relative.

--------------------------------------------------
0. KEYWORDS
--------------------------------------------------

Round              About ten seconds. Six Rounds ≈ one minute.
Turn               One Entity’s chance to act in a Round.
Full Action        The whole Turn. No Half Actions that Turn.
Half Action        Two Halves replace one Full.
Free Action        A split second. On top of Full or Half.
Attack Action      Any Action with “Attack” in the name.
                   Only one Attack Action per Turn.
Initiative         Agility + 1d10. Rerolled each Round.
Surprise           First Round only. No Turn that Round.
Protection         NAC + AC.
Wallace location   First d100. The limb (Injury §3).
Limb location      Second d100. The spot on that
                   limb (Injury §3). Same list as
                   armour Slots.
Hover / Fly        Two kinds of aerial movement. See section 8.
Hampered           Bad ground, weather, or a loaded march.

--------------------------------------------------
1. STARTING A FIGHT
--------------------------------------------------

When combat starts, and at the start of every later Round:

Step 1. Roll Initiative
  Each Entity still in the fight rolls
    Initiative = Agility + 1d10
  Full Agility, not Agility Bonus.

Step 2. Determine order
  Rank every score high to low. That is this Round’s order.
  Reroll and rebuild at the start of every new Round.
  Ties: higher Agility, then higher Perception, then a
  fresh 1d10 for that pair.
  Like beasts with the same stats may share one roll and
  act together. New arrivals roll when they join and slot
  into the current Round or the next, GM call.

Step 3. Surprise (Round 1 only)
  Ask: was anyone hidden (Concealment), quiet (Silent Move),
  distracted, or in odd circumstance (rain on cobbles, a
  rite, a press of bodies)?
  Opposed Awareness / Perception against the ambush Skill,
  or Difficulty if the land itself is the trap.
  Surprised Entities take no Turn in Round 1. After that
  they join the order as normal.

Step 4. Turns
  Highest Initiative acts. Then the next score down.
  On a Turn the Entity takes one Full Action or two Half
  Actions, plus Free Actions (section 2).

Step 5. Round ends
  When everyone who could act has had a Turn (or was
  skipped), about ten seconds have passed.

Step 6. Repeat
  If the fight continues, back to Step 1.
  When it ends, add the minutes to Campaign_Log Current
  Time and return to Narrative Time.

--------------------------------------------------
2. ACTION ECONOMY
--------------------------------------------------

A Turn is a few seconds inside a ten-second Round.

  One Full Action + Free Actions
  or
  Two Half Actions + Free Actions

Rules
  Only one Action whose name includes “Attack” per Turn.
  Only one Cast / Channel per Turn.
  Free Actions have no written cap. The GM cuts the list
  when the seconds break. A speech or emptying a pack
  is not Free.
  Most Actions finish this Turn. Reload, a long Skill,
  or a Channelled working may be Extended (Resolution §7)
  with the increment set to Rounds. Aborting wastes the
  time already spent; start again from the beginning.

Typical packages
  Charge                          Full
  Aim and shoot                   Half + Half
  Called Shot and strike          Half + Half
  Draw and strike                 Half + Half
  Move and strike                 Half + Half
  Shout on the charge             Free + Full

Attacks stat (default 1) is spent by Swift Attack, not
by taking two Standard Attacks.

--------------------------------------------------
3. BASIC ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------

Aim (Half)
  If the next Action this Turn is a Standard Attack,
  that attack gains +10 WS or +10 BS. Anything else
  wastes the Aim.
  Aim and Called Shot cannot both be taken on the
  same Turn. There are not three Halves.

Called Shot (Half)
  Name a location. Spend the time to line the gap,
  not a −20 chip on the same swing.
  If the next Action this Turn is a Standard Attack
  and that hit stands, it strikes the named part.
  No 3 DoS required. A miss is still a miss.
  Anything other than a Standard Attack next wastes
  the Called Shot (the part is not named on a Charge,
  Swift Attack, or All Out).
  This is not an Attack Action.

Charge Attack (Full) — Attack Action
  One melee attack at +10 WS.
  Target at least 4 yards away and within Charge yards
  (section 7). Last 4 yards in a straight line.

Disengage (Full)
  Leave melee and move the Move/Disengage yards.
  Leaving melee without Disengage: each opponent still
  in reach makes one free Standard Attack first. That
  free hit does not spend their Attack Action later.

Move (Half)
  Move the Move/Disengage yards. Difficult ground halves
  (round up).

Ready (Half)
  Draw or stow. One Ready may put one thing away and
  take another out. Drink a flask already in hand.

Reload (Varies)
  Time is on the weapon. May be Extended.

Stand / Mount (Half)
  Rise, or mount a willing creature. Dismount is the
  same cost.

Standard Attack (Half) — Attack Action
  One melee (WS) or ranged (BS) attack.

Swift Attack (Full) — Attack Action
  Strikes equal to the Attacks stat.
  Attacks 1: one strike and the Turn is gone.
  Two loaded one-hand missiles: at most two shots,
  one per hand, and only if Attacks is 2+.

Cast / Channel (Varies)
  One working per Round. An extra Half first allows a
  Channelling test to aid it (Resolution §8).
  Procedure and cost are not in this file. May be
  Extended.

Use a Skill (Varies)
  A Skill that fits the seconds. Cost is Half, Full,
  or Extended as the work demands.

--------------------------------------------------
4. ADVANCED ACTIONS
--------------------------------------------------

All Out Attack (Full) — Attack Action
  One melee attack at +20 WS.
  Until the start of this Entity’s next Turn: no Dodge,
  no Parry.

Defensive Stance (Half)
  No Attack Action this Turn.
  Until the start of this Entity’s next Turn: melee
  attacks against this Entity take −20 WS.

Delay (Half)
  Turn ends. One Half is held and may be spent any
  time before the next Turn. Two Entities spending
  held actions on the same instant: opposed Agility.
  Unused held Half is lost at the next Turn.

Feint (Half)
  Opposed WS. If this Entity wins, its next Action
  this Turn must be a Standard Attack, and that attack
  cannot be Dodged or Parried. Any other next Action
  wastes the Feint.

Guarded Attack (Full) — Attack Action
  One melee attack at −10 WS.
  Until the start of this Entity’s next Turn: +10 on
  Dodge and Parry tests.

Jump / Leap (Full)
  See section 8.

Manoeuvre (Half)
  Opposed WS. Winner moves the target 2 yards in a
  declared direction and may move 2 yards itself.
  Cannot force into another Entity or a solid thing.

Parrying Stance (Half)
  Ready a Parry at +10. Ends at the start of the next
  Turn whether used or not.

Run (Full)
  Move the Run yards. Cannot Run while engaged —
  Disengage first. Cannot Run on difficult ground.
  Until the next Turn: ranged attacks against this
  Entity take −20 BS; melee attacks against it gain
  +20 WS.

--------------------------------------------------
5. HITTING
--------------------------------------------------

Melee: test WS. Ranged: test BS.
Size to-hit = (Target Size − Attacker Size) × 10.
Other modifiers: Resolution §4 and the combat examples
in section 11.

A melee WS test is the exchange — looking for an opening
while the defender already denies some of them. Fail and
the blow never finds a gap. Succeed and it is a telling
blow unless Parried or Dodged (section 6).

Ranged attacks are not Parried or Dodged unless a later
rule says so.

Hit location
  Every successful attack that still stands after Dodge
  or Parry rolls two d100s on a biped (Injury §3).
  First die: Wallace limb. Second die: Slot on that
  limb. Soak uses Armour_Rules.txt §5 on that Slot. These
  dice are not the Coverage d100.

    First d100
    01–10    Head         10%
    11–50    Torso        40%
    51–60    Right Arm    10%
    61–70    Left Arm     10%
    71–85    Right Leg    15%
    86–100   Left Leg     15%

    Second d100 (Arm, locked)
    01–10    Hand         10%
    11–35    Forearm      25%
    36–45    Elbow        10%
    46–80    Upper Arm    35%
    81–00    Shoulder     20%

    Second d100 (Leg, locked)
    01–08    Foot              8%
    09–47    Shin / Calf       39%
    48–57    Knee              10%
    58–95    Thigh             38%
    96–00    Hip / Buttock     5%

    Second d100 (Torso, locked)
    01–40    Chest / Upper Back           40%
    41–45    Armpit                        5%
    46–85    Abdomen / Lower Back         40%
    86–95    Groin / Pelvis
             (Glutes / Sacrum from behind) 10%
    96–00    Collarbone / Base of Neck     5%
    Facing picks the front or back name
    (Injury §3). Armpit is L or R from
    the blow’s side.

    Second d100 (Head, locked)
    01–10    Eyes                         10%
    11–60    Skull / Occipital            50%
    61–85    Lower face / Back of neck    25%
    86–00    Throat                       15%
    Facing picks the pair (Injury §3).

  Three or more DoS on the attack: the attacker may
  nominate the limb, the Slot, or both. That is the
  clean strike that found the open helm on its own.

  Called Shot (Half, section 3) is the other door:
  spend the extra Half so a following Standard Attack
  hits the named limb or Slot even on 0–2 DoS. Better
  chance at the part, worse Turn (no move, no Aim).

  Do not stack Called Shot and a 3 DoS nomination.
  The part is already named.

  DoS is the attack roll’s Degrees, after Size and
  other modifiers, kept through the Opposed defence
  if any. If the defender won, there is no location
  because there is no hit.

  Wrong-shaped bodies: use the analogue (wing, coil)
  or the nearest column.

  Helpless melee that auto-hits still rolls location
  unless Called Shot was taken this Turn, or the
  optional WS roll scored 3+ DoS and the attacker
  nominates.

Helpless (bound, unaware, down at 0 Wounds): the ordinary
exchange does not apply. No Dodge, no Parry.
Melee attacks automatically succeed. No WS test. Damage
is SB + weapon. An optional WS roll may add DoS; failure
on that roll does not miss.
Ranged attacks still roll BS.

--------------------------------------------------
6. DODGE AND PARRY
--------------------------------------------------

A successful melee hit is not yet damage. The last line
is an Opposed Test against the Degrees already on that
hit. This is not a flat test.

  Attacker keeps the Degrees from the WS roll.
  Defender rolls Parry (WS) or Dodge (Dodge Skill).
  Compare as Resolution §5.

  Defender wins: the blow is beaten or slipped. No damage.
  Attacker wins: the blow lands. Damage, section 9.

Do not reroll the attack. Do not roll damage before the
defence.

Limits
  Melee only.
  Must be aware of the attack.
  One Parry per Round. One Dodge per Round.
  Never both on the same blow.
  Surprise, blind, bound, All Out Attack: neither.
  A won Feint’s following Standard Attack: neither.

Parry
  Opposed WS. Ready weapon or shield.
  Paid for by Parrying Stance, or Free if a weapon or
  shield is in the off hand.
  Main-hand only and no Stance: no Parry.

Dodge
  Opposed Dodge Skill. Free. Ordinary Skill — Unknown
  is Skill Base ÷ 2. Not an Advanced closed trick.

--------------------------------------------------
7. COMBAT MOVEMENT (YARDS)
--------------------------------------------------

  M     Move / Disengage    Charge    Run
  1            2               4        6
  2            4               8       12
  3            6              12       18
  4            8              16       24
  5           10              20       30
  6           12              24       36
  7           14              28       42
  8           16              32       48
  9           18              36       54
 10           20              40       60

  Move / Disengage  = M × 2
  Charge            = M × 4
  Run               = M × 6

  Always floor the yards after the formula.
  Difficult ground: halve those yards, then round up.

M = (Agility ÷ 10) + Size adjustment.
Keep one decimal. Do not drop to Agility Bonus.
Agility 30 and Agility 38 are different Movement.

  Size 4, Agility 30 → M 3.0 → Move 6, Charge 12, Run 18
  Size 4, Agility 38 → M 3.8 → Move 7.6→7, Charge 15.2→15,
                               Run 22.8→22
  Size 6, Agility 38 → M 5.8 → Move 11, Charge 23, Run 34

The whole-number rows above are the formula at M 1.0, 2.0, …
They are a lookup, not a requirement to snap M to an integer.

On a mapped grid, 2 yards = 1 square if the table uses
2-yard squares. Narrative description is always legal.
Do not require miniatures.

Mounted: the mount’s M and Charge apply when the rider
uses Charge or Move on the animal. The rider is still
Size 4 as a target unless the blow is meant for the
horse (Characteristics §3).

--------------------------------------------------
8. JUMP, LEAP, FALL, FLY
--------------------------------------------------

Jump (controlled descent)
  Full Action.
  Agility test for each 3 yards of drop (round up).
  Fail: remaining distance is a fall. Stop testing.
  Performer (Contortion) or a later Acrobat specialism
  may replace Agility if the Entity has it Known+.

Leap (horizontal)
  Full Action. Running leap needs at least 4 yards of
  straight run-up.
  Test Strength. Success: distance = M + SB yards.
  Standing leap: same test, distance halved (round up).
  Each DoF reduces distance by 1 yard (minimum 1).
  Result 0 or less: do not clear the gap. That is a fall
  of whatever depth the gap has.
  Vertical leap, if it matters: M − 2 yards, same DoF
  trim, minimum 1.

Fall
  Incoming damage by distance fallen. AC does not soak
  a fall. NAC does.

    Yards fallen     Incoming
         3               3
         6               5
         9               7
        12               9
        15              11
        18              13
        21              15
        24              17
        25+             20

  Then Protection = NAC only. Leftover ticks Wounds.
  If 10+ Wounds get through in one fall, also test
  Agility or take +1d10 incoming before soak (bad
  landing). Standing after a fall that dealt Wounds
  takes a Stand Action.

Flying
  Two kinds, written on the Entity.
    Hoverer  cannot climb more than 2 yards of air.
             A Hoverer that takes no Move on its Turn
             stalls and falls.
    Flier    can use the air as a road.

  Flying Movement is a second M used only in the air,
  listed in parentheses on the profile. Charge and Run
  columns use Flying M while aloft.

  Altitude
    Hovering   under 2 yards. Attacks ground as normal.
               Cannot be attacked from below as if it
               were high.
    Low        about 15 yards. Cannot attack or be
               attacked in melee. Shooting down is
               ordinary. Shooting up from the ground
               at more than 16 yards: −10 BS.
    High       more than about 15 yards. Only attacks
               and is attacked by others at High, or
               by shot from below at −10 BS.

  Changing altitude one step (Hovering ↔ Low ↔ High)
  is a Move or part of Charge / Run.
  A Flier that Charges in air may strike at any point
  along the Charge and finish the remaining yards
  after the blow (pass-through).
  Two flyers at the same altitude fight as on the
  ground.

  Force down
  Instead of damage, a Charge or Standard Attack
  against a flyer may be an opposed Strength test to
  drag it down one altitude step. Fail: no damage, no
  drop. Both fail to break a deadlock: they tangle and
  fall, taking falling damage from current altitude.

--------------------------------------------------
9. DAMAGE AND WOUNDS
--------------------------------------------------

  Incoming = 1d10 + SB + type + DoS
  Type is Cut, Pierce, or Blunt from Weaponry.txt.
  A 0 DoS hit still rolls the d10 and adds SB + type.
  Impact weapons: 2d10 keep the higher die.

  Protection = NAC + Soak AC from Armour_Rules.txt §5.
  Wallace die, then Limb location die, then one
  Coverage die down Modular → Maille → Textile
  on that Slot. Modular present and Coverage
  passes: that piece’s AC plus the layers under
  it. Armour Piercing ignores 1 worn AC after
  that total.
  Incoming ≤ Protection   →  0 Wounds
  Incoming > Protection   →  Wounds lost = incoming − Protection

  A helm’s AC does not cover a bare arm. An unarmoured
  head on an otherwise mailed man is NAC only.

Wounds above 0 are glancing (Characteristics §3c).
The blow that reaches 0 is fatal or debilitating.
Injury will name dead, down, or a table. Until then:
out of the fight, plus one lasting cost that fits the
weapon. Overflow on that blow may worsen the cost.

Unarmed
  Incoming = 1d10 + SB − 4 + DoS (minimum 1 before
  soak if the hit landed).
  Worn AC counts twice against unarmed. NAC is normal.

Natural harm
  Fire, drowning, and the like are incoming damage
  by the scene, not a weapon value.
  Fire: each Round of exposure, incoming set by the
  GM from 1 (ember) to 10 (raging). Two Rounds in
  the same source: Agility to put it out or get clear.
  Fail: +1d10 incoming this Round, no AC, NAC still
  soaks. Repeat until out.
  Suffocation: Toughness test each Round after the
  first. Cumulative −10 after the first fail. Third
  failed test: unconscious. Two more Rounds: dead.
  Disease is not a combat Action. Heal, Outdoor
  Survival, and a later affliction file.

--------------------------------------------------
10. GRAPPLE
--------------------------------------------------

Start with a Charge Attack or Standard Attack that
declares a grapple instead of damage. Then both test
Agility (opposed). Winner: the target is grappled.

While grappled
  Both may not Dodge or Parry.
  Outside melee against either has +20 WS.
  The grappler’s only useful Action is to maintain
  the hold (Full, opposed Strength to keep it) or
  to deal unarmed damage (Half).
  The held Entity’s only useful Action is to break
  (Full, opposed Strength). Success: free.
  Either may let go as a Free Action.

--------------------------------------------------
11. COMBAT DIFFICULTY (EXAMPLES)
--------------------------------------------------

These sit on the Resolution ladder. They do not
replace Size to-hit or darkness already paid.

  +30   attacking someone unaware who can still act
  +20   attacking a Stunned target; ranged at Helpless
  auto   melee at Helpless (no WS test; see §5)
  +10   attacking someone who is Down but not Helpless
   0    a standard exchange
  −10   shooting into melee (friend and foe mixed)
  −20   shooting while engaged in melee; Dodge while down
  −30   shot at extreme range; Parry against a thing
        two or more Size steps larger

Shooting into melee: −10 BS, and a miss may strike
another Entity in the tangle (GM picks the nearest
or rolls).

Extreme range: usually a waste. Called shots at
that distance also take the −30.

Behind cover, fog, or a wall: −10 to −30 BS as the
view dictates. If the attacker cannot see the target
at all, there is no shot.

12. NARRATIVE MOVEMENT
--------------------------------------------------

Out of Structured Time, M still governs travel.
Groups move at the slowest member.

Local (yards per minute)

  M     Hampered    Standard
  1        12          24
  2        24          48
  3        36          72
  4        48          96
  5        60         120
  6        72         144
  7        84         168
  8        96         192
  9       108         216
 10       120         240

  Hampered  = M × 12
  Standard  = M × 24

Overland (miles per hour)

  M     Hampered    Standard
  1       0.5           1
  2       1             2
  3       1.5           3
  4       1.75          3.5
  5       2.25          4.5
  6       3             6
  7       3.5           7
  8       4             8
  9       4.5           9
 10       5            10

Hampered: woods, marsh, hills, weather, a loaded
march, a column in formation.
Standard: road or open country.
The GM may cut the day’s miles further for rain,
hunger, or a press of refugees. Eight hours is a
working travel day. Pushing longer is Fatigue
(Characteristics §3a).

Update Campaign_Log Current Time and Location when
the grain of the journey changes.

--------------------------------------------------
13. FATE
--------------------------------------------------

Fate Points are Player Characters only
(Characteristics §3d).

Until a longer list exists, one FP buys one of:
  — do not die on the finishing blow (stay at 1 Wound,
    down, out of the fight)
  — force a reroll (yours or an incoming attack)
  — walk out of a result the dice already closed

Declared before the fiction moves on. No second FP
in the same scene for the same blow.

There is no separate Fortune pool in this system.

--------------------------------------------------
14. REPORTING A BLOW
--------------------------------------------------

Every resolved attack is told twice. First the
blow. Then the numbers. Do not mix them.

The prose
  High velocity. Raw physical image. Grammar
  stripped until the sentence hits like the
  steel. No inventory of feelings. No witty
  aside. Blood, cloth, bone, dust, the sound
  the iron made. The body does what the dice
  already decided. Do not invent a second
  outcome in the adjectives.
  One swing, one passage. Then stop.

The block
  After the passage, always this list, in this
  order. Nothing else in the block except what
  the blow produced.

    Hit / Miss:
    DoS:                  (or DoF)
    Sub location:         Wallace + Limb location
    Damage / Target AC:   Incoming (dice + SB +
                          type + DoS) / NAC + worn
    Wounds inflicted:     through Protection

  If the blow emptied the Wounds track, add:

    Table:                1d10 + Overflow − TB
                          Column and row title
                          Bleed, Down, dead, Fate

  Called Shot or 3+ DoS: write the named Slot
  in Sub location. Do not roll a second map.

  Miss: prose of the miss, then Hit / Miss and
  DoF only. No location. No damage.

This is the table voice for Structured Time.
Narrative Time does not owe the block.

--------------------------------------------------
15. ENDING THE FIGHT
--------------------------------------------------

The fight ends when no one is still trying to hold
the field. Yield and flight are legal.
Then leave Structured Time. Update the Log. Resume
Narrative Time.
