# Core_Talents_Failings_Afflictions.txt
# Virath — standing modifiers on an Entity.
# Status: DRAFT. Framework only. No catalogue.
# Companion files:
#   Core_Characteristics_Skills.txt     what the test is
#   Core_Resolution.txt                 how modifiers hit the TN
#   Core_Combat.txt                     reactions, conditions in a Round
#   Core_Injury.txt / Injury_Tables_*   wounds that become Afflictions
#   Entity sheet                        the live list

PURPOSE
An Entity is Characteristics, Skills, Size, kit — and then
the things that change a test only in named situations.

This file is the law for those things. It is not a list of
them. Do not generate Talents, Failings, or Afflictions
into this document. Named entries live on Entity sheets
and, later, in a catalogue file written on purpose.

--------------------------------------------------
0. KEYWORDS
--------------------------------------------------

Talent
  A standing situational modifier that helps.
  Example of the form only: Keen Hearing (X) — add X
  to Perception tests that rely on sound.
  A Talent does not raise the Characteristic. It
  modifies the test when its trigger is true.

Failing
  A standing situational modifier that hurts.
  Example of the form only: Hard of Hearing (X) —
  subtract X from Perception tests that rely on sound.
  Same shape as a Talent. Opposite sign.

Affliction
  A disease, a wound-consequence, or another body
  or mind state that is not a Talent or a Failing.
  Temporary or permanent. Named on the sheet with
  a duration and what it does.
  Injury_Tables rows that leave a lasting mark
  become Afflictions. They are not rewritten as
  Talents.

(X)
  The magnitude written on that entry. A TN
  modifier in tens, or a special instruction
  instead of a number.
  X is part of the name on the sheet:
  Keen Hearing (10), not “Keen Hearing” with
  a hidden value.

  Band for sense-type Talents and Failings
    (10)  human peak. A woodsman, a sentry,
          a man who lives by the ear.
    (20)  animal or other kind, one sense.
          A hound’s nose. A cat’s hearing
          in the dark.
    (30)  that sense as the kind’s work.
          An eagle’s sight. A bloodhound’s
          track. Not a human number.

  Do not write (5) or (15) on this band.
  Tens only, unless a later catalogue makes
  an exception for a named entry.
  (20) and (30) are innate to the kind.
  A man does not train Keen Hearing from
  (10) to (20). That is a different body.

  Band for Looks
    Same tens. Humans use all three. This is
    not an animal sense. (30) is rare and is
    a problem as often as a gift.
    (10)  noticed in a room.
    (20)  remembered after the room.
    (30)  the face travels ahead of the name.

Double-edged
  One named entry may list more than one
  trigger, with opposite signs.
  Looks is the type case. It is still one
  Talent or one Failing, not both lists.

Trigger
  The situation the entry cares about. No trigger,
  no modifier. Keen Hearing does nothing on a
  sight test.

Pair
  A Talent and a Failing that name the same sense
  or work. They do not both apply to the same
  test. The live state is one or the other, or
  neither.

--------------------------------------------------
1. WHERE THEY LIVE
--------------------------------------------------

On the Entity, in three lists:

  Talents:
  Failings:
  Afflictions:

Each line is one entry. Name, (X) if it uses one,
trigger, effect, and for Afflictions a duration.

An Entity may have none. Absence is not a Failing.

People templates, careers, and bestiary lines may
grant entries. The grant is copied onto the sheet.
This file is not that grant list.

--------------------------------------------------
2. TALENT
--------------------------------------------------

Form
  Name (X)
  Trigger:
  Effect:

Law
  Helps on its main trigger. A double-edged
  Talent may penalise a second named trigger
  (Looks). If every trigger hurts, it is a
  Failing or an Affliction.
  Situational. It is not +X to a Characteristic
  on the sheet.
  Does not replace a Skill. Keen Hearing is not
  Awareness.
  Stacks with difficulty and other live modifiers
  (Resolution §4). Two Talents on the same test
  stack only if their triggers are both truly on.
  Do not invent a Talent to cover a rule that
  already exists (Size, Enc band, Iron Grade).

How it is gained
  Birth, kind, career, or a later catalogue.
  Until that catalogue exists, write the line
  on the Entity that needs it. Do not batch-write
  a list into this file.

Worked form — Hearing (Talent)
  Names on the sheet, pick one:
    Keen Hearing (10)     human peak
    Keen Hearing (20)     cat, fox, owl
    Keen Hearing (30)     the kind that hunts by ear
  Trigger: Perception or Awareness that relies
  on sound.
  Effect: +X
  Pair Failing: Hard of Hearing (10/20/30)

Worked form — Sight (Talent)
  Names on the sheet, pick one:
    Keen Sight (10)       human peak, a lookout
    Keen Sight (20)       hawk over a field
    Keen Sight (30)       eagle, the kind’s work
  Trigger: Perception or Awareness that relies
  on sight in usable light.
  Effect: +X
  Does not cancel darkness. That is Night Vision.
  Pair Failing: Poor Sight (10/20/30)

Worked form — Smell (Talent)
  Names on the sheet, pick one:
    Keen Smell (10)       human peak, a cook, a tanner
    Keen Smell (20)       cat, wolf
    Keen Smell (30)       bloodhound, the track is
                          the kind’s work
  Trigger: Perception or Awareness that relies
  on scent. Tracking by smell uses this, not
  extra Range.
  Effect: +X
  Pair Failing: Poor Smell (10/20/30)

Worked form — Night Vision
  Special instruction. No (X) unless a later
  catalogue writes one.
  Name on the sheet: Night Vision
  Trigger: Perception or Awareness that relies
  on sight, in the dark under stars, moon, or
  equivalent open-night glow.
  Effect: do not apply the ordinary darkness
  penalty to that test.
  Does not work in a lightless cave, a shuttered
  hold, or any place with no sky-glow at all.
  Does not grant Keen Sight. A man with Night
  Vision and no Keen Sight sees in starlight
  as well as he sees at dusk, not as an owl.
  Kind that also has Keen Sight (20) or (30)
  stacks both: the dark is usable, and the
  sight is sharp.

Worked form — Outdoorsman (Biome)
  Talent. (10) fixed. The bracket is the
  biome, not a magnitude.
  Name on the sheet:
    Outdoorsman (Boreal)
    Outdoorsman (named biome from Geography)
  Each biome is its own entry. Two biomes
  is two lines.
  Trigger: Awareness and Outdoor Survival,
  while outside a settlement, in that biome.
  A town, village, hold, or camp street is
  not outdoors for this Talent. Past the
  pales is.
  Rank on those tests:
    Unknown — full Skill Base, not Base ÷ 2.
    No +10 on top. The Talent is the
    training for this biome, this roll.
    Known or better — Rank value, then +10.
  Does not raise stored Rank. Leave the
  biome and Unknown is Unknown again.
  Outdoor Survival * : in this biome,
  outdoors, the test is allowed at Known
  (full Base) even if the Skill is not
  on the sheet. That is the exception to
  Advanced. Other biomes stay closed.
  Indoor, dungeon, and ship-deck do not
  trigger.

Worked form — Looks (Talent)
  Names on the sheet, pick one:
    Comely (10)
    Beautiful (20)
    Striking (30)
  Trigger A: Charm, and other Fellowship tests
  that run on being wanted in the room.
  Effect A: +X
  Trigger B: staying unknown. Concealment in a
  crowd, Disguise as “nobody”, Silent Move does
  not cover this — this is being seen and kept.
  Effect B: −X to those tests. Observers gain
  +X to Awareness or Gossip to notice or later
  recall the face.
  Jealousy, invitation, and unwanted attention
  are GM calls at the same band. (30) does not
  walk through a market as a shadow.

Worked form — Looks (Failing)
  Names on the sheet, pick one:
    Homely (10)
    Ugly (20)
    Hideous (30)
  Trigger A: Charm, and Fellowship tests that
  run on being wanted in the room.
  Effect A: −X
  Trigger B: being remembered as a threat or a
  mark, or being refused service. Observers
  gain +X to recall the face after. Concealment
  as “nobody” is not helped; a hide is still a
  hide. The Failing is social, not stealth.

--------------------------------------------------
3. FAILING
--------------------------------------------------

Form
  Name (X)
  Trigger:
  Effect:

Law
  Negative only. Same trigger rules as a Talent.
  Not an injury. A missing eye from a blow is an
  Affliction. Born short-sighted is a Failing
  unless the table says otherwise.
  A Failing does not block the test. It modifies
  the TN. A test that cannot be attempted is a
  missing sense or a closed Skill, not a Failing.

Pair with a Talent
  Hard of Hearing and Keen Hearing are a Pair.
  Record one. If a later event would add the
  opposite, replace or cancel as the event says.
  Do not run +10 and −10 on the same roll and
  call it a wash unless the triggers differ
  (one ear, one direction).

--------------------------------------------------
4. AFFLICTION
--------------------------------------------------

Form
  Name
  Source:     disease / wound / other
  Duration:   hours / days / until treated / permanent
  Effect:
  Tests:      what Heal, Surgery, or Toughness
              may do, if anything

Law
  Temporary or permanent. Say which.
  Wounds from Injury_Tables that leave a lasting
  mark (lost hand, ruined knee, Catastrophic
  Bleed still running) are logged here while
  they last. The table row is the source. This
  list is the standing reminder.
  Disease and poison sit here, not under Failings.
  Bleed tiers already named in Injury stay those
  words: Minor Bleed, Major Bleed, Catastrophic
  Bleed. Do not rename them as Talents.

Worked form — Visible Scarring
  Affliction, not a Failing. Comes from a wound
  or a named event. Looks stays on the sheet.
  Both can fire on the same Charm test.

  Names on the sheet, pick one:
    Marked (10)       a line people see
    Scarred (20)      the face or hands tell
    Ruined (30)       the room changes

  Source: wound / other
  Duration: permanent unless Surgery or a later
  rule says the mark can be hidden or eased.
  A scarf or helm can cover a given test.
  Covered: this Affliction does not fire.

  Trigger A: Charm, and Fellowship tests that
  run on being wanted or trusted on sight.
  Effect A: −X

  Trigger B: Intimidate, and Fellowship tests
  that run on threat made visible.
  Effect B: +X

  Trigger C: being noticed and remembered.
  Observers gain +X to Awareness or Gossip to
  mark or later recall the face. Concealment
  as “nobody” takes −X when the scar is shown.
  Same incognito tax as Looks, separate line.

  A Beautiful (20) Scarred (20) face is
  +20 / −20 on Charm from two entries, and
  +20 Intimidate from the scar. Write both.
  Do not merge them into one number.

  Context — the same mark, two rooms
    Camp / campaign / among fighting men
      The scar is proof. Veteran, valor,
      a body that stayed.
      Trigger A flips: Charm and Fellowship
      among those men take +X, not −X.
      Intimidate still +X.
      Notice still +X. They remember who
      stood.
    Tavern / village / market / court
      The scar is a warning. Vagabond,
      trouble, a man who brought the war
      with him.
      Trigger A as written: Charm −X.
      Intimidate +X.
      Notice +X. They remember to watch
      the door.
    Name the room before the roll.
    A soldier drinking in a harvest village
    is tavern, not camp.
    A lord reviewing his company is camp.

End
  Duration runs out, Heal / Surgery succeeds as
  the entry allows, or the sheet is rewritten
  after a permanent settlement (the lost hand
  stays an Affliction; it does not become a
  Failing named “One Hand”).
  Visible Scarring stays an Affliction even
  when permanent. It does not move to Failings.

--------------------------------------------------
5. RESOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------

Declare the trigger before the roll.
Apply (X) as a modifier to the TN, with every
other live modifier (Resolution §4).
Write the name in the declaration so the table
can see why the TN moved.

Opposed tests: each party applies only their
own entries. A deaf guard does not give the
sneak a bonus. The sneak’s Silent Move and the
guard’s Hard of Hearing are two separate TNs.

Dominate, Man-Scale, and Size are not Talents.
They stay in Resolution and Characteristics.

--------------------------------------------------
6. COMBAT
--------------------------------------------------

A Talent that adds a reaction, extra Attack, or
a stance change must say so in its Effect line
and must not break Combat §6 (one reaction per
incoming blow). Until such entries exist, do
not imply them.

Afflictions that say “no Dodge” or “half M”
override the default action list while they
last. Log them at the start of the Round.

--------------------------------------------------
7. CATALOGUE
--------------------------------------------------

Empty on purpose.

When a Talent, Failing, or Affliction is written
for use by more than one Entity, it goes in a
later catalogue file. Not here.

Do not generate entries to fill this section.
