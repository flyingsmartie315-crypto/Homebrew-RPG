# Core_Talents_Failings_Afflictions.txt
# Virath — standing modifiers on an Entity.
# Status: DRAFT. Framework, worked forms, and the catalogue in section 7.
# Companion files:
#   Core_Characteristics_Skills.txt     what the test is
#   Core_Resolution.txt                 how modifiers hit the TN
#   Core_Combat.txt                     reactions, conditions in a Round
#   Core_Injury.txt / Injury_Tables_*   wounds that become Afflictions
#   Pathogens_Poisons_Curses.txt        disease, venom, curse agents
#   Entity sheet                        the live list

PURPOSE
An Entity is Characteristics, Skills, Size, kit — and then
the things that change a test only in named situations.

This file is the law for those things. Worked forms
and Combat Manoeuvre Talents written here are locked.
The catalogue in section 7 is the written list of
Talents. Do not generate extra entries to fill space.
Other named entries live on Entity sheets.

--------------------------------------------------
0. KEYWORDS
--------------------------------------------------

Talent
  A standing situational modifier that helps.
  Example of the form only: Keen Hearing (X) — add X
  to Perception tests that rely on sound.
  A Talent does not raise the Characteristic. It
  modifies the test when its trigger is true.

Failing
  A standing situational modifier that hurts.
  Example of the form only: Hard of Hearing (X) —
  subtract X from Perception tests that rely on sound.
  Same shape as a Talent. Opposite sign.

Affliction
  A disease, a wound-consequence, or another body
  or mind state that is not a Talent or a Failing.
  Temporary or permanent. Named on the sheet with
  a duration and what it does.
  Injury_Tables rows that leave a lasting mark
  become Afflictions. They are not rewritten as
  Talents.

(X)
  The magnitude written on that entry. A TN
  modifier in tens, or a special instruction
  instead of a number.
  X is part of the name on the sheet:
  Keen Hearing (10), not “Keen Hearing” with
  a hidden value.

  Band for sense-type Talents and Failings
    (10)  human peak. A woodsman, a sentry,
          a man who lives by the ear.
    (20)  animal or other kind, one sense.
          A hound’s nose. A cat’s hearing
          in the dark.
    (30)  that sense as the kind’s work.
          An eagle’s sight. A bloodhound’s
          track. Not a human number.

  Do not write (5) or (15) on this band.
  Tens only, unless a later catalogue makes
  an exception for a named entry.
  Section 7c is that exception: Character Talents
  carry (5).
  (20) and (30) are innate to the kind.
  A man does not train Keen Hearing from
  (10) to (20). That is a different body.

  Band for Looks
    Same tens. Humans use all three. This is
    not an animal sense. (30) is rare and is
    a problem as often as a gift.
    (10)  noticed in a room.
    (20)  remembered after the room.
    (30)  the face travels ahead of the name.

  Band for Character Talents
    (5)   temperament and habit. A man who is
          steady in a brawl, a woman who reads a
          room. Smaller than a sense, and often
          bought with a cost. Section 7c.
    Never (5) on a sense or on Looks.

Double-edged
  One named entry may list more than one
  trigger, with opposite signs.
  Looks is the type case. It is still one
  Talent or one Failing, not both lists.

Trigger
  The situation the entry cares about. No trigger,
  no modifier. Keen Hearing does nothing on a
  sight test.

Pair
  A Talent and a Failing that name the same sense
  or work. They do not both apply to the same
  test. The live state is one or the other, or
  neither.

Prerequisite
  An optional line on a Talent. What the holder
  must have before taking it: a Characteristic
  minimum, a Skill rank, time in a place, or a
  lived event. Checked once, when the Talent is
  taken or granted. Later loss (ageing, wounds,
  an Affliction) does not remove the Talent.
  A Talent with no Prerequisite line has none.

Tier
  The XP price of a bought Talent. Written on the entry
  as Tier: N.
    Tier 1    50 XP
    Tier 2    100 XP
    Tier 3    150 XP
  An entry with no Tier line is Tier 1 (50 XP) as a
  placeholder until a Tier is set. A Talent
  gained at creation or by story is not bought and has no
  price. Polyglot and Lettered Mind also change the price of
  Skills (section 7b).

Category
  Every Talent is one of:
    Innate
      Body or kind. Senses, Night Vision, Looks.
    Background
      Upbringing, culture, environment, trade.
      Outdoorsman, Alley Cat, Streetwise.
    Combat Manoeuvre
      An Action or modifier that does not
      exist without this Talent.
      Prerequisite: this file §8.
      Mechanics: Core_Combat.txt §4a.

  Write the Category on the sheet line.

--------------------------------------------------
2. TALENT
--------------------------------------------------

Form
  Name (X)
  Category:
  Prerequisite:   (optional)
  Trigger:
  Effect:

Law
  Helps on its main trigger. A double-edged
  Talent may penalise a second named trigger
  (Looks). If every trigger hurts, it is a
  Failing or an Affliction.
  Situational. It is not +X to a Characteristic
  on the sheet.
  Does not replace a Skill. Keen Hearing is not
  Awareness.
  Stacks with difficulty and other live modifiers
  (Resolution §4). Two Talents on the same test
  stack only if their triggers are both truly on.
  Do not invent a Talent to cover a rule that
  already exists (Size, Enc band, Iron Grade).
  A Talent's bonus never opens a closed Skill.
  An Advanced Skill (*) at Unknown is still no test,
  unless the entry says so (Outdoorsman does).
  Opposed tests: the Talent adds to its holder's test
  only, never to the other side's.
  The GM may cap Talents stacked on one test at
  their sense (Resolution §4).
  Where a Talent names a Skill or specialism, a
  GM may add one for a named scene if the work is
  plainly the same. Say so in the declaration.

How it is gained
  Birth, kind, career, upbringing, or story, as the
  entry says. Section 7 is the catalogue.
  Entries outside it are written on the Entity that
  needs them. Do not batch-write a list into this file.
  Cost in XP is the entry's Tier (§0). With no Tier
  line, the Core_EXP.txt default applies.

Worked form — Hearing (Talent)
  Names on the sheet, pick one:
    Keen Hearing (10)     human peak
    Keen Hearing (20)     cat, fox, owl
    Keen Hearing (30)     the kind that hunts by ear
  Trigger: Perception or Awareness that relies
  on sound.
  Effect: +X
  Pair Failing: Hard of Hearing (10/20/30)

Worked form — Sight (Talent)
  Names on the sheet, pick one:
    Keen Sight (10)       human peak, a lookout
    Keen Sight (20)       hawk over a field
    Keen Sight (30)       eagle, the kind’s work
  Trigger: Perception or Awareness that relies
  on sight in usable light.
  Effect: +X
  Does not cancel darkness. That is Night Vision.
  Pair Failing: Poor Sight (10/20/30)

Worked form — Smell (Talent)
  Names on the sheet, pick one:
    Keen Smell (10)       human peak, a cook, a tanner
    Keen Smell (20)       cat, wolf
    Keen Smell (30)       bloodhound, the track is
                          the kind’s work
  Trigger: Perception or Awareness that relies
  on scent. Tracking by smell uses this, not
  extra Range.
  Effect: +X
  Pair Failing: Poor Smell (10/20/30)

Worked form — Night Vision
  Special instruction. No (X) unless a later
  catalogue writes one.
  Name on the sheet: Night Vision
  Trigger: Perception or Awareness that relies
  on sight, in the dark under stars, moon, or
  equivalent open-night glow.
  Effect: do not apply the ordinary darkness
  penalty to that test.
  Does not work in a lightless cave, a shuttered
  hold, or any place with no sky-glow at all.
  Does not grant Keen Sight. A man with Night
  Vision and no Keen Sight sees in starlight
  as well as he sees at dusk, not as an owl.
  Kind that also has Keen Sight (20) or (30)
  stacks both: the dark is usable, and the
  sight is sharp.

Worked form — Outdoorsman (Biome)
  Category: Background.
  Talent. (10) fixed. The bracket is the
  biome, not a magnitude.
  Name on the sheet:
    Outdoorsman (Boreal)
    Outdoorsman (named biome from Geography)
  Each biome is its own entry. Two biomes
  is two lines.
  Trigger: Awareness and Outdoor Survival,
  while outside a settlement, in that biome.
  A town, village, hold, or camp street is
  not outdoors for this Talent. Past the
  pales is.
  Rank on those tests:
    Unknown — full Skill Base, not Base ÷ 2.
    No +10 on top. The Talent is the
    training for this biome, this roll.
    Known or better — Rank value, then +10.
  Does not raise stored Rank. Leave the
  biome and Unknown is Unknown again.
  Outdoor Survival * : in this biome,
  outdoors, the test is allowed at Known
  (full Base) even if the Skill is not
  on the sheet. That is the exception to
  Advanced. Other biomes stay closed.
  Indoor, dungeon, and ship-deck do not
  trigger.

Worked form — Alley Cat
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP). Placeholder.
  Talent. (10) fixed. No bracket.
  Name on the sheet: Alley Cat
  At home on the streets.
  Trigger: Concealment and Silent Move,
  in an urban place (7a): the built-up area
  of a town or city, its streets, lanes, and
  alleys. A fort, keep, or camp takes the class
  of the settlement it stands in. Not a village
  or hamlet. Not the tree line. Not open field.
  Not a wild cave.
  Rank on those tests:
    Unknown — full Skill Base, not Base ÷ 2.
    No +10 on top.
    Known or better — Rank value, then +10.
  Does not raise stored Rank.
  Does not grant Disguise. A face in a
  crowd is Looks or Disguise, not this.
  Pairs with Outdoorsman: one is pale-side,
  one is street. They do not fire on the
  same test.

Worked form — Looks (Talent)
  Names on the sheet, pick one:
    Comely (10)
    Beautiful (20)
    Striking (30)
  Trigger A: Charm, and other Fellowship tests
  that run on being wanted in the room.
  Effect A: +X
  Trigger B: staying unknown. Concealment in a
  crowd, Disguise as “nobody”, Silent Move does
  not cover this — this is being seen and kept.
  Effect B: −X to those tests. Observers gain
  +X to Awareness or Gossip to notice or later
  recall the face.
  Jealousy, invitation, and unwanted attention
  are GM calls at the same band. (30) does not
  walk through a market as a shadow.

Worked form — Looks (Failing)
  Names on the sheet, pick one:
    Homely (10)
    Ugly (20)
    Hideous (30)
  Trigger A: Charm, and Fellowship tests that
  run on being wanted in the room.
  Effect A: −X
  Trigger B: being remembered as a threat or a
  mark, or being refused service. Observers
  gain +X to recall the face after. Concealment
  as “nobody” is not helped; a hide is still a
  hide. The Failing is social, not stealth.

--------------------------------------------------
3. FAILING
--------------------------------------------------

Form
  Name (X)
  Trigger:
  Effect:

Law
  Negative only. Same trigger rules as a Talent.
  Not an injury. A missing eye from a blow is an
  Affliction. Born short-sighted is a Failing
  unless the table says otherwise.
  A Failing does not block the test. It modifies
  the TN. A test that cannot be attempted is a
  missing sense or a closed Skill, not a Failing.

Pair with a Talent
  Hard of Hearing and Keen Hearing are a Pair.
  Record one. If a later event would add the
  opposite, replace or cancel as the event says.
  Do not run +10 and −10 on the same roll and
  call it a wash unless the triggers differ
  (one ear, one direction).

--------------------------------------------------
4. AFFLICTION
--------------------------------------------------

Form
  Name
  Source:     disease / wound / other
  Duration:   hours / days / until treated / permanent
  Effect:
  Tests:      what Heal, Surgery, or Toughness
              may do, if anything

Law
  Temporary or permanent. Say which.
  Wounds from Injury_Tables that leave a lasting
  mark (lost hand, ruined knee, Catastrophic
  Bleed still running) are logged here while
  they last. The table row is the source. This
  list is the standing reminder.
  Disease, poison, venom, and curse sit here
  as Afflictions. How they arrive and run:
  Pathogens_Poisons_Curses.txt.
  Bleed tiers already named in Injury stay those
  words: Minor Bleed, Major Bleed, Catastrophic
  Bleed. Do not rename them as Talents.

Worked form — Visible Scarring
  Affliction, not a Failing. Comes from a wound
  or a named event. Looks stays on the sheet.
  Both can fire on the same Charm test.

  Names on the sheet, pick one:
    Marked (10)       a line people see
    Scarred (20)      the face or hands tell
    Ruined (30)       the room changes

  Source: wound / other
  Duration: permanent unless Surgery or a later
  rule says the mark can be hidden or eased.
  A scarf or helm can cover a given test.
  Covered: this Affliction does not fire.

  Trigger A: Charm, and Fellowship tests that
  run on being wanted or trusted on sight.
  Effect A: −X

  Trigger B: Intimidate, and Fellowship tests
  that run on threat made visible.
  Effect B: +X

  Trigger C: being noticed and remembered.
  Observers gain +X to Awareness or Gossip to
  mark or later recall the face. Concealment
  as “nobody” takes −X when the scar is shown.
  Same incognito tax as Looks, separate line.

  A Beautiful (20) Scarred (20) face is
  +20 / −20 on Charm from two entries, and
  +20 Intimidate from the scar. Write both.
  Do not merge them into one number.

  Context — the same mark, two rooms
    Camp / campaign / among fighting men
      The scar is proof. Veteran, valor,
      a body that stayed.
      Trigger A flips: Charm and Fellowship
      among those men take +X, not −X.
      Intimidate still +X.
      Notice still +X. They remember who
      stood.
    Tavern / village / market / court
      The scar is a warning. Vagabond,
      trouble, a man who brought the war
      with him.
      Trigger A as written: Charm −X.
      Intimidate +X.
      Notice +X. They remember to watch
      the door.
    Name the room before the roll.
    A soldier drinking in a harvest village
    is tavern, not camp.
    A lord reviewing his company is camp.

End
  Duration runs out, Heal / Surgery succeeds as
  the entry allows, or the sheet is rewritten
  after a permanent settlement (the lost hand
  stays an Affliction; it does not become a
  Failing named “One Hand”).
  Visible Scarring stays an Affliction even
  when permanent. It does not move to Failings.

--------------------------------------------------
5. RESOLUTION
--------------------------------------------------

Declare the trigger before the roll.
Apply (X) as a modifier to the TN, with every
other live modifier (Resolution §4).
Write the name in the declaration so the table
can see why the TN moved.

Opposed tests: each party applies only their
own entries. A deaf guard does not give the
sneak a bonus. The sneak’s Silent Move and the
guard’s Hard of Hearing are two separate TNs.

Dominate, Man-Scale, and Size are not Talents.
They stay in Resolution and Characteristics.

6. COMBAT
--------------------------------------------------

Combat Manoeuvre Talents are the key.
The Action itself is Core_Combat.txt §4a.
This file only answers: do they have it.

Afflictions that say “no Dodge” or “half M”
override the default action list while they
last. Log them at the start of the Round.

--------------------------------------------------
7a. URBAN AND RURAL
--------------------------------------------------

Source: Economy_Agri_Yield.txt settlement classes.
  Hamlet    fewer than 200 people
  Village   200 to 1,500
  Town      1,500 to 8,000
  City      more than 8,000

Urban   Town and bigger. 1,500 or more people.
Rural   Village and smaller. Under 1,500 people.

Rules
  Class by the settlement file's working population.
  Where a range is given, use the middle. At exactly
  1,500 the place is urban.
  Within a town or city, the built-up area is urban.
  The fields, lanes, and farms outside it are rural.
  A keep, fort, hall, or camp takes the class of the
  settlement it stands in. A lone fort or camp with
  no settlement is rural.
  Roads between places are rural.
  Wilderness beyond the settled land is neither. It is
  Outdoorsman's ground.
  A person in a place is in one class at a time.
  Alley Cat uses this definition (town and bigger).

--------------------------------------------------
7b. TALENTS (10)
--------------------------------------------------

Each is a fixed (10) unless the entry says otherwise.
Where the entry names Fellowship or Willpower tests,
the Talent does not change the Characteristic.

Gallows Humor (10)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 2 (100 XP).
  Trigger: Fellowship tests made to steady, rally, or
  share company with others during an ordeal: a siege,
  a hard march, a hungry winter, a field after a fight,
  a night near something Frightening or worse.
  Effect: +10.
  Not a Fear test. It does not help the holder's own
  Willpower.

Unflinching (10)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 3 (150 XP).
  Prerequisite: Willpower 40 or more.
  Trigger: Willpower tests against Fear (Core_Fear.txt
  §0), and Willpower tests to snap out of Shock (§2).
  Effect: +10. One Fear rating step is cancelled.
  It does not remove Shock rows already rolled and does
  not reduce IP gained.
  A character holds Unflinching or Dead-Eyed, not both.

Dead-Eyed (10)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 2 (100 XP).
  Prerequisite: the character has survived something
  traumatic. The GM decides if it was enough. Any one of:
    a Fear test failed by 4 or more Degrees (so they have IP)
    a Fear 3 (Horrifying) or Fear 4 event lived through
    kin or comrades lost to violence, famine, or plague,
    and left to carry on
    a campaign, a siege, or an atrocity witnessed
  The event is named in the BIO (NPCs) or the
  Campaign_Log (PCs). It is a gate, not a trigger.
  Trigger A: Willpower tests against Fear and to snap out
  of Shock.
  Effect A: +10. As Unflinching. They do not stack.
  Trigger B: Fellowship tests that run on being wanted
  in the room (Charm, and similar).
  Effect B: -10.
  Trigger C: Intimidate tests the holder makes.
  Effect C: +10. A flat stare that has seen worse than
  the threat. Does not help an Intimidate test made
  against the holder.
  Double-edged, one entry, as Looks is. The flat stare
  makes people uneasy. Does not affect Gossip, Haggle,
  or Evaluate.

Axiomatic (10)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 3 (150 XP).
  Prerequisite: Intelligence 45 or more.
  Trigger: a test of one of these Skills where the work is
  counting, weighing, measuring, ciphering, or fitting
  numbers to a plan:
    Evaluate (any specialism)
    Navigation
    Academic Knowledge (Engineering), (Science)
    Trade, for building or layout: masonry, carpentry,
    shipwright, surveying, fortification, mill and works
    design. Not craft by hand, such as a smith's feel for a heat.
    Read/Write (any script), only to keep or audit accounts
  Effect: +10.
  Not: Haggle, Gossip, Charm, Awareness, Search, Heal,
  Surgery, or combat tests.
  The list is closed. A GM may add a Skill for one named
  test if the work is plainly arithmetic.

Masked Intent (10)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 3 (150 XP).
  Prerequisite: Fellowship 40 or more.
  Trigger: a social Fellowship test where the holder's aim is
  to hide what they think, mean, or hold while someone
  watches or questions them:
    Charm, used to lie, bluff, or flatter against a doubt
    Haggle, to hide a reserve price or a lack of coin
    Disguise, to hold a false role or cover
    Performer (Acting), as a cover
    gambling tests, in any game with an element of
    bluffing or not giving away your hand (the games
    note names which). Games with no hidden hand or
    bluff do not qualify.
  Effect: +10 to the holder's own test.
  Not: any Willpower test, Fear or Shock tests,
  Concealment, Silent Move, Sleight of Hand, Intimidate,
  or any test to read or catch another person.
  Does not make a checkable lie believable.

Kindred Spirit (10)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP). Placeholder.
  How gained: rolled at creation, 1d100, a result of 01.
  The roll is made only for a character whose upbringing
  was in a wilder or animal-husbandry context (herding,
  stock and stable work, hunting and trapping, frontier
  and forest life, hand-rearing beasts). May also be
  taken later when the story gives the reason, with the
  GM's agreement, written in the BIO or Campaign_Log.
  Applies to: beasts of Type Natural or Wonder
  (Bestiary_Format.txt). Never Type Unveiling.
  Effect A (test): +10 to Charm, Animal Care, and Animal
  Training tests aimed at such a beast, and +10 to Ride
  tests on a mount of that kind. Does not shorten Animal
  Training. A bonded mount is still long work.
  Effect B (standing): a Natural or Wonder beast does
  not start hostile to the holder. A beast that would
  attack on sight holds back unless provoked. Provoked
  means struck, cornered, robbed, or its young threatened.
  Natural beasts that still attack: a beast starving and
  hunting the holder as food, guarding young or territory
  against an approach, in rut or maddened, or under orders
  to attack (a trained war beast or one commanded by a
  rider or handler).
  Wonder beasts (the noble creatures, Type Wonder): only
  provocation breaks the standing effect.
  A gryphon, earthbound or Royal, that meets the holder
  does not attack unprovoked. The holder may approach and
  be heard. The bird still decides what comes next. The
  bond is not given: a Royal Gryphon chooses, and a
  hand-reared bird bonds to one rider for life. The
  Talent adds +10 to the bonding tests only. Taking an egg
  is provocation. A bonded bird is not turned against its
  rider.
  Affects the holder only. Does not make a beast obey.
  Obedience is a Charm or Animal Training test.
  Flavour (no mechanic): small animals gather in a
  settlement. Birds on the shoulders and sills, cats on the
  lap, strays at the door.

Cold Spine
  Category: Innate. Special instruction, no (X).
  Tier: 1 (50 XP). Placeholder.
  How gained: innate only. 1 in 500. Roll 1d1000 at
  creation. A result of 001 or 002 gives the Talent.
  Not bought and not taken by story.
  Effect A (duty): when a trigger is true, the GM must give
  the holder a sign. The sign may not be held back to
  preserve a surprise.
  Effect B (test): +10 to the holder's Willpower tests
  against unnatural phenomena. That covers Fear tests from
  a Type Unveiling beast, a ghost, a veil-drop, or a False
  Moon storm (Core_Fear.txt), and Willpower tests against
  whispers, lures, and veil influence. Not ordinary fear,
  pain, or a social Willpower test.
  Stacks with Unflinching on the same test (+20).
  The holder's tells. Three personal sensations, set at
  creation and written on the sheet, one for each kind of
  wrongness. Physical, specific, and the same each time:
    Watched      something unseen has its attention on them
    Wrong place  the place, or something in it, is unnatural
    Don't        an act they are about to do is a very bad idea
  Examples: palms itch, the old break in the left foot
  aches, cold at the base of the spine, a metal taste, ringing
  in the ears, hair standing on the arms, a stitch under the
  ribs, the dog-tooth throbbing. No two the same.
  Triggers (closed list):
    1. An Unveiling presence is near: a Type Unveiling
       beast or wight, a ghost, a veil-drop, or an Unveiling
       effect at work.
    2. Something with harmful intent has its attention on
       the holder, and the holder does not know it (Watched).
    3. The place is unnatural: a veil-thin site, a False Moon
       night, a ruin that is not empty (Wrong place).
    4. The holder is about to do something that will very
       probably bring death or lasting ruin on them or on
       those they love, and they do not know it (Don't).
       The sign is a strong hint or an ominous description:
       the scene leans in. The air thickens, the light turns,
       the tell burns. It reads as dread, not as an
       instruction. The GM does not say what the danger is.
    The GM may add a trigger for one named scene if it
    plainly fits.
  Not: an ordinary ambush, a trap, a pickpocket, or a
  mundane lie. Awareness, Search, and Perception answer those.
  Strength of the sign:
    Faint      Fear 1 (Disturbing). A hint, easy to put down
               to nerves.
    Clear      Fear 2 (Frightening). Taken seriously.
    Insistent  Fear 3 (Horrifying) or worse, or a Don't with
               death behind it. Impossible to ignore. The GM
               may say outright that something is very wrong.
    Strength follows nearness and danger. It is not a roll.
  Limits:
    One sign per trigger per scene. It repeats only if the
    situation changes.
    A warning only. No names, no numbers, no direction.
    The holder may ignore it. It never forces or forbids.
    No other test bonus, and it does not replace Awareness.
    It cannot be switched off.
  NPCs: the sensation enters the monologue and the FACT CHECK.

Streetwise (10)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 2 (100 XP).
  Prerequisite: at least 5 years living or working in an
  urban place (7a), counted from birth. Childhood counts.
  The years need not be consecutive. The time is named in
  the BIO (NPCs) or the Campaign_Log (PCs).
  Trigger: Charm, Gossip, Haggle, Intimidate, Disguise,

Temperament and habit. A (5) value, a situational +5,
sometimes with a -5 in another context. The exception to
the tens rule in §0.

Form
  Name (5)
  Category: Background or Innate
  Tier: 1 (50 XP)
  Gain:   trigger, +5
  Cost:   trigger, -5 (optional)
  Note:

Law
  The Cost is a different situation from the Gain.
  A Character Talent never cancels its own Gain.
  Some entries have no Cost.
  Do not pay the same fact twice (Core_Combat.txt §11).
  Where a Character Talent and a weapon quality or another
  Talent cover the same test, both apply only if both
  triggers are true. The GM may cap the stack.
  A Character Talent never opens a closed Skill.
  No Prerequisite unless the entry names one.

Combat, Grit and Physicality
----------------------------

Iron-Bellied (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain:  +5 Toughness tests against foul rations, rot, spoiled water, and food-borne
        agents (Pathogens_Poisons_Curses.txt).
  Cost:  -5 Evaluate tests that judge food or drink by taste or smell. A numb palate.
  Note:  Does not help against poison or a deliberate toxin. That is Toughness as normal.

Heavy-Handed (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain:  +5 WS on melee attacks with a weapon that has Pummelling (x) or is two-handed.
        Also +5 WS on Strike to Stun and on the opposed test to Disarm a shield.
  Cost:  -5 WS with a weapon that has Fast (x). Weight, not finesse.
  Note:  Weaponry.txt gives Pummelling (x) as +x Strength on a blow. Pummelling is the
        weapon's effect. This is the wielder's habit. They stack when both are true.

Cat-Footed (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain:  +5 Agility and Athletics tests to keep balance on narrow, slick, or unsteady
        ground: a ledge, wet cobbles, a roof, a plank (Hazards.txt, the ledge entries).
  Cost:  -5 on opposed Strength tests to resist being shoved or pinned. A light frame.
  Note:  Does not stack with Sea-Legged on the same test. Take the higher or let the GM
        choose which footing is the character's own.

Rat-Cornered (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain:  +5 WS and +5 Dodge while the character cannot retreat, is outnumbered 2 to 1 or
        worse, or is at half Wounds or less.
  Cost:  -5 Willpower tests to break off, accept surrender, or stay their hand once the
        fight is on. Ferocity is not easily put down.
  Note:  Unpredictable is a GM colour, not a mechanic. One trigger is enough.

Shield-Brother (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain:  +5 WS and +5 Parry while an ally is adjacent and fighting the same foe or
        holding the line beside them.
  Cost:  -5 WS and Parry when the character has no ally within 2 yards.
  Note:  Shield_Rules.txt (Defensive (x)) adds to Parry as before. The Talent is on top.

Dirty Brawler (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain:  +5 WS with unarmed attacks and with improvised weapons (Weaponry.txt, Improvised),
        and +5 WS on a Called Shot to a soft spot (eye, knee, groin).
  Cost:  -5 Fellowship tests with those who prize honour (a knight, a magistrate, a guild
        warden) once the style is known.
  Note:  Does not grant any Combat Manoeuvre. That needs a Combat Manoeuvre Talent (§8).

Second Wind (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain:  +5 Toughness on the Tired test and on tests to avoid gaining Fatigue
        (Core_Fatigue.txt, Tired Hours; forced march).
  Cost:  none.
  Note:  Does not remove Fatigue already gained and does not shorten a rest.

Cold-Blooded (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 BS when firing from or into a melee, and +5 BS or WS on the first Round of a
        fight when the character has not been surprised.
  Cost: -5 Charm tests to comfort, reassure, or befriend. A cold manner.
  Note: Not Dead-Eyed, which is about Fear. This is aim and nerve in a fight.
        Different triggers. They may be held together.

Thick-Skinned (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Toughness tests against weather and exposure (cold, heat, rain, wind) and
        against the pain of minor harm (scrapes, bruises).
  Cost: -5 Perception tests to notice their own minor injury or illness before it worsens.
  Note: Does not reduce damage and does not change Toughness Bonus or armour.

Hair-Trigger (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Initiative (Agility + 1d10) on the first Round of a fight, and +5 Dodge against
        the first attack of a fight that starts by surprise.
  Cost: -5 Willpower tests to hold back or not react when provoked or startled.
  Note: Initiative is rerolled every Round (Core_Combat.txt §1). The +5 is on Round 1 only.

Bull-Necked (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Strength tests in a Clinch, against being shoved or tackled, and to power
        through a tackle or carry a heavy load.
  Cost: -5 Athletics tests for jumps, leaps, and anything needing a quick, light body.
  Note: Clinch Actions are in Core_Combat.txt §4. Opposed Strength is already the test.

Grim-Jawed (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Willpower tests against the effects of pain and Shock from wounds, and to stay
        on their feet when badly hurt.
  Cost: -5 Heal tests made on them. They hide how hurt they are, so a healer is slower to
        find it.
  Note: Not for Fear. Fear Shock from Core_Fear.txt is Unflinching or Cold Spine.
        Wounds and pain are this Talent. Do not pay the same fact twice.

Wolf-Paced (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Toughness and Athletics tests on a long steady run or jog over rough country,
        for hours (travel, pursuit, a message-run).
  Cost: -5 Athletics for a short sprint or a burst of speed. Steady, not quick.
  Note: Core_Combat.txt §7 covers combat movement. This is the travel scale only.

Knuckle-Scarred (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 WS with unarmed attacks, and +5 Toughness tests against harm to the hands
        (a hot handle, a rope burn, a bite on the fist).
  Cost: -5 Sleight of Hand and other fine-handwork tests. Thick, stiff fingers.
  Note: Overlaps Dirty Brawler (unarmed WS). They stack only if both triggers are true.
        Suggest the GM treat them as a Pair for unarmed WS: the higher +5 applies.

Mule-Stubborn (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Toughness and Willpower tests against hunger, thirst, lack of sleep, and
        long deprivation.
  Cost: -5 Charm tests to concede, compromise, or patch a quarrel. They will not give way,
        even to sense.
  Note: The "or sense" in the brief is read as the Cost: stubborn against good sense.
        Say if it was meant differently.

Saddle-Born (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Ride tests, and +5 WS and BS while mounted.
  Cost: -5 Athletics tests and -5 WS on foot. More at home on a horse.
  Note: Ride is a different test from Kindred Spirit (+10 Ride on a beast of that kind).
        Both apply when both are true, +15 Ride. Worth watching.

Sea-Legged (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Agility and Athletics tests on a pitching deck, a slick gangplank, or rigging,
        and +5 Toughness tests against seasickness.
  Cost: none.
  Note: Does not stack with Cat-Footed on the same test. See Cat-Footed.

Close-Quarters (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 WS in a cramped space (a corridor, a door, an alley under 2 yards wide, a
        stair) with a short weapon (Reach 0.5 or less) or unarmed, and +5 on Clinch tests.
  Cost: -5 WS in the open with a weapon of Reach 2 or more.
  Note: Weaponry.txt §3 already has rules for corridors and a +10 WS for a shortened
        Versatile grip. The Talent stacks with them when the triggers are true.

Merciless (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 WS and BS against a foe who is Prone, Stunned, helpless, or beginning to
        yield, and +5 Willpower tests to press on and not hesitate.
  Cost: -5 Fellowship tests with those who value mercy once the habit is known.
  Note: A Character Talent, not a Failing. Choosing to finish a foe is not a flaw by
        itself. It costs reputation, not skill.

Hard-Bitten (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Toughness tests to stay conscious, to resist being Stunned or knocked down,
        and to resist the lasting harm of an injury (Core_Injury.txt).
  Cost: -5 Agility tests in cold or wet weather. Old breaks that ache.
  Note: Does not change Wounds or Toughness Bonus.
  Overlaps flagged
  - Close-Quarters and Heavy-Handed lean on Weaponry.txt rules (Reach, Versatile, Pummelling,
  Fast). Check the numbers when those entries are locked.
  - Saddle-Born stacks with Kindred Spirit (+15 Ride on a beast of that kind).
  - Cat-Footed and Sea-Legged cover the same balance tests. Take one +5.
  - Knuckle-Scarred and Dirty Brawler both give +5 unarmed WS. Treat as a Pair on that test.
  - Grim-Jawed vs Unflinching and Cold Spine: pain and wounds vs fear. Different tests.

Mind, Lore and Perception
-------------------------

Room Reader (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Awareness and Gossip tests to read a gathering: who stands with whom, where the
        tension lies, who really ranks.
  Cost: none.
  Note: Reading, not hiding. It helps the watcher. It does not touch Masked Intent (+10 on

        to their own sides.

Sceptic (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 on tests to see through, or resist, a lie, a scam, a tall tale, or a false
        promise (the test opposed to the deceiver's Charm, Haggle, or Disguise).
  Cost: -5 on the first Fear test of a scene against a genuinely unnatural sight.
        Denial: the sceptic is slow to believe their own eyes.
  Note: Cost is for Unveiling or Wonder phenomena that are real. It is not for hoaxes.
        Cold Spine's +10 against the unnatural offsets it.

Magpie Memory (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Gossip and Common Lore tests, and Intelligence tests to recall a rumour, a name,
        or a fact heard before.
  Cost: -5 Academic Knowledge tests. A wide, shallow store.
  Note: Recall of what was heard, not new learning. Does not raise any stored Rank.

Well-Read (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Academic Knowledge and Common Lore (History, Religion, Regions) tests, and
        Read/Write tests to find or follow a source.
  Cost: -5 Outdoor Survival and Track tests. Book learning, not field learning.
  Prerequisite: Read/Write at Known or better in at least one script.
  Note: Stacks with Axiomatic on an Academic Knowledge (Engineering or Science) test.
        A skill-rank prerequisite is a new kind (Talent prerequisites).

Light Sleeper (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Awareness and Perception tests to wake to, or notice, a noise or movement
        while asleep or resting.
  Cost: -5 on the Tired test after a rest broken by noise or in a shared or noisy place.
  Note: The rules have no wake test. Core_Fatigue.txt covers rests and wake windows and
        Core_Resolution.txt does not say how a sleeper notices. Proposed gap-filler: a sleeper
        takes an Awareness test at half Base (the Unknown rule) when something happens, and
        the Talent adds +5. A GM call until locked.

Navigator's Instinct (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Navigation and Outdoor Survival tests to keep direction, including underground,
        at sea, in fog, or at night.
  Cost: -5 Navigation tests that must follow a map or another's directions.
  Note: Navigation is Advanced and cannot be attempted at Unknown. Not changed here.
        The Talent is a bonus on a test the character already makes.

Incorrigible Pedant (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Awareness, Search, and Evaluate tests to catch errors: a misspoken word, a flawed
        clause, a false weight, a forged mark. Also +5 Academic Knowledge (Law) to find a
        technicality.
  Cost: -5 Charm tests with anyone whose mistake the character has just corrected.
        They rarely resist saying so.
  Note: The cost is a trigger the GM can see. It happens when the character speaks up.

Cold Logic (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 on Intelligence skill tests (Heal, Surgery, Navigation, Evaluate, Trapping, and
        similar) made under stress, time pressure, or in a crisis.
  Cost: -5 Charm tests to console, share feeling, or win a person over with warmth.
  Note: Close to Cold-Blooded and Dead-Eyed
  . All carry a Charm cost. A character holding two
        takes both costs. Suggest one cold Talent per character.

Superstitious (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Common Lore (Unveiling, Religion, Monsters) tests about omens, charms, salt-lines,
        wards, and the old rules. +5 Willpower tests against the unnatural while a ward or
        charm the character trusts is in place.
  Cost: -5 Willpower tests after a bad omen or a broken ward. They cannot shake it.
  Note: The Talent does not make a ward real. What wards do in the world is a GM matter.
        If a ward has no effect, the bonus is on the character's belief, which is the point.
        Stacks with Cold Spine on an unnatural Willpower test while a ward is up.

Tinker's Touch (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Trade tests to repair, patch, or improvise from scrap (wire, leather, wax, wood).
  Cost: -5 Trade tests to make a new piece to a high standard (fine or original work).
  Note: Trade is Advanced and cannot be attempted at Unknown. Open: should the Talent
        allow a crude repair at Unknown, like Outdoorsman does for Outdoor Survival?

Grave-Wise (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Heal, Search, and Common Lore (Religion) tests at a corpse or burial: cause of
        death, signs of an unnatural death, funerary rites. +5 Toughness against corpse-
        borne disease (Pathogens_Poisons_Curses.txt).
  Cost: -5 Fellowship tests with those who fear death-work, such as a village or a
        superstitious crowd, once the character's work is known.
  Note: Unveiling deaths are for the GM to mark. The Talent says what the body shows.

Weather-Wise (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Outdoor Survival and Navigation tests to forecast weather, frost, and wind
        from cloud, animals, and joint-ache. On a success the GM gives the next day's
        weather in a line. 3 or more Degrees of Success: the next two days.
  Cost: none.
  Note: World_Calendar.txt sets season. Weather is a GM call. This is the forecast. It does
        not change the weather.

Silver-Tongued (5)

I Know a Guy (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).

Blue-Blooded Bearing (5)

  Cost: -5 Charm tests with labourers and common folk who resent the manner.
  Note: Opposed to Salt of the Earth. The trigger needs visible rank. A noble in rags is

Salt of the Earth (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Charm and Gossip tests with labourers, farmers, and common soldiers.
  Cost: -5 Charm tests with nobles, officials, and clerks, who find them rough.

Voice of Authority (5)

  Cost: -5 Charm tests to ask a favour or persuade an equal. It reads as an order.
  Note: Leadership and Warfare are the Command skills. The bonus

Haggler's Instinct (5)

Cardsharp (5)

Whisper-Collector (5)

Looming Presence (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).

Chameleon (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Disguise and Charm tests to pass as another class: speech, posture, manners.

Soft Touch (5)

Well-Connected (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).

Tavern Troubadour (5)

Double-Dealer (5)

Plain-Spoken (5)

  Prerequisite: a notorious deed, named in the BIO (NPCs) or the Campaign_Log (PCs).
  Note: The Campaign_Log faction tracker is the place for reputation. This Talent does not
        change the faction bands. The GM may.

Good Listener (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Charm and Gossip tests to draw out a confession or confidence without direct
        questions.
  Cost: none.
  Note: Not Whisper-Collector (a drink and a stranger). This is a patient friend. They may
        be held together.

Gilded Liar (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Charm tests to tell an outrageous lie, on the holder's own test.
  Cost: -5 Charm tests to be believed when telling a plain truth to anyone who knows the
        character as a liar.
  Note: Masked Intent (+10) adds on a bluff. +15 on a lie in a town with Streetwise is a
        lot. See the stacking rule. At 3 or more Degrees of Success the listener may doubt
        their own memory. That line is colour for the GM, not a mechanic.

Friend of the Watch (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Charm, Haggle, and Gossip tests with guards, watchmen, and jailers: bribes,
        passwords, talking out of the cells.
  Cost: -5 Charm tests with thieves and criminals who know the connection.
  Note: Bribes are Haggle. Passwords are Gossip. Not a licence to ignore the law.

Rabble-Rouser (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Charm and Performer tests to stoke a crowd's grievances and turn them to anger.
  Cost: -5 Charm tests to calm a crowd, and -5 Fellowship with authorities who know the
        name.
  Note: No crowd or mob rules exist. What a roused crowd does is a GM call.

Unassuming (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Concealment and Disguise tests to be overlooked in plain sight: harmless, tired,
        insignificant. Observers take -5 on Awareness tests to see the character as a threat.
  Cost: -5 Intimidate and -5 Charm tests to be taken seriously or given respect.
  Note: Pair with Face in the Crowd on Disguise and Concealment: take the higher +5.

Temperament, Instinct and Nature
--------------------------------

Short Fuse (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Intimidate tests while the character is visibly angry (a shouted threat).
  Cost: -5 Willpower tests to hold their temper against a slight, a delay, or disrespect.
  Note: Opposed to Stoic. Hair-Trigger is a body reflex. This is a mood.

Born Pessimist (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Awareness tests to notice an ambush, a trap, or a betrayal, and +5 Dodge on the
        first attack of an ambush.
  Cost: -5 Charm tests to give or win trust. They expect the worst of everyone.
  Note: Not a warning duty. Cold Spine is. This is a test bonus. The two may be held together.

Live for Tonight (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Charm, Performer, and Gossip tests at a revel: a tavern, a feast, a fair.
  Cost: -5 Willpower tests to resist a pleasure or a wager on offer: wine, song, a bet.
  Note: Not a Failing. The cost is a trigger the GM can see.

Pack Rat (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: a closed list of trifles is always to hand. Twine, chalk, a bent nail, a rag, a
        scrap of wax or leather. Once per scene the character may produce one at no
        cost and without a purchase test.
  Cost: -5 Concealment and Silent Move tests. They rattle and bulge.
  Note: This is a special instruction, as Night Vision is. No test bonus. The trifles
        count as 1 Enc in all. They are never a weapon, a tool above a handful, or coin.
        Tinker's Touch uses them well. They may be held together.

Iron Will (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Willpower tests to resist torture (the subject's roll, Core_Characteristics_Skills
        Torture), bribery, intimidation, and supernatural compulsion, once decided.
  Cost: -5 Charm tests to compromise, concede, or be talked round.
  Note: Torture is the subject's Willpower test and this adds to it. It also adds on a
        compulsion test, where it stacks with Cold Spine (+10 against the unnatural).
        Not a Fear test. Fear is Unflinching and Cold Spine.

Feral Instinct (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Awareness tests that rely on smell, hearing, or the gut, and +5 Dodge against an
        attack the character did not see coming.
  Cost: -5 on Intelligence skill tests that need careful planning or long reckoning
        (Navigation, Evaluate, Trapping, and the like).
  Note: Opposed to Cold Logic.

Bleeding Heart (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Heal and Charm tests made to aid someone who is suffering.
  Cost: -5 Willpower tests to walk past suffering, and -5 Awareness against a hard-luck
        tale.
  Note: Heal is Advanced and cannot be attempted at Unknown. Opposed to Merciless
  . Close to Soft Touch. Hold one.

Lone Wolf (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Concealment, Silent Move, Outdoor Survival, Track, Awareness, WS, and BS tests
        while no ally is within 10 yards.
  Cost: -5 Fellowship tests to coordinate, share, or act as one of a group.
  Note: Opposed to Shield-Brother, which is +5 WS and Parry beside an ally.
        Here the bonus is for being alone.

Stoic (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Willpower tests to endure grief, humiliation, loss, and discomfort without
        showing it, and +5 Toughness tests against hunger and cold.
  Cost: -5 Fellowship tests to show warmth, console, or rally others.
  Note: Not Grim-Jawed (pain and wounds). Not Thick-Skinned (weather and minor harm).
        Toughness against cold overlaps Thick-Skinned and Mule-Stubborn, and stacks only if
        the triggers are the same. Opposed to Short Fuse.

Thrill-Seeker (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Athletics and Willpower tests when the stakes are risk of serious harm: a drop,
        long odds, a dare.
  Cost: -5 on tests where there is no risk and no stakes (a dull watch, a long wait).
  Note: Boredom is the cost. The GM names it when it is plainly true.

Vengeful (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 WS, Track, Gossip, and Intimidate tests against a named wrongdoer who owes the
        character a debt of blood, coin, or honour.
  Cost: -5 Willpower tests to let a grievance go or accept a settlement.
  Prerequisite: a named grievance, written in the BIO (NPCs) or the Campaign_Log (PCs).
  Note: The grievance is a named person. The Talent does not apply to a general hatred.

Restless Feet (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Toughness tests against Tired and Fatigue while travelling, and +5 Outdoor
        Survival tests on the road.
  Cost: -5 Charm and Willpower tests after more than 7 days in one town or safe house,
        until they move on.
  Note: Overlaps Wolf-Paced on the Tired test. They stack only if both apply.

Fey-Touched (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Charm and Animal Care tests with animals, and +5 Common Lore (Unveiling) tests.
  Cost: -5 Fellowship tests with superstitious people, who cross themselves.
  Note: The Pair note applies with Kindred Spirit. The higher bonus on animals applies.
        Not a Wonder beast reading (Type Wonder). Not a veil sense (Cold Spine).

Guarded Soul (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Willpower tests to resist being read, probed, or befriended into confidences
        (the test opposed to another's Gossip or Charm used to pry).
  Cost: -5 Charm tests to form close bonds, rally allies, or take comfort.
  Note: Masked Intent (+10 Fellowship, hiding intent) is a lie told. This is a wall. Different
        triggers. May be held together.

Meticulous (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Trade tests to maintain or inspect kit, +5 Search tests to check for flaws, and
        +5 Navigation tests made to plan a route with a fallback.
  Cost: -5 Initiative on the first Round of a fight and -5 on tests made in haste or by
        improvising.
  Note: Opposed to Reckless Spirit. The contingency plans are colour for the GM.

Reckless Spirit (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Athletics tests for a leap or sudden dash, and +5 WS on a Charge Attack
        (Core_Combat.txt §3).
  Cost: -5 Awareness and Search tests to look before leaping, and -5 on tests that need
        caution or preparation.
  Note: Opposed to Meticulous. Close to Thrill-Seeker. Hold one of them.

Light-Fingered (5)
  Category: Background.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Sleight of Hand tests to take keys, coins, and small trinkets, and +5 Concealment
        to hide a small item on the person.
  Cost: -5 Willpower tests to resist taking a small thing in reach. A habit.
  Note: Sleight of Hand is Advanced and cannot be attempted at Unknown. Cardsharp
  is cards and dice. Light-Fingered is small items. Different triggers.

Shadow-Lover (5)
  Category: Innate.
  Tier: 1 (50 XP).
  Gain: +5 Awareness, Concealment, and Silent Move tests in dim light: dim alleys, dense woods,
        and the quiet hours before dawn.
  Cost: -5 Awareness and Search tests in bright sun and open glare.
  Note: Night Vision cancels the darkness penalty. This adds on top when both are true.
        Stacks with Alley Cat on Concealment and Silent Move in a town. See the stacking rule.

--------------------------------------------------
8. COMBAT MANOEUVRE TALENTS
--------------------------------------------------

Prerequisite only. Every Combat Manoeuvre Talent is
Tier 2 (100 XP). Tests and conditions are Combat §4a. Without the Talent the Action
is not available. No Unknown attempt.

Write on the sheet as Category: Combat Manoeuvre.

  Disarm
    Unlocks Disarm (Full).

  Quick Draw
    Unlocks Ready as Free once per Round.

  Rapid Reload
    Unlocks the Reload cut.

  Strike to Stun
    Unlocks Strike to Stun (Full).

  Riposte
    Unlocks Riposte after a won Parry.

  Slip
    Unlocks Slip after a won Dodge.

  Combat Grappling
    Unlocks Combat Grappling (Half), Dominant
    Clinch Actions, and Reversal.
    Escape from Clinched (Defensive) does not
    need this Talent.

  Specialist Weapon Group (various)
    Weaponry.txt. Each group is its own Talent.

  Mounted Archer
    Not an Action. While mounted, ignore the
    first 10 points of mounted shooting
    penalties (Weaponry.txt bows).
    Does not remove Inaccurate (Aim).
    A high longbow is −30, then −20.
    A low composite is −5, then 0.
}
