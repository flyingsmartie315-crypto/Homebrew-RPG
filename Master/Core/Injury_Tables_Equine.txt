# Injury_Tables_Equine.txt
# Virath Slot injury tables — horse-type Entities
# Status: DRAFT for Core.
# Law: Core_Injury.txt (Bleed, Overflow, how to roll)
# Wallace and Slots live in this file.
# Companion: Core_Combat.txt, Armour_Rules.txt
#
# Landmarks follow the veterinary “points of the
# horse” (poll, withers, barrel, flank, stifle,
# hock, cannon, fetlock, pastern, coronet).
# Distal limb: no muscle belly below the carpus
# or tarsus. Tendon, suspensory, synovial sheath,
# and bone only. Open long-bone and femur /
# humerus fractures are usually career-ending
# or fatal in the field (racecourse orthopaedics).

Roll 1d10 + Overflow − TB.
Floor 1. 10 or more uses the 10+ row.
1 is best. 10+ is worst.
Use the column that matches the blow.

Head and Body rows do not cap Wounds unless
a row says otherwise. Digit amps (hoof off)
may cap.

A horse that cannot stand on three legs is
Down. A horse that cannot stand at all is
Helpless. Some 8+ rows are “put down unless
Fate or immediate Surgery.” That is the
field, not cruelty.

Order: Head, Body, Fore, Hind.

WALLACE (first d100)

==================================================
EYES
==================================================

Side presented. From behind use POLL / SKULL.

CUT
  1   Lid nick. Dress it. No Bleed.
  2   Lid split. −10 Perception until bound.
  3   Canthus opened. Minor Bleed. Globe spared.
  4   Globe grazed. −20 Perception that side
      until Heal. Minor Bleed. Shies.
  5   Cornea laid open. Blind that side until
      Surgery. Minor Bleed.
  6   Lid and cheek flayed. Eye shut. Major Bleed.
  7   Globe sliced. That eye is gone unless
      Surgery this hour. Permanent. Major Bleed.
      Panic: Toughness or bolt.
  8   Orbit opened. Blind that side. Permanent.
      Major Bleed. Down unless rider holds it.
  9   Across both orbits. Blind. Catastrophic
      Bleed. Helpless.
 10+  Face through the sockets into the sinus.
      Blind. Catastrophic Bleed. Toughness now
      or dying.

PIERCE
  1   Grit in the eye. −10 Perception 1 Round.
  2   Lid transfixed. −10 Perception until bound.
  3   Beside the globe. Minor Bleed. Eye spared.
  4   Orbit, globe intact. −20 Perception.
      Stunned 1 Round. Minor Bleed.
  5   Into the white. Heal this hour or that
      eye is gone. Minor Bleed.
  6   Globe burst. Blind that side. Permanent.
      Major Bleed. Bolts unless held.
  7   Through the orbit toward the pouch.
      Blind that side. Major Bleed.
  8   Deep orbit. Brainpan threatened.
      Toughness or unconscious. Major Bleed
      (Difficult).
  9   Through the socket into the cranium.
      Blind. Catastrophic Bleed. Down.
 10+  Both orbits. Dead unless Fate.

BLUNT
  1   Brow bang. Swear. Blink.
  2   Lid hematoma. −10 Perception until seen.
  3   Globe shocked. Eye waters. Agility or
      it shies that Turn.
  4   Orbital rim cracked. −10 Perception.
      No Bleed.
  5   Globe contused. Blind that side 1d10
      hours. Stunned.
  6   Orbit smashed, globe intact. Permanent
      −10 Perception unless Surgery.
  7   Globe burst in the socket. Blind that
      side. Permanent. Stunned.
  8   Both rims gone. Blind that side.
      Down. Minor Bleed.
  9   Poll and orbit together. Unconscious.
      Permanent disability.
 10+  Head staved at the eye. Dead unless Fate.

THROATLATCH
==================================================

Jugular groove. Jugular superficial;
carotid deeper, guarded by muscle.
Recurrent laryngeal and vagosympathetic
trunk in the lower groove.

CUT
  1   Skin nick on the latch.
  2   Groove opened, vessel spared. −10
      Ride until bound.
  3   To the sternocephalicus. Minor Bleed.
  4   Jugular nicked. Minor Bleed. Pressure
      or it becomes Major next Turn.
  5   Jugular opened. Major Bleed. Hold it
      this Turn.
  6   Both sides of the groove. Major Bleed.
      Difficult. Half Move.
  7   Carotid threatened. Catastrophic if
      the GM rules it opened; otherwise
      Major and Down.
  8   Carotid opened. Catastrophic Bleed.
      Dead in minutes unless held now.
  9   Airway and vessel together. Dead
      unless Fate. Catastrophic Bleed.
 10+  Throatlatch taken through. Dead.
