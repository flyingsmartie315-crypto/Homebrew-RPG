# Core_Terrain.txt
# Virath — how Geography scores become ground underfoot.
# Status: DRAFT for Core.
# Companion files:
#   Geography_Rules.txt                 seven scores, season, biome
#   Core_Fatigue.txt                    work-hour band
#   Core_Combat.txt                     difficult ground, travel day
#   Bestiary_Rules.txt                  HSI (not this file)

PURPOSE
Geography already scores a place 0–5 on seven axes.
This file turns those numbers into travel and Fatigue.
It does not replace HSI. Creatures still use Bestiary.

Land meanings and oceanic meanings stay separate
(Geography_Rules). This file is land underfoot unless
a section names the sea.

Use the Local Region scores after season
(Temperature, Rainfall, Flora, Fauna shift;
Harvest is the printed score).

--------------------------------------------------
0. KEYWORDS
--------------------------------------------------

Axis
  One of the seven land scores.

Ideal
  The score on that axis that costs a human
  walker the least.

Worst
  The score that costs the most, or that
  only bites on a long stay.

Hourly
  Hits Fatigue and Combat this hour.

Prolonged
  Hits after a day or more. Not the hourly
  test.

Ground Band
  The combined hourly result:
  Easy / Fair / Hard / Severe / Extreme.

--------------------------------------------------
1. EACH AXIS
--------------------------------------------------

Temperature (heat / cold)
  Ideal  3  Mild temperate
  Worst  0  Arctic / freezing (near lethal)
  Worst  5  Hot, sub-Saharan
  Hourly. Distance from 3 is the load.
  0 and 5 both kill if you stay. Cold eats
  faster without fire. Heat eats faster
  under kit.
  Strain = |score − 3|

Rainfall (precipitation / humidity)
  Ideal  3  Temperate / balanced
  Worst  0  Arid desert
  Worst  5  Rainforest / torrential
  Hourly. Dry air and a torrent both work
  the body. 0 also thirst (prolonged).
  Strain = |score − 3|

Human Density (settlement)
  Ideal  4–5  Towns and cities. Roads,
              inns, a gate, made ways
              out of the pale.
  Worst  1  Sparse / isolated — no road
            worth the name
  0  Uninhabited. Empty, not always harder
     than 1. No one to rob you. No one to
     house you.
  5  Cities. Ideal on the approaches.
     Congestion only inside the
     settlement itself (section 3).
     Not Hard ground.
  Infrastructure (section 2):
    Density 0–1    +1 Hourly Strain
    Density 2–3     0
    Density 4–5    −1 Hourly Strain
                   (roads outside the pale)

Flora (plant cover)
  Ideal  3–4  Moderate to abundant. Shade,
              browse, no wall of green.
  Worst  0  Barren. Prolonged: no fuel,
            no browse, no cover.
  Worst  5  Teeming / overrun. Hourly:
            you push through it.
  Hourly strain: 5 → +2, 4 → +1 if the
  GM names thicket or thorn. 0 → none
  this hour.
  Prolonged: 0 is the long knife.

Fauna (animal life)
  Ideal  3  Moderate / common
  Worst  0  Barren. Prolonged: no game.
  Worst  5  Teeming. Worse as risk and
            noise than as a march tax.
            Not Flora 5. You do not wade
            through beasts every hour.
  Hourly strain: none unless the hour is
  a named encounter.
  Prolonged: 0 starves a hunter. 5 is
  bites, night-noise, lost stock.

Topography (elevation / steepness)
  Ideal  0  Flat plains
  Worst  5  Sheer mountains
  Hourly. This is the spine of Hard ground.
  Strain = score
  (0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 as written).

Hydrology (water features)
  Ideal  3  Rivers / inlets / channels
  Worst  0  None / parched
  Worst  5  Flooded / swamps / wet coast
  0 is thirst and dust (prolonged, and
  hourly if you are already dry).
  5 is underfoot every hour.
  Hourly strain = |score − 3|

Height terms (highland, plateau, lowland)
  Not a 0–5 score. Not in this sum.
  They name where the region sits, not
  how steep the next mile is. Topography
  already has the steepness.

--------------------------------------------------
2. HOURLY GROUND BAND
--------------------------------------------------

Sum Hourly Strain:

  Temperature     |T − 3|
  Rainfall        |R − 3|
  Topography      Tscore
  Hydrology       |H − 3|
  Flora           +2 if 5, +1 if 4 and thick
  Human Density   +1 if 0 or 1
                   0 if 2 or 3
                  −1 if 4 or 5
                   (roads and highways
                   outside the gates)
  Floor the sum at 0.

  Band            Sum     Fatigue hour     Combat
  Easy            0–2     Light Active     normal yards
                          or Active,
                          by Enc only
  Fair            3–4     Active           normal
  Hard            5–6     Strenuous        Difficult
                                           ground
  Severe          7–9     Strenuous        Difficult
                          and the GM may   ground;
                          add −10 to the   no Run
                          Fatigue test
  Extreme         10+     Intense          Difficult
                                           ground;
                          the land is      half again
                          the fight        if the GM
                                           names scree,
                                           waist-flood,
                                           or ice

Worked check
  Mild vale: T3 R3 Dens3 Fl3 Fa3 Top1 Hyd3
  Strain = 0+0+1+0+0+0 = 1  Easy

  Same vale on town roads, Dens 4:
  Strain = 1 − 1 = 0  Easy

  Kannavald-type wooded highland, wet:
  T2 R4 Dens1 Fl4 Fa3 Top3 Hyd3
  Strain = 1+1+3+0 +1 thicket +1 isolated = 7
  Severe

  Same highland on a made road, Dens 4:
  Strain = 1+1+3+0 +1 thicket −1 = 5
  Hard. The turnpike is the reward.

Difficult ground (Combat) is Hard or worse.
Easy and Fair do not halve yards.

--------------------------------------------------
3. TRAVERSAL (THE DAY’S MILES)
--------------------------------------------------

Combat already names Hampered / Standard
for the travel day. Apply after Ground Band.

  Human Density 4–5   Ideal for a road day.
                      Made ways around a city
                      count as 5 still ideal.
  Inside a Density 5
  settlement           congestion. Cut yards
                      and the hour’s miles
                      while in the streets,
                      gates, and crowd. Not
                      a Strenuous test. Step
                      outside the pale and
                      the cut ends.
  Human Density 0–1   no inn, no made road.
                      Hampered unless a known
                      path is in play.
  Ground Band Hard+   Hampered on top of Enc.

Do not double-count Topography in both the
hourly test and a second secret tax. The
Band is the tax. Miles follow the Band.

--------------------------------------------------
4. PROLONGED (NOT THE HOUR)
--------------------------------------------------

These do not change the hourly Fatigue test
by themselves. After a day or more they
become the problem:

  Flora 0, Fauna 0    nothing to eat or burn
  Hydrology 0         nothing to drink
  Temperature 0 or 5  exposure, then death
  Rainfall 0          same as thirst
  Human Density 1     no help when something
                      breaks

Log them as the day’s story, Outdoor
Survival, or an Agent. Not a second
Toughness test each hour.

Fauna 5 is encounters and lost sleep,
not Flora 5’s wall of green.

--------------------------------------------------
5. OCEANIC
--------------------------------------------------

Do not feed oceanic 0–5 into section 2.
Depth and sea-state are not hills.
Ship Fatigue and storm hours wait on a
later sea file. Until then: Hydrology
(sea state) 4–5 is Intense for crew on
deck. Calm is not a work hour.

--------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT THIS FILE DOES NOT DO
--------------------------------------------------

It does not score HSI.
It does not invent a eighth axis.
It does not replace biome names.
Season still happens in Geography_Rules
before you sum.
