# Core_Fear.txt
# Fear, Shock, snapping out, Disorders.
# Trait: Core_Traits.txt §12.
# IP is stored on Characteristics §3d.
# This file spends it. It does not invent
# a second madness track.

--------------------------------------------------
0. FEAR (X)
--------------------------------------------------

Rating is 1–4. The sheet names it.

  1  Disturbing     +0
  2  Frightening    −10
  3  Horrifying     −20
  4  Terrifying     −30

First sight this scene: Willpower test
at that penalty. Success: act.
Fail in a fight: Shock immediately.
Add +10 to the Shock roll per Degree
of Failure.
Fail outside a fight: −10 on any test
that needs concentration, while they
remain in sight of it. Stepping out
and back does not clear it.

A Fear test outside a fight is the
GM's call. It is not mandatory.

  1  A bad murder. A light that should
     not be there. A voice. A statue
     that weeps.
  2  Someone you know is dead, and they
     are standing there. Buried alive.
     A False Moon storm.
  3  The man beside you dies wrong.
     Fangs and tentacles in a mass.
     The dead up across a country.
  4  An unprotected look into the
     Immaterium.

--------------------------------------------------
1. SHOCK
--------------------------------------------------

1d100, +10 per Degree of Failure.

  01–20
    Startled. Next Turn: one Half
    Action only. Then normal.
  21–40
    Shakes. −10 on all tests for the
    Encounter unless they snap out.
  41–60
    Backs away. Will not approach the
    source. Otherwise acts. −10 on
    all tests for the Encounter.
  61–80
    Frozen. No Actions until they
    snap out. After that, −10 on all
    tests for the Encounter.
  81–100
    Flee. As fast as the ground allows.
    If they cannot leave: Half Actions
    only, −20 on all tests. Once clear,
    snap out to regain control.
  101–120
    Faints. Unconscious 1d5 Rounds.
    After: −10 on all tests for the
    Encounter.
  121–130
    Retching 1d5 Rounds. Drops what
    is held. Nothing else. After the
    Encounter: one Half Action a Turn
    until a Short Rest.
  131–140
    Laughs and attacks whatever is
    near, with whatever is in hand.
    Lasts until they snap out or are
    knocked unconscious.
  141–160
    Down. Weeps, gibbers, tears at
    their own flesh for 1d5+1 Rounds.
    After: −20 on all tests until the
    Encounter ends.
  161–170
    Catatonic 1d5 hours. Cannot be
    roused.
  171+
    Heart. Toughness +0.
    Success: catatonic 1d5 hours.
    Fail: dead.

--------------------------------------------------
2. SNAPPING OUT
--------------------------------------------------

Where the row says so: at the start
of their next Turn, Willpower +0.
Success: that Shock ends. They act.
Fail: it holds. Try again next Turn.
A −10 that the row keeps for the
Encounter is not snapped off.

--------------------------------------------------
3. INSANITY
--------------------------------------------------

IP is the running total. Characteristics
§3d. A Fail of 4 or more Degrees on a
Fear test: when the Encounter ends,
Willpower +0. Fail that: 1 Disorder,
and +1 IP. A pass gains no Disorder.
IP thresholds beyond this are not written.

--------------------------------------------------
4. DISORDERS
--------------------------------------------------

1d100 when section 3 says so. One.
A second roll that hits the same row
worsens it. It does not grant a twin.

  01–10   Night twitch
    Sleep is broken. A Short Rest
    removes 1 Fatigue, not 2, if the
    rest was meant to be sheltered.
  11–20   Fixed stare
    −10 Fellowship on Charm. +10
    Intimidate against anyone who
    holds the look.
  21–30   Name-avoidance
    Will not speak the thing's kind.
    −10 on Common Lore and Academic
    tests about it.
  31–40   Clean hands
    Must wash before a meal or a
    Heal. If they cannot: −10 on
    the next test that uses the hands.
  41–50   Light hunger
    Dim light is a threat. −10
    Willpower in the dark unless
    they have a flame in sight.
  51–60   Ear to the wall
    Hears the keening when it is
    not there. Awareness −10 in
    quiet places. +10 if something
    is actually sounding.
  61–70   Meat refusal
    Will not eat flesh that day
    after the dream. One missed
    meat meal is a Fatigue test
    at +0 if the march was Active.
  71–80   Counted steps
    Doors, bridges, thresholds.
    First action through one is
    −10 unless they pause a Half
    Action to count.
  81–90   Blade comfort
    A weapon must be in hand to
    sleep. Without it: no Fatigue
    cleared by that sleep.
  91–97   False Moon watch
    On a False Moon night, −10
    on all tests until dawn unless
    they are under a roof they trust.
  98–00   Open
    The GM names a tic that matches
    the thing they saw. Write it.
    It is this Disorder, not a second
    roll.
