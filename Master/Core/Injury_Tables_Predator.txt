# Injury_Tables_Predator.txt
# Virath Slot injury tables — pawed predators
# Status: DRAFT for Core.
# Wallace and Slots live in this file.
# Law: Core_Injury.txt (Bleed, Overflow, how to roll)
# Use for anything on paws: wolf, hound, great
# cat, gryphon. Not hoofed. Not horse-type.

Roll 1d10 + Overflow − TB.
Floor 1. 10 or more uses the 10+ row.
1 is best. 10+ is worst.
Use the column that matches the blow.

Head and Body rows do not cap Wounds unless
a row says otherwise. Paw amps may cap.

A predator that cannot stand on three legs
is Down. Four-leg collapse is Helpless.

Order: Head, Body, Fore, Hind.
Paw is shared. Wing is Body when tagged.

WALLACE (first d100)
  01–12    Head              12%
  13–52    Body              40%
  53–66    Right Fore        14%
  67–80    Left Fore         14%
  81–90    Right Hind        10%
  91–00    Left Hind         10%

==================================================
EYES
==================================================

Side presented. From behind use SKULL.

CUT
  1   Lid nick. Dress it.
  2   Lid split. −10 Perception until bound.
  3   Canthus opened. Minor Bleed. Globe spared.
  4   Globe grazed. −20 Perception that side
      until Heal. Minor Bleed.
  5   Cornea laid open. Blind that side until
      Surgery. Minor Bleed.
  6   Lid flayed. Eye shut. Major Bleed.
  7   Globe sliced. That eye gone unless
      Surgery this hour. Permanent. Major Bleed.
  8   Orbit opened. Blind that side. Permanent.
      Major Bleed. Down unless Toughness.
  9   Both orbits. Blind. Catastrophic Bleed.
 10+  Face through the sockets. Blind.
      Catastrophic Bleed. Toughness or dying.

PIERCE
  1   Grit in the eye. −10 Perception 1 Round.
  2   Lid transfixed.
  3   Beside the globe. Minor Bleed.
  4   Orbit, globe intact. Stunned. Minor Bleed.
  5   Into the white. Heal this hour or the
      eye is gone. Minor Bleed.
  6   Globe burst. Blind that side. Permanent.
      Major Bleed.
  7   Through the orbit. Blind that side.
      Major Bleed.
  8   Deep orbit. Brainpan threatened.
      Toughness or unconscious. Major Bleed.
  9   Into the cranium. Catastrophic. Down.
 10+  Both sockets. Dead unless Fate.

BLUNT
  1   Brow bang.
  2   Lid hematoma. −10 Perception.
  3   Globe shocked. Agility or it flinches
      that Turn.
  4   Orbital rim cracked. No Bleed.
  5   Globe contused. Blind that side 1d10
      hours. Stunned.
  6   Orbit smashed. Permanent −10 Perception
      unless Surgery.
  7   Globe burst in the socket. Blind that
      side. Permanent.
  8   Both rims gone. Down.
  9   Skull and orbit together. Unconscious.
 10+  Head staved at the eye. Dead unless Fate.

      unless held now.

 10+  Throat taken through. Dead.

      Toughness this Turn (then Major).

  9   Through the throat into the chest.

 10+  Spike through the neck. Dead.

      Cannot rake or grip.
