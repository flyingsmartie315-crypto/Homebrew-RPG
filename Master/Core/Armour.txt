6. CLOTHING (0 AC)
--------------------------------------------------

None layer (against the skin; one only)
  Linen shirt & hose     5 sc   Enc 3
  Wool tunic & hose      8 sc   Enc 5    +10 Toughness vs cold
  Silk shirt & hose     25 sc   Enc 2    +10 Fellowship at court

Outdoor (over kit; may stack with sense)
  Hooded cloak          15 sc   Enc 8    +10 Concealment in
                                         shadow; +10 T vs rain
  Surcoat / tabard      18 sc   Enc 6    heraldry: +10 Fel or
                                         Intimidate among one’s
                                         own
  Short cloak           10 sc   Enc 5
  Fur-lined mantle      30 sc   Enc 12   +20 T vs cold; −5 Fel
                                         in heat
  Leather jerkin        22 sc   Enc 10   +5 Concealment
  Embroidered robe      35 sc   Enc 7    +15 Fel at court or
                                         shrine

Good / Best clothing: +5 / +10 Fellowship, or +10
Concealment, not AC.

People of Virath wear what the climate and the hall
demand. Do not use Calradian culture lists.

7. TEXTILE
--------------------------------------------------

Models below. Sleeves, hem, and collar are Form.

Torso Models
  Aketon            10 sc  Enc 8    2/1/2   wanted under plate
  Gambeson          15 sc  Enc 10   3/1/3   standalone; not
                                            the plate
                                            under-layer
  Arming Doublet    12 sc  Enc 8    2/1/2   always Long Sleeves.
                                            Required under
                                            Modular on Arms.
                                            Also legal under
                                            Body plate.
  Pourpoint         18 sc  Enc 8    3/2/3   wanted under plate

  Default on every torso Model (100% Coverage)
    Chest / Upper Back
    Abdomen / Lower Back
    Collarbone / Base of Neck

  Form — Sleeves
    None            no arm Slots
    Short Sleeves   Shoulder 100%, Upper Arm 50%
    Long Sleeves    Shoulder, Upper Arm, Elbow,
                    Forearm 100%
  Default Short if unsaid.
  Arming Doublet is always Long. Enc 8 already
  includes that step.

  Form — Hem (H Slots, not trouser Slots)
    Cropped Hem     Groin / Pelvis, Glutes / Sacrum,
                    Hip / Buttock 100%
    Short Hem       those same 95%, Thigh 50%
    Long Hem        those same 95%, Thigh 80%,
                    Knee 80%
    Robe Hem        Groin / Pelvis, Glutes / Sacrum,
                    Hip / Buttock 100%,
                    Thigh 95%, Knee 95%, Shin 95%
  Default Short Hem if unsaid.

  Form — Collar (Textile only)
    None            no throat Slot
    Collar          Throat 50%
    High Collar     Throat 75%,
                    Lower face / Back of neck 50%
  Default None. Mail has no Collar Form.

  Hem and dedicated legs (chausses, hose, winingas)
  may both be worn. Their AC does not stack.
  Use the higher column. Do not add them.

  Form Enc: +3 per raised sleeve, hem, or collar
  step. Round Enc to 5. Form never changes AC.

--------------------------------------------------

Gaps are the point. Coverage is not 100%.
Same Modular layer as solid plate. Slots decide mix.

Torso Models (minimum Form: front only)

  Model            Cost Enc    AC      Cov   Slots
  Coat of Plates   45 sc  60   5/6/4   50%   Chest, Abdomen
  Laminar          50 sc  55   5/5/4   40%   Chest, Abdomen
  Cuir Bouilli     40 sc  50   4/4/5   40%   Chest, Abdomen
  Brigandine       48 sc  58   5/5/4   50%   Chest, Abdomen

  Iron grade (Coat of Plates and Brigandine only).
  Laminar and Cuir Bouilli do not take this table.
  Cost is per 5% Coverage and replaces the listed
  Model cost. Enc and AC are modifiers on the
  listed Model.

  Grade              Cost/5%   Enc    AC
  Wrought iron         3 sc     +5    −1/−1/−1
  Low-carbon steel     4 sc      0     0/ 0/ 0
  Hardened steel       5 sc     −5    +1/+1/+1

  Example: Coat of Plates, 50% is ten steps.
  Wrought: 30 sc, Enc 65, AC 4/5/3.
  Low-carbon: 40 sc, Enc 60, AC 5/6/4.

--------------------------------------------------
11. BOOTS
--------------------------------------------------

Always cover the foot. Leg % is height.
Soft boots take the Foot slot only as clothing
weight unless a Modular material is added.
Cuir bouilli or splinting on a boot is Modular
(segmented) on Foot. Greaves on a boot are
Modular (solid) on Shin.

Types
  Turnshoe           5 sc  Enc 10   1/1/1   Leg 15%
  Ankle boot         8 sc  Enc 12   2/1/1   Leg 15%
  Riding / cuffed   12 sc  Enc 15   2/1/2   Leg 40%
  Poulaine          10 sc  Enc 12   1/1/1   Leg 15%
  Clog / slipper     4 sc  Enc 10   1/2/2   Leg 15%

Materials (add to type)
  Leather            +2 sc   +0 Enc   +0/+1/+0
  Suede              +3 sc   −2 Enc   +0/+0/+0
  Felt & leather     +1 sc   +3 Enc   +0/+0/+1
  Padded             +4 sc   +3 Enc   +1/+0/+1
  Cuir bouilli      +10 sc   +5 Enc   +2/+2/+3   Modular
  Splinting          +8 sc   +4 Enc   +1/+1/+2   Modular
  Greaves           +12 sc   +6 Enc   +2/+2/+1   Modular

Layer over Textile legs or under plate greaves.
If the outer boot/greave applies, inner Textile
legs apply at full AC (section 3).
