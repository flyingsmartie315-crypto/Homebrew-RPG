9. MODULAR — SEGMENTED
--------------------------------------------------

Gaps are the point. Same Modular layer as solid
plate. A piece tagged to a Slot is 100% of that
Slot unless the line says otherwise.

Iron Grade
  One table. Listed cost, Enc, and AC on an
  eligible Model are Low-carbon.
  Apply Grade % to listed cost and Enc.
  Then apply the AC shift to the listed AC.

  Grade              Cost     Enc      AC
  Wrought iron       ×0.80    ×1.15    −1/−1/−1
  Low-carbon steel   ×1.00    ×1.00     0/ 0/ 0
  Hardened steel     ×1.25    ×0.85    +1/+1/+1

  Eligible
    Coat of Plates
    Brigandine
    Scale
    Splinted (including Jack chains)
    Laminar
    Solid Plate (torso Plate and its iron
    Forms, limb Models, iron helmets)

  Not eligible
    Cuir bouilli (torso or limb)
    Maille
    Textile
    Clothing
    Boot Base Material

  Stack order: Model listed → Material %
  (Splinted / Laminar / Cuir on limbs) →
  Iron Grade % and AC (if eligible).
  Cost may be listed as sc.bc. Do not round Enc.

Material (segmented). Listed Model cost and
Enc are Splinted (Low-carbon). Material
applies % and AC. Splinted and Laminar then
may take Iron grade. Cuir may not.

  Material       Cost     Enc      AC
  Cuir bouilli   ×0.70    ×0.70    2/2/3
  Splinted       ×1.00    ×0.90    3/3/3
  Laminar        ×1.15    ×1.25    4/4/5

Laminar is overlapping iron bands. Best soak,
most mass.
Splinted is iron strips on a foundation.
Lighter than laminar. Worse blunt (gaps
between strips). Takes iron grade.
Cuir bouilli is boiled leather. Lightest.
Lowest AC. No iron grade.

Example: Splinted Bracers. 24 sc, Enc 22
(24 × 0.90), AC 3/3/3, Forearm 95%.
         Hardened Splinted Bracers. Cost 30,
         Enc 20, AC 4/4/4.
         Laminar Bracers. 28 sc, Enc 30, AC 4/4/5.
         Cuir Bracers. 17 sc, Enc 17, AC 2/2/3.
Jack chains are Splinted only, listed 10 / 10,
then iron grade if wanted.

--------------------------------------------------
11. BOOTS
--------------------------------------------------

A listing is a pair. Enc is the pair.

Models have no AC. Base Material is mandatory
and is the Textile layer. Its Coverage matches
the boot’s Slots 1:1. It applies cost, Enc,
and AC.

Armour Material is optional and only legal on
Cuffed boots or Riding boots. It is always
the Modular layer on the listed Slots, even
when the piece carries the Textile keyword
for Upgrades. It does not occupy the Textile
track. The boot’s Base Material already does.

Models

  Slots: Foot
  Base cost: 5 sc
  Base Enc: 5
  Note: pair.

  Slots: Foot
  Base cost: 8 sc
  Base Enc: 10
  Note: pair.

Armour Material is illegal on Turnshoes,
Ankle boots, Poulaine, and Clog / slipper.
Those Models have no Shin / Calf tag. You
cannot invent tibia Coverage on a Foot-only
boot. Cuffed and Riding only.

Plate greaves on the shin still sit as
their own Modular piece. They do not stack
AC with boot greaves on the same Slot.
