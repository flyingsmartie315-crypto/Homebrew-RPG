8. MAILLE
--------------------------------------------------

Mail Models list Slots and Forms. They do not
list AC. Material is Riveted or Butted.
This layer is Maille. Scale lives with
Modular Segmented.

Material (Mail only)

  Material    AC      Cost        Enc
  Riveted     4/4/4   listed      listed
  Butted      2/2/2   listed ×0.6 listed ×1.2

Round cost to 1 sc. Round Enc to 5 after
Forms, except voiders (round with the kit).
Riveted is the combat baseline. Butted is
cheaper, heavier, worse soak.

Sleeves and Hem use the Textile Form list
and the same Enc % modifiers. No Collar
Form. Default Short Sleeves and Short Hem
if unsaid. Mail hem and mail chausses may
both be worn. Higher column only.
