# Injury_Tables.txt
# Virath Slot injury tables — Cut / Pierce / Blunt
# Status: DRAFT for Core.
# Law: Core_Injury.txt (Bleed, Overflow, how to roll)
# Companion: Core_Combat.txt, Armour_Rules.txt

Roll 1d10 + Overflow − TB.
Floor 1. 10 or more uses the 10+ row.
1 is best. 10+ is worst.
Use the column that matches the blow.

A Slot with no table here still uses
Core_Injury.txt §4z.

==================================================
HAND
==================================================

CUT (Slicing / Laceration)

  1   Minor Cut. Superficial split. Dress it.
      No Bleed.
  2   Skin Laceration. Deep skin split
      requiring stitches. −10 to tests with
      that hand until bound.
  3   Fleshy Flay. Laid open to the muscle
      fascia. Minor Bleed.
  4   Tendon Laceration. Tendon nicked or
      partially severed. −10 Agility tests
      using that hand. Minor Bleed.
  5   Deep Flaying. Fingers split to the
      tendons. Drop what it held. −20 tests
      with that hand until treated with a
      Heal test. Minor Bleed.
  6   Volar Laceration. Palm sliced open to
      the bone. Drop held items. Major Bleed.
  7   Functional Severance. Deep tendons
      destroyed. Permanent −20 with that
      hand unless fixed via Surgery.
      Major Bleed.
  8   Sub-Total Amputation. Thumb or multiple
      digits left hanging by a flesh bridge.
      Grip is a permanent disability without
      Surgery. Major Bleed.
  9   Clean Amputation. Sliced clean off at
      the wrist joint. Max 1 Wound inflicted.
      Permanent disability. Catastrophic Bleed
      (vessels remain wide open).
 10+  Messy Amputation. Hand violently torn
      or hacked away. Max 1 Wound inflicted.
      Permanent disability. Major Bleed
      (Difficult to Control due to ragged
      tissue, extreme infection risk).

PIERCE (Puncture / Ballistic)

  1   Minor Puncture. Clean entry prick.
      Dress it.
  2   Webbing Pierced. Through the soft web
      between fingers. −10 to hand usage
      until bound.
  3   Flesh Channel. Deep muscle puncture.
      Minor Bleed.
  4   Joint Pierced. Nail bed or finger joint
      capsule transfixed. −10 with that hand.
      Minor Bleed.
  5   Transfixing Wound. Palm completely
      pinned through. Instantly drop held
      item. Minor Bleed.
  6   Through-and-Through. Point exits the
      back of the hand, ripping soft tissue.
      Major Bleed.
  7   Shattered Metacarpals. Deep structural
      bones of the hand split or broken by
      the point. −20 to all tests until
      Surgery. Major Bleed.
  8   Palmar Arch Breach. Deep arterial
      network inside the palm opened.
      Major Bleed (Difficult to Control).
  9   Destructive Cavitation. Point or
      ballistic force blows the core of the
      palm entirely out. Permanent disability.
      Major Bleed.
 10+  Blast Disarticulation. Hand burst
      entirely off the wrist by concentrated
      kinetic puncture. Max 1 Wound inflicted.
      Permanent disability. Catastrophic Bleed.

BLUNT (Crush / Fracture)

  1   Bruised Knuckles. Soft tissue
      contusion. Swear.
  2   Crushed Nail. Subungual hematoma.
      Severe throbbing pain; −10 to tests
      until seen to.
  3   Nerve Concussion. Digital nerves
      shocked. Fingers go numb; drop held
      items on a failed Agility test.
  4   Simple Fracture. Single finger bone
      cracked. −10 to tasks using that hand.
      No Bleed.
  5   Multiple Fractures. Two or more fingers
      broken. Drop held item. −20 to tests
      until bound.
  6   Broken Hand. Metacarpal cluster
      crushed. Hand cannot grip or support
      weight. No Bleed.
  7   Crushed Palm. Severe soft tissue mash
      with stable bones. Permanent −10 to
      hand usage unless repaired via Surgery.
      Target is Stunned for 1 Round.
  8   Shattered Carpal Cluster. Bones of the
      palm / wrist turned to splinters.
      Permanent disability without immediate
      Surgery. Target is Stunned.
  9   Traumatic Crush Amputation. Hand
      mangled into unviable, hanging pulp.
      Permanent disability. Minor Bleed
      (vessels completely flattened /
      occluded).
 10+  Crush Detachment. Hand completely
      destroyed or sheared off by heavy,
      structural pressure. Max 1 Wound
      inflicted. Permanent disability.
      Major Bleed.

EYES
==================================================

Facing: from behind this Slot is Occipital.
Use SKULL / OCCIPITAL instead.

CUT (Slicing / Laceration)

  1   Brow nick. Blood in the eyebrow.
      Dress it. No Bleed.
  2   Lid laceration. −10 Perception until
      bound. Looks worse than it is.
  3   Canthus opened. Minor Bleed. Eye
      spared.
  4   Globe grazed. −20 Perception on that
      side until Heal. Minor Bleed.
  5   Cornea laid open. That eye is a
      blur until Surgery. Drop what needs
      depth. Minor Bleed.
  6   Lid and cheek flayed. One eye shut.
      Major Bleed.
  7   Globe sliced. That eye is gone unless
      Surgery this hour. Permanent −20
      Perception if it fails. Major Bleed.
  8   Both lids ruined, one globe opened.
      Blind on that side. Permanent.
      Major Bleed.
  9   Across the orbits. Both eyes ruined.
      Blind. Max 1 Wound inflicted.
      Catastrophic Bleed.
 10+  Face opened through the sockets.
      Blind. Max 1 Wound inflicted.
      Permanent disability. Catastrophic
      Bleed. Toughness now or Down,
      Helpless.

PIERCE (Puncture / Ballistic)

  1   Grit in the eye. −10 Perception
      1 Round. Dress it.
  2   Lid transfixed. −10 Perception until
      bound.
  3   Beside the globe. Flesh channel.
      Minor Bleed. Eye spared.
  4   Orbit pierced, globe intact. −20
      Perception that side. Minor Bleed.
      Stunned 1 Round.
  5   Through the lid into the white.
      That eye is failing. Heal this hour
      or it is gone. Minor Bleed.
  6   Globe burst on the point. Blind that
      side. Permanent. Major Bleed.
  7   Through the orbit toward the sinus.
      Blind that side. Stunned. Major Bleed.
  8   Deep orbit. Brainpan threatened.
      Blind that side. Toughness or
      unconscious. Major Bleed (Difficult).
  9   Through the eye into the skull.
      Blind that side. Down, Helpless.
      Catastrophic Bleed.
 10+  Point through the orbit into the
      brain. Dead unless Fate. Catastrophic Bleed.

BLUNT (Crush / Fracture)

  1   Black eye. Swear. No Bleed.
  2   Swollen shut. −10 Perception until
      the swelling falls.
  3   Orbit bruised. Double vision. −10
      WS and BS until treated.
  4   Broken orbital rim. −10 Perception.
      No Bleed.
  5   Globe compressed. That eye is a
      smear until Heal. Stunned 1 Round.
  6   Orbit cracked, globe intact. −20
      Perception that side. Stunned.
  7   Globe burst in the socket. Blind
      that side. Permanent without
      Surgery. Minor Bleed.
  8   Both orbits smashed. Blind. Down.
      Stunned. Minor Bleed.
  9   Face stove in at the eyes. Blind.
      Permanent. Down, Helpless.
 10+  Front of the skull collapsed through
      the sockets. Dead unless Fate.

SKULL / OCCIPITAL
==================================================

Face-to-face: Skull (vault, forehead).
From behind: Occipital (nape of the skull).
Same rows. Describe the right bone.

CUT (Slicing / Laceration)

  1   Scalp nick. Dramatic blood. Dress it.
      No real Bleed.
  2   Scalp laceration. −10 Fellowship
      until bound. Looks fatal. Is not.
  3   Galea opened. Minor Bleed. Bone
      not showing.
  4   To the bone. Scalp flap. Minor Bleed.
      Stunned 1 Round.
  5   Periosteum scored. Drop concentration.
      −10 all tests 1d5 Rounds. Minor Bleed.
  6   Skull scored, no crack. Major Bleed
      from the scalp. Stunned 1 Round.
  7   Through the scalp into a crack.
      Down. Stunned 1d5 Rounds. Major Bleed.
  8   Vault opened. Brain matter threatened.
      Down, Helpless until Heal. Major Bleed
      (Difficult).
  9   Calvarium laid open. Unconscious.
      Catastrophic Bleed. Toughness each
      Turn or die.
 10+  Skull cap lifted or hewn. Dead unless Fate.

PIERCE (Puncture / Ballistic)

  1   Glancing off the vault. Ringing.
      Dress the bruise.
  2   Stuck in the scalp. −10 until pulled
      and bound.
  3   Against the bone, no entry. Minor
      Bleed. Stunned 1 Round.
  4   Outer table chipped. −10 all tests
      1d5 Rounds. Minor Bleed.
  5   Through the outer table. Down.
      Stunned. Minor Bleed.
  6   Depressed puncture. Unconscious 1d10
      minutes. Major Bleed.
  7   Through the vault. Brain bruised.
      Down, Helpless. Major Bleed.
      Toughness or a seizure this hour.
  8   Deep cranial puncture. Unconscious.
      Major Bleed (Difficult). Heal this
      hour or dying.
  9   Transcranial. Catastrophic Bleed.
      Down, Helpless. Toughness now or
      dead.
 10+  Through-and-through the brainpan.
      Dead unless Fate.

BLUNT (Crush / Fracture)

  1   Bounced off. Sees stars. No Bleed.
  2   Concussed. −10 all tests 1 Round.
  3   Ringing skull. Stunned 1 Round.
      −10 until the hour ends.
  4   Linear fracture. Stunned 1d5 Rounds.
      No Bleed. Heal or the headache stays.
  5   Down. Cannot stand this Round.
      −20 all tests until treated.
  6   Depressed fracture. Unconscious 1d10
      minutes. No Bleed yet.
  7   Vault cracked. Down, Helpless until
      Heal. Permanent −10 Int unless
      Surgery.
  8   Comminuted vault. Unconscious.
      Permanent injury without Surgery.
      Minor Bleed from ear or nose.
  9   Skull collapsed. Unconscious,
      Helpless. Catastrophic Bleed inside
      the vault. Toughness each Turn or
      die.
 10+  Head crushed. Dead unless Fate.

LOWER FACE / BACK OF NECK
==================================================

Face-to-face: Lower face (jaw, mouth, nose).
From behind: Back of neck (nape, cervical
soft tissue). Rows that name a jaw become
the nape from behind; rows that name the
spine only apply from behind.

CUT (Slicing / Laceration)

  1   Split lip or nape nick. Dress it.
  2   Cheek or nape opened. −10 Fellowship
      until bound.
  3   Through the cheek / thick nape.
      Minor Bleed. Talks badly.
  4   Nose or ear flapped; from behind,
      nape to the ligament. Minor Bleed.
  5   Jaw flesh opened to the bone; from
      behind, nuchal strap cut. −10 Speak
      and Fellowship, or −10 Agility.
      Minor Bleed. Drop concentration.
  6   Mouth corner to the jaw hinge; from
      behind, nape laid to the spine.
      Major Bleed. Cannot speak clearly.
  7   Jaw half severed / nape to the
      vertebrae. Permanent −20 Fellowship
      or −20 Agility unless Surgery.
      Major Bleed.
  8   Lower face hanging; from behind,
      cord threatened. Down. Major Bleed
      (Difficult).
  9   Face off the bone / nape opened on
      the spine. Max 1 Wound inflicted.
      Permanent disability. Catastrophic
      Bleed. Airway is a problem now.
 10+  Head half taken from the jaw or
      nape. Dead unless Fate.

PIERCE (Puncture / Ballistic)

  1   Through the cheek or a graze at
      the nape. Dress it.
  2   Pinned lip or nape skin. −10 until
      pulled.
  3   Through the cheek out the other
      side; nape flesh channel. Minor Bleed.
  4   Through the tongue or into a
      cervical muscle. −10 Speak. Minor
      Bleed. Gag or flinch.
  5   Into the jaw hinge or against a
      cervical vertebra. Stunned 1 Round.
      Minor Bleed.
  6   Through the floor of the mouth /
      deep nape. Airway noisy. Major Bleed.
  7   Into the face bones / toward the
      canal. −20 Perception or Agility.
      Major Bleed. Toughness or Down.
  8   Behind the jaw into the great
      vessels, or nape toward the cord.
      Major Bleed (Difficult). Down.
  9   Through the face into the skull
      base, or through the nape into the
      canal. Catastrophic Bleed. Helpless.
 10+  Point out the far side of the neck
      or face. Dead unless Fate.

BLUNT (Crush / Fracture)

  1   Fat lip or stiff nape. Swear.
  2   Loose teeth or a cricked neck. −10
      Speak or Agility until seen to.
  3   Broken nose / wrenched nape. −10
      Perception or Agility. Eyes water.
  4   Cracked jaw / strained cervical.
      −10 Speak. No Bleed.
  5   Jaw broken in one place / cervical
      sprain that drops the gaze. Drop
      held item if it needed the mouth
      or the eyes. Stunned 1 Round.
  6   Jaw broken in two; from behind,
      cannot turn the head. Cannot bite
      or shout. No Bleed.
  7   Face bones smashed / cervical
      fracture without cord. Permanent
      −10 Fellowship or Agility unless
      Surgery. Stunned. Down.
  8   Mandible ruined / neck broken and
      they still breathe. Permanent
      disability without Surgery.
      Helpless. Minor Bleed.
  9   Face caved; from behind, cord
      bruised. Down, Helpless. May not
      stand. Toughness or the breath
      shallows.
 10+  Jaw torn off or neck snapped.
      Dead unless Fate.

THROAT
==================================================

From behind this is Back of neck. If the
blow was from behind, use LOWER FACE /
BACK OF NECK instead.

CUT (Slicing / Laceration)

  1   Razor line. Skin only. Dress it.
      The room goes quiet.
  2   Platysma opened. −10 Fellowship.
      Minor look, no Bleed yet.
  3   To the muscle. Minor Bleed. Voice
      rough.
  4   Strap muscles cut. −10 Toughness
      tests to shout or march. Minor Bleed.
  5   Close to the pipes. Voice a whisper.
      Minor Bleed. Heal or it stays hoarse.
  6   Jugular territory. Major Bleed.
      Cannot hide it. Stunned 1 Round.
  7   Larynx opened. Cannot speak. Airway
      noisy. Major Bleed. Heal this hour
      or drowning in blood.
  8   Carotid spray. Catastrophic Bleed.
      Down. Control it this Turn or the
      clock starts.
  9   Throat opened across. Max 1 Wound
      inflicted. Catastrophic Bleed.
      Airway gone unless someone holds
      it. Dead in minutes without that.
 10+  Head half off. Dead unless Fate.

PIERCE (Puncture / Ballistic)

  1   Grazed the collar line. Dress it.
  2   Into the muscle beside the pipes.
      −10 Speak until bound.
  3   Flesh channel through the side of
      the neck. Minor Bleed.
  4   Against the larynx. Cannot shout.
      Minor Bleed. Stunned 1 Round.
  5   Through a strap into the space
      beside the vessel. Minor Bleed.
      Toughness or Down from the shock.
  6   Through-and-through the side of
      the neck, pipes spared. Major Bleed.
  7   Larynx or trachea pierced. Airway
      failing. Major Bleed. Heal now or
      start drowning.
  8   Vessel on the point. Catastrophic
      Bleed. Down. Difficult to Control.
  9   Through the great vessels. Max 1
      Wound inflicted. Catastrophic Bleed.
      Dead in minutes without control.
 10+  Point out the far side through the
      pipe and the vessel. Dead unless Fate.

BLUNT (Crush / Fracture)

  1   Sore throat. Swear. No Bleed.
  2   Bruised larynx. −10 Speak 1d5 Rounds.
  3   Winded at the neck. Stunned 1 Round.
      Cannot shout.
  4   Crushed cartilage, still open.
      −10 Toughness. Voice broken.
  5   Collapsed note. Cannot speak. Must
      pass Toughness to take a full
      breath each Round until Heal.
  6   Larynx broken. Silent. Airway
      narrow. No Bleed. Heal this hour
      or it swells shut.
  7   Hyoid or trachea smashed. Down.
      Permanent voice loss without
      Surgery. Stunned.
  8   Neck crushed, cord spared. Helpless.
      Airway is a Heal test now. Minor
      Bleed inside.
  9   Throat stove in. Cannot breathe
      without aid. Down, Helpless.
      Toughness each Round or die.
 10+  Neck pulped. Dead unless Fate.
