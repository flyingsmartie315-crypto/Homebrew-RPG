# Shield_Rules.txt
# Virath shields — held cover. Not a Slot. Not a Layer.
# Status: DRAFT for Core.
# Companion files:
#   Core_Combat.txt                     Parry, Actions, shooting
#   Weaponry.txt                        Defensive, Blunt profiles
#   Armour_Rules.txt                    soak after overflow
#   Core_Injury.txt                     overflow lands on the Slot

PURPOSE
A shield is held. It has an AC rating and a weapon
profile. It does not use Slots. It does not fill
Textile, Maille, or Modular.

KEYWORDS

  Held
    Occupies one hand. That hand cannot hold another
    weapon or the other end of a two-handed weapon.
    Drop or sling (Half) to free the hand.

  Defensive(x)
    While the shield is in a hand:
      Free Parry once per Round (no Stance).
      That Parry may be used against shooting.
      +x to the Parry test.
    Weapon Defensive without (x) stays +10 Parry
    and does not catch shots (Weaponry.txt).

  Shield AC
    Cut / Pierce / Blunt on the shield. Used only
    when a Parry with that shield succeeds.

  Overflow
    Incoming minus Shield AC. What remains is a
    hit on the wielder at the Slot already named
    by the two location dice. Worn layers and NAC
    then soak as Armour_Rules §5. If Incoming is
    ≤ Shield AC, the wielder takes nothing.

  Bash
    Every shield is a Blunt weapon in that hand.
    Use the shield’s weapon line. Reach 0.5 unless
    the line says otherwise.

LAW

  1. A shield requires a hand. No hand, no AC,
     no Defensive(x), no Bash.

  2. Parry with a shield is Opposed WS + (x)
     against the attacker’s DoS, Combat §6.
     Shooting Parry uses the same test against
     the BS hit’s DoS.

  3. A won shield Parry does not delete the blow.
     The blow hits the shield. Roll damage as
     normal (1d10 + SB + type + DoS). Compare to
     Shield AC on the matching column. Overflow
     goes to the wielder.

  4. A lost Parry: the blow never met the shield.
     Soak is worn layers only, Armour_Rules §5.

  5. One Parry per Round still. Shield or weapon,
     not both on the same blow. Dodge still cannot
     pair with that Parry.

  6. Enc counts on the kit. Slung on the back is
     Enc only. No Defensive(x), no AC, no Bash.

  7. Craftsmanship and Iron Grade do not apply
     unless a later line says so.

Type on the incoming blow picks the Shield
AC column. A thrust uses Pierce. A cut uses
Cut. A bash uses Blunt.
