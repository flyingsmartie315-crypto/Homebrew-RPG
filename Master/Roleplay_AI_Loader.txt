=== MASTER FILE INDEX (Full Repository) ===

=== ECONOMY FILES ===

Economy_Rules.txt
Core rules of Virath’s economy and currency system. Covers the three-tier coinage (gc/sc/bc), barter vs coin use, social class attitudes toward money, debasement, and guidelines for roleplaying economic interactions.

Economy_Labour.txt
Daily wage and economic value guidelines for all major categories of work. Includes Common Labour (rural & urban), Skilled Hazardous Labour, Skilled Trades, and Specialists, with definitions, example occupations, and scaling multipliers.

Economy_Primary_Goods.txt
Base prices for raw primary commodities (grain, meat, ore, timber, fibres, etc.) measured primarily per kilogram. Forms the foundation of the mundane economy.

Economy_Processed_Goods.txt
Prices for goods refined or manufactured from primary materials (beer, wine, iron, steel, leather, textiles, tools, etc.), including typical markups and common trade lots.

Economy_Livestock.txt
Purchase prices, upkeep costs, space requirements, and primary uses for common livestock, beasts of burden, and war mounts.

Economy_Unveiling_Goods.txt
High-risk, high-value materials harvested from Unveiling monsters and phenomena (venom, chitin, troll hide, veil-drop metal, etc.), including black-market considerations and specialised buyers.

Economy_Unveiling_Beasts.txt
Economic and logistical treatment of rare, intelligent, or supernatural creatures (Gryphons, Royal Gryphons, Pegasus, Direwolves, River/Swamp Trolls, etc.). Covers theoretical prices, extreme upkeep, bonding rules, and why most cannot be treated as normal livestock.
