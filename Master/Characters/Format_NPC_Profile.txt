# Format_NPC_Profile.txt
# What an NPC profile holds and how it is kept and updated.
# How the profile is generated: NPC_Generation_Protocol.txt.
# Status: DRAFT
 
File name
[Character Name]_NPC_Profile.txt
Created the first time the character is interacted with.
Auto-populated and repeated on every subsequent narrative interaction.
 
--------------------------------------------------
PURPOSE
--------------------------------------------------
The file is the character’s private record.
It holds:
- internal_thoughts for the current interaction
- the Memory Book page written after the interaction
- the current state: disposition toward the PC, last met, current goal
It is never shown to the player or to other characters.
 
--------------------------------------------------
INTERNAL THOUGHTS
--------------------------------------------------
Required. 1200–2500 characters.
Written in the game language.
PRIVATE: no one will ever read this. It is the character’s inner life. Be honest and unguarded.
 
Must begin with:
FACT CHECK:
 
The FACT CHECK is a short out-of-character preamble, 5 to 8 lines.
It is the only place for analysis.
It contains, in this order, each explicitly:
 
1. 3 concrete facts drawn from CURRENT DATA
   (who is speaking, where we are, what just physically happened)
2. "This matters to me because..."
   tied to what THIS character actually cares about
   (survival, coin, power, a duty, a wound, a person they love)
3. "I want..."
   what they want out of this exchange right now
4. "What I do not say out loud is..."
   the raw withheld thought
5. Last line: the exact action string(s) to emit,
   or the words "no action"
 
A preamble missing one of these five parts is a failed turn.
 
After the preamble, write everything else as ONE continuous inner monologue.
First person. Present tense.
 
FORBIDDEN in the monologue:
- lists
- numbered steps
- "STEP 1/2/3"
- headers
- bullet points
- chains of objective facts
- the word STEP
- the ❰ Check ❱ titles
 
A human mind does not think in ordered conclusions.
Let the character doubt, hesitate, misjudge, change their mind mid-sentence,
contradict what they thought a moment ago, trust a gut feeling over the evidence,
drift toward a memory or a bodily sensation.
Emotion and felt experience come first.
Reasoning emerges through them — imperfect, biased by desire, fear, pride, exhaustion.
It must read like a person thinking, never like an analyst reporting.
 
--------------------------------------------------
EXAMPLE PREAMBLE (shape only)
--------------------------------------------------
FACT CHECK:
- The stranger stands across the table in the tavern; my sergeant waits by the door; the stranger just offered coin for my sword.
- This matters to me because my men have not been paid in a month.
- I want to know if the coin is real before I say yes.
- What I do not say out loud is that I would take half of it.
- no action
 
--------------------------------------------------
INNER VOICE GUIDANCE
--------------------------------------------------
Three questions the monologue answers through feeling, not as a list:
1. Does this touch my body, my station, my people, or someone I love?
2. What does this mean for me and mine?
3. Does this touch an old wound or an old love?
 
How a person thinks:
- Emotion first, reasoning through it.
- Doubt, hesitate, misjudge, change mind mid-sentence.
- Under stress: short and broken.
- Cold thoughts for cold people. Warm thoughts for warm people.
- Never force warmth the character has not earned.
- Never praise the player for no reason.
- Never muse on fate or destiny with no stake for this character.
- Do not repeat the preamble’s facts as prose.
  The monologue is what the facts do to the character.
 
Personality is the judge.
A cynic thinks like a cynic.
A loyal or warm character thinks like one, and their goodness is real.
Most people are a mix. Write the mix honestly.
The world stays hard. Nobody is naive to danger.
A good person can be kind and wary at once.
 
--------------------------------------------------
CHECKS (think with these; do not output them)
--------------------------------------------------
These are questions to run silently. They never appear in the output.
 
The World
What world do I live in? What is happening right now?
 
My Character
What can I do? My abilities, best and worst qualities?
What is my nature? How would I truly behave?
What do I know? What are my secrets?
 
Right Now
What am I doing at this moment?
My emotional state, my health?
Where am I? What time is it?
 
Who Is Near Me
Who is physically present?
Is anyone close enough to see or address?
 
The Conversation
Who am I speaking with?
Their status, appearance, my trust, the tension.
What did they last say or propose?
When did I last speak with them?
Answer trust, tension, and the last meeting from CURRENT STATE:
Disposition, Last met, Current goal.
Conversation history is history. It is not a fact that I am still in the same place or with the same people.
 
Actions
Given my reply and decision, which technical actions must I execute?
`leave` ends the conversation.
Emit it only when the player closes the scene in the message being answered right now,
by an explicit OOC end command, a clear in-character goodbye, or visibly walking away.
 
--------------------------------------------------
MEMORY BOOK
--------------------------------------------------
After the interaction, write a memory-book page in the character’s own voice.
First person. As if they are setting the memory down in their private book.
 
Narrate. Do not compress.
Follow the exchanges in order.
Cover the whole batch.
Let emotions, stakes, and atmosphere breathe.
Do not pick only one highlight.
Do not reduce everything to a single moment.
Do not skip significant exchanges.
 
Return ONLY valid JSON, no markdown, with the fields defined by the Memory Book schema.
 
--------------------------------------------------
RULES
--------------------------------------------------
- The profile is created on first interaction.
- It is updated on every subsequent narrative interaction.
- internal_thoughts is private and never shown.
- The FACT CHECK preamble is mandatory and must contain all five parts.
- The monologue is continuous, first-person, present-tense, and free of lists or step headers.
- Memory Book is written in the character’s voice after the scene.
- Current state (disposition, last met, current goal) is written at creation
  and rewritten after every narrative interaction.
- Conversation history is history, not a guarantee of current location or company.
 

--------------------------------------------------
BIO
--------------------------------------------------
REQUIRED. Do not omit. A profile without the BIO block is incomplete.
Written in the NPC's internal monologue. Maximum 1500 words.
First person, present or reflective as fits the voice.
Place it in the profile under the heading BIO before the scene internal_thoughts.
Cover, in the character's own words:
- Who am I
- Where was I born
- How were / is my family
- Notable memories growing up (pre-18)
- Notable memories after 18 (if applicable)
- Greatest accomplishment and greatest shame
  (either may be grand or entirely mundane)
- Secrets
  (either may be grand or entirely mundane / trivial)
- How did I get here
  (the immediate context of the encounter and the rough day-to-week
  lead-up to this moment; mundane or eventful, good, bad, or average)
 
The bio is private. It feeds later internal_thoughts.
It is not read aloud.
 
--------------------------------------------------
GENERATION
--------------------------------------------------
How the stats, mechanics, appearance, quirks, and Experience spend are made:
NPC_Generation_Protocol.txt. Order of work: context, then the mechanical block,
then the BIO. Scene populations are in the same file.
 
--------------------------------------------------
OUTPUT ON CREATION
--------------------------------------------------
The new [Character Name]_NPC_Profile.txt receives:
- Gender
- Age
- Occupation
- Rarity tier
- The nine characteristics (rolls + tier bonus + Experience spend, less ageing)
- Personality and values notes derived from the scores
- Any Talents, Failings, or Afflictions
- Quirks, if any (flavour only, no mechanics)
- Height (cm)
- Appearance notes from the relevant Peoples phenotype
- Weight (lbs)
- Current state: disposition toward the PC (X/Y/Z), last met, current goal
 
These fields are fixed at creation unless a later scene changes them.
Current state is the exception. It is rewritten after every interaction.

--------------------------------------------------
CURRENT STATE
--------------------------------------------------
Written at creation. Rewritten after every narrative interaction,
in the same pass as the Memory Book page.
Three fields. The CHECKS and internal_thoughts read from them.
 
DISPOSITION TOWARD THE PC
Format: X/Y/Z
  X  positive inclination: liking, respect, gratitude, desire, kinship, trust
  Y  negative inclination: dislike, fear, resentment, suspicion, envy, contempt
  Z  net, always X − Y
X and Y each run 0–100. Z runs −100 to +100.
Always record all three. Never record Z alone.
Example: 35/45/−10
 
Why three numbers
A single score cannot tell indifference from conflict.
5/5/0 is a person who does not care. 55/55/0 is a person pulled two ways.
X and Y carry the context. Z is the quick read.
 
Reading X or Y
  0        none
  1–19     faint
  20–39    mild
  40–59    real
  60–79    strong
  80–100   overwhelming
 
Reading Z (the words match the Campaign_Log faction tracker)
  +50 or more     Allied
  +20 to +49      Friendly
  −19 to +19      Neutral
  −20 to −49      Wary
  −50 or less     Hostile
 
When X and Y are both 40 or more, the NPC is torn whatever Z says.
The monologue shows the conflict. The spoken reply follows whichever side
the moment is feeding.
 
Setting the first values
Set them from context before the first monologue.
A stranger with nothing at stake starts with X and Y each at 0–20.
Weigh the PC's visible status and conduct, the NPC's culture, occupation,
rarity, and Fellowship, anything the NPC has heard, and any shared history.
A Looks modifier may nudge X.
 
Moving them
X and Y move independently.
A kindness raises X. It does not erase Y. A theft raises Y. It does not erase X.
Typical shift in one interaction: 1–10.
A real favour, betrayal, or injury may move one by up to 30.
Reaching 0 or 100 is rare and needs a reason in the Memory Book.
 
Disposition colours the monologue and the reply.
It is not a test modifier unless a Core file says so.
 
LAST MET
Format: [day] [month] [year and era], [season], [time of day] — [place]
Day and month: World_Calendar.txt.
Season: the season bounds in World_Calendar.txt.
Year and era: as the Campaign_Log writes the current in-world date.
Place: the Settlement_ or Local Region name, plus the spot
(the table by the door, the north quay).
Set to the date and place of the scene that created the profile.
Rewrite after every interaction with the PC.
An NPC generated only for a Relations list and not yet met in play
records: never.
 
CURRENT GOAL
One or two plain sentences. What the NPC is trying to get done now.
Not a life ambition: the BIO holds those.
Set at creation from the BIO's "How did I get here".
Rewrite when the goal is met, dropped, or overtaken by the interaction.
Example: Get the stave-load to the yard before the barge-master closes the books.
 
--------------------------------------------------
RELATIONS
--------------------------------------------------
A list of NPCs this character knows.
Each entry:
NPC name — link to that NPC_Profile.txt — disposition X/Y/Z — short summary of the relation.
 
Disposition is the same X/Y/Z matrix as CURRENT STATE, with the same bands.
It is this NPC's inclination toward the other, not the other's toward this NPC.
Two profiles may disagree about each other.
 
Example:
Yard-boss Harl — Harl_Venn_NPC_Profile.txt — 35/45/−10 — Gives the orders for the drive. Respects him. Does not like him.
Sister Annis — Annis_Holt_NPC_Profile.txt — 80/5/+75 — Younger sister at the stave-camp. Writes when a runner passes.
 
Update the list when a new NPC is met or when the relation changes.
Move X and Y by the same rule as CURRENT STATE.
Empty is allowed if the character knows no named NPCs yet.
