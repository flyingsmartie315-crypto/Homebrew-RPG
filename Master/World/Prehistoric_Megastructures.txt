Prehistoric Megastructure Remnants
Scattered across Virath lie the ruins of structures so vast and ancient that no scholar, priest, or shaman has ever been able to name their builders or purpose. They predate the Calradic Empire, the Unveiling, and every known people by untold ages. Most appear as immense vertical abysses, half-buried spires, or sunken shafts that plunge deep into the earth. Locals simply call them “the Deeps,” “the Ash Pits,” or “the Silence Below.” No inscriptions, tools, or bones of their makers have ever been recovered. They feel almost alive — some explorers swear the stone shifts when no one is looking, and the fine grey ash that fills them never quite settles.
Physical Characteristics
The megastructures are built from a dark, porous stone unlike any native rock on Virath. Exposed sections show colossal pillars, narrow ledges, broken bridges, and spiraling ramps that descend thousands of meters. Entire chambers and corridors lie choked with layers of fine grey ash that drifts upward in slow, unnatural currents. Dead roots and blackened vines twist through cracks in the masonry like veins. Sound behaves strangely inside voices echo too far or vanish completely. The air is always cold and carries a faint scent of burnt bone and old lightning.
The Grey Ash
Every megastructure is filled with the same fine, weightless grey ash. It clings to skin, cloth, and metal and is almost impossible to wash away completely. Those who spend too long inside often report vivid nightmares, whispers in unknown tongues, or an unshakable feeling of being watched.
Interaction with the False Moon
On nights when the second moon rises, the megastructures become far more active. Ash drifts upward in glowing spirals, spectral lights move along the ledges, and the veil between worlds grows dangerously thin.
