# Theology_Purpose.txt
# How religion is written for Virath, and how it is kept from becoming a costume of Earth.

WHAT THIS FILE IS
The lock for all later Faith_ and Rite files.
Peoples files describe who a stock is. Geographic and Customs files describe how they live on a given floor.
Theology files describe what they hold the world to be, what they owe it, and what they do when the veil thins.
Do not copy liturgy into a Peoples file. Point to a Faith_ file and keep only the local colour in Customs.

--------------------------------------------------
THE PROBLEM THIS LAYER SOLVES
--------------------------------------------------
Peoples documents stall without a working account of rite, taboo, afterlife, and the False Moon.
They also stall if that account is a renamed Earth church. A reader who can name the source will stop believing the hall.

The Immaterium does not require clerical correctness.
It requires mass, duration, and heat.
Four inherited currents left a print in the Multitude. Those prints are GM-only substrates.
Living churches are Dark-Age rebuilds: scraps of law, scraps of miracle, scraps of wave-myth, and the night the second moon rose.
Names, offices, and ethics have been broken and reset. Do not print Earth-names in a hall-book.

--------------------------------------------------
FOUR TESTS FOR EVERY LIVING FAITH
--------------------------------------------------
1. Internal consistency
   Cosmology, ethics, taboo, and rite must answer one another. A people who hold that desire feeds the Night Face cannot also run a feast of excess as a high holy act without calling it a controlled burn or a heresy.

2. Authenticity of use
   Faith must do work. It must decide who may cut a grove, who may hang a skinwight, who may speak over a grave, who keeps the house on a False-Moon night, who is owed hospitality, who is clean after childbirth, who may marry across stock.
   If a doctrine never reaches the door-latch, it is flavour text. Cut it or ground it.

3. Cultural consequence
   Each major faith must generate at least one custom, one conflict, and one institution that would still exist if the GM meta were deleted.
   Stewardship versus extraction, image versus measure, cooling versus ordering, wound-keeping versus sealing — these are usable quarrels.

4. Unrecognisability without meta
   A reader who has never seen World_Fundamentals.txt should not be able to point at a rite and say the Earth name.
   Forbidden tells: a crucified-and-risen saviour; a single named last prophet with a closed book; a wheel of rebirth ending in extinguishment; a caste-dharma with recognisable avatars.
   Permitted residues: blood as bond; law as worship; cooling of appetite; stations of a Work. These are human, not proprietary.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META VERSUS HALL SPEECH
--------------------------------------------------
GM META (never spoken in a 16th-century hall)
- The Immaterium is reactive. Thought, fear, desire, and disciplined belief take form.
- Negative heat (fear, hatred, despair, cruelty) coalesces into entities that feed by spreading the same heat and thinning the barrier.
- Positive heat (disciplined faith, collective devout belief) can raise protective entities, interventions, and forms the faithful name as gods or saints.
- The Multitude carried four faith-masses large enough to stain the Immaterium. Aperti count, not a living census:
  Nazarene current ~29–31%
  Submission current ~24–26%
  Middle Path current ~15%
  Eternal Law current ~6–7%
- Those currents are substrates. They colour how miracles tend to look. They are not churches.
- The Unveiling was a partial barrier failure. The False Moon is a symptom. No living culture knows this mechanism.
- Entities recognised as gods can form. Whether a named Power in a Faith_ file has coalesced is a locked GM decision in that file.

HALL SPEECH
Speak only in the faith’s own terms: Work, Measure, Wound, Stillness, Seal, Trust, Night Face, True Moon, Openers, First Blood, Raisers.
Miracle is rare, feared when it is not institutional, and argued over when it is.
Most people live by custom and weather. Theology is for priests, oath-takers, and nights when the second moon hangs.

Peoples_[Name].txt may carry a short block:

  FAITH AND RITE
  Dominant: [Faith_Name]
  Minority / tolerated: [list]
  Taboo overlap: [grove / iron / image / measure / ash]
  False-Moon house-rule: [one line]
  Detail: Faith_[Name].txt and the local Customs file.

Do not invent a fifth world-religion to cover a gap. First ask whether an existing faith takes a local face.

--------------------------------------------------
NAMING LOCK
--------------------------------------------------
In-world names are locked once a Faith_ file is written.
GM substrate names (Nazarene, Submission, Middle Path, Eternal Law, and any Earth word) stay in GM META blocks only.
Liturgical high-tongue may use worn Latin fragments where Pionari or Ordinari stocks keep them. That is ruin-speech, not Church-Latin as a tell.

--------------------------------------------------
WHAT THEOLOGY IS NOT
--------------------------------------------------
Not a complete afterlife atlas. Most halls have a working answer, not a proof.
Not a spell list. Miracle is not a skill tree.
Not a morality score for player characters. A faith judges; the table records what the hall would say.
Not an excuse to reprint Earth catechism with the serial numbers filed off.

--------------------------------------------------
NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
Write Faith files in the same voice as Peoples files: locked names, shared claim, relations, branches, GM META separated.
Campaign_Log records schisms, condemned rites, and whether a named Power has been seen to act in the present age.
The_Unveiling.txt remains the phenomenon file. Theology interprets it. It does not replace it.
