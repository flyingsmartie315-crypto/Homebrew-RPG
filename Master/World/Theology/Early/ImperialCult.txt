WHAT THIS FILE IS
The working sheet for one contemporary faith derived from the Wound-current.
Founder physics stay in Faiths_Founders.txt.
This file holds what an imperial hall recites, what orders do, and which crises it claims to have closed.

Do not print Earth-names in a hall-book.
Do not call a saint a god or a goddess.
Do not lock the other six pillars until each has a historical life, a heat, a cost, and a living order.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META
--------------------------------------------------
Parent current: Wound.
Braid in this offshoot: Wound spending, with a Still discipline inside the grave-orders.
Chief form: one Real, remembered as the Vessel / the Spent One / the Opened.
Imperial halls add titles. Titles are claims. They are not extra gods.

Saints are documented persons. Recitation, crisis, and duration can densify them into keeper-forms in the Immaterium.
Same physics as walkers. Opposite diet.
Whether a named saint has coalesced is locked per saint, below.

The empire’s name, capital, and reach are not locked in this file.
A state church that spans many stocks must later be checked against Peoples and Geography.
Syllvani plateau-hold and Kannavi wood-law will not accept this cult as native without a conquest, a bargain, or a refusal.

Hall-doctrine is not GM truth.
If the cult says souls return to a High Source, that is what priests teach.
The_Unveiling.txt still governs what the veil actually does.

--------------------------------------------------
PILLAR FIVE — THE WARDEN
--------------------------------------------------

SAINT
Liturgical name: Phylax.
Office: Warden of the committed dead.
Core spending: knowing when to stop living, and keeping others to that measure.
Imperial school-word: Temperance.
Heat faced: the gluttony of years — refusal to return one’s share of life.

HALL CLAIM
Phylax was a yard-priest, not a prelate and not a rider.
He kept a remote churchyard. He had no title worth a chronicle until the yard was attacked.
He tended poor dead with the same care as named dead.
He guarded ground against takers of flesh.
When a stitched walker and its cult came to harvest his graves, he did not run.
He had already declared the yard sacred ground — committed, watched, closed to taking.
He met the thing at the threshold.
He spent what years he still had, and his blood, to hold that line.
The walker fell inert at the gate. One entity. Witnessed.
The cult could not raise the dead under his charge.
The survivors who saw it became the first company that later fought the heresy down.
The empire named the holding a miracle and the man a keeper.

GM META — WHAT IS LOCKED
- The man existed. The yard existed. The spending was local.
- One stitched walker fell at the declared threshold. Witnessed.
- The buried in that yard were not raised. That refusal is the second half of the miracle.
- Survivors of the attack became the seed of the later force and order. The crisis was fended off by that company and its heirs, not by a province-wide collapse of walkers.
- A keeper-form now answers recitation at committed ground if the rite is kept and paid for. Narrow. Costly. Not artillery.

GM META — WHAT IS NOT LOCKED
- The scholar’s personal name and full life. Marked for a later character file.
- How much pre-landing text the scholar truly had. Aperti residue may leave words and diagrams that do not behave. It does not leave operable landing-age engines.
- Whether Phylax’s keeper-form is as dense as the capital claims.

--------------------------------------------------
THE CRISIS — GLUTTONY OF YEARS
--------------------------------------------------
HALL SPEECH
An Aperti-descended scholar of medicine, cutting, and the study of dying looked at the thin places and saw more years instead of a door-risk.
He joined harvested flesh to harvested flesh and sealed the seams with ambient wrong-heat.
The thing that stood did not eat bread. It kept moving by taking the warmth of living bodies and of occupied ground nearby — breath, blood-heat, the ordinary life of a street or a yard.
Dying lords and merchant princes bought the method.
Private cults spread in towns that could pay.
Uncommitted dead woke as doors.
Whole quarters threatened to become a feast of rot that would not end.

PHYSICS (GM)
This is Wound-doctrine made visible.
The scholar did not invent a foreign magic. He fed the Face that was already in the buyers: terror of the grave, appetite for a second share of life.
Stitched walkers are leak-things with extra craft.
They are not a fifth species.
Calling them flesh-golems is permitted as a chronicle slur. Hall liturgy should say walker, stitched dead, or uncommitted door.

The scholar’s Aperti descent is flavour and blame, not proof that Openers caused the Unveiling.
Keep the line thin. One household, banned books, a method that works just enough to damn a city.

--------------------------------------------------
THE SPENDING
--------------------------------------------------
What Phylax spent
His remaining years. His blood. His post.
He did not spend a speech or a title.

What the spending did
It held one declared threshold.
One walker fell there and did not rise.
The charges already committed in that yard stayed dead.
It did not drop walkers elsewhere.

What followed in history
Witnesses stayed.
They formed the first band that knew a committed yard could be kept.
That band, grown and copied, is what eventually broke the heresy’s back.
The saint is the threshold and the unraised dead. The war is the survivors.

What the empire made of it
A pillar.
A proof that the dead have a measure and that exceeding it is treason against occupied ground.
A licence to put yards under military rule.

HARD-WOUND READING
The buyers were already halfway Faces. Hanging them was mercy to the town.
SOFT-WOUND READING
Some buyers can turn, restore the bodies they took, and live under ration.
The Wardens tend Hard. Town priests may tend Soft. The capital uses both as policy.

STILL BRAID
The yard went still.
That is not the whole of Phylax. He spent; he did not only withdraw.
The living order keeps an ascetic cold so that appetite finds no seat inside the walls.
Name that cold as borrowed Walk-discipline, or the order will drift into a second church.

--------------------------------------------------
AFTERLIFE — HALL ONLY
--------------------------------------------------
The cult teaches: the dead are to be committed, named once, watched, and given back.
“Given back” means returned to the Real’s keeping, not kept as a second life among the living.
Do not write a mapped country of the dead in this file.
Do not write a ferry across a named river.
Phylax is warden of the crossing as a duty, not as a boatman-god.

An uncommitted body is a door.
A poorly watched funeral is a small Unveiling.
A private vault bought to cheat the measure is the old heresy in clean stone.

--------------------------------------------------
LIVING ORDER — WARDENS OF THE SHROUD
--------------------------------------------------
Place
Yards are not open fields where the veil is thin.
They are walled, ditched, barred with cold iron: crypt-garrisons.
The order lives inside the necropolis.
Care of the dead is treated as a siege that does not lift.

Work
- Receive the body.
- Enrol the name once.
- Commit the ground.
- Watch through the first nights, longer on False-Moon.
- Cool patchy ground with still-rite and spent comfort.
- Refuse unlicensed cutting of the buried.
- Treat an uncommitted or stolen corpse as an open door and a crime.

Ration
The monks keep a hard ration of food, sleep, and speech.
The point is not holiness-as-brand.
The point is a house so poor in appetite that a year-glutton finds nothing to sit in.

Door-latch law the order forces on a town
- No body left above ground after the set hours.
- No midwife-crowd in a yard.
- No paid vault that bypasses the common commitment.
- No calling of a name after the enrolment.
- False-Moon nights: yards locked, extra watch, no new bargains with the dead.

Cost of cheap invocation
If a town recites Phylax to bless a war-pit or to hide a bought immortality, the rite is spectacle.
Spectacle feeds the heat the pillar claims to have closed.
The file treats that as how the heresy returns.

--------------------------------------------------
WHAT THIS PILLAR DOES TO OTHER STOCKS
--------------------------------------------------
Isern marks already hang leak-things. They will understand the Hard reading and resist foreign monks on their hanging-trees.
Plateau Trust-halls will accept commitment of dead to named ground and reject imperial monopoly of the yard.
Measure-halls will call Phylax-icons a partner-face and may smash them while still wanting the doors closed.
Hollow floors will pay for a decent burial and ignore the college.
