# Theology_Rules.txt
# Table-facing rules for faith, miracle, and the Immaterium.
# Phenomenon remains The_Unveiling.txt. This file only says how to run consequence.

PRINCIPLE
Most scenes are harvest, debt, weather, and honour.
Faith is loaded when a taboo is crossed, a False Moon hangs, a grave is wrong, a grove is cut, an oath is spoken, or a player asks what the hall believes.

--------------------------------------------------
FAITH-MASS (GM ONLY)
--------------------------------------------------
An entity, saint-form, or lasting miracle requires three things in combination:
- Number: many minds, not a lone enthusiast.
- Duration: years of the same heat, not a night’s panic.
- Discipline or intensity: liturgy, terror, or both.

A village curse can be real and small.
A named god of a continent is a locked decision in that Faith file, not an improvisation.

Positive heat tends toward protection, closure of leaks, clean light, held oaths, healed land.
Negative heat tends toward appetite, contagion, wrong faces, thinning, and the wish to be fed again.

The four inherited substrates bias the *shape* of what forms. They do not force Earth doctrine.

--------------------------------------------------
MIRACLE AT THE TABLE
--------------------------------------------------
Miracle is not a skill.
When a rite is performed under fitting conditions (right faith, right heat, right night or place), the GM may allow an intervention.
Interventions are narrow: a leak stills, a wound closes, a watch holds, an animal returns to true behaviour, a lie tastes of ash.
Interventions that flatten a war or raise the dead as policy are setting-breaking unless a Faith file has locked that Power as coalesced and costly.

Cost is the point. Blood, silence, years, sterility of a grove, a name given up, a night without fire.
If there is no cost, it was luck or craft.

False-Moon nights make both protection and predation easier. They do not make priests into artillery.

--------------------------------------------------
THE DEAD
--------------------------------------------------
Each Faith file states a working hall-answer.
Common risks if the local answer is ignored:
- Uncommitted bodies become doors.
- Loud, prolonged heat at a grave feeds the wrong attention.
- Naming the dead too often, or not at all, both have local prices.

This is not a unified afterlife. It is a unified danger: the veil is thin around death.

--------------------------------------------------
ABOMINATIONS AND SKINWIGHTS
--------------------------------------------------
Treat them as theological categories in hall speech, not as a separate religion.
Iron Oath and Kept Wound tend to hang or burn.
Green Trust tends to unname and refuse the floor.
Still Walk tends to withdraw and cool the site.
Single Measure tends to deny them any face, including a martyr’s.

Do not give them a church unless Campaign_Log records one, and then treat it as a negative-heat cult, not a peer faith.

--------------------------------------------------
HERESY AND SCHISM
--------------------------------------------------
Useful quarrels, already seeded:
- Keepers of the Wound versus Sealers of the Wound.
- Measure versus any use of faces, icons, grove-offices, or saint-forms.
- Trust versus extraction.
- Walk versus watch.
- Listeners of the Ash versus wardens of the Deeps.

A schism should change a custom (who may marry, who may cut, who eats first, who keeps the False-Moon house) or it is only an argument in a loft.

--------------------------------------------------
CALENDAR
--------------------------------------------------
Holy time attaches to the four seasons and to Lorn, never to the False Moon as a feast.
Suggested attachments, to be locked per Faith file:
- Thaw / Budmoon: first-cut, return of true green, oath-renewal.
- Highsun: watch, craft-offering, measure of weights and bounds.
- Harvest / Reaping: debt, bread, the body’s share, enrolment of the year’s dead.
- Longnight / Deepcold: vigil, silence, kept fire, no new bargains.

False-Moon nights are dated on the ordinary calendar and treated as closures, not festivals.

--------------------------------------------------
WHAT PLAYERS KNOW
--------------------------------------------------
A character knows the house-rules of their stock and the insults of their neighbours.
A character does not know substrates, faith-mass percentages, or that gods are condensates.
Learned Syllvani may have a longer memory of First Blood and a colder theory of the tear. That is still myth, not Aperti mechanics.
