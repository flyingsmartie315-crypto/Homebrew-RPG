LOCKED IN THIS PASS
- The Spent One is a real human life and the Real at once. The spending counts because a will was in it.
- The personal name stays lost. The manner of the spending stays lost.
- A mortal mother is permitted. A mother-office is not.
- No narrated childhood. No companions. No instrument. No third-day return. No fetched father.
- The father is a champion who fell against the perils of the Unveiling, outside the birth-window.
- The younger wore his likeness. The floor called the younger the hero’s. The gap is not closed.
- The brother is the eldest. Hall titles: the Witness, the Chronicler.
- He is the first saint, and the embodiment of humility.
- The Spent One is not himself a saint. Saints are later joinings. The eldest sets the pattern.
- His witness is house-witness, not the Opener use of “Witnesses.”
- The chronicle omits. It does not save. Bread joins the body. The page does not.
- Pillar One of the College is the empty seat this saint may fill. Not filled in the Imperial file yet.

STILL OPEN

- Which of the five Dark Age endings a given hall recites. The house does not finish the tear.

DO NOT DRIFT
- Lorn is not a god. The False Moon is not feasted.
- Faces are human wickedness made dense, not an invading species.
- The spending holds. It does not finish.
- Measure may call the likeness and the book a partner-face. That attack stays available.
