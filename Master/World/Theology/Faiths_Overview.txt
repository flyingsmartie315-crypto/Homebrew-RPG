WHAT THIS FILE IS

Until a Faith file exists, this overview is the crutch.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — HOW THE FOUR CURRENTS SURVIVE
--------------------------------------------------
They do not survive as four churches.
They survive as habits the Immaterium still answers:

- Wound-current (Nazarene stain): meaning in suffering; a body that stands for many; blood and bread as bond; a tear that saves by remaining open or by being kept.
- Measure-current (Submission stain): the Real admits no partner; image and second face are danger; law is the worship; community of the bound.
- Still-current (Middle Path stain): heat of wanting feeds what hangs in the night; holiness is cooling; attention is a tool; withdrawal can be piety.
- Station-current (Eternal Law stain): the world is a Work with offices; a man out of station sours the floor; many local faces are functions of one order, not a parliament of gods.

A living church usually braids two currents with one wave-myth and one Unveiling answer.
That braid is why a reader cannot point and name the Earth source.

No culture knows the stain. Priests argue from miracle, custom, and old words.

--------------------------------------------------
THE UNVEILING PROBLEM EVERY FAITH MUST ANSWER
--------------------------------------------------
On a windless night a second moon rose. The world did not end. It became less certain.
Every serious faith answers four hall-questions:

1. What is the False Moon?
2. What may be done on the nights it hangs?
3. What are the things that walk when the veil is thin — enemy, test, hungry kin, or leak?
4. What happens to the dead, and what must the living do so the dead do not become a leak?

If a draft faith cannot answer those four in one page, it is not ready for a Peoples file.

LESSER AND LOCAL FORMS
--------------------------------------------------

LORNSWARD / TRUE-MOON WATCH
Not a church. A practice layer almost every northern hall keeps.
Lorn is the proper light. The False Moon is not dated and not feasted.
House-rules on False-Moon nights (iron, salt, covered wells, no midwifery if it can wait) sit in Customs files.
Do not promote Lorn to a sun-god. It is the true satellite and a clean light.

ASH AND THE DEEPS
Megastructure cults and anti-cults.
The Deeps, Ash Pits, and Silence Below feel alive. Ash clings. Nightmares speak in unused tongues. On False-Moon nights the ash climbs.
Two opposed local answers:
- Sealers and wardens: the Deeps are a wound-mouth. No descent without leave. Ash is buried, not worn.
- Listeners: the ash is speech. A few Aperti-scented cells treat descent as the old Question.
Neither is a world-religion. Both generate disappearances, banned relics, and settlement law.

OPENER CELLS
Tiny. Hunted, hidden, or misnamed.
Three postures, not three churches:
- Closers — the tear can be shut if the old readings are recovered.
- Witnesses — the Question is still owed; looking is piety.
- Reverents — the tear is the true event; to heal it is to starve the world of miracle.
Most halls meet these as rumour, a household, or a diagram that does not behave.

FOLK TRUSTS
Household spirits, field-wards, drowned-name offerings, first-cut bread, hanging-trees.
These are not “the fourth current.” They are how a floor stays livable.
When folk practice hardens and draws miracle, decide whether it has joined a major faith or become a named local face of the Work / the Wound / the Measure.
