# World_Fundamentals.txt
# Basic planetary and civilisational overview of Virath

WORLD NAME
Virath

PRIMARY MOON
Lorn (the true moon)

SECONDARY CELESTIAL BODY
The False Moon (appears irregularly; see The_Unveiling.txt)

SIZE
Pole-to-pole distance (surface meridian): 15,360 miles.
Equator circumference: 27,300 miles.
Larger than Earth, still a human-habitable terrestrial world.
Pole-to-pole here is the surface run from pole to pole, not the through-axis diameter.

AXIAL TILT
24.5 degrees.
Engineered in the deep past by Ingenari machines in collaboration with the Pionari (see THE DEEPER PAST). Inhabitants do not know this.
Seasons are a little sharper than Earth's 23.4° tilt. Still four seasons: Thaw, Highsun, Harvest, Longnight.

PLANETARY CHARACTER
Virath is a terrestrial world broadly comparable to Earth in gravity, atmosphere, and temperature range. Humans can live and thrive across most of its surface without extraordinary aid. Seasons, weather, and day length function in familiar ways.

Despite this familiarity, Virath contains far more extreme topography and climate zones than a typical settled world:
- Vast, near-endless tundras and polar wastes
- Immense deserts of sand, stone, and salt
- Sheer, near-impassable mountain ranges
- Dense, lightless jungles and old-growth forests that resist mapping and settlement
- Deep, cold oceans with abyssal trenches

These extremes shape culture, trade routes, warfare, and the practical limits of empire.

GEOLOGY
Virath is a cooling crust. In the deep past it was violently active: rifting, stacking, magma, and a sky that still threw stone. That age carved the cave-nets (old lava tubes, emptied chambers, karst) and raised the extreme fault-walls. When the Pionari first worked the world, most continents had already settled. Isolated arcs remain live. Dauðavík is one. The Ebon Spire is not. The Bowls leak; they do not erupt.
The Ebon Spire is the tallest peak on Virath: an inactive black shield volcano, Olympus-class in bulk. Footprint is huge. Fire is dead. See Geographic_Region_Ebon_Peninsula.txt.

Human chronicles do not reach this. Recorded history is a few thousand years. Mountain-time is not hall-time.

Age of landforms (north-west, oldest first)
- The Appulse. A giant impact. The Appolasian Plateau is its basin. The ring-hills and crags are the oldest standing topography in this quarter of the world. A structure this large can steer mountains, rivers, and climate for hundreds of millions of years. It stops looking like a fresh wound long before it stops existing. The Plateau is worn, stubborn, and first.
- The volatile tectonic age. Fault-scarps, the Rivenstrand Walls, the Marchwall, the Orosian Spine and its clusters. Younger than the Appulse. Sharper. The “upstart” ranges. Most of this fire is dead. The walls remain.
- Quiet continents. Caves are fossil plumbing. Heat is local.
- Late small falls. The Bowls on the Rhennod foot. Young pits on an old lip. Cousin of the Appulse or a later stone — not locked.

A giant impact does not need to stay sharp to stay in charge. Vredefort-scale wounds on a wet world still own the map after the knife-ridges of a later orogeny have grown up beside them. The Appolasian crags are old bone. The Spine and the Marchwall are the new argument.

DOMINANT SPECIES
Humanity is the dominant and most widespread intelligent species on Virath.
Other fully sapient races exist, though they are generally fewer in number, more geographically limited, or less politically central than humans.
Their exact natures, origins, and relations with humanity vary widely by region and culture.
Mutants, beastmen, and other abominations also appear, but these are regarded as corruptions or curses rather than natural peoples.

TECHNOLOGY
The highest consistently achievable technology across Virath is roughly equivalent to Earth’s 16th century. This level is not uniform:
- Some wealthy coastal and riverine states maintain advanced metallurgy, plate armour, complex fortifications, early firearms (rare and expensive), and sophisticated shipbuilding.
- Many inland, highland, or frontier regions remain closer to high medieval or even earlier material culture.
- Gunpowder exists but is uncommon, unreliable, and often treated with suspicion.
- No functioning relics of higher technology are understood or operable by present-day peoples.

HISTORY (KNOWN)
Recorded history stretches back only a few thousand years at most, and even the oldest chronicles are fragmentary. All cultures share a distant memory of a great fracture known as the Unveiling (see The_Unveiling.txt). Before that event, knowledge dissolves into myth.

THE DEEPER PAST (UNKNOWN TO INHABITANTS)
This sequence is GM-only. Living peoples have myth, not this order.

Pionari first. An explorator vessel found Virath already fit for habitation — promising, almost ready, only light work needed. They lacked the mass and the kit to finish a world. They sent home for it.

Those who answered were the Ingenari: heavy engineers and the military sent to secure the claim. Years in transit.

While they waited, the Pionari put down on the Appolasian Plateau and began seeding Terran life.

The Ingenari arrived. With the Pionari they turned the axial tilt to widen the temperate belts. With their machines they raised the first true cities and the first infrastructure. Lesser Terran administrators came with them: the Ordinari.

In that first century the Unveiling was already present. The human count was still a thin specialist contingent. Manifestations were subtle. Men found rational names for them.

Then the Multitude came — Novari in the later books — millions fleeing calamity on the homeworld. That weight of souls fed the Unveiling. What can fairly be called demons first took form.

By then the entrenched first-comers — Pionari, Ingenari, and the first Ordinari — had laboured near a century on a home they would not hand to Terran overreach and a horde of interlopers. They were ready to hold it.

The Aperti watched with cold detachment.
In that same span they mapped what they took to be the mechanics of the Unveiling. They measured that the old-world religions carried by the Multitude were leaving a print on Virath — visible only to those who knew the signs.
Aperti working count of the Multitude’s inherited faith-mass (not a living census):
- The Nazarene creed (later halls will not know the word Christianity) — about 29–31%
- The Submission (later halls will not know the word Islam) — about 24–26%
- The Middle Path (later halls will not know the word Buddhism) — about 15%
- The Eternal Law (later halls will not know the word Hinduism) — about 6–7%
Other affiliations, in their reading, never reached the mass needed to steer the Immaterium.
Those four currents are a foundation under many contemporary cults and churches. The living forms are near-unrecognisable. Names, law, and rite have been broken by the Dark Age and rebuilt from scraps. Do not print Earth-names in a 16th-century hall-book.
This is Aperti observation, not a proof offered to the marks. The Aperti file, when written, holds the cold version. The_Unveiling.txt holds the phenomenon.


The Multitude and later Ordinari forced a place in the less desirable country. Low hostility remained. A cold war. Then an uneasy peace.

Then the False Moon first eclipsed the sun.

Records from that hour grow sparse, then broken, then gone. The human count falls. Fewer souls, and the Unveiling recedes. A remnant on the order of ten thousand is left, scattered across the globe.

The old “one colony ship, five to ten thousand settlers” line is retired. That number is the *aftermath*, not the landing.
Megastructures and ash-choked works remain. No present people operate them. This origin is unknown to the halls. It survives, if at all, as quarreling legend.

CULTURAL DEVELOPMENT
Enough time has passed since the collapse for distinct ethnic groups, languages, religions, and cultures to form and diverge widely. Regional identities are strong. Universal institutions are rare.

PLANETARY ANTIQUITY & PRE-FALL ORIGINS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE ANCIENT INFRASTRUCTURE:
   - During Virath’s golden zenith, Haxonia was the global locus of maximum 
     technological saturation, housing orbital transit hubs and space-age cities.
   - Core Architecture: Dominated by massive pre-historic megastructures, sub-
     crustal bunker matrices, and automated planetary containment networks.

2. THE GREAT FALL & MUTUAL DESTRUCTION:
   - The continent served as the central arena for the catastrophic civil war 
     between the original Pionari/Ingenari settler coalition and the incoming 
     Novari/Ordinari interlopers.
   - Outcome: Simultaneous total orbital bombardment and atomic deployment 
     triggered a permanent nuclear winter, leaving the soil completely poisoned.
   - Modern State: Millennia of severe regression have reduced the remaining 
     human populations to fragmented techno-barbarian tribes.

3. THE PLANAR TEAR (THE UNVEILING BLEED):
   - The industrial violence of the Fall was so physically and metaphysically 
     traumatic that it ruptured the planar barrier (The Unveiling) across the 
     entire continental interior.
   - Consequence: High concentration of malevolent, non-material entities 
     manifest within the radioactive wastes, acting as apex territorial predators.

COLONIAL ANTIQUITY & THE STRATEGIC BYPASS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE IDYLLIC WILDERNESS:
   - During the first planetary survey phase, Erevania (Cataloged as TER-VNT) was 
     noted as the most habitable and biologically pristine landmass on Virath.
   - The Industrial Bypass: Because the Ingenari heavy manufacturing syndicates 
     required flat, gentle topography to lay down their space-age factories, they 
     concentrated 90% of their industry on Haxonia. Erevania was left as a sparse, 
     low-impact, idyllic frontier settled by agrarian Pionari pioneers.

2. THE CONTINENTAL CITADEL:
   - When the global civil conflict erupted, the invading Ordinari and Novari 
     interlopers directed 90% of their offensive payloads against Haxonia to 
     neutralize the Ingenari power base. 
   - Erevania's severe, vertical topography offered no clean landing zones or 
     sweeping armor corridors. The invaders bypassed it during the opening phases, 
     shielding the continent's ecosystems from total nuclear grid-fire.

3. THE ERA OF THE EXODUS (THE WAR OF ATTRITION):
   - As Haxonia collapsed, the outgunned Pionari and Ingenari settler coalition 
     executed a massive fighting retreat across the ocean, falling back into 
     Erevania to turn the entire landmass into a defensive redoubt.
   - The First Men used the continent's jagged geography as an interlocking system 
     of fortress walls, bleeding the high-tech Novari and Ordinari divisions 
     dry in a grinding war of attrition that lasted generations.
   - Modern State: The current feudal kingdoms, castle holds (Karbēo), and river 
     garrisons (Ísá-Mūth) of your active sandbox are built directly upon the 
     bones of these ancient, isolated trench lines and frontier holdouts.

REGIONAL GEOGRAPHY & DEFENSIVE FRICTION SECTORS (AI RUNNER INSTANCE)
================================================================================
Because Erevania functions as a planetary fortress, your AI assistant enforces 
severe defensive terrain modifiers across the unexplored 90%:

1. THE CENTRAL EXPANSE: THE VAST STEPPES (MID & CENTER)
   - Topography: A boundless, uninterrupted ocean of wind-swept grasslands.
   - Tactical Role: The great sally-port of the continent. It provides maximum 
     mobility for wide-range cavalry maneuvers and animal husbandry, allowing the 
     pastoral defenders to harass invading supply lines across hundreds of miles.

2. THE SOUTHERN ARID FLANK (SOUTH-CENTRAL)
   - Anatolia/Persia Vibe: High-altitude arid plateaus, steep clay ravines.
   - Tactical Role: The mineral and chemical redoubt. Its deep, maze-like canyon 
     systems hide the ancient bunker vaults where early volatile components and 
     ore foundries were secured during the conflict.

3. THE COLOCOLO SPINE & THE EASTERN MULTI-STEP STAIRCASE (THE FAR EAST)
   - The Great Spine: The largest and tallest mountain wall on Virath. It acts as 
     the ultimate inner-keep curtain wall of the continent.
   - The Staircase Descent: A series of massive, drop-stepping high-altitude 
     tablelands descending to the eastern coast. Each step functions as a massive, 
     self-contained defensive terrace that must be scaled or breached sequentially, 
     making an eastern maritime invasion path a tactical impossibility.

4. THE FROZEN WALL & THE NARROW SEA (THE NORTHERN COAST)
   - The Coastal Chain: A razor-sharp, unbroken mountain ridge running the full 
     length of the north coast, serving as a permanent geographical barrier.
   - The Narrow Frozen Sea: Fully frozen over most of the year. It prevents naval 
     blockades along the northern front, forcing any approach onto a treacherous 
     march across a shifting sheet-ice plain under constant high-altitude missile 
     or ordnance threat from the coastal ridges.

THE ARCHITECTURE OF TERRAFORMING & MOUNTAIN SPINES
================================================================================
Vandria represents the supreme achievement of pre-Fall macro-engineering on 
Virath. Rather than a natural landmass left untouched, it functions as a highly 
modified, self-contained environmental engine.

1. THE FLANKING RAMPARTS (THE EAST & WEST CHAINS):
   - The continent is bracketed on its eastern and western borders by two prominent, 
     jagged mountain spines. These natural rock barriers rise sharply from the sea, 
     acting as titanic walls that shield the interior basin from open-ocean storms.
   - Economic Output: High stone-hardness quarry bed-faces (Cross-ref: Economy_Primary_Stone) 
     and deep mineral veins that provide structural resources for the local channels.

2. THE FLOODED INTERIOR (THE THREE SLUICES):
   - Left untouched, the central lowlands between the mountain ranges would have 
     stagnated into a dead, rain-shadowed arid desert. To correct this, the 
     Ingenari cartels sliced deep into the continental crust, carving out three 
     miniature interior oceans that run completely through the landmass.
   - Function: These engineered seas pulled maritime moisture directly into the 
     heart of the continent, transforming a barren basin into a lush, fertile, 
     and highly habitable cradle capable of supporting massive populations.

3. THE MODERN REGRESSED ERA:
   - When the Great Fall shattered the automated terraforming networks, the 
     systems locked the channel flood-gates open. While the modern interior has 
     dry-drifted slightly into sun-baked grasslands and scrub-steppes, the 
     three interior oceans remain perfectly deep, uniform, and navigable.
   - Population: Inhabited by elite canal-pilots, salvage clans who harvest pre-fall 
     alloys from the concrete-lined banks, and fortified lock-gate kingdoms.

CLIMATIC ZONATION & SOLVASTIAN BIOMES
================================================================================
Unlike the rugged, uniform fortress profile of Erevania, Solvastia is a continent 
of extreme, layered environmental transitions as one travels from North to South.

1. THE EQUATORIAL SCORCH (NORTH & CENTER)
   - The Dune Seas: Vast, shifting expanses of barren, sun-bleached sand desert 
     running the full length of the northern coast directly along the Axian Sea.
   - Economic Output: The premier sourcing ground for high-purity crystalline salts, 
     sulfur flowers, and natural acids bubbling from exposed geothermal fissures. These 
     elements are required for elite explosive formulas managed in Siege_Volatiles.txt.

2. THE CANYON BRUTALISM: HIGH EQUATORIAL PLATEAUS
   - Topography: Sheer, sun-baked rocky plateaus carved by deep, labyrinthine crevices. 
     This is the primary zone of human survival along the harsh equator.
   - The Sietch Artifice: Ancient evidence of the Pionari and Ingenari infrastructure 
     remains locked inside the stone. Civilizations here survive exclusively within 
     underground and sub-surface Sietches—colossal, cliff-carved bunker-cities and 
     canyon communities built using heavy, brutalist stone-masonry rules.
   - Factions: Inhabited by stationary sietch-cartels or highly insular nomadic groups 
     of hardy, water-hoarding survivalists.

3. THE COALITION CANOPY: THE TWIN RAINFORESTS
   - The Western Coastal Jungle: Positioned on the Atlantic flank, pinned tightly 
     beneath a titanic coastal mountain rain-trap. A dense, humid, primeval canopy.
   - The Eastern Coastal Jungle: Positioned on the Pacific flank, similarly fed by 
     heavy maritime clouds broken against sheer interior peaks.
   - Economic Output: High-density exotic hardwood, rubber binders, medicinal saps, 
     and specialized toxic resins used to manufacture lethal chemical warfare agents.

4. THE SOUTHERN TRANSITION (THE CLIMATIC GRADIENT)
   - The Humid Belt: South of the deserts and rainforests, the continent transitions 
     into a dense, swampy, sub-tropical humid plain.
   - The Temperate Heartlands: Shifting smoothly into rolling, fertile grasslands 
     and temperate woodlands suitable for large-scale agrarian kingdoms.
   - The Cold Void: The absolute southern tip of the continent terminates in a 
     wind-blasted, sub-polar tundra and jagged ice-cliffs, mirroring the extreme 
     cold variables of the northern Leviathan's Abyss.

METEOROLOGICAL HAZARDS & ENVIRONMENTAL LOGISTICS
================================================================================
The Calderan Basin functions as a massive, volatile atmospheric heat-trap. Because 
the basin is partially enclosed by heavy continental landmasses, its weather patterns 
are defined by extreme, sudden atmospheric shifts.

1. THE STEAMING DOLDRUMS (THE STILL SEA):
   - The primary operational state of the basin is absolute stillness. The air 
     grows thick, heavy, and hot, trapping moisture inside the high continental 
     contours like steam inside a sealed vault.
   - The surface of the water frequently settles into a mirror-smooth, oily calm 
     where a ship’s sails sag listlessly against the masts for days or weeks at 
     a time. Under the unmoving equatorial sun, a heavy, warm sea-fog blankets 
     the basin, forcing crews to strictly ration water as they sit stranded.

2. THE PRESSURE-POT RUPTURE (SUDDEN CYCLONES):
   - The intense heat building within this stagnant atmospheric bowl acts as a 
     natural pressure pot. When the heavy, silent air finally reaches a breaking 
     point, it ruptures with catastrophic violence.
   - These sudden cyclones strike with almost no warning, tearing through the 
     heavy fog with screaming, multi-directional winds. 
   - Because the storm systems are trapped within the semi-bowl of the basin, 
     the winds churn violently, kicking up massive, erratic cross-seas that slam 
     into a vessel from multiple angles at once, threatening to drive stranded 
     ships blind onto hidden coastal reefs.

ABYSSAL GEOLOGY & TWO-TIERED LATITUDINAL CLIMATE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE HYDRAULIC TRANSITION (THE 50/50 CLIMATE SPLIT):
   - The northernmost half (approximately 11.3 million square miles) exists under 
     a permanent polar freeze. This sector is a solid, shifting sheet-ice plain 
     choked by massive ice-floes and jagged pressure ridges.
   - The southernmost half transitions into a brutal, open sub-polar sea. While 
     free of solid sheet-ice, this open water is filled with colossal wandering 
     icebergs, freezing spray, and violent gale-force storms.

2. THE PIONARI TRENCH:
   - During the first planetary survey, the Pionari automated scans logged this 
     22-million-square-mile oceanic basin as a massive structural crustal drop-
     fault. Its staggeringly hostile bathymetry represents the deepest water 
     column on Virath, averaging a crushing 8,000 metres.
   - Beneath both the northern ice sheets and the southern open sea lies a dark, 
     freezing, and hyper-pressurized water column dropping down into pitch-black trenches. 
     At 15,000 metres, the mechanical pressure completely bypasses organic 
     tolerance indices, leaving it an untamed, silent graveyard.

THE DAUÐAVÍK LIFELINE & CHOKEPOINT DYNAMICS
================================================================================
Because navigating a 22-million-square-mile abyssal ocean is mathematically lethal 
for 16th-century naval logistics, the central island function is absolute:

1. THE CENTRAL ANCHOR (DAUÐAVÍK):
   - Rising directly out of the center of the Deeps, the sheer basalt cliffs 
     of Dauðavík sit precisely on the climate fault line. It acts as the anchor 
     point separating the northern ice pack from the southern open sub-polar sea, 
     functioning as the supreme resupply checkpoint for the northern hemisphere.
   - Any fleet executing a trans-oceanic crossing between Haxonia and Erevania 
     is structurally bound to dock at Dauðavík to check for ice-crushed hull 
     degradation (Durability.txt) and replenish fuel stores.

2. ACUMEN OF LORE: THE SUB-ICE ECOSYSTEM:
   - Due to its proximity to the active tectonic plates of The Leviathan's Abyss, 
     low-frequency acoustic tremors and seismic thuds echo continuously through 
     the 8,000-metre water column. 
   - Regional Lore: Northern mariners claim that the abyssal trenches of the Kyron 
     Deeps act as a subterranean migration path for the Leviathans, allowing 
     colossal entities to drift completely unseen beneath the ice sheet from the far 
     north down toward the deep equatorial waters.

TECTONIC MECHANICS & SUB-GLACIAL GEOLOGY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. THE CONTINENTAL CRUNCH (ACTIVE SUBDUCTION):
   - The tectonic plates of Haxonia and Erevania are not static; they are actively 
     grinding into one another along a colossal, 15-million-square-mile fault 
     line running directly beneath the seabed of the Abyss.
   - Consequence: The 50-foot-thick surface ice sheet is under non-stop physical 
     compression. It groans, fractures, and thrusts upward into massive, jagged, 
     slate-grey pressure ridges that make straight land transport impossible.

2. CRYO-VOLCANIC BLOWOUTS:
   - Deep magma vents along the subduction zone superheat subterranean pockets of 
     water. This creates immense, pressurized steam chambers that violently 
     rupture the ice sheet from below, leaving boiling, black water fissures 
     that churn with ash before quickly freezing back into warped iron-hued ice.
================================================================================
FOLKLORE & SYSTEMIC REALITY: THE THINGS IN THE ICE
================================================================================
At a 16th-century technological ceiling, the local cultures interpret the literal 
seismic friction of the continental collision as the movements of living beasts.

1. THE ACOUSTIC RUMBLES (THE SLEEPING GIANTS):
   - The deep-crust friction generates constant low-frequency acoustic vibrations 
     and rhythmic, heavy booming thuds that can be felt through the soles of winter 
     boots or sledge runners for hundreds of miles.
   - Regional Myth: Coast-dwellers and polar sled-teams believe these tremors are 
     the breathing cycles and shifting masses of colossal, ancient marine 
     Leviathans slumbering in the warm, hydrothermal black abyss beneath the ice.

2. THE "WAKING" FIELDS:
   - When a major seismic shift creates an unexpected open-water lead, northern 
     hunters claim a "Leviathan has broken the surface to breathe." These steaming, 
     unstable water pools are treated with profound religious dread.

   - During the first planetary survey, the Pionari automated scans locked this 
     9-million-square-mile body of water as the primary structural axis for 
     global planet-side deployment, cargo looping, and population movement.
   - The Shield-Line Pipeline: When the catastrophic civil war erupted, the outgunned 
     settler coalition used this internal sea lane as the literal supply spine of 
     their continental fortress, running continuous military cargo barge fleets 
     to sustain their isolated mountain and forest redoubts.

2. THE MODERN MACRO-ECONOMIC LIFELINE:
   - Because crossing the massive interior trans-continental steppes or rugged mountain 
     ravines via land vehicles is logistically exhausting, The Axian Sea functions 
     as the primary cardiopulmonary engine for modern civilization on Virath.
   - It acts as an open, friction-free trade highway allowing heavy transport 
     barges to link diverse regional markets, moving massive volumes of resources 
     across thousands of miles at unparalleled speed.

CHOKEPOINT DYNAMICS & REGIONAL TARIFF RULES (AI HELPER ENGINE MODULE)
================================================================================
Because The Axian Sea pinches into narrow, cliff-walled straits as it transitions 
toward the western rim, it creates critical geopolitical bottlenecks:

1. THE EBON PENINSULA CHECKPOINTS:
   - The western exits of the sea are heavily bounded by the rugged fingers of 
     the Ebon Peninsula. Controlling the river inlets and coastal gates bordering 
     this sector (such as Settlement_Isa_Muth.txt) grants total economic 
     sovereignty over all incoming trans-continental interior cargo.

2. MARITIME TRANSPORT PROFILES (Cross-ref: Vehicle_Sea_Profile.txt):
   - Cargo Efficiency: Any resource shipment (Bulk Lumber, Dressed Stone, Grained 
     Powder Kegs) moved across the Axian Sea zone completely bypasses land-terrain 
     friction penalties. Heavy barges gain a mandatory 12× weight capacity 
     multiplier compared to heavy wagons at triple the daily transit speed.
   - The Trade Drift: Master foundries (Eirblast) and luxury monopolies (Karbēo) 
     rely on this sea to export finished military hardware and high-value wax 
     cargo to the deep interior empires, returning with vital raw minerals.

GENERAL TONE OF THE WORLD
Virath is a hard, lived-in world of steel, blood, trade, faith, and weather. The supernatural exists but remains mostly at the edges — rare, feared, and poorly understood. Most days are governed by the same concerns that have always ruled human life: harvest, winter, war, honour, debt, and survival.
