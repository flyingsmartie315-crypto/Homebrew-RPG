Svart-Viðr (Svartwood) is hard-locked to Dauðavík fault-ground. Do not place it on other polar floors.

Svart-Viðr
Working name: Svartwood
Domain: Land
Biome: Polar
HSI: Temperature: 0–2 | Rainfall: 2–4 | Human Density: 0–2 | Flora: 3–5 | Fauna: 2–3 | Topography: 3–5 | Hydrology: 3–5
Rarity: 2
Use: timber (hard, slow), limited fuel (burns foul) | Notes: Dauðavík only. Midnight-dark conifer. Grows in ribbons along live geothermal faults, not as a blanket forest. Inside a stand the air is damp and about 10°C while the blizzard runs outside. Needles take low-angle light and ground-heat. Deep roots drink hot ground without cooking. Bark holds vent-metals. Pre-collapse engineered shield-wood; inhabitants know a fault-wood, not a machine. Better ship-timber if a plank can be taken. Too heavy for a whole hull. Strategic members only: keel, waterline, stem and stern. Not a super-material.
