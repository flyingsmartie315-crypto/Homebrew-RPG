--------------------------------------------------
BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

A pair holds the crag for life. They do
not keep a pride. They hunt first light
and last light. Highsun is the snow-edge.
Longnight is the last open ledge. False
Moon pulls them lower, onto paths men walk.

They cache on the nest. Both sit the egg.
Both kill anything that comes up the face.
Knights take the egg, not the adult. The
pair treats that as a slain chick and
comes down the mountain.

A widow is the survivor, male or female.
Young still on the crag: the widow keeps
the rock. No young: the widow leaves and
roams. One clean kill off a herd is hunger.
A fold broken and the meat left is a debt.
They grieve. They do not eat the mate.
They hunt the killers past the old boundary.
That hunt can last days.

Wild adults do not take a master. A
hand-reared bird bonds to one rider for
life. A broken bond is not a safe beast.
A widow bonding to a living soul is rare.
It is not the Knight’s schooling.

Hurt: it does not flee the crag while the
mate or the egg is on it. Under half its
Wounds, and the nest empty, it breaks for
height. Harm 6. A pair is Collective 4,
not a host.

--------------------------------------------------
RUN
--------------------------------------------------

Three attacks. Beak, talons, or a mix.
The leap is the Charge, then the talons.
It does not Parry. It may Dodge on 40.
A blow that empties 52 drops it.
Do not give it the air. The royal file
is the winged bird.
