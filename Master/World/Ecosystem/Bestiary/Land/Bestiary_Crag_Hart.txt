Overview
Crag-harts are the great ibex of Virath’s faces. Men say spire-goat or scree-goat. Slate in Highsun, dirty cream in Longnight. They take a drop a man would not, and they keep a ledge a horse cannot. They are the massif’s meat: what a gryphon pair eats when the hunting is honest. They are not vale-goat and they are not a Wonder. The veil did not invent a hoof.

Signs
- Ridged horn-scuff on wet basalt
- Pellets on a shelf no sheep could reach
- A skyline buck, still, watching the path
- Hair slate or winter-cream in a talon-cache on a gryphon ledge
- A blocked col: herd packed on the only tread
- Kid-bleat under an overhang in Thaw

Behaviour
A herd of eight to twenty holds a face: the ewes and the year-stock, one old male on the highest rock. They do not migrate between countries. They move up and down the same mountain. Highsun they take the snow-edge and the lichen. Longnight they drop to the last open shelves and the heads of hanging valleys. Deepskaard Col is a crossing in hard weather, not a home.

They eat lichen, juniper, rowan-browse, and the grass that hangs on a step. They drink melt and drip. They do not use the Deepwood floor or the lake-strand.

The hoof is the weapon and the road. They drop a man’s height as a habit. Cornered on a ledge they charge *up* into the problem — horn first, the drop behind you. A single hart is a knock and a gore. A herd on a narrow tread is a fall.

Rut is late Harvest into the first cold. Horns ring on the face. Kids come in Thaw on a shelf the fox cannot walk. The young stay a year. Then the bucks are driven to the skyline or they die on the next massif.

Gryphons and harts keep the same rock in a hard peace. The pair takes the slow and the stray. If the hart-count fails, the gryphon comes down to the vale fold. That is hunger, not a new range.

False Moon makes them still and tight to the wall, or it sends a panic across a face. It does not put them in Kolby.

Men of Skaardhold and Askgruv take a kid and school it to pack. That is a rope-beast, not a destrier. The wild herd is not livestock. A hold-strain, if the Pionari ever set one, is a later Economy line. This file is the face.

Encounter
A skyline buck watching is a warning, not a fight.
A herd on the only tread is Collective 4: horns and the drop.
One animal in a choke is Harm 3.
Gryphon-sign on the same face means the hart are food, not allies.
Kannavald: Ebon Spire, Svarthorn, Brocken, Stetbjorg, Deepskaard as a pass. Not Kolmire. Not Underwood Shores. Not the Hornwood floor.
