--------------------------------------------------
BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

This is the arm the pack points at, or the
liar who stands next to it. Not a king.
False Moon does not make one.

It still will not hold a shield-wall. It
throws, then it closes if the target is
down. A chief calls the pit and the silk.
He does not stand in the open to be shot.

Hurt: it bolts later than the common body.
Under half its Wounds it breaks for the
scrape. Harm 3. The ground is still
Collective 6. The body is just harder to drop.

--------------------------------------------------
RUN
--------------------------------------------------

It throws once. Dart or javelin. It closes
only if the target is down or snared.
It may Parry on 35. It may Dodge on Agility 40.
