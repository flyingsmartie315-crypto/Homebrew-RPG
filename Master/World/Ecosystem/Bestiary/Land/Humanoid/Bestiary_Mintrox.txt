Overview
Mintrox came through the Unveiling with the great bugs. Men say goblin. They are small, hunched, long-armed, short-legged: built to throw and to bolt, not to march. Scrap-flint, bone, stolen iron. They do not forge. They do not stand a shield-wall. They set a pit, a silk trip, a smeared thorn, and they put a dart into the man who looks down. The spiders and the lesser crawlers are their home-game, not a miracle. Humans first took spider-silk as a craft from watching them.

Signs
- A scrape under a ruin-floor, bank, or fallen wall
- Flint flakes, sinew-wrap, stolen nails
- Trip-silk at shin-height that is not an Orb-wright’s circle
- Stone-scatter and dart-holes in a wagon-tilt
- Skitterling lines denser than a cellar should hold
- A small rider on a 2–4 ft spider, if you are unlucky
- False Moon: more scrap-fires, more joined packs

Behaviour
Loose packs. A few dozen on most ground; a hundred is a bad year and a fat ruin. The head is the biggest arm or the best liar. They squabble. They do not keep a crown.

They scavenge and they raid the isolated: a lone farm, a slow cart, a straggler. They read ground and weather better than they read a book. Tools are crooked. The throw is not. Stones, darts, short javelins. Then they are gone.

Husbandry is the difference from a Ghoul-pack. They keep Skitterlings the way men keep dogs: trip-lines, corner-nests, the odd ride. They steal silk from an Orb-wright stretch when they can and they die on that silk when they cannot. Sky-hornets they bribe with sweet-water, same as a southern farmer. They do not stable a Pale Lurker. They do not plough with an Earth-eater. They do not own a Hill-host.

They use the bugs because those bugs are *theirs* — same crack in the world. Men came later to that trick.

False Moon joins packs and makes the traps thicker. It does not make a host for a king.

Encounter
Six to twenty in a ruin-edge or a cut-bank is the usual (Harm 3 each, Collective 6 for the ground).
One in a fair fight against steel is a short fight. The pit and the silk are the fight.
A spider-rider is a Skitterling, not a Lurker.
Boreal floor only as a thin waste-pack on the warm edge. Polar and high Alpine are a skip until a strain-file exists.
