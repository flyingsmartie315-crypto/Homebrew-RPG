LOADOUT
--------------------------------------------------

Greenskin (name). Adapted from Weaponry.txt.
Brass is the stats base only. −1 to each
type, floor 0. The piece in the hand is
scrap: flint, bone, stolen iron, a bent
nail. Roll the metal if it matters. Do not
change the line for it.
Apprentice craft unless the piece is looted.
A loot may be better.
Parry only with the round shield in hand.
Weapon Skill 25. No shield, no test.

Half the pack is ranged. Half is melee.
They favour the throw. They close only
when the ammunition is gone and they
think they can win.

  Ranged
    Shield in hand. A clutch of javelins,
    3–5. Throw the lot. Then the belt:
    short single-edged, star mace, or
    bearded axe.
    Or no board. Pouch sling and stones.
    50 g a shot, Enc 0.5. Twenty are Enc 10.
    The dagger stays in the belt until
    the scalp.
  Melee
    Two in three: round shield and a
    one-handed spear, or the belt weapon
    already out.
    One in three: cast net. 1d10, 1–3
    spider-silk. No board. The belt
    weapon comes after the throw.

--------------------------------------------------
BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

Loose pack. A few dozen. A hundred is a
bad year. The head is the longest arm or
the best liar. No crown.

It raids the isolated. A farm, a slow cart,
a straggler. It does not stand a shield-wall.
The pit, the silk at shin height, then the
dart into the one who looks down. Then it
is gone.

Bugs are the home-game. Skitterlings as
men keep dogs. Silk taken from an Orb-wright
when they can. Sky-hornets bribed with
sweet-water. Not a Pale Lurker. Not an
Earth-eater.

False Moon joins packs and thickens the
traps. It does not make a host.

Hurt: it bolts. Under half its Wounds it
does not stand. One against steel, in the
open, is a short fight. Harm 3. The ground
is Collective 6.

--------------------------------------------------
RUN
--------------------------------------------------

It throws once. Javelin, sling, or spear.
It closes only if the ammunition is gone
and it thinks it can win, or the target
is down or snared.
It may Parry only with a shield in hand, at 25.
It may Dodge on Agility 40.
A blow that empties 4 drops it.
