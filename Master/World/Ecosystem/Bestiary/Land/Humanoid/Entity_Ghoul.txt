--------------------------------------------------
BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

Nest, not range. Crypt, plague-pit, cellar,
last year’s field. It does not hold a forest.
Light and a press of the living drive it under.

Hungry: human meat, fresh or dead. A corpse
comes out of the ground faster than a sexton
puts one in. A living walker is prey if he is
alone, drunk, or already down. It will not
charge a prepared line for preference.
A detail that is not looking is swamped.

Pack: one keeps the best of the meat. No crown.
They breed in the nest. That is how a stretch fills.
Intelligence 8. Cunning enough to dig and to wait.
Not enough to parley. Fellowship 5.

The air of a live nest is Mort-Vapor.
Scholars say Vapor Mortiferus.

False Moon: bolder on the surface, faster at a
fresh grave. Not a march down the high street
in daylight.

Hurt: it breaks for the hole. Under half its
Wounds it does not stand. Leave the body in
the dark and the hole stays live. Burn it.

--------------------------------------------------
RUN
--------------------------------------------------

It closes on four limbs. One attack. Jaws or
claws, not both, unless Attacks rises.
It does not parry. It may Dodge on Agility 35.
A blow that empties 9 drops it.
Burn it, or the bestiary rule stands.
Harm 3 for one. Collective 6 for the nest.
