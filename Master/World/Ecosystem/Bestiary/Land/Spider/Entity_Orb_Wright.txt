--------------------------------------------------
BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

One orb. One female. The burrow is den
and sac-house, behind the web, not a
second monster. She sits the hub or the
bark above it. The silk kicks. She drops
or runs the line.

Prey is moth, bat, hare, roe, the odd
young boar, and the walker on the night
path. A full deer is a good year. She
wraps the living and hangs the bundle.

The male does not sit this sheet. He
wanders. Canopy lines and small snares.
A tangle with no forty-foot orb is him,
or last year’s ruin. He dies on her silk
as easily as a man.

Destroy the orb and she goes to the
burrow or climbs. She will not stand a
prepared line in the open. If the boles
still stand she throws another span in
days.

False Moon puts a faint light in the
great web and hunger in the sit.

Hurt: she leaves the open. Under half
her Wounds she takes the burrow. Harm 5.
Not a pack. A stretch can hold more than
one orb. That is Collective 4, not this
body.

--------------------------------------------------
RUN
--------------------------------------------------

The silk is the fight. Cutting it or
walking it starts her.
She Bites once the body is held or in
reach 1. She does not Parry.
A blow that empties 12 drops her.
Open ground, off the orb: she climbs
or she holes. She does not charge
out of the burrow.
