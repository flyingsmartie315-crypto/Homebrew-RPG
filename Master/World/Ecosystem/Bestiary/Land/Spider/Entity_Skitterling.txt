--------------------------------------------------
BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

One to a room, until the ruin is empty
long enough to stack. Prey is rat,
beetle, pigeon, the reaching hand.
They follow warmth and slops. A cold
alpine hole without rats is not this
sheet. That hole is the Pale Lurker.

They sit the corner or the knee-high
line. They do not stand a door-fight.
Light and a ready blade end one.
Fire ends the stretch. False Moon
makes them bolder on the stair. It
does not string an orb across a street.

Hurt: it bolts for the crack. Under
half its Wounds it does not stand.
Harm 3 for one. Collective 5 for
the nest.

--------------------------------------------------
RUN
--------------------------------------------------

The line is the fight. One bite if
the body is held or in reach 0.5.
It does not Parry. It may Dodge on
Agility 50.
A blow that empties 3 drops it.
Burn the nest, or the lines stay.
