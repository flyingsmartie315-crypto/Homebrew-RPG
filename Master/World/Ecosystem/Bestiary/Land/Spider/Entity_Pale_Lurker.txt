--------------------------------------------------
BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

One sheet. One female. She sits the web
and the dark behind it. Stone: the first
bend. Fir: the hollow root-mass. Slow
until the silk pulls. Then the reach is
long and the haul is into the black.

She does not drop from a hub. She comes
out. Prey is bat, stray stock, the
explorer. A bear in the mouth is wrapped
and waited on. She does not run a bear
on open ground.

Bright open wood ruins her. A ride, a
logged fringe, the scree at noon. The
dense canopy is the adit with bark.
False Moon puts her on the edge after
dusk. Dawn puts her back, or kills her.

Hurt: she goes for the hole, not a
stand in the light. Under half her
Wounds she abandons the sheet if the
fire is in it. Harm 6. Not a pack.

--------------------------------------------------
RUN
--------------------------------------------------

The sheet is the fight. Cutting it or
pushing past it starts the haul.
She Bites once the body is on the silk
or in reach 2. She does not Parry.
A blow that empties 27 drops her.
Open ground: −20 WS, and she leaves.
