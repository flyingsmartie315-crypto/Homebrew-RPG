--------------------------------------------------
BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

It does not scout. Workers do that.
Strike the line, the store, or the door
and this body comes. Waves. Clack. Acid
in the face. It does not hunt men for sport.
It kills what stands on the nest.

Fire and a hard wall hold until the wave
breaks. Dawn often ends it. The next night
they are under the same flagstones.

False Moon: the spray is ready, and the
scouts are bold. A hunger-year brings the
nest up. That is still a raid.

Hurt: it does not flee the door while the
wave is coming. Under half its Wounds it
breaks for the hole. Individual Harm 4.
Collective 7.
