--------------------------------------------------
BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

Nest-bound. The clutch is constant in
warm ground. A hard Longnight kills the
shallow brood. She lasts a decade if
the nest stands.

Do not run her on the surface unless
the party has opened the heart. Soldiers
are the door. She is the end of the
gallery. Pouring the nest is the only
end, and the ground may already be a
gallery.

No speech. No parley. Intelligence 5.

Hurt: she does not flee. The chamber
is the body. Under half her Wounds the
brood is already the loss. Killing her
does not empty the holes that night.

--------------------------------------------------
RUN

She does not Parry. She does not Dodge.
A blow that empties 12 drops her.
