--------------------------------------------------
BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

One gallery. The soil is the roof. Leave
the door and it feeds: root, rotten wood,
fungus, easy carrion. A sheep in the hole
is meat. A man in the hole is meat. It
does not stalk the high street.

Cut the door, drive a cart over a thin
roof, or put a spade in the mouth, and
it comes up. A short charge. Plates first.
Shears working. A siege-ram. Not a hunt.

Two doors may share a hill if they stay
apart. It does not swarm a wall. That
is the Hill-host.

False Moon: restless at the mouth. It
does not walk into frost. North of the
warm line they are few and small.

Hurt: it does not flee the hole while
the door is being cut. Under half its
Wounds it goes back down. Fire-oil and
a bolt in the joint drive it. Filling
the gallery is the work. Harm 6.
Collective 4 is two doors, not a host.

--------------------------------------------------
RUN
--------------------------------------------------

One attack. Mandibles. The charge is
the opener if the range is there.
It does not Parry. It may Dodge on 25.
A blow that empties 31 drops it.
The collapse of the road is the other
enemy. It is not on this sheet.
