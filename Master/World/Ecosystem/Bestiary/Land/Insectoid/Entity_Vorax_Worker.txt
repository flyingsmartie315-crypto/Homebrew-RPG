--------------------------------------------------
ATTACK PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

  Shears                  Natural
    (1/0/0)  Reach 0.5   Cut
    1d10 + SB. No Parry. No Disarm
    unless the head is off.
    It cuts grain, sacking, and a
    hand in the way. It does not spray.
    The soldier has the acid.

--------------------------------------------------
TRAITS
--------------------------------------------------

  Dweller (warm soil, granary, yard, packed road)
    Temperature 3–5. A store is a lure.
  Wall-Runner
    A wall, a sack-stack, a rafter.
  Bestial
  Fear (1)
    One is a fright. The line is the nerve.

--------------------------------------------------
BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

Dumb labour. No chief a man can parley with.
It finds grain, dried meat, a carcass, a
rival hole. It cuts and it carries. A sheep
in the hole is taken apart. It is not
swallowed whole.

Leave the nest and the line ignores the road.
Strike the line, the store, or the door and
this body does not hold. Soldiers come.
Dawn often ends the raid.

Hurt: it does not stand. It goes for the hole.
Harm is low for one. Collective 7 is the nest.

--------------------------------------------------
RUN
--------------------------------------------------

One shear. It does not Parry. It may Dodge
on 40. A blow that empties 4 drops it.
Fire and boiling water at the door. Pouring
the nest is the end. This body is the tax.
