Overview
Somnagors are the sloth of the Cloven,
on the imp’s scale. Not killers by want.
A rot that sits until it is pushed. The
herd despises them and leaves them. They
will not work. They sleep in the task.
Before a fight the herd prods them with
spears and puts them in front. A wall
that does not flinch. Arrows and the
first charge go into the moss.

A shaman is not written. If the prod
has a speaker, it is that page later.
Do not invent the shaman here.

Signs

- No horn. Cracked bone on a bear brow
- An imp-camp that walks around a ditch
- Men who passed it and did not see it breathe

Behaviour
Travel is on all fours. A shuffle. The
claws plow. Limbs do not lift clean.
It can stop at once. Lean on a tree, or
drop in a ditch, and stay for weeks.
Breath every few minutes. A rock. A log.
Walkers pass it.

Pain is dull. An arrow or a cut gets a
slow blink. It does not dodge. It does
not flinch. The energy is not spent.

The strike, when it comes, is one overhead
swipe. No speed. The shoulder’s weight.
A miss and it often does not swing again.
It leans. Pins. The dander and the musk
do the rest. Suffocation by staying there.
The dander is not a pathogen file yet.
Name it when that page exists.

False Moon: it does not drum. It is
herded. The raid uses it as the face
that does not care.

Encounter
One is Harm 3, if it is awake. Most

Do not give this body a bow. That is
the ditch-imp. Do not give it pride.
That is the brute.
