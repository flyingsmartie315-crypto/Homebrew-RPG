Overview
Centaurs are the lust of the Cloven.
Not the drink. That reading is dead.
They want the chase, the near-miss, the
scream held one moment longer. The herd
disdains them and watches them. The musk
and the mania push a brute into a
reckless frenzy. They stay on the fringe
for that reason. They are still the shock,
when a target excites them.

They do not kill from wrath. The minotaur
has that. They do not hoard. The harpy
has that. They take a body or a stone
thing to the limit, then drop it. A short
lethargy. Then the next glint of a hunt.

Signs
- A hoof-cadence, even, too fast for a horse
- Silk and ribbon on a swept horn
- A lone runner cut out from a line
- Whip-marks, and the prey still alive
- A fringe scrape, upwind of the herdstone
- Musk. Heads thick. No casks required

Behaviour
The run is the drug. They sprint for the
sprint. Fluid. Hooves in a fast cadence.
They take the stupid line: the gap, the
tight corner, the spear’s length, for
the rush. In a fight they leap, rear,
and turn to be seen. They want the chase
as much as the end.

They ignore a line. One high-value body,
cut out, toyed with. The anticipation is
the point. Weapons pierce or whip. A long
lance, a barbed net, a heavy whip. The
scream is kept. A club is the brute’s.

The herd uses them as shock cavalry and
keeps them upwind when it can. A brute
near the musk gets reckless. That is
useful once. It is a camp-fight if it
lasts. When the novelty dies they discard
the prize. Then the lull, until the next
obsession.

False Moon: the raid is a hunt they can
show off. They do not drum with the imp.
They take a runner off the pale.

Encounter
One on a path is Harm 6. It will not
stand and trade. It cuts one body out.
A handful are the shock, not a rank.
Collective 5 until the sheet exists.
Do not put one in a ditch-imp thicket
and expect the imp’s slip. The show
wants ground to run.
