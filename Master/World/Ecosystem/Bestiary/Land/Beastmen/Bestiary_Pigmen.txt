Overview
Pigmen are the gluttony of the Cloven.
The centaur page used to carry that.
It does not. This body eats. The herd
treats them as dangerous livestock.
A brute stays clear of a feeding frenzy.
A starving pigman takes an imp and does
not notice which kin it was.

Signs
- Rooted ground, logs flipped, a boulder turned
- A wide cloven print, short stride, deep
- Wallow-stink and old blood, not the centaur’s musk
- Cleavers, not a matched pair of axes
- An imp missing after the feed, no fight-sign
- High squeal, then the wet chew

Behaviour
Not charging, they trot. Snout in the dirt.
Neck flips a log or a stone for anything
under it. The charge is the other gait.
Snout down. Low. They do not turn and they
do not stop. The skull and the snout take
the shield wall. Wood splits. Bone goes.

The fat reads as slow. The lunge is not.
Short limbs, a snake-shut of the jaw on
a body that walked past.

The tool is a cleaver, a spiked tenderizer,
a stone mallet. Armour is smashed until
the piece fits the mouth. In the fight
they pin with a hoof, open with a tusk,
and eat while it is still going. Squeal,
chew, snap. Intimacy, not a duel.

False Moon: the raid is a trough. They
do not drum. They follow the blood and
the midden.

Encounter
One is Harm 5. It roots, or it comes
through a door. A feed is Collective 5.
Do not stand between it and meat.
The brute’s pride will not parley with
a hungry maw. The imp’s envy does not
matter once the jaw is open.
