Overview
Ditch-Imps are the common under-caste of
the Cloven. Man-sized, and they stay low.
A twist of beast and man-shape. Stubby
horn buds, a bad prong, or a bare brow.

From the beast: predator fury, and the
herd panic when the line breaks. From
men: malice, spite, and a need for the
very pale they hate. The larger horned
kin look down on them. These are the
hands, the scouts, and the sting.

They take thicket and crag. They track
by scent. They close the ring from the
undergrowth. Bullied by the bigger
bodies, they are cowards when the line
breaks, and cruel to a captive when
they hold the upper hand.

Signs
- A man-sized hand in the mud, nails split
- Cloven tracks, and a drag of scrap-leather
- A wayside stone: ash, dung, a fresh dark cut
- A shortbow string from the wrong angle
- A chimney-stone smashed, the hearth-ash thrown
- No crows. They take the plumage for fletching

Behaviour
Eyes and hands of the herd. The larger
kin are clumsy or impatient. These weave
crude cloth, mend stolen iron, and raise
a hidden camp. They stay hunched. That
keeps the casual blow off them.

Coat follows the ground. Grey on the
massif. Bristle, swine-dark, in the wood.
They break the herd’s dislike of missiles.
Shortbows and javelins on the pale.

Courage is numbers. Wicker shields and
long spears, because the hide is not thick.
False Moon turns the spite into a rite.
Herdstones: dark volcanic blocks, or old
masonry, fouled with blood. Drum, howl,
then the raid on the pale.

Encounter
Scouting party: 3–6 in the undergrowth.
Shoot and flee. The pursuit is the ambush.
Foraging skirmish: 12–20 on a barn or a
timber-yard. Sacks on the back. Archers
on the road.
Herd-wall: the front rank, or the flank.
Meat in front of the larger warriors.
That rank is another file when it is written.
