Overview
Minotaurs are the wrath of the Cloven.
The brute envies them and does not
challenge lightly. The ditch-imp dreads
them. A wrong look paints the trees.
They do not do herd politics. They are
given the berth, and they keep the wood
around the herdstone.

They are not hungry the way the centaur
is hungry. The feast after the kill is
hate. Erase the foe. Eat the evidence.
That is wrath, not gluttony.

Signs
- A path broken through trees, not around them
- Horn-gouges at twice a man’s height
- Blood-matted fur on the bark, old and new
- A herdstone with a wide berth of crushed brush
- Horse or man tossed, not merely gored
- No imp-camp inside that berth

Behaviour
They do not run. They stampede. The hind
hooves build the mass. A tree, a wall, a
smaller body: through, not around. Even
still, the frame twitches. Hooves scrape.
Steam at the nostril. The muscle does not
rest.

Bloodgreed is the wrath once the smell
is up. Frantic. The nearest living thing.
No feint, no rank, no leaving a wounded
kin if the blood is closer. Complex
manoeuvres are not this body.

The weapon is too large for a man. A
great axe, a cleaver, a trunk banded in
iron, or two clubs. The swing is meant
to obliterate. In the aftermath, or in
the middle of it, the weapon drops. Hands
and teeth. Raw flesh. Not a meal. A wiping
out.

They dwell in the tangled wood around the
herdstone. Territorial. Anything that
enters is the fight. Peace, patience, and
parley are not available.

False Moon: the stone is already theirs.
The raid leaves them. They do not drum
with the imp. They meet what comes up
the path to the stone.

Encounter
One in its wood is Harm 7. It does not
scout. It comes through.
Two are rare. Collective 4 is a stone
with a guardian, not a host.
Do not put this body in the ditch-imp
line. The imp is in front of the brute.
The brute is in front of men. This is
behind the stone.
The centaur is the shock on the path.
This is the wreck in the trees.
