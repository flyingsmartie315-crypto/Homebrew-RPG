# Bestiary_Cloven
# Family index. Not a creature.
# The Cloven. Beastmen. One herd, distinct bodies.
# Each caste is its own bestiary page and, later,
# its own entity sheet.

Locked family: Cloven. Spoken: beastmen,
the herd, the horned, when a speaker does
not know the caste.

--------------------------------------------------
PENINSULA
--------------------------------------------------

The height of the dark ages is this herd.
Not a later plague. Not a veil-storm that
passed in a season. The Cloven were thick
on the Ebon Peninsula when the wood was
still the wood.

The Kannavi were already in that wood,
small and scattered, against Unveiling
things. The Isernvolk landed on the
western shore. Osric. Osricford. The
compact is in Peoples_Isernvolk.txt.
The mutual aid that made the alliance
hold was the cleansing of this herd.
Two small peoples. Centuries of grinding
fight. Not one battle.

A hundred skinwights hanged at the landing
is the other founding. Do not swap them.
Skinwights are the shore-story. The Cloven
are the long war in the trees.

They were near exterminated on the
peninsula. Small bands persist in the
deepest reaches. The Deepwood is what
the taming did not finish. A herdstone
in that deep is a remnant, not a realm.
The pale, the path, and the open field
are Isern and Kannavi ground. A raid
from the deep is the old war, thin.

Knowledge on the caste pages is regional
lore. Peninsula folk know the names.
They do not know a host. A host is the
dark-age memory.
