Overview
Sky-hornets are the Unveiling wasps of warm vale, plain, and wooded hill. Black-and-yellow or amber bands, a sting like a short hooked knife. One sting is swelling and a fever that can last days. A nest-defence is a cloud. They paper a grey globe in a high bole, a cliff, or an overhang — cart-sized, sometimes hut-sized — and they forage nectar and sap unless the comb needs meat. They do not stay where Longnight kills the brood. They are not Hill-hosts and they are not Orb-wrights.

Signs
- A grey paper globe in a crown, on a face of rock, or under a lip of stone
- Drone you feel in the teeth before you see the banded body
- Bird- or squirrel-husk under the nest-tree
- A limb blown huge, hot, and useless after one hit
- Sugar-crock or sap-pail left at a known tree
- False Moon: wider patrol, more drone at dusk

Behaviour
Leave the nest and they feed the flowers and the sap-wounds. They take small flying things and a squirrel if the brood is hungry. They do not raid granaries in a stream. That is the Hill-host.

Strike a worker on the path and you may live with a bad arm. Strike the globe and the cloud comes. Dozens, not hundreds. Stings stack: shock, swell, fever. Twenty is a grave for an unready patrol. Smoke, fire-oil, and a wet cloth on the skin are the known answers. Burning the nest ends the year if the queen dies. Survivors circle for days.

They sleep a cold winter inside the paper. Southern nests may run the year. Boreal and Polar are a skip.

False Moon: extra eggs, longer beats, patrols off the home tree. It does not put a nest on a northern massif.

Southern folk curse them and still leave sugar-water at a known globe so the swarm stays on nectar. Children and stock are the worry, not the plough-horse.

Encounter
One on the road is Harm 3: dodge or take the limb.
The nest-cloud is Collective 6. Retreat is the first skill.
Do not crush them under a boot. That line is for flies.
Hill-host is the grain-line on the ground. Orb-wright is the silk in the ride. This is the drone in the crown.

Notes
