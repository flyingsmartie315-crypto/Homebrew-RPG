BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

Solitary. A range, not a bend of river.
It does not patrol a road. It waits on a
shelf, or in the cave mouth, still.

Hungry: the nearest warm thing on the
height. A goat, a winter kill, a man
alone on the pass. It does not stalk a
town. Intelligence 5. The lioncloth is
the craft. Torn hide. Prey-hair left on.

Met below the shelf: the boulder comes
first. Then it comes down. One swing.
Fists, claws, or the trunk. Not all three.
If the prey climbs, it follows. If the
prey reaches open lowland and other men,
it does not.

Young: it stays until it can live, then
it roams. It settles where the range is
empty. It does not go home.

Hurt: it does not flee at the first wound.
The hide is why. A blow to the belly is
the one it feels. Under half its Wounds,
and the next blow worse, it breaks for
the cave. It does not fight on the road
once it has stone to put between.

It does not parley. Fellowship 8.
A carcass may hold it for a minute.
A speech does not.

--------------------------------------------------
RUN
--------------------------------------------------

It throws once if it has the height, then
closes. In reach it swings once.
It does not parry. It may still Dodge on
Agility 10.
The belly is the gap. The back is the hide.
A blow that empties 135 is the finishing blow.
NAC 5 and the natural armour come off first,
unless the blow is the abdomen.
A chance meeting is the shelf and the boulder.
The Wrac-Stān hunt is not this sheet. It is
the bestiary: weeks of lanes and pitons, then
a False Moon, then the throat. The plate is
the pay. Damage after the throat spikes is a fault.
