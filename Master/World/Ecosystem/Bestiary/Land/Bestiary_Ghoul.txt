Overview
Ghouls are an Unveiling strain of the grave and the old field. Folk say they were men who ate men and could not stop. What walks now is hunched, stocky, dog-gaited, skin tight on yellow bone, the smell of wet rot well ahead of the body. Eyes pale in the dark. A jaw that unhinges for the take. They want human meat, fresh or dead. One is a pest. A nest is a broken burial-ground. Steel drops them. Fire is what finishes the work.

Signs
- Wet-rot stink in a crypt, ditch, or plough-furrow
- Graves opened from below, not by spade
- Hair, cloth, and gnawed bone in a scrape
- Glints in a cellar or a drain
- Bite-fever in a burial party
- Birds and dogs staying off a field that should have been cleared

Behaviour
They live where men put their dead and where men have fallen: crypt, plague-pit, forgotten cellar, last year’s battle. They do not hold a forest. They do not hold a fen. Light, straight streets, and a press of the living drive them under.

They go on all fours. They dig. They pull a corpse out faster than a sexton can put one in. They will take a living walker if the walker is alone, drunk, or already down. They will not charge a prepared line for preference. They will swamp an unwary detail.

Packs hold a nest of scrap and hair. One dominant does not wear a crown; it keeps the best of the meat. They breed in the nest. That is how a stretch fills. A bite brings plague: fever, pus, a rotten wound. Most bitten men die as men. They do not stand up as ghouls for that reason alone.

An unburned ghoul can quicken again if it is left in the dark. A neglected pit feeds the nest and draws more. Burn the bodies they have used. Leave one in the hole and the hole stays live.

False Moon makes them bolder on the surface and faster at a fresh grave. It does not march them down the high street in daylight.

Encounter
One or two in a ditch is a nasty fight, not a battle (Harm 3).
A nest is numbers, dark, and the smell (Rarity 7, Collective 6).
Battlefield and pit-edge are the likely ground, not the Hornwood floor.
Blade ends the one in front of you. Fire ends the stretch.
Biome is Any. Domain is Land. Then HSI: they want the dead of men, not a climate.

Notes
