# Entity_Format.txt
# Quick format for a new entity sheet.
# One body. Not the bestiary essay.
# Law lives in the Core files. This file is the shape.

--------------------------------------------------
0. NAME
--------------------------------------------------

File: Entity_(Locked name).txt
  Entity_Ghoul.txt
  Entity_Rock_Troll.txt
  Entity_Pale_Lurker.txt
Do not use Entities_Unnatural_.
One sheet is one body. A second size is a
second file, or a note under the mass line.
Point the bestiary at this file. Point this
file at the bestiary.

Header, in this order:
  # Entity_(Name).txt
  # GM sheet. One line on which body.
  # Bestiary: Bestiary_(Name).txt
  #   Height. Weight band. Sheet uses X kg.
  # Traits: Core_Traits.txt
  # Injury: Injury_Tables_(table).txt
  #   Why this table, if it is not obvious.
  # Size law: Core_Characteristics_Skills.txt §3
  # Pathogens, venom, armour: only if it has them.

--------------------------------------------------
1. TITLE BLOCK
--------------------------------------------------

--------------------------------------------------
NAME
--------------------------------------------------

Size N. Category. Injury: table.
Bestiary mass. Sheet uses one number in that band.
Height or span. What does not change Size.
Locked name. Spoken name, if it differs.

--------------------------------------------------
2. CHARACTERISTICS
--------------------------------------------------

Nine, then Attacks. Then the derived line.

  S  Per  T  Fel  Int  Ag  WP  WS  BS
  Attacks

  SB   TB
  M    show the sum. Agility ÷ 10, then Size.
  NAC
  Wounds    floor(TB × 3 × Size modifier)
  Initiative    Agility + 1d10

Size add to S and T, from the size table:
  1 −30   2 −20   3 −10   4 +0
  5 +10   6 +20   7 +30   8 +40
  9 +50   10 +60
Write the sum and the parts.
  S  60    (40 + 20 Size)

Wound modifier after TB × 3:
  1 ×0.25   2 ×0.50   3 ×0.75   4 ×1
  5 ×1.25   6 ×1.5    7 ×2.5    8 ×5
  9 ×7.5    10 ×10
Floor it.

M = (Agility ÷ 10) + Size adjustment.
Keep one decimal. Size adjustment is
AB − 3 up to AB + 6. Do not drop to AgB.

To-hit against a man (Size 4):
  difference in Size, tens.
  Larger striking smaller is a penalty.
  Smaller striking larger is a bonus.
  Fluid Bulk (x) offsets both, and stealth.
  Floor 0. It does not change Size.

--------------------------------------------------
3. MOVEMENT
--------------------------------------------------

Sheet reference. Not live.
Agility and Fatigue do not rewrite this.

  Half        floor(M × 2)     Move, Half Action
  Full        floor(M × 4)     two Moves
  Charge      floor(M × 4)
  Jump / Leap floor(M + SB) running
              half that, round up, standing
  Run         floor(M × 6)

Quadruped doubles the ground columns.
Say so on the line. Leap uses that doubled M.
Difficult ground is not on this block.

--------------------------------------------------
4. NATURAL ARMOUR
--------------------------------------------------

  Name (Cut/Pierce/Blunt)
  Slots. Name the gaps.
  NAC is already N. Do not add it twice.
Skip the block if it has no hide.
Worn kit points at its armour file.
Do not paste Armour.txt into the sheet.

--------------------------------------------------
5. SKILLS
--------------------------------------------------

(X) is known, at the skill base.
(10) is a bonus on top of known.
Unknown is half the base. Do not list those.

  Athletics (X)      number
    Mean of the characteristics it uses.

Base is the mean of the characteristics
the skill draws on, then the training step.
Known is the base. (10) is base + 10.

No Parry unless the sheet has Parry *
or a shield that grants the test.
Say which.

--------------------------------------------------
6. ATTACK PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

Write it like Weaponry.txt.

  Name                    Natural / group
    (Cut/Pierce/Blunt)  Reach     qualities
    1d10 + SB, unless the line says otherwise.
    No Parry. No Disarm, unless a limb is off.

Qualities use (x). Snare (0). Entangle (10).
AP (2). Impact. Fast (10).
A venom names the Agent and the file.
A Wound past Protection is the door,
unless the Agent says otherwise.
Bestial bodies do not Aim or Called Shot.

--------------------------------------------------
7. TRAITS
--------------------------------------------------

Law: Core_Traits.txt.
Name and (x). One line on what it does
on this body. Do not paste the trait essay.

Common:
  Dweller (biome tags)
  Bestial          no complex list
  Wall-Runner      spiders
  Dog-gaited       four-limb scrapes
  Quadruped        doubles ground yards
  Fluid Bulk (x)   offsets Size hit and hide
  Fear (x)         1–4. Core_Fear.txt
  Night Vision     stars. Not a black cave.
  Dark-Sight       no light at all
  Flyer            only if it flies
  Regeneration (x)

--------------------------------------------------
8. BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

How it acts. Not the bestiary essay.
Hungry. Pack or solitary. What it will
not do. Hurt: when it breaks.
False Moon, if it changes anything.
Harm and Collective, from the bestiary.

--------------------------------------------------
9. RUN
--------------------------------------------------

The turn. Attacks. Parry or not. Dodge on
what. The blow that empties the Wounds.
One line on the finish: burn, throat, nest.

--------------------------------------------------
10. DO NOT
--------------------------------------------------

Do not put the HSI table on the sheet.
Do not put coin on the sheet.
Do not give flight to an earthbound body.
Do not give Parry to a Bestial body.
Do not give a man-market price to kit
that does not fit a man.
Do not leave Entities_Unnatural_ as the name.
