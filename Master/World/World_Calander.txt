# The Common Reckoning of Virath

HEADER NOTE
The year is the same length and shape as a real terrestrial year.
Twelve months. Four seasons. The same number of days.
Only the names differ.
This is the trader’s and scholar’s calendar. Local cultures keep their own feast-names on top of it.

The natural moon is Lorn.
The False Moon is not used for reckoning.

---

THE YEAR

365 days in a common year.
366 in a leap year, every fourth year.
Weeks of 7 days, if a culture bothers to count them that way.

---

SEASONS

Thaw
The spring. Snow-melt, first plough, lambing.
Months: Budmoon, Lambmoon, Greenleaf

Highsun
The summer. Long days, hay, dry roads.
Months: Flowering, Highsun, Aftersun

Harvest
The autumn. Cutting, pressing, slaughter for winter.
Months: Reaping, Fallwine, Leafbare

Longnight
The winter. Short days, store-eating, closed high passes.
Months: Frostfall, Deepcold, Icewane

---

SEASON BOUNDS

Thaw        Greenleaf 1 – Flowering 30
Highsun     Highsun 1 – Aftersun 30
Harvest     Reaping 1 – Leafbare 30
Longnight   Frostfall 1 – Budmoon 31

Budmoon and Lambmoon sit in Longnight’s tail and Thaw’s first breath.
For modifiers, treat:
- Budmoon, Lambmoon as late Longnight / early Thaw
- Use the month if a finer grain is needed

---

NOTES

- Equatorial and southern lands keep the same names and reverse the heat.
- A leap day is added at the end of Lambmoon.
- False-moon nights are dated on this calendar; they do not change its length.
- Seasonal price modifiers, when written, key off these four seasons.
