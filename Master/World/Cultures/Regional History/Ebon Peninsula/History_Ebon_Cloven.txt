# History_Ebon_Cloven
# Note for the Isernvolk and Kannavi pages.
# Not a people-file. Peoples_Isernvolk.txt
# has the landing, Osric, the compact, and
# the hundred skinwights. It does not yet
# name the Cloven. Copy this across when
# those pages are open.

The Ebon Peninsula was near as densely
forested as Kannavald Deepwood still is.
The Kannavi were already there, small,
scattered, against Unveiling things in
the wood. The Isernvolk landed on the
western shore and aided their fellow man.

The mutual aid the alliance was built on
was the cleansing of the Cloven. Beastmen.
Bestiary_Cloven.txt. The height of the
dark ages is that herd. Centuries of
grinding fight. Not one battle and not
one hanged hundred.

The skinwights at Osricford stay the
shore-founding. Do not replace that
telling with this one. Both are true.
The long war was in the trees.

They were near exterminated. Small bands
persist in the deepest reaches. The
Deepwood is what the taming did not
finish. A herdstone there is a remnant.
The pale is not a dark-age range.

Later the Kannavi resented how the
Isernvolk grew and consumed. They still
honour the compact. The compact’s first
work was this cleansing. The canopy-watch
against Unveiling things is the same duty,
thinned.
