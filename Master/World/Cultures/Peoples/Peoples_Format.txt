# Peoples_Format.txt
# How to write a Peoples file.
# Locked names, dates, and cross-references win. Do not invent major history that would fight an existing file.
# Status: UPDATED — phenotype and Architecture tiers added.

File name
Peoples_[Name].txt
Use the locked formal name.
Spaces become underscores.
Hyphens in a people-name may be kept.

--------------------------------------------------
HEADER
--------------------------------------------------
# Peoples_[Name]

Umbrella name
[Formal]. [Common]. [Short / slang]. [Regional usage].

What this file is
[One to three sentences. Stock, linguistic base, core identity, place in the larger cultural layer.]

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
[Wave, parent stock, settlement pattern, what was retained or lost. GM-only context.]

--------------------------------------------------
DEEP NAME VS SURFACE NAME
--------------------------------------------------
Deep / formal: [Name].
Surface / contemporary: [Names].
Nickname: [If any. Note whether it is allowed, common, or forbidden in formal use.]

--------------------------------------------------
SHARED CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------
- [Descent or right]
- [Pride or core value]
- [Identity marker]
- [Suspicion or taboo]

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION TO OTHER PEOPLES
--------------------------------------------------
[Named people]: [One to three sentences on standing, tension, alliance, or absorption.]
[Repeat as needed.]

--------------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY AND CULTURE
--------------------------------------------------
[Where they live. Core skills. What shaped the customs.]

--------------------------------------------------
[OPTIONAL MAJOR HISTORY BLOCKS]
--------------------------------------------------
Use named sections when a people has a locked event.
Examples:
THE TRANS-OCEANIC SCHISM
MARK HISTORIES — [REGION]
LANDING HISTORY — [EVENT]
POLITY — [REGION]

Keep each block self-contained. Cross-reference other files rather than repeating full detail.

--------------------------------------------------
LANDMARKS
--------------------------------------------------
Named places, monuments, or sites that belong to this people.
Cross-reference Geography or Architecture files where they exist.

--------------------------------------------------
PERSONS OF NOTE
--------------------------------------------------
Dated headings.
Lock the person, the date (B:E / A:D), and the one-line claim.
Do not expand into full biography here.

--------------------------------------------------
NAMING
--------------------------------------------------
Patterns for personal names, house names, and place-names.
Note what is formal, what is common, and what is slang.

--------------------------------------------------
CUSTOMS — RESERVE
--------------------------------------------------
Short locked customs that do not fit the nine-point structure
or that must be visible at a glance.
Empty is allowed.

--------------------------------------------------
SOCIETAL CUSTOMS
--------------------------------------------------
Standalone. Does not replace prior sections.
Empty lines mean the point is not locked here.
Nine points:

1. Social Order & Stratification
   * Social Hierarchy
   * Power Dynamics
   * Social Mobility

2. Etiquette, Honor, & Taboos
   * Public Deference
   * Hospitality Codes
   * Conversational Norms
   * Shame vs. Guilt

3. Dress, Grooming, & Aesthetic Expression
   * Class Signifiers
   * Modesty and Exposure
   * Grooming & Modification
   * Utility vs. Status

4. Cultural Virtues & Vices
   * The Supreme Virtues
   * The Celebrated Vices
   * The Unpardonable Sins

5. Family Structure & Kinship
   * Household Units
   * Lineage & Inheritance
   * Marriage & Partnership
   * Childhood & Old Age

6. Religious Expression & Cosmic Worldview
   * The Pantheon / Belief System
   * Sacred Spaces & Clergy
   * Rituals & Holidays

7. Law, Justice, & Conflict Resolution
   * (as locked per people)

8. Leisure, Art, & Material Culture
   * Artistic Mediums
   * Recreation & Sports
   * Stimulants & Cuisine

   Architecture
   How the people build, in three tiers.

   Rural
     Homes, farmsteads, village halls, field walls, mills.
     Materials, plan, and what is ordinary.

   Urban
     Town and city fabric: streets, houses, markets, guild halls, ordinary walls.
     How density and trade change the build.

   Grand
     Fortifications, citadels, monuments, palaces, great churches or works.
     What is built to last or to be seen.

   Keep each tier short. Name materials and the characteristic form.
   Empty is allowed if not yet locked.

9. Economic & Subsistence Foundation
   * Mode of Production
   * Attitude Toward Wealth

--------------------------------------------------

Cephalic_Index = "..." (note)
Vertical_Index = "..." (note)
Facial_Structure = "..."
Nasal_Index = "..." (note)
Frontal_Region = "..."

Optional Notes line for range and use.
Terms follow standard anthropological descriptors.
Not a rigid type. Individuals vary.
Use for visual consistency in art, description, and NPC generation.

--------------------------------------------------
NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
Cross-references, open questions, and locks that do not fit above.
Do not invent major history here.
