# Peoples_Palatini

Umbrella / parent
Pionari stock with an Ordinari admin line. Valenidia. High-seat people of the Spine.

Formal name
Palatini (locked).

Common names
First-Seat. True Heirs (their mouth).
Spine-lords / hollow-lords (lowland mouth).

What this file is
The conservative Pionari–Ordinari house that fled the valley in the Unveiling and holds Le Conche Verdi.
They claim all Valenidia by ancient charter and by hydrological right they no longer operate.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
Wave 1 Pionari core with a Wave-of-order Ordinari administrative line.
Working blood-claim: about 80% Pionari, 20% Ordinari admin lineage.
Treat those numbers as identity and marriage-book, not as a lab.
Pre-collapse, the Spine and Valenidia were premium living space. A portion of the first Ordinari sat here. Pionari engineered the water and the green.
Palatini meaning: from Palatinus — the palace / the founding hill — softened. In-world: the First-Seat bloodlines, the True Heirs.
High-born bureaucrat-engineers. Charter first. The ditch second.

--------------------------------------------------
DEEP NAME VS SURFACE NAME
--------------------------------------------------
Deep: Palatini.
Surface: First-Seat, True Heirs.
Place-anchor: Le Conche Verdi.

--------------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY AND CULTURE
--------------------------------------------------
Seat: Le Conche Verdi — the inhabited hollows of the middle Orosian Spine.
Insulated, sun-warmed, low alpine valleys. They did not stay to fight the malaria floor.
Culture of charter, pedigree, memory of the Golden Age when Palatini and Idraulici ran Valenidia together.
Look down on the valley city-states as squatters, even when those states still quote Ordinari law.

--------------------------------------------------
THE BREAK
--------------------------------------------------
Unveiling.
The engineered valley failed. Malaria swamp. Warring tribes.
Conservative Palatini went up the Spine into Le Conche Verdi.
They kept the books. They lost the races.

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION
--------------------------------------------------
Idraulici: old partners. Pragmatists who stayed in the wet. Respected as wrights. Resented as people who would not climb.
Valley city-state elites: lingering Ordinari pedigree. Legal cousins who stole the chairs.
Novari: not in this file. Later peoples.
Syllvani of the Plateau: elder Pionari cousins over Macigno Serrata. Isolationists. Not a compact yet.
