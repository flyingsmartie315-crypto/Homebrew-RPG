# Peoples_Isernvolk

Umbrella name
Isernvolk (formal). Isernmen (common). Iserns / Irons (short / slang). Markings (regional / frontier usage).

What this file is
An Ingenari-descended people of the Northwestern continent. Anglo-Germanic-Scandi linguistic base. Iron-working, frontier, and sea-crossing identity. One of the core stocks that will form the “fantasy Europe” cultural layer.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
Second Wave Ingenari stock that retained a strong craft-and-arms memory.
Settled the western shores and marks after the Pionari had already shaped the green world.
Over millennia developed a distinct Anglo-Germanic-Scandi spoken base rather than preserving the old Latin Ingenari root.
Iron, sea-crossing, and frontier mark identity became central to their self-image.

--------------------------------------------------
DEEP NAME VS SURFACE NAME
--------------------------------------------------
Deep / mythic lean: Isernvolk / Isernmen (iron-people, iron-men).
Surface / contemporary: Vestrings / Westmen / Markings (western sea, western men, the mark).
Jarns / Jarnfolk noted as seed for a future related but distinct branch (purer Scandi-iron feel).

--------------------------------------------------
SHARED CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------
- Descent from the Raisers who built and held the first works
- Pride in iron, craft, and the strength to cross the western sea
- Frontier identity: the mark as both home and edge
- Word, oath, and iron as binding forces
- Suspicion of lone strangers and of those who claim without labour

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION TO OTHER WAVES
--------------------------------------------------
Pionari / Syllvani: elder stewards of the green. Respected as first blood; tension when stewardship limits extraction or settlement.
Ordinari: Seal and elite who came with the Multitude. Useful when they bring order and charters; resented when they command without craft.
Novari: the Multitude. Labour and numbers that filled the marks. Some Isernvolk stocks absorbed or ruled over Novari-descended locals; others clashed with them.
Aperti: small, ill-omened. The Openers who probed the thinning. Rarely central to Isernvolk myth.

--------------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY AND CULTURE
--------------------------------------------------
Western shores and marks of the Northwestern continent.
Iron-working, shipcraft, and frontier settlement are core skills.
Customs shaped by dense forest, lake-and-burn country, and the need to hold ground against older or denser neighbours.
Will form one of the two main linguistic-cultural poles of the “fantasy Europe” layer alongside the Syllvani.
