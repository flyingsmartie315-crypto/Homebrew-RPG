# Peoples_Isernvolk

Umbrella name
Isernvolk (formal). Isernmen (common). Iserns / Irons (short / slang). Markings (regional / frontier usage).

What this file is
An Ingenari-descended people of the Northwestern continent. Anglo-Germanic-Scandi linguistic base. Iron-working, frontier, and sea-crossing identity. One of the core stocks that will form the “fantasy Europe” cultural layer.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
Second Wave Ingenari stock that retained a strong craft-and-arms memory.
Settled the western shores and marks after the Pionari had already shaped the green world.
Over millennia developed a distinct Anglo-Germanic-Scandi spoken base rather than preserving the old Latin Ingenari root.
Iron, sea-crossing, and frontier mark identity became central to their self-image.

--------------------------------------------------
DEEP NAME VS SURFACE NAME
--------------------------------------------------
Deep / mythic lean: Isernvolk / Isernmen (iron-people, iron-men).
Surface / contemporary: Vestrings / Westmen / Markings (western sea, western men, the mark).
Jarnvolk — first offshoot. Locked. File: Peoples_Jarnvolk.txt. Polar stock of Dauðavík. Not a second Isernvolk.

--------------------------------------------------
SHARED CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------
- Descent from the Raisers who built and held the first works
- Pride in iron, craft, and the strength to cross the western sea
- Frontier identity: the mark as both home and edge
- Word, oath, and iron as binding forces
- Suspicion of lone strangers and of those who claim without labour

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION TO OTHER WAVES
--------------------------------------------------
Pionari / Syllvani: elder stewards of the green. Respected as first blood; tension when stewardship limits extraction or settlement.
Kannavi: peninsula wood-Syllvani. Ancient allies from the landing, when both were small scattered groups carving a home out of a terrifying wood.
Isernvolk view them as distant, slightly odd outsiders — harsh, aloof, honest to a fault.
The alliance is still honoured. Kannavi will also mark a border and mean it.
Detail in Peoples_Kannavi.txt.
Cortani: horse-hall Syllvani of Coritania. Later war and occupation, then absorption of the Adernvolk elite.
Ordinari: Seal and elite who came with the Multitude. Useful when they bring order and charters; resented when they command without craft.
Novari: the Multitude. Labour and numbers that filled the marks. Some Isernvolk stocks absorbed or ruled over Novari-descended locals; others clashed with them.
Aperti: small, ill-omened. The Openers who probed the thinning. Rarely central to Isernvolk myth.

--------------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY AND CULTURE
--------------------------------------------------
Western shores and marks of the Northwestern continent.
Iron-working, shipcraft, and frontier settlement are core skills.
Customs shaped by dense forest, lake-and-burn country, and the need to hold ground against older or denser neighbours.
Will form one of the two main linguistic-cultural poles of the “fantasy Europe” layer alongside the Syllvani.

--------------------------------------------------
THE TRANS-OCEANIC SCHISM
--------------------------------------------------
The Isern crossing of the Atlantic-scale ocean is the first fracture of the blood.
The fleet tracked the Ebon Latitude Line — cool temperate maritime corridor.
A multi-week polar storm and a north-setting current took the rear wing off that line.
Jarnvard, younger brother of High King Osric, commanded that wing.
They did not recover the heading. They were spent onto the ice-rim, some 2,400 miles north of the intended track.

Jarnvard put ashore. Masts gone. Winter on them. The ships would not live on the open current.
Osric kept the vanguard on the easterly path. He would not turn into unmapped arctic mist.
Osric’s hulls grounded on the Ebon Peninsula. Landing-seat: Osricford / Ocs Landing.

The distance and the unmapped grid did the rest.
Osric’s people counted Jarnvard’s fleet dead in the ice. They mourned them as ghosts.
Jarnvard trusted Osric to have led the remaining hulls to a better future than the ice-rim. That trust turns, generation by generation, into resentment of the soft southerners on the mainland.

Two civilizations from one fleet. Neither is a failed copy of the other.
Jarnvolk detail: Peoples_Jarnvolk.txt.
Island lock: Dauðavík — the Dead-Bight / the Killing Gulf. Where the ice dies. From the sea it reads as a grave. From the shore it is dead-rock walls and killing-hot water under the fields.

--------------------------------------------------
MARK HISTORIES — EBON PENINSULA
--------------------------------------------------
The Ebon Peninsula was once nearly as densely forested as Kannavald Deepwood still is.
The Kannavi were already there: small, scattered, in constant struggle against Unveiling perils in the woods.

Isernvolk landed at the western shore and aided their fellow man.
First king in this history: Osric.
Landing-seat: Osricford. Common and older shore-name: Ocs Landing.
There a hundred skinwights were hanged. That hanging is told as a founding of the tamed peninsula.

Over centuries the peninsula was opened. Floors, strands, and pits became Isern work. The Deepwood is what the taming did not finish.
The Kannavi — Hollow-folk, cousins in custom to the Still Stones — were raised into alliance and standing. They were not erased. Outside the Deepwood they became a distinct minority. Inside it they remain the majority.
The first years were amicable: two small peoples against a bad wood.
Later the Kannavi resented how Isernvolk grew and consumed. They still honour the ancient alliance. They became isolationist and fiercely independent. Careless Isern are reminded where Kannavald’s borders are.
Isernvolk, for their part, call them harsh and aloof, a little odd, and honest to a fault — outsiders who remain allies.

Eyrenal was already a remnant megastructure. Isernvolk colonised the hanging vale.
There an Isern lord bonded a gryphon and became the first rider. That bond is the seed of the Order.
Early riders were the Eyrenhost, not knights. That name predates Knights Gryphon and is still used in contemporary speech for the vale-host.
The knightly tradition comes later, from Coritania. Knights Gryphon is the later style. Eyrenhost remains the older and living name.

Detail lives in Peoples_Kannavi.txt and the Eyrenal / Order files when those are written.

--------------------------------------------------
MARK HISTORIES — CORITANIA
--------------------------------------------------
Later incursion. Not a second landing-feast. War.

A small Isern ruling class — the Adernvolk, Adelings by rank, Whitmarks by the look of their towers — fought the Cortani host.
Isernvolk brought ordered foot, iron coverage, pavise and crossbow, and the long pole and godendag learned against horse.
Cortani brought the saddle, the hill-hall, and a warrior caste of lance and bow.

Opening years: Cortani win the fields.
Middle years: Adernvolk win the holds. White keeps on old hall-hills. Land partitioned. Crops at scale.
Long war: neither people dies. Near centuries of an unruly host.
End: strategic marriages. The Cortani absorb the occupier. The living people become Coritanian.
Adern blood remains a right-to-rule claim in the upper caste.
The knight and the tourney — Cortani saddle plus Isern iron — travel back west. The Eyrenhost learn knightly custom from that influence, not the other way around. Knights Gryphon is the later style-name. Eyrenhost is still said.

Detail lives in Peoples_Cortani.txt, Peoples_Adernvolk.txt, and Wars_of_the_Whitmarks.txt.
Peoples_Coritanian.txt is the fusion file, not yet written.
