# Peoples_Isernvolk

Umbrella name
Isernvolk (formal). Isernmen (common). Iserns / Irons (short / slang). Markings (regional / frontier usage).

What this file is
An Ingenari-descended people of the Northwestern continent. Anglo-Germanic-Scandi linguistic base. Iron-working, frontier, and sea-crossing identity. One of the core stocks that will form the “fantasy Europe” cultural layer.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
Second Wave Ingenari stock that retained a strong craft-and-arms memory.
Settled the western shores and marks after the Pionari had already shaped the green world.
Over millennia developed a distinct Anglo-Germanic-Scandi spoken base rather than preserving the old Latin Ingenari root.
Iron, sea-crossing, and frontier mark identity became central to their self-image.

--------------------------------------------------
DEEP NAME VS SURFACE NAME
--------------------------------------------------
Deep / mythic lean: Isernvolk / Isernmen (iron-people, iron-men).
Surface / contemporary: Vestrings / Westmen / Markings (western sea, western men, the mark).
Jarnvolk — first offshoot. Locked. File: Peoples_Jarnvolk.txt. Polar stock of Dauðavík. Not a second Isernvolk.

--------------------------------------------------
SHARED CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------
- Descent from the Raisers who built and held the first works
- Pride in iron, craft, and the strength to cross the western sea
- Frontier identity: the mark as both home and edge
- Word, oath, and iron as binding forces
- Suspicion of lone strangers and of those who claim without labour

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION TO OTHER WAVES
--------------------------------------------------
Pionari / Syllvani: elder stewards of the green. Respected as first blood; tension when stewardship limits extraction or settlement.
Kannavi: peninsula wood-Syllvani. Ancient allies from the landing, when both were small scattered groups carving a home out of a terrifying wood.
Isernvolk view them as distant, slightly odd outsiders — harsh, aloof, honest to a fault.
The alliance is still honoured. Kannavi will also mark a border and mean it.
Detail in Peoples_Kannavi.txt.
Cortani: horse-hall Syllvani of Coritania. Later war and occupation, then absorption of the Adernvolk elite.
Ordinari: Seal and elite who came with the Multitude. Useful when they bring order and charters; resented when they command without craft.
Novari: the Multitude. Labour and numbers that filled the marks. Some Isernvolk stocks absorbed or ruled over Novari-descended locals; others clashed with them.
Aperti: small, ill-omened. The Openers who probed the thinning. Rarely central to Isernvolk myth.

--------------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY AND CULTURE
--------------------------------------------------
Western shores and marks of the Northwestern continent.
Iron-working, shipcraft, and frontier settlement are core skills.
Customs shaped by dense forest, lake-and-burn country, and the need to hold ground against older or denser neighbours.
Will form one of the two main linguistic-cultural poles of the “fantasy Europe” layer alongside the Syllvani.

--------------------------------------------------
THE TRANS-OCEANIC SCHISM
--------------------------------------------------
The Isern crossing of the Atlantic-scale ocean is the first fracture of the blood.
The fleet tracked the Ebon Latitude Line — cool temperate maritime corridor.
A multi-week polar storm and a north-setting current took the rear wing off that line.
Jarnvard, younger brother of High King Osric, commanded that wing.
They did not recover the heading. They were spent onto the ice-rim, some 2,400 miles north of the intended track.

Jarnvard put ashore. Masts gone. Winter on them. The ships would not live on the open current.
Osric kept the vanguard on the easterly path. He would not turn into unmapped arctic mist.
Osric’s hulls grounded on the Ebon Peninsula. Landing-seat: Osricford / Ocs Landing.

The distance and the unmapped grid did the rest.
Osric’s people counted Jarnvard’s fleet dead in the ice. They mourned them as ghosts.
Jarnvard trusted Osric to have led the remaining hulls to a better future than the ice-rim. That trust turns, generation by generation, into resentment of the soft southerners on the mainland.

Two civilizations from one fleet. Neither is a failed copy of the other.
Jarnvolk detail: Peoples_Jarnvolk.txt.
Island lock: Dauðavík — the Dead-Bight / the Killing Gulf. Where the ice dies. From the sea it reads as a grave. From the shore it is dead-rock walls and killing-hot water under the fields.

--------------------------------------------------
MARK HISTORIES — EBON PENINSULA
--------------------------------------------------
The Ebon Peninsula was once nearly as densely forested as Kannavald Deepwood still is.
The Kannavi were already there: small, scattered, in constant struggle against Unveiling perils in the woods.

Isernvolk landed at the western shore and aided their fellow man.
First Dar Emait in this history: Osric.
Landing-seat: Osricford. Common and older shore-name: Ocs Landing.

First sight of the settled land: the Ebon Spire, black, with the white sun on its crown in the east.
The fleet came from the western ocean. The continent lay ahead, eastward. Dawn stood behind the mountain. That is Earth-normal sky. Do not reverse the sun for this image — a west-rising sun would crown the sea behind the hulls, not the Spire.
Osric took that sight as his arms: black shield-mountain, white sun in the east. Isern heraldry of the peninsula still carries it.
The colours stay in the cloth. Even when dyes are cheap, Isern dress on the peninsula runs black and white — direct, unambiguous, harsh contrast. The people like the look because it is the look.

There a hundred skinwights were hanged. That hanging is told as a founding of the tamed peninsula.

Over centuries the peninsula was opened. Floors, strands, and pits became Isern work. The Deepwood is what the taming did not finish.
The Kannavi — Hollow-folk, cousins in custom to the Still Stones — were raised into alliance and standing. They were not erased. Outside the Deepwood they became a distinct minority. Inside it they remain the majority.
The first years were amicable: two small peoples against a bad wood.
Later the Kannavi resented how Isernvolk grew and consumed. They still honour the ancient alliance. They became isolationist and fiercely independent. Careless Isern are reminded where Kannavald’s borders are.
Isernvolk, for their part, call them harsh and aloof, a little odd, and honest to a fault — outsiders who remain allies.

Eyrenal was already a remnant megastructure. Isernvolk colonised the hanging vale.
There an Isern lord bonded a gryphon and became the first rider. That bond is the seed of the Order.
Gryphons are earthbound. Leap and shock. Not royal flight. Not a sky-host.
Early riders were the Eyrenhost, not knights. That name predates Knights Gryphon and is still used in contemporary speech for the vale-host.
The knightly tradition comes later, from Coritania. Knights Gryphon is the later style. Eyrenhost remains the older and living name.

Detail lives in Peoples_Kannavi.txt and the Eyrenal / Order files when those are written.

--------------------------------------------------
MARK HISTORIES — CORITANIA
--------------------------------------------------
Later incursion. Not a second landing-feast. War.

A small Isern ruling class — the Adernvolk, Adelings by rank, Whitmarks by the look of their towers — fought the Cortani host.
Isernvolk brought ordered foot, iron coverage, pavise and crossbow, and the long pole and godendag learned against horse.
Cortani brought the saddle, the hill-hall, and a warrior caste of lance and bow.

Opening years: Cortani win the fields.
Middle years: Adernvolk win the holds. White keeps on old hall-hills. Land partitioned. Crops at scale.
Long war: neither people dies. Near centuries of an unruly host.
End: strategic marriages. The Cortani absorb the occupier. The living people become Coritanian.
Adern blood remains a right-to-rule claim in the upper caste.
The knight and the tourney — Cortani saddle plus Isern iron — travel back west. The Eyrenhost learn knightly custom from that influence, not the other way around. Knights Gryphon is the later style-name. Eyrenhost is still said.

Detail lives in Peoples_Cortani.txt, Peoples_Adernvolk.txt, and Wars_of_the_Whitmarks.txt.
Peoples_Coritanian.txt is the fusion file, not yet written.

--------------------------------------------------
POLITY — EBON PENINSULA
--------------------------------------------------
Two titles for the one crown. War-files use Dar Emait for the landing-line rulers (Osric, Harthold, Thormund). Dar Kaiserarch is the later imperial style, after the marks are pulled under one appointment-system.

Dar Emait (The Iron Lord / Catalyst)
Ancestral title. Contraction of Eisen-Meister / Element-Macht.
Authority rooted in the foundries, metal-pits, and Ingenari ruins. Osric is remembered as the first Dar Emait of the landing. Osric held it. Harthold and Thormund hold it in the war-files. Dar Kaiserarch is later.

Dar Kaiserarch (The Kaiser-Archon)
Contemporary imperial title. Kaiser plus Archon.
Taken after the local regions of the Ebon Peninsula were pulled under one crown. Signals the shift from a loose forge-lord confederation to a central empire — on paper. In the yard a Kaiserarch still commands only from strength.

The marks sit on two legitimacies.

1. Crown appointments — standard regions
Der Zonarch (The Zone-Archon). Spoken: Arch [Name].
Imperial magistrate over a designated sector. Ordinari “zone” plus Archon.
Non-hereditary. Serves at the Kaiserarch’s pleasure. Taxes, resource grids, white keeps.
A failed quota can end a Zonarch. Replacement or the axe.
In fact each Zonarch is a power: own resources, own spears, own vassals. Noble houses compete for the chair. The Kaiserarch who treats them as clerks loses the chair himself.

Westmark, Ebormark, Highmarch, and the Rhennod Marches sit under this system unless a later lock says otherwise. Eyrenal / the Eyrenhost is an order-seat inside Ebormark, not a second crown.

2. Hereditary exception — the Deepwood
Dar Waldward (The Wood-Warden). Wald + ward / warda.
Held only by the ruling house of Kannavald Deepwood.
By the Ancient Landing Compact with Osric: hereditary, outside the Zonarch list, outside the Kaiserarch’s gift.
Duty: keep the canopy against Unveiling things. Autonomy at the forest border.
The compact is still honoured. Careless Isern are still shown the line.
Detail: Peoples_Kannavi.txt.

Osricford is the Kaiserarch’s port. The Waldward does not keep a banner on that quay.

--------------------------------------------------
NAMING — SHADOW AND CANOPY
--------------------------------------------------
Early Isern names on this peninsula were wood-names. They named what closed the sky, not the fields they later cut.
Draw first from Old Germanic (Proto-Germanic / Old High German / Old Saxon / Old Norse). Contemporary Isern is the worn mouth of that stock. Westmark is the later, offhand name of the cleared floor.

Old stems
- Shadow: PG *skadwaz → OHG scato / OS skado / OE sceadu / ON skuggi / Goth. skadus
- Valley: PG *dalaz → OHG tal / OS dal / ON dalr
- Wood, canopy: PG *walþuz → OHG / OS wald; also *hursti (hurst), ON skógr
- Cover, blanket: PG *þakją (thatch/cover) → OHG dah / OS thak; hide-cover: *huljaną → OHG hullen
- Deep: PG *deupaz → OHG tiof / OS diop / ON djúpr
- Endless / lasting: *endilaus; ever: *aiwin- → OHG ēwa; long: *langaz
- Home: *haimaz → OHG heim
- Mark / border-land: *markō → OHG marca
- Under: *under → OHG untar
- New: *neujaz → OHG niuwi
- The mountain as a person: Ebon / Eben / Spire — a presence. Do not rebuild it from *swartaz (that fight belongs to the Jarn).

Early compounds (old mouth)
- Valley in the shadow of the Spire: Scatodal, Untarscato, Ebendal
- Endless canopy: Ēwald, Langwald, Endilōswald
- Blanket of shadow: Thakscato, Hulisterwald, Scatowald
- Deeps under the Spire: Deop-untar-Eben, Tiofwald, Djúpgrund

Worn contemporary (what a 16th-century mark-man says)
- Schattendene, Ebenheim, Schattenmark, Hulsterholt, Spiregrund, Neuheim (from Niuwiheim)

Do not use Ebonmark. Ebormark is already the south vale.
Modern name of the cleared heartland: the Ardairn Valley. From *aiwin- → Ard + dair + n. Said (ard)(dern). Westmark remains the common offhand name.

--------------------------------------------------
CUSTOMS — RESERVE
--------------------------------------------------
They are human. They like drink, song, a hunt, a table, a joke. That is not the argument.
Among the well-to-do, polite society, being *seen* conservative and reserved is desirable. Fun happens. Display of fun is the fault.
A retreat is not a revel. Stígarborg in Highsun is the type: the capital elite go up, the terrace is beautiful, the house is quiet on purpose. By Isern polite measure that already counts as a feast. Do not write them as joyless. Do not write a Whitmark hall as a Cortani tourney-night.


--------------------------------------------------
SOCIETAL CUSTOMS
--------------------------------------------------
Standalone. Does not replace prior sections. Empty lines mean the point is not locked here.

1. Social Order & Stratification
* Social Hierarchy: Dar Emait (war-age) / Dar Kaiserarch (contemporary) over Zonarchs in standard marks. Dar Waldward is the hereditary Deepwood exception. Houses compete for Zonarch chairs.
* Power Dynamics: Crown commands from strength. A Zonarch is a magistrate on paper and a warlord in the yard. Quota-failure can mean the axe.
* Social Mobility: Appointment, not birth, for the Zonarchate. Noble lines still fight for the chair. Waldward is closed blood.
2. Etiquette, Honor, & Taboos
* Public Deference: Rank shown in iron, whitewash, and quiet. Display of heat is cheap.
* Hospitality Codes: A retreat is not a revel. Stígarborg is the type. Guest-right exists; noise is the insult.
* Conversational Norms: Blunt when work is the subject. Polite society is reserved on purpose.
* Shame vs. Guilt: Being *seen* unreserved is the polite fault. Fun happens. Display of fun is the fault. See CUSTOMS — RESERVE.
3. Dress, Grooming, & Aesthetic Expression
* Class Signifiers: Black and white contrast persists even when dyes exist. Direct, harsh, like the people. Ebon Spire and white sun on the arms.
* Modesty and Exposure: Not locked.
* Grooming & Modification: Not locked.
* Utility vs. Status: Iron and cloth that work. Pretty comes after the line holds.
4. Cultural Virtues & Vices
* The Supreme Virtues: Order, supply, a kept line, a kept compact, work that can be counted.
* The Celebrated Vices: Hard drinking and a hunt if they stay quiet enough to deny tomorrow.
* The Unpardonable Sins: Breaking Osric’s compact with the Kannavi; wasting a levy; treating a Zonarch as a clerk if you cannot replace him.
5. Family Structure & Kinship
* Household Units: Mark-households, keep-households. Not locked in detail.
* Lineage & Inheritance: Crown appointment vs Waldward blood. Adern offshoot later claims blood in Coritania.
* Marriage & Partnership: Strategic marriages used on the Cortani. Not the first tool at home.
* Childhood & Old Age: Not locked.
6. Religious Expression & Cosmic Worldview
* The Pantheon / Belief System: Not locked as a church. Unveiling is a fact they hang skinwights for. Ebon is a presence on the skyline.
* Sacred Spaces & Clergy: Not locked.
* Rituals & Holidays: Landing-memory. Osricford hanging-story.
* The Afterlife: Not locked.
7. Language, Lore, & Transmission of Knowledge
* Linguistic Style: Anglo-Germanic-Scandi. Old Germanic first on early names. Worn contemporary Isern.
* Mythology & History: Crossing, Osric, lost Jarnvard wing mourned as dead, taming of the peninsula, Whitmark wars.
* Education: Counting-house, foundry, levy-drill. Not locked as a school-system.
8. Leisure, Art, & Material Culture
* Artistic Mediums: Black-and-white cloth and arms. Whitewash and timber-on-stone in the Coritanian offshoot.
* Recreation & Sports: Hunt, table, Highsun retreat. Not tourney-first; that comes back from Coritania later.
* Stimulants & Cuisine: Not locked. They drink.
9. Economic & Subsistence Foundation
* Mode of Production: Iron, grain, livestock, sea-trade, quarry, pit. River as road (Värdå). Prison-labour in the Laed-Crags.
* Attitude Toward Wealth: Counted, stored, shipped. New pit-money is real and resented. Old watch-right is older.
