# Peoples_Idraulici

Umbrella / parent
Pionari stock. Valenidia. Water-wrights of the lowlands.
Ordinari influence is thin and practical, not a palace line.

Formal name
Idraulici (locked).
The Engineers. Master-Wrights.

Common names
Wrights. Race-men. Ditch-lords ( Palatini insult that stuck).

What this file is
The pragmatic Pionari who stayed on the valley floor when the Palatini climbed.
They fought the malaria swamp. They cut the races. Valle Nevale’s making is their work.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
Wave 1 Pionari, kept closer to the craft than to the charter.
Working blood-claim: about 99% Pionari, 1% fragmentary Ordinari root.
Treat 99% as a purity-story the wrights tell. It is a banner, not a count.
Pre-collapse they were the hands on the premium country the Palatini administered.
Name: the hydraulic engineers. Master-wrights of water, masonry, and the dam.

--------------------------------------------------
DEEP NAME VS SURFACE NAME
--------------------------------------------------
Deep: Idraulici.
Surface: Wrights, Engineers.
Place-anchor: the drained lowlands. Valle Nevale first among them.

--------------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY AND CULTURE
--------------------------------------------------
They live where the water sits if no one moves it.
Terraces, channels that trickle in Longnight and run in Highsun, bank-trees, the dammed lake, the margin-outflow.
Culture of maintenance. A race that is not cleaned is a swamp coming back.
They did not flee the Unveiling. They lost people to it and kept the work.

--------------------------------------------------
THE BREAK
--------------------------------------------------
Unveiling.
Valley becomes swamp and war.
Idraulici factions stay. Centuries of tribal fight to a city-state floor.
Walled cities rise on the drained ground.
Some lingering Ordinari descendants use legal pedigree to sit as sovereign elite of those city-states.
The Idraulici remain the reason the cities do not drown. That is leverage. It is not always a crown.
Novari inflow is not this people’s story. Held for later peoples.

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION
--------------------------------------------------
Palatini: old partners who left. First-Seat claims over water they do not stand in. Useful charters. Bad neighbours in a dry year.
City-state Ordinari elites: pay the wrights or the lake rises. Pedigree does not lift a sluice.
Novari: not in this file. Later peoples.
Cortani of the Clypeo-Barra shadow: horse-neighbours on the wet north face. Trade and raid, not kin.
