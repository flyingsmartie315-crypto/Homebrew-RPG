# Peoples_Syllvani

Umbrella name
Syllvani (formal). Syllvans (common). Syllvs (short / slang).

What this file is
A Pionari-descended people of the Northwestern continent.
Romance linguistic base that evolved into a softer, more sing-song, almost elvish sound over millennia, with a Welsh-flavoured naming layer.
They have held the Appolasian Plateau — the most natural fortress of the north-west — since before the Isernvolk arrived on the western shores.
One of the two core stocks that form the fantasy Europe cultural layer alongside the Isernvolk.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
Primarily Wave 1 — Pionari stock.
The original Latin base softened and phonetically drifted over thousands of years into a lighter, more melodic, almost elvish-sounding tongue.
A secondary Welsh-flavoured naming layer developed alongside it, giving many personal and place names a Celtic lilt within the broader Romance sound.
They are the elder blood on the Northwestern continent — the people whose stewardship claim predates the Isernvolk sea-crossing.
Their heartland is the Appolasian Plateau: the impact basin, central lake, and mountain-ringed forest that forms the most defensible natural fortress in the region.

--------------------------------------------------
DEEP NAME VS SURFACE NAME
--------------------------------------------------
Deep / mythic lean: Syllvani (from silva — the woodland people, the forest-born)
Surface / contemporary: Syllvans
Short / common: Syllvs

Naming note: Personal and place names often carry a Welsh-flavoured layer (e.g. soft consonants, melodic endings, occasional “ll”, “dd”, “rh” sounds) blended into the sing-song Latin base. This gives the Syllvani a distinct phonetic texture without breaking the overall Romance feel.

--------------------------------------------------
SHARED CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------
- Descent from the First Blood who shaped Virath’s living face
- Stewardship of the green world their ancestors made fit for blood
- The Appulse as sacred origin; the Appolasian Plateau as their unyielding heartland
- Taboo ground, named groves, and the memory of the First Men’s shaping hand
- Elder right on the Northwestern continent — they were here before the sea-crossers
- The Plateau as fortress: they hunkered down and held when others came

--------------------------------------------------
CULTURAL DEVELOPMENT — DARK AGE SURVIVAL
--------------------------------------------------
Because of their natural terrain advantage, the Syllvani outlasted the Dark Age period far better than most other groups.
This security allowed them to retain written records and long-standing intact historical memory.
As a result, they possess a clearer, more continuous understanding of their origins, the First Men’s shaping of Virath, and the true sequence of events leading to the Unveiling.
This has given them a distinctly long-term outlook on the world — decisions are weighed across generations rather than seasons or lifetimes.

--------------------------------------------------
CULTURAL TRAITS SHAPED BY SURVIVAL
--------------------------------------------------
Insular and isolationist outlook. The Plateau’s natural defences encouraged a closed, self-contained society that looks inward rather than outward.
Deep historical continuity. Written records and oral tradition survived largely intact, creating a culture that remembers its past with unusual clarity.
Long-term thinking. With a clearer view of history, the Syllvani plan across centuries. They are cautious, deliberate, and slow to act, but also deeply knowledgeable and resistant to short-term folly.
Stewardship as lived practice rather than myth. Because they remember what their ancestors actually did, the duty to maintain the green world feels immediate and practical, not abstract.

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION TO OTHER WAVES
--------------------------------------------------
Isernvolk: later arrivals from the western sea. Respected as craft-strong kin in the deep colony story; tension when they cut, mine, or press against the Plateau’s edges.
Ordinari: Seal and elite who came with the Multitude. Bargained with or resisted when they try to allot elder land by charter.
Novari: the Multitude. Some absorbed into Syllvani lands as labour or client peoples; others pushed into harder country. Mixed blood exists but is tightly controlled on the Plateau itself.
Aperti: the Openers. Often blamed in Syllvani myth for the souring of the land and the coming of the Unveiling.

--------------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY AND CULTURE
--------------------------------------------------
Heartland: Appolasian Plateau — the mountain-ringed impact basin with its central lake and dense forest. The most natural fortress in the north-west.
They have held this ground through the Isernvolk arrival, the loss of the Cradle, the Unveiling, and the long Dark Ages — emerging more intact than most.
Customs shaped by forest stewardship, lake-and-basin living, and the memory of shaping the green.
Will form one of the two main linguistic-cultural poles of the fantasy Europe layer — the softer, more melodic Romance pole (with Welsh-flavoured naming) against the Isernvolk’s harder Anglo-Germanic-Scandi pole.

--------------------------------------------------
NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
The Syllvani are defined by their long, unbroken hold on the Appolasian Plateau and their unusually intact historical memory.
Their name and identity are tied directly to the forest and the stewardship of the living world.
The Welsh-flavoured naming layer is a surface phonetic feature only — it does not change the underlying Romance linguistic base.
Jarns / Jarnfolk noted earlier as a future Isernvolk-related branch — not connected to the Syllvani line.
A separate terraforming branch of the original Pionari stock is noted as existing in the deep past (the ones who engineered Virath’s axial tilt); their relationship to the Syllvani is still to be defined.
