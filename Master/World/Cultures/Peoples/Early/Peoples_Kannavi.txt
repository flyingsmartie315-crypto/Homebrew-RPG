# Peoples_Kannavi

Umbrella / parent
Syllvani offshoot. Early category. Ebon Peninsula stock. Not the Plateau. Not the Cortani.

Formal name
Kannavi (locked).

Common names
Kannavs / Kannavsfolk (Isern and local speech).
Hollow-folk (nickname — allowed, common, not the formal name).
Deep Syllvs / wood-Syllvs (foreign shorthand).

What this file is
The Syllvani branch of the Ebon Peninsula.
Scattered across the peninsula at large as a distinct minority.
Still the majority in the Kannavald Deepwood local region.
Largely absorbed elsewhere into Isernvolk farm, town, and pit country.
Wood-edge trades remain their tell: lumber, trapping, charcoal, bowyery.
Linguistic base: Syllvani under a Nordic layer.

Coedani was a working proposal. Do not use it now.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
Wave 1 — Pionari / Syllvani line.
Never the fortress-people of the Appolasian Plateau. Never the horse-hall Cortani.
They held the peninsula’s woods, burns, and mountain-edges.
Isernvolk took the floors, strands, and pits. Absorption was the rule on open ground.
Under the Deepwood canopy they remained the people of the place.
Kannavald is their country-name as the Isern marks later wrote it. Kannavi is the folk-name that still matches the land in the woods.

Once the whole Ebon Peninsula was nearly as densely forested as the Deepwood still is.
The Kannavi were small, scattered, and in constant struggle against Unveiling perils in the woods.
When the Isernvolk landed they aided their fellow man. Over centuries the peninsula was tamed.
The Hollow-folk — cousins in custom to the Still Stones — were raised from that struggle into alliance and standing. The stone-law was kept: do not approach.
At Osricford (common: Ocs Landing) a hundred skinwights were hanged. Osric is locked as the first Isernvolk king in this people’s history.
Eyrenal was already a remnant megastructure. Isernvolk colonised the hanging vale. There an Isern lord bonded a gryphon and became the first rider — the seed of the Order. Early name: the Eyrenhost. Not knights. Knights Gryphon is later Coritanian-influenced style. Eyrenhost is still said.

Plateau Syllvani kept the basin and the record.
Cortani kept the horse and the hill-hall.
Kannavi kept the wood.

--------------------------------------------------
DEEP NAME VS SURFACE NAME
--------------------------------------------------
Deep / formal: Kannavi.
Surface: Kannavs, Deepwood folk.
Nickname: Hollow-folk.
Use Hollow-folk in common talk, stories, and Isern mouths. Do not put it on the people-file header as the only name.
The nickname touches the Still Stones / Hollows / Quiet Watchers without making the whole people the stones. They know the law: do not approach. Others call them Hollow-folk because they live nearest that law.

--------------------------------------------------
SHARED CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------
- Descent from First Blood who took the peninsula woods
- Right to the canopy trades: timber, trap, charcoal, bow
- Majority-right in the Deepwood even when the mark-law is Isern
- Suspicion of pit and plough when those eat stave-wood and trap-ground
- Quiet, local stewardship — not Plateau doctrine
- Memory that the Deepwood was a country before it was a mark

--------------------------------------------------
NUMBERS AND RANGE
--------------------------------------------------
Ebon Peninsula at large: scattered minority. Present in Hornwood edges, Westwood, high burns, leftover groves. Not the people of Ostbruck, the Leeshore farms, or the Lowstacks pits.
Kannavald Deepwood: still the majority. Lake-towns and named water-settlements lean Isern in law and look; the woods around them lean Kannavi.
Blood is mixed on the lake-shore. Affiliation is the year a household keeps.

--------------------------------------------------
ABSORPTION AND THE DIVIDE
--------------------------------------------------
What went Isern
- Farm
- Urban trade and quay
- Mining and quarrying

What stayed Kannavi
- Nearer the mountain
- In the woods
- Lumber
- Trapping
- Charcoal-burning
- Bowyery

Kolby’s yards can be Isern and still buy Kannavi timber.
Oldfort kilns, trap hamlets, stave-camps, and houses that will not live on the open shore sit Kannavi.
A pit-reeve counts Isern. A wood-right counts Kannavi.

--------------------------------------------------
LINGUISTIC BASE
--------------------------------------------------
Under-speech: Syllvani — Romance, melodic, light old grove-words.
Surface layer: Nordic. This is the Kannavi tell, and why Kannavald already sounds more like Kolvatn, Askflow, and Svarthorn than like Rhennod or a Plateau grove.

How to name
- Wood, water, burn, and hamlet names may sit on Nordic roots over a Syllvani sense.
- Soft Syllvani vowels can remain inside those names.
- Do not write them as Jarnfolk. The Nordic layer is contact and long dwelling in cold forest, not a separate Isern branch.
- Plateau ll / dd / rh is rare here. Hollow, vatn, beck, holm, and stave-words are common.

Speech in practice
Town and mark-law: Isern.
Wood-hamlet: mixed. Nordic layer on Syllvani base, Isern creeping in from Kolby.

--------------------------------------------------
CULTURE — LIVING MAJORITY IN THE DEEPWOOD
--------------------------------------------------
Small hamlets, seasonal camps, kiln-rows, traplines.
No white keeps as a people. No horse-halls as a people.
Rank: a good bow, a clean kiln, a winter the house survives.
Longbow memory is stronger here than among the Cortani. Bowyers are the loudest craft-pride.
The Still Stones are local law, not a temple. Hollow-folk is a nickname earned by living near that law, not by serving it.

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION TO OTHER PEOPLES
--------------------------------------------------
Isernvolk of the peninsula: ancient allies. Both were small scattered groups who carved a home out of a terrifying country.
The alliance still holds. Kannavi honour it.
Resentment came later, when Isernvolk grew and consumed floor, strand, and pit, and would consume the last woods if left to it.
Kannavi are more isolationist and fiercely independent than the landing-tale suggests.
They will not hesitate to remind a careless Isern where Kannavald’s borders are.
They sell timber. They do not surrender the canopy.
Cortani / Adernvolk: another country’s war.
Plateau Syllvani: elder cousins. Stewardship as record there; as this year’s grove here.
Novari: labour in pits and on quays. Little claim in the deep hamlets.
Aperti: omen. The Deepwood already feels quietly wrong.

--------------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY
--------------------------------------------------
Scattered remnant: Ebon Peninsula woods and mountain-edges.
Living majority: Kannavald Deepwood — Westwood Heights, upper burns, charcoal belt, trap country, stave-woods, the country around the Still Stones.
Lake Kolvatn’s named towns are Isern-facing. The canopy between them is Kannavi.

--------------------------------------------------
BRANCHES / LOCAL STOCKS
--------------------------------------------------

- Mountain-edge camps — lumber under Spire and Westwood
- Peninsula scatter — Hornwood and other leftover groves; minority

--------------------------------------------------
LANDING HISTORY — ISERNVOLK AND THE TAMING
--------------------------------------------------
Once the entire Ebon Peninsula was nearly as densely forested as the Deepwood remains.
The Kannavi were small and scattered. The woods were not empty of Unveiling perils. Their year was a struggle to stay human in canopy that did not always stay a wood.

The Isernvolk landed and aided their fellow man.
They did not land to a Whitmark war. They landed to a green country that needed iron, fire, and numbers.
Over centuries the peninsula was tamed. Floors opened. Shores worked. Pits cut. The Deepwood is what the taming did not finish.

The Hollow-folk — cousins in custom to the Still Stones — were raised. Alliance, standing, and the keeping of the stone-law. They were not made into Isern. They were no longer left alone with the perils.

Osric is locked as the first Isernvolk king in Kannavi memory.
His landing-seat: Osricford. Common and older shore-name: Ocs Landing.
There a hundred skinwights were hanged. That hanging is a founding story of the tamed peninsula, told on both sides of the divide.

Eyrenal
Already a remnant megastructure. Isernvolk colonised the hanging vale.
There an Isern lord bonded a gryphon and became the first rider. That bond is the seed of the Order.
Early and living name: the Eyrenhost. Not knights.
Knights Gryphon is the later style-name, after Coritanian influence.
Eyrenhost predates that style and remains correct in contemporary speech for the vale-host.
