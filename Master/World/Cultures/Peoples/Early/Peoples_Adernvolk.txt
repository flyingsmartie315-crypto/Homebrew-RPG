# Peoples_Adernvolk

Umbrella / parent
Isernvolk offshoot. Early category. Occupying elite in Coritania, not the host people.

Formal name
Adernvolk (locked folk-name and file-name).

Common names
Adelings (noble / caste speech — “those of rank”).
Whitmarks (nickname — from the whitewashed towers and walls).
Adern (short). The Tower-Iserns (foreign shorthand).

Blood-claim
“I carry Adern blood.”
Spoken by anyone asserting closeness to the old occupying lines. In the later Coritanian age this is a claim of right, not a proof of separate nationhood.

What this file is
The Isernvolk stock that fought, partitioned, and ruled Coritania as a small Norman-like upper class.
They remain a distinct Early people until the Cortani absorb them by time, custom, and strategic marriage.
This file is the occupier claim. It is not modern Coritania.
Modern Coritanians are the fusion; they get their own file when that age is written.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
Isernvolk mark-stock. Iron, stone (or brick), charter, and a ruling minority.
They entered Coritania by war, not by empty-shore landing.
They were never the people of the grass. They were the people of the keep, the roll of land, and the white wall.
They copied Cortani custom where it gave an edge — the saddle first.
They built where they could command: river, hill-hall site, road.

Spelling lock
Adernvolk / Adern blood / Adelings / Whitmarks.
Do not write Adenvolk. Do not write Adernvolk and Adelings as two nations. Adelings is the rank-name inside the same stock.

--------------------------------------------------
DEEP NAME VS SURFACE NAME
--------------------------------------------------
Deep / mythic: Adernvolk. Iron-blood that sat the Cortani halls.
Surface / caste: Adelings.
Look-name: Whitmarks — pale keeps, pale rings, dark swept roofs.
Later Coritanian usage: Adern blood as pedigree, not as a living separate folk.

--------------------------------------------------
SHARED CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------
- Descent from Isernvolk who took Coritania by war and held it by tower and roll
- Right to partition land: hedge, tree-line, road, and charter
- The white keep on the old hall-hill as the proper seat
- Rank shown in stone or whitewashed brick, then in horse and lists
- Suspicion of hall-right that will not answer to written bound
- Pride in having learned the Cortani saddle without laying the lance aside

--------------------------------------------------
HOW THEY RULED
--------------------------------------------------
Small class. Loud in law. Few in the field compared with the host.
Like the Normans in England: they took the high seats, married when they had to, and spoke their own compounds in the counting-house while the country went on being Cortani underneath.

They catalogued the land. Hedgerows, tree-lines, and roads marked every bound.
Crops went in at scale to feed towns and river-cities.
Population rose because the occupier needed surplus, not because the hills asked for it.

Where stone could be cut, they raised towers.
Where it could not, they fashioned clay brick and whitewashed it.
The Cortani hall-on-a-hill became a gleaming white keep inside white walls, with a bustling town against the ring.

They adopted what won:
- Cortani horse and riding-green
- Hall-gift and tourney-custom, turned into a caste tool
- Timber craft, lifted onto the new towers as swept roofs and spires

They did not become Cortani in the first generations. They remained Adernvolk on purpose. That is why the Early files stay separate.

--------------------------------------------------
WAR AND CASTE
--------------------------------------------------
Cortani cavalry plus Isernvolk metallurgy.
The first Virath iterations of the mounted knight — lance, iron, horse — as a social caste and a method of war.
The lists become how an Adeling proves he still deserves the white tower.
Coritania is already becoming the land of the tourney while the two peoples are still named apart.

--------------------------------------------------
ABSORPTION
--------------------------------------------------
Not a treaty. A long grind.

Near centuries of an unruly host. The Cortani do not vanish. They outlast the posture of occupation.
Strategic marriages of noble lines do the work the last wars could not.
House by house the Adeling pedigree sits down inside Cortani halls until the occupier stock is a blood-claim inside the country, not a people beside it.

After that the living folk are Coritanian.
Adernvolk remains as:
- old house names
- Whitmark look of the towns
- “I carry Adern blood” in the upper caste
- a right-to-rule argument, not a census category

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION TO OTHER PEOPLES
--------------------------------------------------
Cortani: the host. Enemy, then spouse, then the mouth that swallows the occupier.
Isernvolk (wider): parent sea-and-iron stock. Kin across the marks; not the same as the Coritanian Adeling houses.
Plateau Syllvani: distant elder cousins of the host, not of the occupier. Useful to blame or to bargain with; not family.
Ordinari: liked when they bless partition and pedigree. Useless in a riding-green.
Novari: labour on the new fields and quays. The Adernvolk counted them. The Cortani absorbed some as riders and hall-folk.
Aperti: omen only.

--------------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY
--------------------------------------------------
Seat of rule: the river highway and the white keeps raised on old Cortani hall-hills.
Look of their country: pale ring, pale tower, dark high timber roof, hedged fields, riding-green kept on purpose.
They do not own the Deepwood or the Plateau. Coritania is the only land this file cares about.
