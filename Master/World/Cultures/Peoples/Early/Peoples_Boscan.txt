# Peoples_Boscan

Umbrella / parent
Cortani offshoot. Syllvani line under that.
Valenidia. Frontier wood-kings of the Clypeo-Barra’s north-west foot.

Formal name
Boscan (locked).

Common names
Buscan (rugged / frontier mouth).
Grove-Lords. Timber-Shield. Wood-Kings.

What this file is
The people of the timber-clusters on the open country north of the Clypeo-Barra, between the western sea and the river that runs the east of that foot.
They live in the open for the herds. They will not plant a hall in the open. Every fortified stead sits inside a wood-cluster. The trees are the palisade.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
Cortani offshoot. Horse-hall people who took the timber-foot and stopped building on the naked green.
The name is an old grove-word (bosco) hardened by Cortani outdoor speech. Boscan / Buscan.
In-world meaning: Masters of the Groves. Those of the Timber-Shield.
Not Palatini. Not Idraulici. Northern cousins are the Heartlands Cortani — same stock, different law. The charge is why the hall sits in the wood.

--------------------------------------------------
DEEP NAME VS SURFACE NAME
--------------------------------------------------
Deep: Boscan.
Surface: Buscan, Grove-Lords, Timber-Shield.
Place-anchor: Buscany. Wooded foot north of the Clypeo-Barra. Working map-area about 21,600 square miles.
Local region file: Local_Region_Buscany.txt.

--------------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY AND CULTURE
--------------------------------------------------
Sea to the west. Clypeo-Barra to the south. A river on the east of the country. Timber in clusters, not one Deepwood.
Open fields between the groves are for herds.
Halls go in the heart of a wood-cluster. The stand is the wall against the massive cavalry of their northern cousins, the Cortani.
They refuse a hall on the naked plain. That is the law of the stead.

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION
--------------------------------------------------
Cortani of the Heartlands: parent stock and northern cousins. Horse and charge. The reason the hall sits in timber. Still kin. Not the same people.
Palatini: Spine-lords in Le Conche Verdi. Charter-kin at a distance. Not grove-law.
Idraulici: valley wrights east and south of the wall. Water-people. Trade, not a shared house.
Clypeo-Barra Cortani offshoot (the namers of the shield-bar): nearest mountain-shadow neighbours.
Hyrstwoods / Holtfenns: western neighbours in Holtwald. Isern splinter. They sit on knolls and mires. Boscan speech gave them Holtfenns.
