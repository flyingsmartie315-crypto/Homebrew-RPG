# Peoples_Hyrstwoods

Umbrella / parent
Isernvolk offshoot. Early category. Not peninsula Isern. Not Jarnvolk. Not Adernvolk.

Formal name
Hyrstwoods (locked).
Hyrst-Wudas in the oldest mouth.

Common names
Holtfenns / Holt-Fennas — Boscan and other eastern neighbours. Dwellers of the wooded mires.
Keepers of the Wooded Knolls (their own gloss).

What this file is
A forgotten south-running fragment of the Isern crossing, shoved into the wet western buttress of Valenidia when the dry floor was already held.
Ridge-steads on oak-and-chestnut knolls. Log-roads on the mire. Oak dories through the skerries.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
Same Second Wave Ingenari stock as the Isernvolk.
Not Jarnvard’s wing. Not Osric’s vanguard.
A smaller splinter tracked too far south down the western coast while the main host held the Ebon line.
The fertile dry lowlands of Valenidia were already locked by Idraulici masonry. They would not bend the knee to Ordinari charter.
They took the rain-pocket no one else wanted: Holtwald.

--------------------------------------------------
DEEP NAME VS SURFACE NAME
--------------------------------------------------
Deep / own: Hyrstwoods — hyrst (wooded hillock) + wudu (standing timber). Keepers of the Wooded Knolls.
Neighbour-name: Holtfenns — holt + fenn. What a Boscan horseman sees: grove and bog.
Land-name: Holtwald — holt + weald. The thick wooded hill-country. Local_Region_Holtwald.txt.

--------------------------------------------------
GEOGRAPHY AND CULTURE
--------------------------------------------------
Seat: Holtwald. About 14,700 square miles. Intensely hilly. No plain.
Stone-flagged longhouses on the crested knolls. The grove on the high drained rock is sovereign ground.
They abandoned open-field farming. The bottoms are mire.
Holtfenn work: oak from the holts, revetments, log-bridges, corduroy roads so the silt does not eat the country.
Hill work: iron and lead tunneled from limestone crags. Not a fat ore-mark. Enough to arm and to trade.
Sea work: deep-netting, cliff-quarry, coastal haul. Wide oak-ribbed dories through skerries and Highsun gale.
They smuggle northern iron-work down the coast to southern city-states for grain and Valle Nevale wine.

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION
--------------------------------------------------
Isernvolk of the Ebon Peninsula: lost main-host. This splinter is not in Osric’s landing-song. Not Jarnvolk.
Jarnvolk: another lost piece of the same crossing, polar where these went wet-south. No compact written.
Boscan of Buscany: eastern neighbours. Grove-halls and herds. They coined Holtfenns. Kinship of timber, not of blood.
Idraulici: the masonry that closed the dry floor. Trade partners. Not lords.
Palatini / Ordinari charter-houses: refused. The reason this people is in the rain.
Cortani of the Heartlands: horse to the north of the Spine. The mire and the knoll are the answer to a charge.
