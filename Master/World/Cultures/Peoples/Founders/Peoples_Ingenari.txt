# Peoples_Ingenari

Umbrella name
Ingenari (proper). Ingens (common / foreign short form). The Raisers / Second Blood (myth and high speech).

What this file is
The second colonial wave and the peoples that formed from them.
They came after the Pionari had made Virath livable. Their work was engineering, extraction, the first true cities — and the arms that guarded them.
Branch files hold the living ethnic groups. This file holds what they share.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — SECOND WAVE
--------------------------------------------------
After the Pioneers seeded climate and life, a later arrival brought material science and heavy engineering.
They built space elevators, opened extraction, and raised the first real cities — the investment that made the colony a world-economy.
Technology is forgotten as technique. It survives as myth: the Lift, the Tower, the first walls, the right to take value from stone and deep metal.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — SCALE AND COMPOSITION
--------------------------------------------------
The Pionari were a small founding crew. The Ingenari were a full investment wave:
- Work crews — lift-wrights, miners, masons, surveyors, harbour-makers
- Engineers — makers and planners of infrastructure
- Soldiers / guards — posted to protect what was built and extracted

Craft and arms are both spines of the Second Blood. Later branches lean guild-city or watch-city; the umbrella remembers both.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — PLACE IN THE THREE WAVES
--------------------------------------------------
1. Pionari — wake the world.
2. Ingenari — raise and hold the works.
3. Late wave — Ordinari, Novari (Multitude), Aperti (Openers).
Cradle falls silent. Factions friction. Unveiling. Dark Ages.
Ingenari city-stocks and tower-claims survive as blood, ruin-rights, and the memory of wall and watch. Some later “Vesting” mark-peoples may be Ingenari offshoots who kept iron and conquest and lost the Lift in their story.

--------------------------------------------------
ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
They did not claim the first footfall. That belongs to the Appulse and the Pionari.
They claimed the raising — cable and city, mine and road — and the watch on the first night while the mortar dried.
Sacred geography: old lift-roots, first city-stones, engineered scars in myth and ruin.

Mythic line:
The First Men woke the world. The Raisers built upon it — and stood armed at the gate while the mortar dried.

--------------------------------------------------
SHARED CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------
- Descent from the Second Blood / the Raisers
- Pride of making and holding: wall, bridge, gate, memory of the Lift
- Inheritance of crew and watch
- Honour for builder and guard; contempt for the idler who claims the city without labour or risk
- Right to extract and build, bounded by crew-oath and city-law (forms differ by branch)
- Ruins of tower, cable-foot, and under-city as inheritance — dangerous, holy, or both
- Rank tied to craft, arms, hold, and city-stock blood

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION TO OTHER WAVES
--------------------------------------------------
Pionari: elder stewards of the green. Cooperation is the deep truth; contempt goes both ways when ore meets taboo grove.
Ordinari: Seal and elite who claimed the right to order what Ingenari built. Useful when they fund and recognise the wall; hated when they command without craft.
Novari: the Multitude that filled and strained the cities. Labour and market; also riot, pressure, and the long push into harder lands after the Dark.
Aperti: small, ill-omened. The probe that sat badly with men who wanted measures to stay measures.
