# Peoples_Ordinari

Umbrella name
Ordinari (proper). The Seal-Blood / The Ordainers (myth and high speech). Ordins (common / foreign short form).

What this file is
The late-wave authority: charter, rank, and elite claim to Virath.
They arrived with the Multitude (Novari) and used that mass as cudgel and wedge to plant themselves on a world already woken by Pionari and raised by Ingenari.
Branch files hold surviving houses and seal-stocks. This file holds what they share.

--------------------------------------------------
GM META — THE SEAL
--------------------------------------------------
Ordinari were colonial administration, law, and entitled elite from the Cradle — not a small founding crew and not the labour multitude.
They brought paper, hierarchy, and the assumption that Virath was theirs to allot.
The Novari were the demographic tool: numbers to occupy, pressure elder holds, and make the Seal’s map real on the ground.
Then the Cradle went silent. The Unveiling came. Charters without a homeworld became faith, nostalgia, or empty ink.
Surviving Ordinari lines are few compared to Novari mass, but punch above their weight in law, lineage myth, and the memory of “legitimate” rule.

--------------------------------------------------
ORIGIN
--------------------------------------------------
They claim neither first footfall nor the Lift’s raising as their special craft.
They claim **Ordainment**: the right to order the world — boundaries, offices, taxes, and rank — in the Cradle’s name.
Sacred objects in their myth are seal, chain of office, census-roll, and the last true warrants from the sky-home.
When the Cradle died in the sky, those objects became relics. The claim did not.

--------------------------------------------------
SHARED CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------
- Descent from the Seal-Blood / those who held Terra’s warrant on Virath
- Legitimacy through charter, office, and blood of the ordaining houses
- The Multitude as historical instrument: brought under Seal, settled under Seal, still owed hierarchy in hard Ordinari speech
- Contempt for pure might without warrant — and fear of Novari number when warrant fails
- Memory of cities and provinces drawn on maps that no longer match the broken world
- Rank, precedence, and written or recited right as the spine of self-respect

--------------------------------------------------
RELATION TO OTHER WAVES
--------------------------------------------------
Pionari: elder stewards. Treated as native princes to be bargained with or overridden; their taboo ground is an obstacle to allotment.
Ingenari: necessary builders and watch-captains. Respected as infrastructure; challenged when they act as if the city is theirs alone without Seal.
Novari: the flood they rode in on. Without the Multitude the Ordinari could not have seated power. In myth that dependence is often rewritten as pure command.
Openers: dangerous specialists under late charter; later blamed or buried in the story of the tear.

--------------------------------------------------
AFTER THE UNVEILING
--------------------------------------------------
Some Ordinari houses became priest-kings of charter-faith.
Some became ordinary nobles who forgot the Cradle and kept only pride.
Some were destroyed when the Multitude turned and the elder waves pushed back.
Descendants may still speak of warrant and proper order in lands where no Cradle official has existed for millennia.
