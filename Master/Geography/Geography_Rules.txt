# Geography_Rules.txt
# How to read environmental scores, biomes, and season on Virath.

These scores describe any land or oceanic place.
They are compared to creature HSI in Bestiary_Rules.txt.

How to write the files is not in this document.
- Local_Region_Format.txt
- Geographic_Region_Format.txt
- Settlement_Format.txt

Every scored place uses seven measures, written on one line:

Temperature: X | Rainfall: X | Human Density: X | Flora: X | Fauna: X | Topography: X | Hydrology: X


--------------------------------------------------
LAND – Environmental Scores (0–5)
--------------------------------------------------

Temperature (heat / cold)
0 – Arctic / freezing
1 – Sub-arctic / cold
2 – Cool temperate
3 – Mild temperate
4 – Warm subtropical
5 – Hot (sub-Saharan equivalent)

Rainfall (precipitation / humidity)
0 – Arid desert
1 – Dry / sparse
2 – Semi-arid
3 – Temperate / balanced
4 – Humid / wet
5 – Rainforest / torrential

Human Density (settlement level)
0 – Uninhabited wilderness
1 – Sparse / isolated
2 – Low / scattered
3 – Moderate (villages)
4 – High (towns)
5 – Dense (cities)

Flora (plant cover)
0 – Barren / lifeless
1 – Limited / scarce
2 – Sparse / occasional
3 – Moderate / common
4 – Abundant / thriving
5 – Teeming / overrun

Fauna (animal life)
0 – Barren / lifeless
1 – Limited / scarce
2 – Sparse / occasional
3 – Moderate / common
4 – Abundant / thriving
5 – Teeming / overrun

Topography (elevation / steepness)
0 – Flat plains
1 – Gentle slopes
2 – Undulating hills
3 – Rugged terrain
4 – Steep cliffs
5 – Sheer mountains

Hydrology (water features)
0 – None / parched
1 – Sparse trickles
2 – Limited streams
3 – Rivers / inlets / channels
4 – Lakes / major through-water
5 – Flooded / swamps / wet coast


--------------------------------------------------
OCEANIC – Environmental Scores (0–5)
--------------------------------------------------

Same seven categories, different meanings.
Do not mix land meanings and oceanic meanings in the same comparison.

Temperature (water temperature)
0 – Polar / permanently frozen surface or near-freezing
1 – Freezing cold
2 – Cold currents
3 – Temperate seas
4 – Warm waters
5 – Tropical hot

Rainfall (storm frequency / humidity over water)
0 – Extremely dry air / almost no precipitation
1 – Minimal storms
2 – Light fog / occasional rain
3 – Moderate rains
4 – Frequent storms
5 – Constant high humidity / monsoon conditions

Human Density (maritime activity)
0 – No human presence (unexplored or avoided waters)
1 – Isolated routes
2 – Sparse traffic
3 – Moderate trade
4 – Busy shipping lanes
5 – Crowded ports / heavy traffic

Flora (marine plant and reef cover)
0 – Barren / sterile
1 – Limited / scarce
2 – Sparse weed or poor reef
3 – Moderate growth
4 – Abundant kelp / rich reef
5 – Teeming / overrun

Fauna (marine animal life)
0 – Essentially lifeless
1 – Barren depths
2 – Sparse fish
3 – Moderate schools
4 – Abundant life
5 – Teeming ecosystems

Topography (water depth)
0 – Intertidal / exposed flats at low tide
1 – Shallow waters
2 – Coastal shelf
3 – Moderate depths
4 – Deep sea
5 – Abyssal depths / trenches

Hydrology (sea state / surface conditions)
0 – Dead calm / mirror-like
1 – Calm seas
2 – Mild swells
3 – Moderate waves
4 – Rough currents
5 – Extreme storms / violent seas


--------------------------------------------------
BIOMES
--------------------------------------------------

Biome is a quick filter for flora, fauna, and Bestiary placement.
HSI remains the finer tool.

Biome and Sub-biomes use this same list.
Do not invent new tags without adding them here.

A Local Region names one Biome and may name Sub-biomes.
A Geographic Region does not take a biome line. It inherits the set of its Local Regions.


LAND – CLIMATE
These ten carry seasonal templates.

Polar
Tundra, ice-waste, and the high frozen floor.

Boreal
Cold forest, taiga, and dark winter wood.

Alpine
High mountain climate: short thaw, hard Longnight, rock and snow.

Temperate
Mild seasonal country. The default of vale, ridge, and mixed wood.

Oceanic
Wet coast and year-round damp air. Land climate, not the sea itself.

Steppe
Grass, dry wind, and open distance.

Mediterranean
Dry-summer coast and maquis.

Arid
Desert of sand, stone, or salt.

Tropical wet
Hot rainforest and standing wet.

Tropical wet-dry
Hot monsoon country. Thaw is the wet; Highsun is the drought.


LAND – WATER
Standing and running water on the land.
Use as Biome when the Local Region is that water.
Use as Sub-biome when it is a real part of a land region.

Burn
Headwater, mountain stream, and small fast water.

River
A named ordinary river and its floor.

Great river
Through-water that defines a country. Days to follow, not hours.

Pond
Small still water: mere, pool, farm-pond, wood-pond.

Lake
Standing water of regional consequence.

Great lake
Inland sea. A crossing is a voyage.

Wetland
Fen, marsh, swamp, and flood-meadow.


OCEANIC
Use oceanic HSI meanings when scoring these.

Polar sea
Ice-edge, near-freezing water, polar haul-outs.

Cold sea
Cold currents and boreal water.

Temperate sea
Open temperate water.

Warm sea
Subtropical and warm current.

Tropical sea
Hot water, reef, and monsoon sea.

Strand
Shore, beach, tidal flat, and the intertidal.

Shelf
Coastal shallows and the drowned shelf.

Gulf
Enclosed or half-enclosed sea, bight, or sound.

Open sea
The deep road between lands. No shore in the day’s work.

Abyssal
Trench and the lightless floor.


READING THE TAGS

Biome is the country of the floor. One only.
Sub-biomes are other biomes that occupy real ground or water in that region.
At least one climate biome must appear, as Biome or as a Sub-biome. Season needs it.

If Biome is a climate tag, that is the seasonal template.
If Biome is a water tag, take the seasonal template from a climate tag listed under Sub-biomes.

Features inherit the region’s Biome unless they carry their own biome line.
A feature biome should be one of the region’s Biome or Sub-biomes.

QUICK FILTER
Flora, fauna, and Bestiary entries list one or more tags from this list, or Any.

- Region Biome is in the entry’s list → candidate for the country as a whole
- Only a Sub-biome matches → candidate on those features only
- No overlap → skip
- Any → skip the biome gate
- Then apply HSI against the region, or against the matching feature

Domain is defined in Bestiary_Rules.txt. Domain remains a hard gate. Biome is the quick gate. HSI is the score.


--------------------------------------------------
SEASONAL MODIFIER TEMPLATES
--------------------------------------------------

Looked up from the climate biome in use.
Applied to Temperature, Rainfall, Flora, and Fauna only.
Harvest is the printed score.
Shifts are +1, 0, or −1 only.
A modified score cannot go below 0 or above 5.

Water biomes do not have their own templates. They take the climate biome of the region or feature.

--------------------------------------------------
HOW TO READ A LOCAL REGION
--------------------------------------------------

The seven-number line is Harvest.
It is the rounded mean of the land-cover features.
Point sites (stones, ruins, a summit used only as a landmark) are scored for HSI at that pin. They are not in the average.

If the date is known:
1. Take each feature’s climate template. A feature with no climate of its own uses the region’s.
2. Shift that feature’s Temperature, Rainfall, Flora, and Fauna by +1 / 0 / −1.
3. Average the modified features again. That is the region in that season.

Human Density, Topography, and Hydrology do not change with season.

A dated encounter tied to one feature uses that feature’s modified scores, not the region line.


--------------------------------------------------
HEIGHT
--------------------------------------------------

Height is an optional label for the Local Region as a whole.
It is not a 0–5 score. It is not averaged. It is not used for HSI.
It is not a biome.

It names the region’s seat in the world, not the steepness of its ground.

highland basin
A high, enclosed or half-enclosed floor, ringed by higher ground.

high range
A mountain mass as the main body of the region.

highland
High country that is not a closed basin and not a single range.

plateau
A raised table. The floor is high and relatively even.

upland
Hills and high ground above the neighbouring low country, short of a true highland.

lowland
Broad country near the regional base level.

coastal plain
Low, open land against the sea.

coastal highland
High ground that meets the sea.

rift / trench
A deep cut below the surrounding land. Use rarely.

Do not read a basin floor as lowland if that floor still sits high in the world.


--------------------------------------------------
HIERARCHY
--------------------------------------------------

1. Geographic Region
   Large-scale physical area, roughly Western Europe in size.
   Defined by natural geography. No political ownership.

2. Local Region
   Physical subdivision. Holds Environmental Scores, Biome, and Sub-biomes.

3. Settlement
   City, town, village, fortress, ruin.
   Political control is recorded here only.

Geographic and Local Regions do not change ownership.
Control is who holds the settlements inside them.

A Local Region may belong to more than one Geographic Region when it is a natural border.
The Local Region file exists once. Each Geographic Region lists it under Shared / Border Local Regions.


--------------------------------------------------
USAGE
--------------------------------------------------

- Scores are relative tools, not laboratory measurements.
- Flora and Fauna are separate. Closed canopy can be Flora 4, Fauna 2. Steppe can be Flora 2, Fauna 4.
- Creature comparison uses Bestiary_Rules.txt.
- Extreme scores (0 or 5) should be rare. Most settled country sits in the middle.
- Unveiling, megastructures, or local anomalies can override the printed scores.
