Appolasian Plateau
From the Latin appulsus — “the striking of a ship upon the shore” or “the landing.”
In-world, the name refers to the mountain-ringed impact basin where the First Men’s vessel first touched Virath. Over thousands of years the word softened and drifted in Syllvani speech into Appolasian, keeping the sense of origin and first contact while losing the hard technical edge of the original Latin.
It is both a geographic name and a quiet statement of primacy: this is the place of the Landing.
