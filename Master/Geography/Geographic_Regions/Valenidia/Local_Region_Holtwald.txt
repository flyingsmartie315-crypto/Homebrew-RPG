# Local_Region_Holtwald

Overview
The jagged western stone buttress of Valenidia. Intensely hilly throughout. No open plain. The limestone bulk of the Clypeo-Barra stands on the southern horizon and breaks north and west into knolls, ridges, flagstone crests, and narrow rock-gills. The land takes the full western ocean fetch and holds it. About 14,700 square miles on the working sheet.

Biome: Temperate / Wet hill-wood
Sub-biomes: Alpine foot, Forest, Wetland, Strand, River
Milder than the polar north. Wetter than Valle Nevale. Not a dry rain-shadow floor. Atlantic-wet temperate, not Provençal dry.
