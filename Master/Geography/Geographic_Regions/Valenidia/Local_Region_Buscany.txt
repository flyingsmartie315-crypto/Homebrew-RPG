# Local_Region_Buscany

Overview
The wooded north-west foot of the Clypeo-Barra. Open pasture between timber-clusters. Sea to the west. A river on the east. Boscan country: herds on the green, halls only in the heart of a grove.
About 21,600 square miles on the working sheet.

Biome: Temperate / Wood-pasture
Sub-biomes: Forest cluster, Pasture, Coast, River
Wet face of the shield-bar. Not rain-shadow basin. Not Le Conche Verdi.

Environmental Scores
Temperature: 3 | Rainfall: 4 | Human Density: 2 | Flora: 4 | Fauna: 3 | Topography: 2–3 | Hydrology: 3
Harvest aggregates of the land-cover features below.
Point sites are not averaged in.

Height: foot / low hill

Seasonal modifiers: Temperate (wetter than Valenidia south of the Spine)
