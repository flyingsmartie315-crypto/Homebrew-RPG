Overview
A south-of-Heartlands country defined by one wall. The Orosian Spine runs some 690 miles along the northern edge and separates Valenidia from the Coritanian Heartlands. It is an alpine thermal wall. Northwestern ocean wind hits the north face first; rain dumps on the Coritanian border. South of the crest the central river basins sit in rain-shadow: sun-baked, drier, fed more by snowmelt off the Spine than by local storm. The south coast is the other edge of the region, toward open sea.

Location & Borders
South of the Coritanian Heartlands. Southwest of the Appolasian Plateau. West of Sauronia. Sea on the south.

Notable Features
- The Orosian Spine — 690-mile alpine wall separating Valenidia from the Coritanian Heartlands.
  Thermal wall. Traps northwestern prevailing ocean moisture and dumps it on the Coritanian border to the north.
  Leaves the central Valenidian river basins behind it sun-baked and dry.
  South-face water is snowmelt and whatever the shadow lets through, not a second Coritania.
- The Clypeo-Barra — western cluster of the Orosian Spine.
  Cortani offshoot in the northern shadow took softened Latin Monte-Clypeo (Shield Mountain) and smashed it against Barra — their word for a fence, barrier, blockade.
  Vowels clipped. Blunt military compound. The shield that is also the bar.
  West end of the wall, toward the sea and the Heartlands’ south-west.
- Le Conche Verdi (the Inhabited Hollows) — heart of the middle Spine.
  A sun-warmed network of interconnected, low-altitude alpine valleys, insulated from the outside world.
  The green exception in a rain-shadow country. Settlement sits here, not on the wet Coritanian face and not on the baked open basins.
- Macigno Serrata — eastern end of the Orosian Spine. The name is joined: a jagged monolith.
  Macigno is the unyielding dark-stone mass. Serrata is the saw-notch and the lock.
  One object. Closed gate toward Sauronia / the Plateau join.
  The eastern spine runs about 170 miles. Black jagged basalt. High peaks hold a heavy winter pack.
- Valle Nevale — the Snow-Washed Valley / Valley of the Glacial Melt.
  Floor region at the southern base of Macigno Serrata.
  Contemporary country: terrace farms on the volcanic foot. Man-made channels that trickle in Longnight and run in Thaw and Highsun.
  Neat rows of planted trees hold the riverbanks.
  All of it is led to a dammed lake on the valley floor. Aqueducts and cuts take the overflow when the water reaches a set margin. The live outflow runs toward the sea.
  Older ground: a swamp. Melt came down the fissures and sat. A people cut the races, drained the stand, and planted.
  Wine and grape capital. Mercantile powerhouse on the drained benches, trade down the outflow and out the east gate.
  Waterworks sit inside Virath’s 16th-century ceiling. The type is older: Roman dams and leads, alpine bisses, Andalusí acequias. Living maintenance, not a relic machine.
  People-file not yet written.
