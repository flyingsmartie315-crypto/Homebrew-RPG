Overview
A large southern block of the north-west continent, bigger than a single Iberian analogue and carrying a serious mountain spine. Low country and long coasts sit against a highland core that behaves like an alpine wall. The climate splits: milder or hotter open land on the floors and shores, cold and steep country on the ranges. A major river on the northern edge divides Valenidia from the Coritanian Heartlands. This is the first big southward step of the continent: more light, more heat, and a harder upland than the heartlands above it.

Location & Borders
Valenidia occupies the large southern body of the mapped north-west: south of the Coritanian Heartlands, west of Saturnia, and open to the southern and western seas.

Notable Features
- A substantial internal mountain spine (the “Swiss Alps” element of the template)
- Long southern and western coasts
- The northern hinge river against Coritania
- A climate split between hot or dry floors and high alpine country
- Larger land area than Spain proper (about 359,000 square miles)

Notes
Treat Valenidia as Iberian in the lowlands and alpine in the high spine, not as a single uniform climate. Travel is easy on the floors and coasts, hard across the ranges. Local Regions should be split on that line: coastal/lowland blocks versus highland blocks, with the northern river as the contact with Coritania.
