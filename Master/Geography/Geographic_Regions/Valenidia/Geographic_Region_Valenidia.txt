Overview
A south-of-Heartlands country defined by one wall. The Orosian Spine runs some 690 miles along the northern edge and separates Valenidia from the Coritanian Heartlands. It is an alpine thermal wall. Northwestern ocean wind hits the north face first; rain dumps on the Coritanian border. South of the crest the central river basins sit in rain-shadow: sun-baked, drier, fed more by snowmelt off the Spine than by local storm. The south coast is the other edge of the region, toward open sea.

Location & Borders
South of the Coritanian Heartlands. Southwest of the Appolasian Plateau. West of Sauronia. Sea on the south.

  Thermal wall. Traps northwestern prevailing ocean moisture and dumps it on the Coritanian border to the north.
  Leaves the central Valenidian river basins behind it sun-baked and dry.
  South-face water is snowmelt and whatever the shadow lets through, not a second Coritania.
