# Local_Region_Westmark

Overview
A broad, well-watered peninsula heartland on the western approaches of the Ebon Peninsula. The original landing ground of the Isernvolk, now the seat of the region’s capital and principal port. Sheltered central valleys run between Ebon Spire and its twin, Isenhorn, while the broken northern ranges form a natural barrier against the northern seas. Rivers are navigable, cultivated, and heavily travelled. The land is more open and settled than Kannavald, but still defined by iron, sea, and the memory of first arrival.

NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
Access is by sea into the western port, by the navigable rivers of the central valley, or over the high passes toward Kannavald and Ebormark.
The region is the political and commercial heart of the Ebon Peninsula for the Isernvolk.
Population is markedly higher and more continuous than in Kannavald Deepwood.
Iron, grain, livestock, and sea trade form the economic spine.
The Rivenstrand Walls remain a meaningful natural defence against northern threats.
