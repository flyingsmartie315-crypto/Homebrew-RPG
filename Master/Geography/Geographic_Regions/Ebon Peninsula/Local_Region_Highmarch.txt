# Local_Region_Highmarch

Overview
The elevated middle country of the Ebon Peninsula, between the eastern flanks of Ebon Spire / Svarthorn / Ebonclock and the rising Marchwall Range. A broken highland of ridges, high valleys, and passes. The Värdå is the through-road. Settlement is thinner than Westmark; watch, herd, and river-hub matter more than open fields.

Biome: Temperate / Highland
Sub-biomes: Alpine, River, Highland, Forest
The high ground is cold and steep. The Värdå valleys are milder but still elevated.

--------------------------------------------------
VÄRDÅ (HIGHMARCH REACH)
--------------------------------------------------
Miles from the sea are Värdå path-miles (meander length along the river), not a straight shot from the coast.
Main stem enters from Westmark 216 path-miles above Osricford.
From the Westmark / Highmarch border, notable joins:

- +20 mi (236 path-miles from the sea): Rimenau — first Marchwall burn
- +45 mi (281 path-miles from the sea): the Spireflow
- +24 mi (305 path-miles from the sea): Dreiau — three-strand Marchwall foot-woods river
- +72 mi (377 path-miles from the sea): Holtau

Sea to lake on this listed chain: about 377 path-miles.
Westmark file holds the lower five joins.

Access
From Westmark up the Värdå. From the Spire country down the Spireflow and the NE burn. From the Plateau by the unnamed eastern river. From the Marchwall by the listed burns only.

Holtau is the Highmarch hinge. The mouth at Osricford is the Westmark hinge. Do not give this lake Westmark’s 50,000. It is second, not equal.

Ebon Spire and Svarthorn are shared; this file only scores the faces that fall here.
Marchwall spelling stays Marchwall, not Marchwell.

When no (bracketed detail) is included, the feature is wholly within this region.
