# Local_Region_Highmarch

Overview
The elevated middle country of the Ebon Peninsula, lying between the eastern flanks of Ebon Spire / Svarthorn Massif and the rising Marchwall Range. A broken highland of ridges, high valleys, and passes. Settlement is thinner and harder than in Westmark; the land serves as both shield and corridor toward the Syllvani frontier. Watch-posts, herding holds, and pass-tolls matter more than open fields.

Biome: Temperate / Highland
Sub-biomes: Alpine, River, Highland
The high ground is cold and steep. The deeper valleys are milder but still elevated.

NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
Access is by the high passes from Westmark, by routes descending from the Spire country, and by the limited corridors that cross toward the Rhennod / Plateau frontier.
The region is the shield of the peninsula — thinner in people, heavier in watch and toll.
Economy rests on highland herding, limited mining, timber, and control of the through-roads.
Culturally Isernvolk, but harder and more frontier-minded than the green heartland.
Ebon Spire and Svarthorn Massif are shared landforms; detailed scores and full descriptions live in Kannavald Deepwood (and the south face in Ebormark). Highmarch only records the northern faces that fall into this region.
