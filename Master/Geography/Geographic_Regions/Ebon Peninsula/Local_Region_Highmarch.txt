# Highmarch

Overview
The elevated middle country of the Ebon Peninsula, between the eastern flanks of Ebon Spire / Svarthorn / Ebonclock and the rising Marchwall Range. A broken highland of ridges, high valleys, and passes. The Värdå is the through-road. Settlement is thinner than Westmark; watch, herd, and river-hub matter more than open fields. The region’s character is Kannavi–Isern fusion, strongest at Karvarn on Holtau.

In past centuries Highmarch was nearly as densely forested as Kannavald Deepwood still is. Clearing followed the Värdå, the lake shores, and the high valleys that could be pastured or terraced after the Isern landing and the Kannavi alliance at Caer Varn. Dense forest now survives only where the ground rises hard against the mountain bases. Everywhere else the woodland has been reduced to valley copses, terrace edges, and managed high pasture. Name drifts (Caer Varn → Karvarn, Oltaval Basis → Holtau, Lloerllyn → Lurlin) are ordinary Isern wear on Kannavi stems.

North of the Marchwall the land falls away into the steep, rough face of the Rivenstrand Walls. This northern ground (8,591 square miles) lies outside Highmarch proper. It has little shelter and faces the open northern seas. Access from Highmarch is limited to a couple of rough high passes or the long loop west through the Ardairn Valley.

Biome: Temperate
Sub-biomes: Alpine, River, Lake, Highland, Forest
The high ground is cold and steep. The Värdå valleys are milder but still elevated.

Access
From Westmark up the Värdå. From the Spire country down the Spireflow and the NE burn. From the Plateau by Avenhir. From the Marchwall by the listed burns only.
Northern face (outside Highmarch proper): only via a couple of rough high passes through the Marchwall or the long loop west into the Ardairn Valley.

Holtau is the Highmarch hinge. The mouth at Osricford is the Westmark hinge. Do not give this lake Westmark’s 50,000. It is second, not equal. Karvarn is the locked name of the Holtau hub.

Ebon Spire and Svarthorn are shared; this file only scores the faces that fall here.
Marchwall spelling stays Marchwall, not Marchwell.
Rhennod’s Bow northern tip is shared / border.

[One closing line on distance and travel feel.]
The Värdå is the spine. From Karvarn the high valleys rise quickly; a day’s ride reaches the first watch or the edge of residual forest. Longnight closes the higher passes. The Spire’s shadow falls across Holtau on clear mornings.
