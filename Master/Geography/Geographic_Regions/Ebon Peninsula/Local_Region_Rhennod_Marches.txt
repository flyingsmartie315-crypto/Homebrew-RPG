# Local_Region_Rhennod_Marches

Overview
The south-eastern border country of the Ebon Peninsula. Rolling green hills and river floors lie under the western shadow of the forested Rhennod Mountains. A fertile but watched land: good soils on the lower Rhennod slopes and river terraces support vines, orchard fruit, and mixed farming that the heavier or colder soils of the rest of the peninsula cannot match in quality. The great eastern wall is always present.

Biome: Temperate
Sub-biomes: Highland, River, Strand (if coastal contact exists)
The hills and river country are mild seasonal land. The upper Rhennod slopes are another climate.

NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
Access is by the border river from the western heartlands, by hill roads, and by limited tracks that climb toward the Rhennod passes.
The region’s distinction is soil quality rather than climate: the lower Rhennod slopes give reliable vines and finer fruit that the rest of the peninsula produces less consistently.
Culturally Isernvolk, with a stronger visible Syllvani substrate than the western heartland.
The Rhennod Mountains dominate the eastern skyline and remain the cultural and political frontier with the Appolasian Plateau.
