# Kannavald_Labels.txt
# Working label list for the Ebon Spire / Kannavald region
# Brief location blurbs only. Political control is not recorded here.

--------------------------------------------------
MAJOR LANDMARKS
--------------------------------------------------

Ebon Spire
Inactive volcano and the dominant peak of the peninsula. Black, craggy rock. Source of the rivers that feed Lake Kolvatn.

Westwood Heights
Lower coastal ridge on the west of Kannavald. Forest covers most of the slopes; only the highest points rise above the canopy. Milder than the Ebon Spire massif.

Svarthorn Massif
The black-rock mountain mass surrounding Ebon Spire. The true highland of the peninsula. Craggy, volcanic in origin, and far higher than the coastal hills.

Brocken Peak
(Southern most peak of the Svarthorn Massif range)

Kannavald Deepwood
The great boreal forest of the region. Dense, dark, and thinly settled. Timber, charcoal, trapping, and small mining.

Lake Kolvatn
Central lake formed from Ebon Spire runoff. Economic heart of the Deepwood. Timber and ore come downriver to its shores.

Kolmire Fens
Swampy western margin of Lake Kolvatn. Sunken ground. Site of the drowned former capital, The Sunkern Burg.

The Still Stones
Also called: The Black Circle, The Hollows, The Quiet Watchers.
Ancient standing stones in a large clearing in the deepest woods. Local law is absolute: do not approach. Misfortune in the region is often blamed on someone who did.

Mistcap Crags
Western heights at the seaward end of Westwood Heights. One of the few places the ridge breaks the canopy.

Watchers Peak
A forested height in Kannavald Deepwood, west of Lake Kolvatn. Named in the same local speech as the Quiet Watchers, though it is a mountain, not the stones.

Westwood Peak
(Tallest peak of Westwood Heights)

Stetbjorg Height
(Brief description)

Askflow
Inlet to Lake Kolvatn. The main runoff from Ebon Spire.

Hornbeck
Inlet to Lake Kolvatn. The darker forest-fed water from the Westwood Heights

Rookburn
Inlet to Lake Kolvatn. A lesser stream off the Ebon Spire / Svarthorn Massif.

The Sund
The two-mile outflow draining Lake Kolvatn past Twaford. The water-gate of the region.

--------------------------------------------------
SETTLEMENTS
--------------------------------------------------

Kolby-on-Vatn
The lake town. Primary hub for processing timber, charcoal, and mine produce. The only real town in Kannavald.

The Twaford Twins
Pair of castles, one on each side of the outflow at Twaford. Seat of regional power. Gate to the Kolvatn basin. Main access into the economic region.

Kolholm "The Sunkern Burg"
Ruined former regional capital, now lost in the Kolmire Fens. Drowned when rains and runoff from Ebon Spire flooded the western marshes.

Hornby
(Brief description)


--------------------------------------------------
LESSER / SCATTERED
--------------------------------------------------

Upper-river villages
Small logging and mining settlements nearer the river sources. Timber and ore are floated down to Kolby-on-Vatn.

Charcoal hamlets
Many small, scattered hamlets throughout Kannavald Deepwood. Charcoal-burning, trapping, and isolated woodland work.
