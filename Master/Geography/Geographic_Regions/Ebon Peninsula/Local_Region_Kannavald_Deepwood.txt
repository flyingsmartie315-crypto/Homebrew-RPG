# Kannavald Deepwood

Overview
A mountain-ringed highland basin of dense boreal forest, black volcanic rock, and standing water. The canopy is heavy, the light is poor, and most travel follows the rivers that drain from Ebon Spire into Lake Kolvatn. Settlement clings to the water; beyond the lake-shore and the upper burns the Deepwood is empty, wet, and quietly wrong.

Biome: Boreal
Sub-biomes: Alpine, Lake, Wetland, River, Burn, Strand
The floor is cold forest. The massif is another climate. The water is another country again.

Upper-river villages
Small logging and mining settlements nearer the river sources. Timber and ore are floated down to Kolby-on-Vatn.

Charcoal / Trapping hamlets
Many small, scattered hamlets throughout Kannavald Deepwood. Charcoal-burning, trapping, and isolated woodland work.

NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
Access
By the Sund, by Deepskaard Col, by the west coast, or over the mountains.
Mile detail lives in settlement Access notes (neighbours only).

Goods
Timber, charcoal, hides, and ore move downriver to the lake.

Population
Land area: 19,827 square miles.
Permanent population: about 16,000 (range 12,000–22,000).
Overall density: roughly 0.8 people per square mile.
Almost all of that population sits on the water. River and lake corridors, a small fraction of the land, hold the town, villages, and working landings. A thin margin of forest is used for charcoal, trapping, and small mines. The rest of Kannavald — the great majority of the Deepwood — has no permanent settlement.

Working split
- Kolby-on-Vatn: 4,000–6,000
- Named villages and the Twaford crossing: 3,000–5,000 combined
- Scattered charcoal hamlets and isolated households: 5,000–7,000
- Remainder: seasonal camps, traplines, and mine-heads

The extra land beyond those corridors is empty forest. Distance between named places should feel long.
