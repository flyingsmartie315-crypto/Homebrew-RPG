# Local_Region_Kannavald_Deepwood

Overview
What the taming of the Ebon Peninsula did not finish. Dense canopy, black volcanic approaches, burns, and one Isern-facing lake. Kannavi majority in the woods. Isern law on the lake-shore and in the pits. The Still Stones keep their own law: do not approach. The southern wood-edge meets Ardairn at Waidwald. The western crag-tail hooks the sea at Kappvic.

Biome: Temperate / Forest
Sub-biomes: Alpine, Lake, Coast, River, Highland
Seasonal template is temperate. Alpine on the Spire and the named heights.

NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
Access
Slow. Burns, tracks, lake-boats. Waidwald’s 29-mile west–east road dies at this border unless a compact says otherwise.
Kappvic is entered from the Ardairn strand or by coastwise boats that know the mist.

People
Kannavi are the majority in the woods. Lake-shore law and the pits lean Isernvolk. Hollow-folk is a common nickname, not a cult-name.
Dar Waldward is hereditary and outside the Kaiserarch’s Zonarch gift. Peoples_Kannavi.txt. Peoples_Isernvolk.txt POLITY.

When no (bracketed detail) is included, the feature is wholly within this region.
The Mistcap tail-peninsula has no brackets. It is wholly Kannavald.
