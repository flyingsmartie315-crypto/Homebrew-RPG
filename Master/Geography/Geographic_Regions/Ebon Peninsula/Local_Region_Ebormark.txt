# Ebormark

Overview
The lower southern country beneath Ebon Spire. Black rock still owns the skyline, but the land falls into wooded ridges, river-floors, and the sheltered Leeshore. There is no lake. Melt and rain run to the gulf. The Hornwood Range walls the east. Ostbruck holds the mark’s law on the floors; the Knights Gryphon hold only the hanging vale under the eyrie. Most people live on the rivers and the strand.

Biome: Temperate
Sub-biomes: Alpine, Oceanic, Strand, River, Burn, Wetland, Gulf
The floors and shore are mild seasonal country. The Spire and Die Schulter are another climate. The Leeshore is wet coast. Swamp Bruchsee is failed water.

NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
Access
By Deepskaard Col from Kannavald, by the Leeshore and Leebight from the sea, by the Hornwood tracks from the east, or over the Spire.

Goods
Grain, hides, timber, basalt from Ebenbruch, ore from the Lowstacks, fish from the gulf. Gryphon work is Order business, not mark trade.

Population
Land area: about 24,750 square miles.
Permanent population: about 65,000 (range 50,000–80,000).
Overall density: roughly 3 people per square mile.
People sit on the rivers and the Leeshore. Hornwood, Die Schulter, and the high Spire-foot stay thin. Flachberge holds a fraction of the total.

Working split
- Ostbruck: 5,000–8,000
- Hornmünd and the east cluster: 3,000–6,000
- Forstmund and the Südhorn cluster: 2,000–4,000
- Eyrenhorst: 800–1,500
- Colfurt, Hornwacht, and other stone holds: 2,000–4,000
- Named villages: 8,000–14,000
- Scattered hamlets and farms: 30,000–45,000

Ostbruck is the political seat of the floors. Eyrenhorst is sovereign Order ground. Distance on the floors is shorter than in Kannavald; the Hornwood and the Spire still take a day.
