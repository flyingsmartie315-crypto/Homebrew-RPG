# Ebormark

Overview
The lower southern country beneath Ebon Spire. Black rock still shows on the skyline, but the land falls away into wooded ridges, river-floors, and the sheltered Leeshore. There is no lake. Melt and rain run straight to the gulf. The Hornwood Range walls the east. Most people live on the rivers and the strand; the Knights hold the Spire-foot as if it were their own realm.

NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
Access
By Deepskaard Col from Kannavald, by the Leeshore and Leebight from the sea, or over the Hornwood.

Goods
Grain, hides, timber from the Hornwood, fish from the gulf. Gryphon work and knight-service sit above ordinary trade.

Population
Land area: about 20,000 square miles.
Permanent population: about 40,000 (range 30,000–55,000).
Overall density: roughly 2 people per square mile.
People sit on the rivers and the Leeshore. The Hornwood and the high Spire-foot are thin.

Working split
- Greifhorst and its immediate lands: 4,000–7,000
- Named river and shore places: 10,000–16,000
- Scattered hamlets: 12,000–20,000
- Remainder: seasonal pasture, timber camps, and the Order’s out-posts

Lower and opener than Kannavald. Distances are shorter on the floors; the Hornwood still takes a day to cross.
