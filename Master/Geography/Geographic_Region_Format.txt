# Geographic_Region_Format.txt
# How to write a Geographic Region file.
# How to read geography is in Geography_Rules.txt.

File name
Geographic_Region_[Name].txt
Use the locked in-world name. Spaces become underscores.
Example: Geographic_Region_Svarthorn_Peninsula.txt

# [Geographic Region Name]

Visual Reference
[Link to a map or image clearly showing the shape of this Geographic Region and its position relative to neighbouring regions]

Overview
[3–6 sentences describing the overall physical character, climate patterns, major landforms, and general feel of the region. Keep this purely geographical.]

- [Local Region Name]
- [Local Region Name]

Notable Features
- [Major mountain ranges, river systems, forests, coastlines, plateaus]
- [Significant natural resources or large-scale environmental characteristics]

Notes
[Optional: travel difficulty, overall character, physical detail]

A Geographic Region does not take a Biome line or Environmental Scores.
It inherits the biomes of its Local Regions.

A Local Region that forms a natural border exists as one file.
Each Geographic Region lists it under Shared / Border Local Regions, not under its primary list.
