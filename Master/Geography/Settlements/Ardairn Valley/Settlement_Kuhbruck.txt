# Kuhbruck

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Eastern Heckengau. Two miles north of the Värdå. Crossroads against Kohlholt.
From Kornberg: 35 miles north-east on the Värdå road, past Mühlreih.

Overview
Castle-village. Pasture and agri. The keep sits the fork where hedge-country meets charcoal-wood. Kohlholt begins here. Heckengau ends here.

Control
Castle-holder under the Ardairn Zonarch. Attached to Kornberg for market and levy unless a later lock splits the east gau.

Population
Working figure: 300–400 plus the keep.

Economy
Stock and field. Wood-edge trade with Kohlholt. Not Nistburg. Not Giassrad.

Defences / Access
Castle on the crossroads. Two miles of lane south to the Värdå road.
35 miles down-river road to Kornberg, past Mühlreih. Wood-roads into Kohlholt.

Linked Features

Notes
Locked: Kuhbruck. Kuo / kū + bruck. Cowbridge in the worn Isern mouth.
Heckengau path-miles run to ~70. This seat is the east door.
