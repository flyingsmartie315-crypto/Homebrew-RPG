# Mühlreih

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Heckengau. 7 miles east of Kornberg along the Värdå road. Attached to Kornberg.

Overview
The mill-row of Heckengau. Wheels before the town wheels. Agri first, grinding second. Modest keep.

Control
Village lord under Kornberg. Mill-right argued with the town.

Population
Working figure: 260–340.

Economy
Fields and lesser mills. Sends meal and grain into Kornberg.

Defences / Access
Modest village keep. An estate and small castle sit in proximity (working: Aumühlhof). The house looks at the river-road.
Lanes through Heckengau. Does not own the town quays.
