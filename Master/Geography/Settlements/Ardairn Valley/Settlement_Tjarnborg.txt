# Tjarnborg

Type
Town

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Markholt. East bank of River Tjarn at Littmere. Castle and small town.

Overview
Mere-keep of Markholt. The Tjarn leaves Littmere under this wall and runs for Sanftgau and the Värdå. Kannavald water comes in as Grenbek and Trébek. Lārifenn sits on the far bank of the mere. This is a border-town that pretends to be a lake-town.

Control
Castle-house under the Ardairn Zonarch. Bound villages: Grenstatt, Kreuzholt. The Nithing Fort is a ruin, not a vassal.

Population
Working figure: 1,400–1,800.

Economy
Lake, wood, a little field. Road-toll toward Jagdsūd and Waidwald. Not pit-silver.

Defences / Access
Castle on the east bank of the Tjarn. Town against the keep.
Boat on Littmere. Road NE 12 miles to Kreuzholt. Ruin 13 miles east, crow.
