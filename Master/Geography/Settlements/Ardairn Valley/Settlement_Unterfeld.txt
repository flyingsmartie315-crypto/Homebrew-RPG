# Unterfeld

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Sanftgau. 7 miles north-east of Sċīrward Castle. Agri village.

Overview
The field-village of the lookout-keep. Grain and garden for Sċīrward Castle. No stables-claim — that is Rosshof.

Control
Under Sċīrward Castle.

Population
Working figure: 280–360.

Economy
Agri. Feeds the hill.

Defences / Access
Village keep modest. Lanes to Sċīrward Castle and across the gau.
