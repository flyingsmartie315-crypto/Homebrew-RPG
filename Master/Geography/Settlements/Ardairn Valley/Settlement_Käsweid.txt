# Käsweid

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Cattle-pasture village 13 miles west of Rissmarkt by road. Features a castle.
Road links: 11 miles north-east of Heckenfeld; from Giassrad 3.5 miles south to the crossroads then 12 miles east.

Overview
A pastoral village of cattle pastures and dairy holdings, known across the south of Rissfeld for the fine cheese that comes off its herds. A modest castle stands on the higher ground above the main pastures, watching the road to Rissmarkt. The place is agricultural first; the castle is the local mark of control and storage rather than a major fortress. Grain and stock move east to the Rissmarkt crossroads; cheese is the named product that leaves the valley.

Control
Under the Ardairn Zonarch. Local hold sits with the castle household. Not contested.

Population
Working figure: 450–700
Herders, dairy households, and the castle retainers. Swells at market seasons when the cheese goes to Rissmarkt.

Economy
Cattle pasturing and fine cheese. Secondary grain and dairy. Cheese is the reputation that travels; surplus stock and grain move with it to Rissmarkt. The castle holds stores and the local toll on the road.

Defences / Access
Castle on the rise above the pastures. Village itself is open, with the usual yard walls and stock fences.
Road 13 miles east to Rissmarkt.
Road 11 miles south-west to Heckenfeld.
From Giassrad: 3.5 miles south to the crossroads, then 12 miles east.

Notes
Name locked: Käsweid (*kasjus + *weidō — “Cheese-pasture”). Castle is local, not a crown appointment. Campaign hooks: cheese monopoly or spoiled batch, castle stores held against the Rissmarkt road, herd driven off toward the ravines, or a Zonarch tax on the named product.
