# Stallreihe

Type
Village (free stalls + smaller yard)

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Inside the Laed-Crags. Inward-facing cluster. Free stalls plus a smaller convict yard. Palisade faces the yard.

Overview
Top of the three inward-facing clusters. Free miners hold stall-right here; they run the work and keep the convicts off the better rock when they can. A smaller yard of convicts still sits inside the same palisade. Ore moves to Laedburg. The free stalls are the overseers and foremen of the Crags; the smaller yard is still chains.

Control
Free stalls under Laedburg’s smelter rights and the Ardairn Zonarch. Stall-right is held, not granted lightly. The smaller yard answers the same gate.

Population
Working figure: 300–500 convicts in the smaller yard
Plus the free stall-holders and their households (counted in Laedburg’s free/half-free 5,000–7,000, not here). Scales with the Crags’ overall numbers.

Economy
Free stall-right on the better rock. Smaller convict yard on the remainder. Ore leaves for Laedburg. The free miners keep the good veins; the yard takes what is left.

Defences / Access
Palisade faces the yard. Free stalls open toward the rock they hold. Approaches controlled from Laedburg.
