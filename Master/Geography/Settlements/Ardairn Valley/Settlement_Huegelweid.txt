# Hügelweid

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: 15 miles south of Vardgaard by the road. 13 miles inland from the western coast. Attached village of Vardgaard. Light rolling hills. Herding ground first.

Overview
Open hill-pasture behind Little Oc. Sheep and cattle on the rises. A modest keep on a knuckle of ground. Grain is secondary. The wind off the west sea gets here without becoming the Rivenstrand’s punch.

Control
Village lord in the keep, under Vardgaard. Not contested.

Population
Working figure: 260–340.
Herders, a few plough-teams in the folds, keep-garrison small.

Economy
Stock first. Cheese, hides, live beasts to Vardgaard and the mouth. Some field-crop in the lee of the hills.

Defences / Access
Modest keep. Wide views. Hard to sneak a herd-theft past it in good weather.
Road 15 miles north to Vardgaard. 13 miles west to the sea-board. Holtward and Waidwald lie further south along the same road.
