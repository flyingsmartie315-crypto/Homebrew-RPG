# Litflath

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Small village on a little plateau top, nestled in gentle wooded hills that precede the cliffs and ravines of Rissfeld’s north. Attached to a prominent castle (standby — not yet detailed).

Overview
A quiet plateau village sitting on the level top of a low rise, held inside the gentle wooded hills that run before Rissfeld’s northern cliffs and ravines. The plateau is small and open enough for yards and stock; the surrounding hills are wooded and easy. A prominent castle stands with the village — the local mark of control — though its full detail is not yet locked. The place feels like the last settled ground before the ground drops and hardens toward the quarry country and the dropping ravines.

Control
Under the Ardairn Zonarch. Local hold sits with the castle household.

Population
Working figure: 300–550
Households on the plateau, herders using the wooded hills, and the castle retainers.

Economy
Mixed pasture and light agriculture on the plateau. Wood from the surrounding hills. Secondary stock. Goods move south toward the lower roads and Rissmarkt when they leave.

Defences / Access
Prominent castle attached to the village (detail standby).
Village itself is open, with the usual yard walls.
Access via road from Deneshaw: 2 miles east to the crossroads, then 10 miles north, winding up the hills to Litflath.

Linked Features
- Rissfeld (northern cliffs and ravines beyond the hills)
- Deneshaw
- Castle (standby)

Notes
Name locked: Litflath (*lītilaz + *flata — “Little flat”). Plateau village on the gentle wooded hills that precede Rissfeld’s cliffs and ravines. Castle is attached but not yet detailed. Campaign hooks: the last easy ground before the ravines, castle stores on the plateau, or a watch kept on the climb from Deneshaw.
