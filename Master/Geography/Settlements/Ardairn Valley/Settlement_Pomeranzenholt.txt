# Pomeranzenholt

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: North of the Värdå, on Herrenbruck’s side. Attached village of Herrenbruck. Agri village and glass-house ground for the Herrenau estates.

Overview
A village that should not be able to smell of orange-rind this far north. The wonder is the glass: long houses of dear panes and stoves, where citrus and estate-grapes are forced for tables in Ebenheim. Men come from Osricford to look. They do not eat. The fruit is not a crop. It is a boast.

Control
Estate glass-wrights and a Herrenbruck reeve. The houses that paid for the glass treat the village as a yard of their own. Not a second town.

Population
Working figure: 250–320.
Gardeners, stove-men, glass-menders, tied agri folk. A few specialists worth more than the reeve.

Economy
Ordinary fields on the edge.
The money is the glass-houses: bitter orange, lemon, forced grape, herbs the estates want in Longnight.
Nothing here feeds the peninsula. It feeds pride. Broken glass is a disaster. A cold snap with dead stoves is a feud.

Defences / Access
Modest, well-made stone walls. Kept pretty. The estates look down on this village the same as Hofstatt; no one wants a slum under the glass.
The glass is still the thing that must not burn or shatter.
Road to Herrenbruck and Hofstatt. Estate tracks up the Herrenau. Visitors come to stare and are moved on.

Notes
Pomeranze = the bitter orange the old world already knew. Holt because the first houses sat in a stand they did not fully clear.
Viable at this tech: brick stoves, painted walls, expensive glass, summer wheels into yard, winter shut. Isern iron and craft make it plausible. Do not give them sheet-glass acres.
Campaign hooks: a pane from Spire-country that is too clear; a gardener who can keep trees alive and wants a name; frost the night a Kaiserarch is due to dine.
