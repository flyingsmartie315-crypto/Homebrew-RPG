# Kaerpact

Type
Castle (with joined town)

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: On the west bank of the Sund at the Ardairn / Kannavald border. Inside the Adweid (703 square miles). 50 miles south-east of Sundbruck following the Sund upstream (timber trail).

Overview
Border fort signifying the bordermark between Isernvolk lands and Kannavi holdings. The original treaty of Arnulf Vatnhar and Grimwald — where the ruling of Dar Waldward was declared — was signed in a small fortified watchtower that remains as a feature inside the later construction. A small town has grown against the castle, sustained by the Adweid’s agri and pasture. Most traffic moves on the Sund; the timber trail is secondary. At the border point the woodland rises like a solid wall on a steady incline toward the highlands.

Control
Border seat under the Ardairn Zonarch. Toll on river traffic is held here. Not a separate crown appointment. The Waldward compact is remembered in the older stone.

Population
Working figure: 1,100–1,500
Castle household, garrison, town households, and the river-toll staff. Seasonal labour from the Adweid estates.

Economy
River toll on the Sund. Agri and pasture from the surrounding Adweid. Secondary timber along the trail. The town is sustained by the feature it sits in; surplus moves downstream toward Sundbruck or stays on the border.

Defences / Access
Castle on the west bank. Original treaty watchtower preserved inside the later walls. Town joined to the fort.
Most access is by the Sund. Timber trail (road) 50 miles north-west to Sundbruck. 
62 miles south up the timber trail or the Sund to reach the Twaford Twins.
Woodland wall and incline limit easy approaches from the Kannavald side.

Linked Features
- Adweid (703 sq mi)
- Sund (west bank; river toll)
- Timber trail
- Sundbruck
- Original treaty watchtower (internal feature)

Notes
Name locked: Kaerpact.
Original sense: treaty-watchtower of Arnulf Vatnhar and Grimwald (Dar Waldward declared here prior to the final campaign of the Great Clearing).
Small town joined to the castle, sustained by the Adweid. Campaign hooks: toll dispute on the Sund, older stone used as a mark of the Waldward compact, or the woodland wall as a hard border in practice.
