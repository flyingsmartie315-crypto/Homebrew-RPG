# Rosshof

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Sanftgau. 6 miles east of Sċīrward Castle. Small castle, stables, pasture.

Overview
Horse-and-stock seat. Small castle. Pasture first. The gau’s riding-yard, not its granary.

Control
Under Sċīrward Castle. Castle-holder may be a cadet or a stable-lord.

Population
Working figure: 200–280 plus yard-hands at the stables.

Economy
Pasture, remounts, stock for Sċīrward Castle and the south-bank road. Not a mill-town.

Defences / Access
Small castle. Open approaches on purpose — you see a thief coming across grass.
6 miles west to Sċīrward Castle. Further east toward Sundbruck.
