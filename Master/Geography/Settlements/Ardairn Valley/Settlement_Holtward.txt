# Holtward

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: 25 miles south of Vardgaard by the road. Attached village of Vardgaard. Sits by Waidwald.

Overview
Agri village and forest-gate. Modest keep. The rows feed Vardgaard. The wardens keep Waidwald. Wealth hunts here if the village lord says they may. If he does not, the wood is only trees.

Control
Village lord in the keep, under Vardgaard. The hunt-right is his to grant. That right is worth more than the plough.

Population
Working figure: 300–380.
Agri workers and forestry wardens. Keep-garrison small.

Economy
Ordinary fields.
Warden-labour and hunt-leave are the political crop. Poaching is a feud, not a sport.

Defences / Access
Modest keep. Handsome enough that a guest-lord will sleep there.
Road 25 miles north to Vardgaard. Tracks into Waidwald.

Linked Features
- Vardgaard — Settlement_Vardgaard.txt
- Waidwald — 494 square miles. Noble / wealthy hunting reserve. Entry by leave of Holtward’s lord.
- Hügelweid — Settlement_Huegelweid.txt (closer to the coast road)

Notes
Waidwald is the 494-square-mile stand. South edge is the Kannavald border. A 29-mile road runs west–east through the middle. Named hunting-weald. Worked woodland in the Ardairn tally, not Kannavald wild.
