# Kornberg

Type
Town

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: North bank of the Värdå, 14 path-miles upriver from Herrenbruck (29 from Osricford). Keep on a hill over the river. Town on the water.

Overview
The second hub on the Värdå after Herrenbruck. A small town under a prominent keep. Mills on the current. Warehouses and granaries on the bank. Behind it lies Heckengau: hedges, pasture, and cultivated fields pinched between the Herrenau to the north and the Värdå to the south. This is not villa-glass country. This is where the estates’ grain becomes freight.

Control
Keep-lord under the Ardairn Zonarch. Not contested. Lives off mill-right and store-right.

Population
Working figure: 2,200–2,700.
Millers, warehousemen, barge-hands, keep-garrison, market folk.

Economy
Watermills. Granaries. Warehouse on the Värdå.
Takes Heckengau grain and stock. Sends it down to Herrenbruck and Osricford.

Defences / Access
Prominent keep on the hill. Town wall modest.
Access: Värdå barge. North-bank road. 14 path-miles down to Herrenbruck across Wagenbruck’s country. Heckengau lanes to the three attached villages.
