# Speicherstatt

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: 14 miles north of Kornberg, on the southern bank of the Herrenau. Attached to Kornberg. Agri village tied to the town granaries.

Overview
Store-village of the hedge-country. Grain leaves the fields here and becomes Kornberg’s warehouse problem. Modest keep. Hedges and pasture on every lane.

Control
Village lord under Kornberg.

Population
Working figure: 280–360.

Economy
Grain, some stock. Labour for the town stores.

Defences / Access
Modest village keep. An estate and small castle sit in proximity (working: Speicherhof), on this same south bank of the Herrenau.
Heckengau lanes to Kornberg and the other two attached villages.
