# Grenstatt

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Markholt. One mile up Grenbek from Littmere. South of Lārifenn. West-water of the mere.

Overview
Beck-village on the Kannavald inflow. Boat-country. The fen is next door.

Control
Bound to Tjarnborg.

Population
Working figure: 240–320.

Economy
Mere-fish, small fields, wood-edge.

Defences / Access
From Tjarnborg: 6 miles by boat across Littmere, then 1 mile up Grenbek.
Or by rim: 4.5 miles north around Littmere, cross the Tjarn, then 7 miles around the east rim and the swamp — the long wet way.
Working name. The “east bank of Littmere” line in the first note is Tjarnborg’s bank, not this village.
