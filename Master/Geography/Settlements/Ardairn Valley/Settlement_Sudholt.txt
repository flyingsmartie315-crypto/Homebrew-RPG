# Sūdholt

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Kohlholt. 1.5 miles south of Nistburg.

Overview
South-holt. The village under the nest. Wood-edge agri and charcoal-row for Nistburg. Nistau is already past, running SE to Holtbeck.

Control
Under Nistburg.

Population
Working figure: 250–330.

Economy
Small fields at the wood-foot. Charcoal and timber-labour. Game when the keep allows.

Defences / Access
Modest keep or stockade. The castle is 1.5 miles north in the stone.
Tracks to Nistburg and down-holt toward Holtbeck / the Värdå.
