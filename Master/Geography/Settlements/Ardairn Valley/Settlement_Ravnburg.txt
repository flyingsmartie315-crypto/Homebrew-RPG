# Ravnburg

Type
Castle (ruling seat)

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Rissfeld’s ruling seat. 5 miles north of Litflath by road, set among the tiered plateaus and ravines of the Flat Tops (31 square miles). One of the most defensible castles on the continent by virtue of the terrain.

Overview
Ravnburg stands on the tiered plateaus that rise north of Litflath, cut by dropping ravines on every approach. The ground itself is the defence: successive flats, steep drops, and narrow climbs that turn any assault into a series of isolated fights. It is the ruling seat of Rissfeld. In the early years of the Breaking of the Horns it was a prominent outpost, holding the northern edge of the then-cleared ground. The castle remains the mark of local control; the Zonarch’s authority in Rissfeld is exercised from here.

Control
Ruling seat of Rissfeld under the Ardairn Zonarch. Direct hold. Not a separate crown appointment.

Population
Working figure: 800–1,200
Castle household, retainers, and the small settlement that has grown on the nearest plateau. Seasonal labour from the quarry country and the lower hills.

Economy
Control of the northern ravine and quarry approaches. Toll and store on the road from Litflath. Secondary stock and timber from the surrounding tiers. The seat’s wealth is positional more than productive.

Defences / Access
Layout: Ravnburg (The Continental Tiered Bastion)
Footprint: 250 × 200 metres (spanning multiple vertical terrain shelves).
Strategic Intent: Serving as the supreme ruling seat of Rissfeld; engineered to exploit dropping ravines and successive flats to force attackers into isolated, multi-tiered uphill fights.
Structural Layout:
- Perimeter (The Lower Revetments & Curtains): A series of heavy, 12-metre-high masonry revetment walls that hug the natural stone shelves. The lower perimeter stretches 250 metres across the southern approach, anchored directly into the vertical cliff faces. Outward-facing walls are 4 metres thick, built from local quarry stone to withstand heavy bombardment.
- The Great Gatehouse (The Chokepoint Complex): Situated at the entry of the winding road from Litflath. It is not a single gate, but a massive 40-metre-long fortified corridor featuring an outer barbican, two portcullises, a central killing ground overlooked by high murder holes, and an angled ramp that prevents the use of direct-line siege machinery.
- The Middle Tier (Settlement & Retainer Wards): A sprawling 150 × 100 metre flat plateau nestled safely behind the lower walls. This tier acts as the primary economic hub. It houses the permanent settlement (quarters for 800–1,200 residents, retainers, and garrison), vast timber yards, and vaulted storehouses holding tolls and provisions from the southern road.
- The Upper Keep (The Zonarch's High Seat): Perched on the highest, most isolated northern rock plateau. Measuring 60 × 50 metres, this ultimate stronghold features its own 15-metre-high curtain wall, a grand three-storey palas hall for executing regional authority, a deep cistern cut down through the bedrock, and a high watch-tower commanding a flawless view over the northern ravine and quarry approaches.
Access: road 5 miles south to Litflath (winding descent). Further south the road continues toward Deneshaw and the lower country. No easy approach from the ravine faces.
