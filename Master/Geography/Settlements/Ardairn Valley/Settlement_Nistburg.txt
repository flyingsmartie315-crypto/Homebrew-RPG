# Nistburg

Type
Town

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Kohlholt. South base of Lūtaberg, nestled into the stone.

Overview
Nest-keep in the charcoal wood. Lūtaberg (321 m) stands over the canopy behind it. Nistau runs out of the stone and 8 miles south-east to join Holtbeck; Holtbeck is the water that reaches the Värdå. Sūdholt sits 1.5 miles south at the keep’s foot-road.

Control
Castle-house under the Ardairn Zonarch. Wood-right and game-right in Kohlholt. Not Waidwald’s hunt-lord. Not Laedburg’s pit-money.

Population
Working figure: 600–900 in keep and yard.
Sūdholt is the agri/wood village, not this count.

Economy
Timber, charcoal, game. A little stone. Not a smelter.

Defences / Access
Keep in the rock. The hill watches the holt.
Access: vale-track down to Sūdholt; Holtbeck toward the Värdå; wood-roads through Kohlholt (path-miles 70–90, north bank).

Linked Features
- Kohlholt
- Lūtaberg — 321 m. Peers over the trees
- Nistau — from the castle, 8 miles SE to Holtbeck
- Holtbeck — joined by Nistau; this is the stream that enters the Värdå
- Sūdholt — 1.5 miles south. Settlement_Sudholt.txt
- Värdå — north bank

Notes
Names locked. Nest, not a white keep.
