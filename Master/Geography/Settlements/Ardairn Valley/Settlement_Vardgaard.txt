# Vardgaard

Type
Town

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: South bank of the Värdå at the mouth. Two miles across the water from Osricford. Common name: Little Oc.

Overview
The south-bank half of the landing-mouth. Not the crown. Not the yards. Little Oc is houses, byres, lesser quays, and the people who wait on a barge. In law it answers to Osricford. In the street it is its own town — cheaper rents, more stock in the yards, a shorter temper when the north bank hoards hulls or news. Transit is the river. There is no bridge.

Control
Isern crown of Westmark, administered from Osricford. Local reeves and quay-masters run the day. Not contested. Periodic sulk, not revolt.

Population
Working share of the 50,000-mouth: 15,000–18,000.
Isern mark-folk, drovers, barge-hands, small craft. Fewer shipwrights than the north. More mouths that live off the crossing.

Economy
South-bank service to the capital: food, lodgings, stock-pens, small repair, ferry labour.
Takes overflow when the north yards are full. Does not lay the great keels. Those stay on Osricford’s drydocks.
Lives or starves by the barge-run. A closed mouth is a closed town.

Defences / Access
Lighter wall than the north rise. Quay-stakes, a river-gate, watch toward the sea and toward the two-mile crossing.
Access: Westmark south-bank roads, the river inland, and barge to Osricford. No standing bridge.

Notes
Little Oc is the name used on the strand. Vardgaard is the charter name.
Do not give it a second king. Do give it a reason to delay a barge.
Campaign hooks: a ferry-guild with more power than its rank; south-bank men who saw a Jarn sail first; a levy that cannot cross in a blow.
The 50,000 figure is the mouth. Do not add Osricford and Vardgaard as if they were two capitals.
