# Heckenfeld

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Centre of Heckengau. 22 miles north-east of Kornberg. Attached to Kornberg. Hedgerows and pasture.

Overview
The hedge-village. Fields cut into small closes. Stock in the lanes. Modest keep. Looks like the country it is named for.

Control
Village lord under Kornberg.

Population
Working figure: 250–330.

Economy
Mixed agri and herd inside the 467. No mill-right worth a war.

Defences / Access
Modest village keep. An estate and small castle sit in proximity (working: Heckensitz). The house sits in the hedges, not in the village street.
Heckengau lanes only.
