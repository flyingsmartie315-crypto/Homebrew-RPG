# Mühlstatt

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: South bank of the Värdå, upstream of Vardgaard. Attached village of Vardgaard. Agri village with a watermill on the river.

Overview
The mill-village of Little Oc’s hinterland. Grain comes in by cart and river. Flour goes down to Vardgaard and across the mouth when the barges run. A modest keep watches the wheel and the road. No one writes songs about it. The city eats because it turns.

Control
Village lord in the keep, under Vardgaard. Not contested.

Population
Working figure: 280–360.
Mill-hands, plough-folk, a small keep-garrison.

Economy
Agriculture and the mill. Toll on the wheel is the living.

Defences / Access
Modest keep. Village wall enough for thieves, not for a host.
Access: south-bank Värdå road and the river itself, up from Vardgaard.
