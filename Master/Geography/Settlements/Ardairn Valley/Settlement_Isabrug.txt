# Isabrug

Type
Town (walled capital)

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Principal market-hub and capital of the Grasgau. Exactly 20 path-miles east upstream from Graswick, at the river-junction where the Íså empties into the Värdå north bank (Värdå path-mile 118). Sits entirely on the western bank of the Íså.

Overview
Walled capital of the Grasgau. All overland trade between the eastern valley and the inner Grasgau pastures is forced through its checkpoint gates. A massive stone-arched bridge spans the Íså. The southern docks are a high-volume river port that handles the district’s agricultural exports and operates the primary, tightly guarded ferry networks crossing south across the wide Värdå channel directly into the Laed-Crags penal mining concessions.

Control
Capital seat of the Grasgau under the Ardairn Zonarch. Checkpoint and ferry rights held here. The bridge and the port are the town’s leverage.

Population
Working figure: 3,500–4,800
Town households, market, garrison, port crews, ferry staff, and the checkpoint. Seasonal surge at harvest and when the Crags call for labour.

Economy
Principal market of the Grasgau. Agricultural exports leave by the southern docks. Toll and checkpoint on the bridge and the eastern road. Ferry traffic across the Värdå into the Laed-Crags. The town lives on the pastures it gates and the river it commands.

Defences / Access
Heavily fortified stone curtain-wall. Massive stone-arched bridge over the Íså. Checkpoint gates on the overland approaches.
Southern docks and guarded ferry network across the Värdå to the Laed-Crags.
Road 20 path-miles west downstream to Graswick.
Sits wholly on the western bank of the Íså.
