# Vitriolstatt

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Inside the Laed-Crags. 7 miles east of Laedburg. 2 miles north of the village to the Värdå.

Overview
Palisade faces in. The air is the punishment. Indebted labour is thick here — bound years in the acid-yards — with a convict core on the worst vats. Harsher than a street. Better than Kesselgrube if “better” means you might leave when the debt is paid.

Control
Warden out of Laedburg. Free-miner foremen on the vats.

Population
Working unfree: 400–650 (convicts plus indebted).
Convict share sits in the lower half of that band. Indebted fill the rest.
Warders and foremen extra.

Economy
Vitriol, some antimony. Barrels down to Laedburg. Do not store them in the town if the wind is wrong.

Defences / Access
Inward palisade. The smell is a wall of its own.
Tracks to Laedburg and to the other two clusters.
