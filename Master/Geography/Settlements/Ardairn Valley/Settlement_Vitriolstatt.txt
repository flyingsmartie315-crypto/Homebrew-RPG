# Vitriolstatt

Type
Village (bound-year yard)

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Inside the Laed-Crags. Inward-facing cluster on the vats and mid-tier veins. Palisade faces the yard.

Overview
Vats and bound years. The middle tier of the prison-industrial complex. Indebted labour — still harsh, still inside the palisade, but not the noose and not the worst rock. Vitriol (the old green and blue sulfate salts) is worked here alongside the mid-grade lead. Ore and salts move to Laedburg; the bound do not leave until the year is served.

Control
Bound-year yard under Laedburg’s smelter rights and the Ardairn Zonarch. Not free. Not the bottom tier.

Population
Working figure: 400–650 unfree (mixed convict and indebted)
Scales with the Crags’ overall standing numbers. Bound years are the bulk; a smaller convict share works the harder vats.

Economy
Vitriol and mid-tier ore. Bound labour. Output leaves for Laedburg. No free stall-right inside this yard.

Defences / Access
Palisade faces the yard. Vat-works and barrack rows. Approaches controlled from Laedburg.
