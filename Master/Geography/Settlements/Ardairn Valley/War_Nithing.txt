# War_Nithing

Working title. From nīðing / nīð — the worst Germanic insult: a person utterly unworthy, a coward, a breaker of sacred oaths or pacts.

Not a Whitmark war. Older. Ebon Peninsula. Isern–Kannavi border.

LOCKED
--------------------------------------------------
Place: the razed border fort 13 miles east of Tjarnborg (crow). File: Settlement_Nithing_Fort.txt.
Country: Markholt / Kannavald lip.
Name of the fight (Isern chronicle): the Nithing War.
Kannavi name: not locked.

SHAPE (not yet a campaign)
--------------------------------------------------
A border skirmish that became a naming.
Someone broke a compact — or was said to. The fort fell. It was not rebuilt.
Osric’s Landing Compact is the later, surviving pact. This fight is why men still spit nīðing when a wood-right is treated as a pit-charter, or a stave-line is crossed as if it were open floor.

Do not write it as the taming of the peninsula. That is a longer story.
Do not write it as Kannavi turning on Hallvard’s later wars. Those honour the compact.
This is an earlier souring at one fort.
