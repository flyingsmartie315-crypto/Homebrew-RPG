# Rissmarkt

Type
Town

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: South border of Rissfeld. Crossroads where Rissfeld meets Heckengau and Kohlholt.
12 miles north-east of Kuhbruck.

Overview
Walled market town. The door between hedge, charcoal-wood, and rift-country. Quarry-stone from Rissfeld’s north cliffs comes through here. Grain from Heckengau. Burn from Kohlholt. Not Kuhbruck’s cow-bridge. Not Nistburg’s nest.

Control
Walled town under the Ardairn Zonarch. Rissfeld’s south market.

Population
Working figure: 1,800–2,400.

Economy
Market. Stone, grain, charcoal. Road-toll on the three-way fork.

Defences / Access
Town wall. Keep inside.
Road 12 miles south-west to Kuhbruck. Heckengau lanes. Kohlholt wood-road. Rissfeld climb north toward the ravines.

Notes
Working name: Rissmarkt. Kill it if the wall should own the word (Rissburg) or the fork (Risskreuz).
