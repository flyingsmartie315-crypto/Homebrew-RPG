# Kesselgrube

Type
Village (convict yard)

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Inside the Laed-Crags. Inward-facing cluster on the worst veins. Palisade faces the yard.

Overview
Worst veins of the Laed-Crags. Convict labour. Barrack-pits. The palisade faces inward; the rock faces out. This is the bottom tier of the prison-industrial complex. Ore moves to Laedburg; the people do not.

Control
Convict yard under Laedburg’s smelter rights and the Ardairn Zonarch. Sentence is served here. Not a free town.

Population
Working figure: 500–850 convicts
Barrack-pits. Scales with the Crags’ overall 1,200–2,000 standing convicts. War-surge can push the whole Crags toward ~3,000; this yard takes the heaviest share.

Economy
Convict labour on the worst lead and antimony veins. Ore leaves for Laedburg. No free market inside the palisade.

Defences / Access
Palisade faces the yard. Barrack-pits. Approaches controlled from Laedburg.
No open road for the sentenced.

Notes
Bottom tier: egregious criminals serving sentence. Worst veins. Campaign hooks: yard break, ore shortfall that forces a surge, or a sentence that was meant for a higher tier and ends here.
