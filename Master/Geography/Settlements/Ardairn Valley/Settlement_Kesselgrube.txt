# Kesselgrube

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Dead centre of the Laed-Crags. 5 miles south-east of Laedburg. Deepest cluster. Worst veins.

Overview
A “village” whose palisade faces in. The basin-pit. Most of the Crags’ convicts sit here. Egregious sentences. The houses are barracks. The street is a yard. Free miners come as foremen and leave. No one pretends this is Hofstatt.

Control
Warden appointed out of Laedburg / the Zonarch. Free-miner foremen run the vein day to day.

Population
Working convicts: 500–850 (share of the Crags’ 1,200–2,000).
Warders and free-miner overseers: a few hundred who do not sleep in the yard if they can help it.

Economy
Worst lead and the work no stall-holder wants. Ore to Laedburg.

Defences / Access
Palisade faces inward. Towers look at the yard first, the cliffs second.
Access: tracks to Laedburg. No market road worth the name.

Notes
Not a village in the Heckengau sense. Count it as one because men eat and die in one place.
Campaign hooks: a man who should have gone to Stallreihe; a palisade gate that opens the wrong way in a panic.
