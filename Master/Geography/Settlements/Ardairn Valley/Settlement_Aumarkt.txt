# Aumarkt

Type
Town

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Sanftgau, west end as now written. South bank of the Värdå.
13 miles west of Sċīrward Castle.
15 miles upstream from Kornberg (Kornberg is the opposite, north bank).

Overview
Market town with a castle. Mills on the Värdå. Grain and stock of the west gau become freight here before the river is Laedburg’s problem. Not Kornberg’s north-bank warehouses. Not Sċīrward’s hill-view.

Control
Castle-house under the Ardairn Zonarch. Sanftgau west-market. Sċīrward is the lookout, not this quay.

Population
Working figure: 2,000–2,800.

Economy
Mills, market, river-freight. Sanftgau agri. Does not smelt.

Defences / Access
Castle on the south bank. Mills on the current.
Road 13 miles east to Sċīrward. River 15 miles down to opposite Kornberg. South-bank road toward Sundbruck / Laedburg further east.
