# Giassrad

Type
Town

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: West Hamarholt, at the base of Krummnagel. 4 miles north along the Herrenau from the Heckengau / Hamarholt border.

Overview
Foundry-town under an ugly peak. Krummnagel rises out of the Dalhamar foothills like a crooked toenail. Ore comes off that stone. Charcoal comes from Hamarholt hamlets.
The name is the work: Giassrad — pour and wheel (*giutan + *rad. Worn mouth Giassrad). Water-wheels drive the blast on a manufactured steep race of the fast Herrenau. Foundries sit the length of that incline. The river was cut to serve the fire.

Control
Town-keep under the Ardairn Zonarch. Not Herrenbruck’s satellite. Not Nistburg’s charcoal-keep. Ore and burn.

Population
Working figure: 1,600–2,100.

Economy
Foothill ore. Woodland charcoal. Sends both down the Herrenau road toward Herrenbruck and the Värdå.

Defences / Access
From Herrenbruck: the Herrenau road already detailed (Wagenbruck / north-bank up-water).
Then 11 miles through the top of Heckengau along the south bank of the Herrenau origin, to the Heckengau–Hamarholt border.
Then 4 miles following the Herrenau north to this town.
Keep at the crooked foot. Hamlets in the wood are not this file.
