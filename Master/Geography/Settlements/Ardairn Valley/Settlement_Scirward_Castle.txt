# Sċīrward Castle

Type
Town

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Sanftgau. Prominent hill-castle overlooking the gau. 24 miles west of Sundbruck as the crow flies.

Overview
The lookout-keep of the gentle gau. Hill over pasture. Not a smelter. Not a dam. A seat that watches grain and stock instead of vitriol. Sundbruck and Laedburg are the dirty east. This is the clean west of the same south bank.

Control
Castle-house under the Ardairn Zonarch. Attached villages: Unterfeld, Rosshof.

Population
Working figure: 800–1,100 in keep and hillside town.
Seasonal swell at muster and harvest.

Economy
Sanftgau grain and stock. Tithe from Unterfeld and Rosshof. Does not feed on pit-silver.

Defences / Access
Prominent hill-castle. Commands the gau below.
Access: south-bank country. 24 miles crow east to Sundbruck and the Sund-mouth bridge.

Notes
Locked name: Sċīrward Castle. Scīr + ward. The view is the point.
Not Laedburg’s keep. Not Stígarborg.
