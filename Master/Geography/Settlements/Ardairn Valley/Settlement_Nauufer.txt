# Nauufer

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: West bank of the River Tjarn. 15 miles south-west of Aumarkt by road.
10 miles down the Tjarn to the Värdå join.

Overview
Aumarkt’s river-village. West bank. Fields and a modest keep. The market-town is the mill-and-castle; this is the water-lane that feeds it.

Control
Bound to Aumarkt.

Population
Working figure: 250–330.

Economy
Agri. A little river-carry up to Aumarkt’s market.

Defences / Access
Road 15 miles north-east to Aumarkt.
Tjarn 10 miles down to the Värdå.
