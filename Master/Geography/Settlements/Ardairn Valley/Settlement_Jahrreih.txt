# Jahrreih

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: West Hamarholt. 11 miles west of Giassrad. Road climbs a hilly incline.

Overview
Charcoal village. The road in is the tell: rows of identical planted trees, each block last year’s harvest, the next block the year before that. Coppice as a clock. Hamlets around the village burn what the rows grow. Giassrad’s furnaces eat this road.

Control
Bound to Giassrad.

Population
Working figure: 280–360 in the village. Hamlets extra and unfiled.

Economy
Charcoal first. Timber only as the rows allow. Not ore. That is Krummnagel’s.

Defences / Access
Modest keep. The incline is the approach.
Road 11 miles east, downhill toward Giassrad and the Herrenau race.

- Krummnagel — visible if the weather is clean; not this village’s rock
