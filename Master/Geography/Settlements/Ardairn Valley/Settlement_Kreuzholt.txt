# Kreuzholt

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Markholt. 12 miles north-east of Tjarnborg by road. South of Jagdsūd, at the crossroads of the road that runs through the wood’s centre.

Overview
Cross-wood village. Hunt-traffic from Jagdsūd and Markholt meets here. Not a mere-village.

Control
Bound to Tjarnborg.

Population
Working figure: 220–300.

Economy
Road, wood, a little field. Game when Jagdsūd’s leave is sold.

Defences / Access
Road 12 miles SW to Tjarnborg. Wood-road through Jagdsūd’s centre.
