# Kappstrand

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: On Kappvic, under the Mistcap Crags. Small port on the Western Sea-Board. Sheltered by the 29-square-mile tail-peninsula of the crags.

Overview
Fish and timber under standing mist. The harbour is not Osricford. It is a notch the north wind does not fully own, closed by a sharp little peninsula that is the last tail of Mistcap. Lumber comes off the crag-foot. Boats work the 103 square miles of sheltered water. Kannavald is close enough to smell.

Control
Village reeve and harbour-master under the Ardairn Zonarch. Not a Vardgaard attachment. Not Osricford’s second yard.

Population
Working figure: 220–300.
Fishers, timber-hands, a few coast-pilots who know the vic.

Economy
Fish, lumber, a little coastwise carry into Kappvic. No glass, no citrus, no drydock of note.

Defences / Access
Modest wall and a harbour-chain if they have warning. The peninsula is the real shelter.
Access: coast-road along the Western Sea-Board; tracks to Mistcap foot; sea through the vic.

Linked Features
- Kappvic — 103 sq mi sheltered coastal water
- Mistcap tail-peninsula — 29 sq mi. Kannavald land. Sharp tail of the Mistcap Crags. Makes the shelter; not Ardairn ground.
- Western Sea-Board
- Mistcap Crags — Kannavald. Kappstrand takes the strand and the vic, not the tail.

Notes
Kappvic and Kappstrand locked.
114 miles of Western Sea-Board is the wriggle of the coves, not a straight ruler.
Working pop only.
