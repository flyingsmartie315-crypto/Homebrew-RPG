# Deneshaw

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Rissfeld. Base of the mid-country hills and light woods.
8 miles north-east of Rissmarkt.

Overview
Agri and forestry village. The wall-market is down-slope. The light wood starts here. Not the north ravines. Not Grenzweid’s open pasture.

Control
Bound to Rissmarkt.

Population
Working figure: 240–320.

Economy
Field and timber. Sends both to Rissmarkt.

Defences / Access
Modest keep. Road 8 miles south-west to Rissmarkt. Tracks into the mid-hills.

Linked Features

- Rissfeld — mid-country hills and light wood

Notes
Locked: Deneshaw. Dene + shaw — vale-wood at the foot of the mid-hills.
