# Laedburg

Type
Town (smelter-town)

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: South bank of the Värdå, 98 path-miles up from the mouth. Immediate west of the Laed-Crags. East of the Sund.

Overview
Smelter-town for the Laed-Crags. Ore comes in from the Crags; metal and slag leave by barge down the Värdå toward Osricford. The town itself sits outside the chains. Free and half-free pit-folk, smelter hands, and the streets that serve them. The prison-industrial work stays inside the Crags; Laedburg is the gate and the market for what comes out.

Control
Under the Ardairn Zonarch. Smelter rights and the barge toll are held here. The three inward-facing clusters inside the Crags answer through Laedburg for ore and sentence, but the yards themselves are not part of the town streets.

Population
Working figure: 5,000–7,000
Free and half-free pit-folk, smelter hands, barge crews, and town households. Outside the chains. Does not include the convict and indebted labour inside the Crags.

Economy
Smelting. Lead, antimony, and vitriol from the Crags. Barges of ore and metal down to Osricford. Secondary trade with the free stalls. The town lives on the metal that leaves the yards.

Defences / Access
Town wall on the river side. Smelter yards open toward the Crags.
Barge on the Värdå. Road west toward the Sund and east into the Crags approaches.
The three clusters (Kesselgrube, Vitriolstatt, Stallreihe) lie inside the Crags, not in the town.
