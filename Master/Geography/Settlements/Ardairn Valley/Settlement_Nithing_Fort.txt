# The Nithing Fort

Type
other

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Markholt / Kannavald lip. 13 miles east of Tjarnborg as the crow flies. Razed border fort.

Overview
Ruin and hazard. A border fort thrown down in an ancient skirmish with the Kannavi. The Isern name for that fight is the Nithing War — nīðing / nīð: the man who is nothing, coward, oath-and-pact breaker.
Do not camp it as a village. Do not restock it as a Zonarch seat without a later chronicle.

Control
Nobody living. Markholt claims the ground. Kannavald remembers the insult.

Population
None.

Economy
None. Bones and a story.

Defences / Access
Rubble. 13 miles east of Tjarnborg, crow. The border is the point.
