# Dracan-Vane

Type
Village

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Western fringe of Drakenfeld. Directly east, 10 miles up-slope from Eirblast.

Overview
Premium smelting-fuel settlement on the western edge of the primeval timber. The village lives under the shadow of the dense canopy. Its people are hereditary charcoal-burners, wood-splitters, and kilnmakers who harvest the ancient growth to produce softwood charcoal for the blast furnaces at Eirblast and the wider iron works. The centre and hall stand raised on a stone revetment earthwork; the central hub stores the produce of the surrounding hamlets before it moves to market.

Control
Timber and charcoal hub under the Ardairn Zonarch, with the fuel itself tied to the Kaiserarch’s iron. The revetment and the hub answer here. Not a free cut.

Population
Working figure: 550–850
Charcoal-burners, wood-splitters, kilnmakers, and the households of the surrounding hamlets whose produce is stored at the hub.

Economy
Premium softwood charcoal. The ancient dense growth is the raw material. Output moves west down-slope to Eirblast and the Eisenberg furnaces. Secondary timber and the stored surplus of the hamlets leave through the central hub. The village’s wealth is the fuel it makes, not open fields.

Defences / Access
Village centre and hall raised on a stone revetment earthwork. Central hub for storage.
Road 10 miles west down-slope to Eirblast.
Timber tracks run east and north into Drakenfeld; these are burner and warden paths more than open lanes.
