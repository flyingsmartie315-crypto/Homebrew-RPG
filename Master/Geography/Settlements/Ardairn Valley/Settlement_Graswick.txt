# Graswick

Type
Village (with castle)

Location
Local Region: Ardairn Valley (common: Westmark)
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Southern edge of the Grasgau, on the north bank of the Värdå. Across the Värdå to the south lie Sundbruck and Laedburg. 15 miles by road from Sūdholt.

Overview
Prominent village and surrounding hamlets inside the Grasgau. Sits on the north bank of the Värdå at the southern edge of the Great Pasture-District. A watermill serves the village and the nearby holdings. Castle Vardbucht and its estate stand above the bank; the castle is the local seat. The road from Sūdholt brings traffic from the west; the river brings barges from the south.

Control
Castle-house under the Ardairn Zonarch. Estate and village answer here. Not a separate crown appointment.

Population
Working figure: 800–1,200
Castle household, estate hands, village households, mill, and the surrounding hamlets of the southern Grasgau.

Economy
Pasture and cultivated land of the Grasgau. Watermill on the Värdå bank. Estate surplus. Road trade from Sūdholt and river traffic across to Sundbruck and Laedburg. The Grasgau’s grain and stock move through here toward the Värdå.

Defences / Access
Castle Vardbucht on the rise above the bank. Estate walls. Village open toward the mill and the river.
Road 15 miles from Sūdholt.
North bank of the Värdå; barges cross or run to Sundbruck and Laedburg on the south bank.
