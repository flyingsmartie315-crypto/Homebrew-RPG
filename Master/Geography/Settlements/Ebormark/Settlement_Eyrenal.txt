# Eyrenal

Type
Castle / Order seat

Location
Local Region: Ebormark
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Hanging vale on the south face of Ebon Spire; the keep is Eyrenhorst

Overview
A closed sun-bowl under the black peak. Meltwater and volcanic soil make a green notch where the mountain should be barren. Outsiders call it the hanging gardens under the eyrie. The Knights Gryphon hold it as their own ground — not a fief of Ostbruck. Gryphons nest on the crags above; the hall and fields sit on the vale floor. Few are invited in. Fewer still are allowed to stay.

Control
Knights Gryphon
Sovereign within the vale and the eyries. No local mark-lord answers here.

Population
About 800–1,500
Order brethren, stable-hands, vale farmers, and a small staff of smiths and stewards. Not a town. Numbers swell when fledglings are reared or when the Order musters.

Economy
Self-supply from the vale fields and herds. Fresh melt-irrigation, hay, garden crops, a little orchard.
Gryphon rearing, training, and the trade in trained mounts and skilled riders.
Stone and labour for Eyrenhorst; little ordinary market. Surplus is political (service, alliance, rare sale of a mount), not cart-loads of grain.

Defences / Access
The mountain is the wall. One main path up from the Spirebeck side; easy to watch, easy to close.
Eyrenhorst: stone keep and yard on a spur inside the vale, above the flood of the melt-burn.
Eyrie-ledges above the gardens. No harbour, no through-road. Deepskaard and Ostbruck are a hard ride below.

Linked Features
- Eyrenal (the vale)
- Ebon Spire (south face)
- Spirebeck (far below)
- Schulterhöhe / Schulterzacken (east, outside the notch)

Notes
Season: Alpine template on the surrounding rock; the vale floor runs a step milder in practice — sun, shelter, water — without changing the printed Alpine tag for the massif.
Do not treat Eyrenal as Ostbruck’s hinterland. Toll and law stop at the notch.
Gryphon upkeep is the real cost: meat, space, and trained people. The gardens exist to keep the Order fed, not to enrich the mark.
Campaign hooks: egg-theft, failed bonding, Ostbruck envoys left waiting at the gate, a melt-year that floods the lower plots, a lean year when the Order must buy grain from the floors below.
