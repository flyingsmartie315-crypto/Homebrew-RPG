# The Twaford Twins

Type
Castle (paired holds)

Location
Local Region: Kannavald Deepwood
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Both banks of the Sund at the head of the Kolvatn outflow; the water-gate of the Deepwood

Overview
Two stone holds face each other across the Sund where the lake begins to run. Together they are the Twaford Twins — the lock on the only water-gate out of Kolvatn toward the north. Traffic on the outflow answers to the bridges and the chains. Halls and stores are built as fortresses, not open yards. This is not a market town; it is a gate with people under it.

Control
[Local Kannavald authority — lock house later]
One hand should hold both banks. If it does not, the Sund becomes an argument.

Population
About 600–1,000 across both sides
Garrison, boatmen, bridge-hands, a few shore folk. Swells when the lake trade is heavy or the north road is open.

Economy
Toll and gate-work on the Sund. Shore-fishing and a little service trade for traffic moving lake-to-north or into Kolby. Not a timber village and not a mine. Supplies come from Kolby and the lake ring.

Defences / Access
Two keeps, one each side of the outflow. Bridges and/or chain between them. Kannavald fortress-building throughout — stone ground, heavy timber, iron-bound doors. The water-gate is the point of the place.

To Oldfort Kilnstead (road)
- From the Twins, 13 miles south to the crossroads (Kolby visible on the western side of the lake from this road).
- From the crossroads, about 13 miles west on the shadowed covered trail into Oldfort Kilnstead (smoke marks the yards).
(Reverse of Oldfort’s road to the Twins.)

To Kolby-on-Vatn (road)
- From the eastern side of the Sund, 7 miles west along the northern shore of Kolvatn to Kolby-on-Vatn.

To Kolby-on-Vatn (water)
- Short lake-work from the head of the Sund into Kolby’s water — lock exact miles on the map if needed; the road figure is the working land line.

To the northern border of Kannavald
- 60 miles north along the western side of the Sund through dense Deepwood to the northern border of the region.
- Long, wet, and thin of people. Not a cart-road for strangers.

Linked Features
- The Sund
- Lake Kolvatn
- Kolmire Fens (south, distant)
- Deepwood north of the outflow

Notes
The Twins are the north door of the lake country. One side without the other is a half-gate.
Campaign hooks: chain cut at night, east and west banks under different loyalties, something coming down the Sund from the north border, Kolby pressing for lower tolls, a boom of timber or ore held between the keeps until dues are paid.
