# Kolholm

Type
other

Location
Local Region: Kannavald Deepwood
Geographic Region: Ebon Peninsula
Position: Kolmire Fens, west of the Rookburn and the Askflow mouth into Kolvatn; the drowned former capital.
The first stretches of the Kolmire lie about 6 miles north-west of Kolby-on-Vatn. The deeper swamp and the ruins of Kolholm sit about 22 miles from Kolby. On clear days the Sunkern Burg is visible across the Kolvatn water.

Overview
Stone and timber under black water and reed. Kolholm was the old seat of this lake country before the floods and the long wet took it. Roofs break the surface in low water; most years only spires, pillars, and the line of a drowned street show. The living call it the Sunkern Burg and leave it alone.

Lore (common knowledge)
Everyone on the lake knows the name. The Burg drowned when the waters rose and the fen spread; the old paved road from the pillar on the Burnford–Kolby trail still points south into the mire and dies after two miles of broken stone. Good people do not go in. Bad luck, fen-sickness, and things that walk the flooded halls are the ordinary talk. Salvage is spoken of in whispers and denied in daylight.

Control
None that the living admit.
The fen holds it. Claims by Kolby or old blood are paper on water.

Population
None permanent.
Occasional scavengers, lost drivers, or fools on the pillar-road. Few return on schedule.

Bestiary
- Fenwights — common in the outer reed and the drowned margins; denser toward the old streets
- [Lake and fen fauna per Bestiary — eels, bog vermin, whatever the Kolmire list holds]
- Unveiling things — rare, tied to deep halls and still nights; do not place on every visit

Environmental hazards
Standing water, soft ground, and sudden deeps over old cellars.
Mist that kills direction.
Flood after thaw; ice traps in Longnight.
Foul air in closed rooms when the water drops.
The pillar-road is a lure: sound paving, then swamp.

Economy
None lawful.
Salvage (stone, old iron, drowned goods) is the only take and is paid for in risk.

Defences / Access
No living gate. Water and reed are the walls.
From Kolby-on-Vatn
- About 6 miles north-west to the first fen ground.
- About 22 miles to the deeper swamp and the ruins.
- Clear days: the Burg can be seen across the lake from Kolby’s water and shore.

From the Burnford–Kolby road
- At about 15 miles from Burnford, the stone pillar and south-pointing sign; old pave runs into the fen and fails after roughly two miles.

From the Askflow / Rookburn water
- The Burg looms west over the confluence and the fen-tip where the Askflow meets Kolvatn.
No safe dock. Small craft only; poles and local knowledge.

Linked Features
- Kolmire Fens
- Rookburn
- Askflow
- Lake Kolvatn
- Burnford (road pillar)
- Kolby-on-Vatn (lake and north-of-fen roads)

Notes
GM: the drowning is history plus Unveiling thinness if you want it — keep the common story simple (flood, fen, curse).
The Burg is a dungeon of streets, half-floors, and underwater doors, not a single keep.
Campaign hooks: a float of timber or ore driven into the fen and lost, a salvage crew that found a dry hall, the pillar-sign turned or replaced, Fenwights thick after a dry summer when more stone shows, something old still counting the rooms under the water.
