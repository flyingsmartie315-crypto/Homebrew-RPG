--------------------------------------------------
BEHAVIOUR PROFILE
--------------------------------------------------

Solitary. A bend of river, a pond, a ford.
It does not patrol. It waits where the
water narrows and the bank is soft.

Hungry: it takes the nearest warm thing
that steps in the water or on the mud.
A goat, a washer, a horse at the drink.
It does not stalk a town. It does not
plan. Intelligence 5.

Met on the bank: it rises, shows the tusks,
and closes. One swing. If the prey runs
into the water, it follows. Amphibious.
It does not drown. It will pull a body
under rather than chase onto open stone.

Hurt: it does not flee at the first wound.
Regeneration is why. It stays while the
knitting is winning. If a blow drops it
under half its Wounds and the next blow
is worse, it sinks. Mud, weed, the deep
of its hole. It does not fight on the
road once it has a pool to hide in.

It does not parley. Fellowship 10.
A thrown carcass may hold it for a
minute. A speech does not.
It will not leave the tagged water to
chase a man past the treeline.

--------------------------------------------------
RUN
--------------------------------------------------

It swings once. Fists or tusks, not both,
unless Attacks rises.
It does not parry. It may still Dodge on
Agility 10.
Under water it does not drown.
In its river it does not roll Survival.
At the start of its Turn, if it is not dead,
Toughness against 90 to knit 4 Wounds.
NAC 4 comes off a blow before Wounds.
A blow that empties 135 is the finishing blow.
Regeneration does not undo slain.
